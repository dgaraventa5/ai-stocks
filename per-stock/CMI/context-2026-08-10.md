## 1. Pre-research mental model (written BEFORE pulling data)

Cummins is a diesel/natural-gas engine maker whose AI-supply-chain relevance runs entirely through
Power Systems — standby and prime generator sets for data centers — while the larger Engine and
Distribution segments remain tied to the North American heavy-duty truck cycle, which has been soft.
My working model was that data-center gensets were a real but secondary growth driver bolted onto a
cyclical industrial, that the truck cycle was the swing factor on the P&L, and that an EPA 2027
emissions pre-buy was a possible 2026 tailwind. I believed Accelera (zero-emission) was a
loss-making drag under restructuring. I did not have a specific contracted hyperscaler commitment
in the model — I assumed data-center demand was book-and-ship, not contracted years forward.
