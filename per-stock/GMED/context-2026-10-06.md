### 2.2 Question 1 — is the revenue slowdown Nevro lapping? **Yes, and the organic rate is now visible.**

- Nevro closed **2025-04-03** (10-Q Note 3). It is therefore in essentially all of both Q2 2025 and Q2 2026. The +27% of Q1 2026 had $82.7M of Nevro against a zero base (prior briefing, Q1 10-Q MD&A); Q2 has none of that help. **The prior briefing's "closed 2026-04-03" was a typo — correct date is 2025-04-03.**
- **Reported +5.9% is effectively the like-for-like growth rate of the combined company.** There is no acquisition contribution left in it.
- **Nevro itself shrank.** 10-Q MD&A: U.S. Nevro sales **down $15.1M**, international Nevro **up $1.5M** → net **−$13.6M**. Management put the decline at 14.3% YoY on the call (transcript, link in §4), which implies Nevro ≈ $95M in Q2 2025 and ≈ $81.5M in Q2 2026 (**my derivation: 13.6 ÷ 0.143; the company does not print the Nevro dollar level in the 10-Q**).
- **Ex-Nevro ("base business") growth ≈ +9%** — stated by the CEO in Ex-99.1 and reproducible: (789.6 − 81.5) ÷ (745.3 − 95.1) − 1 = **+8.9%**.
- Composition (10-Q MD&A, three months): U.S. +3.0% ($619.1M) — spine implants +$32.3M, neuromonitoring +$10.4M, offset by Nevro −$15.1M and **U.S. Enabling Technologies −$12.3M "driven by lower unit placement"**. International +18.0% ($170.5M; +16.2% cc) — Musculoskeletal +$21.2M, Enabling Tech +$3.2M on higher unit placement; Europe/Middle East +$15.7M, LatAm +$8.8M, APAC +$1.4M.
- Ex-99.1: U.S. spine +7%, international spine +14% reported / +12% cc; "fifth straight quarter of above-market" U.S. spine growth (management's characterization).

**Comparison with the prior quarter's organic rate:** Q1 2026 ex-Nevro was +13.2% (prior briefing); Q2 ex-Nevro is ~+9%. So the underlying business did slow by roughly four points even after stripping the acquisition — the Enabling Technologies decline accounts for part of it ($9.1M swing ≈ 1.2 points of growth).

### 2.3 Question 2 — is the −25% GAAP EPS change one-time-dominated? **Yes, unambiguously (rule 15).**

Two disclosed non-operating items sit in the **prior-year** quarter:

1. **Bargain purchase gain, $110.561M** — non-taxable, from the Nevro merger (10-Q Statements of Income; MD&A "Bargain Purchase Gain": "no comparable event in the current period"; Note 3 gives the final gain at $118.8M after measurement-period adjustments). Ex-99.1 values it at **$0.80/share**.
2. **One-time tax benefit, $34.815M** — "state valuation allowance release" (10-Q Note 14 and MD&A income-tax section); Ex-99.1 non-GAAP reconciliation line "Provision for income tax benefit from non-recurring tax adjustments," **$0.26/share**. This is why the Q2 2025 effective tax rate was **−7.8%** vs 20.1% now.

Together **$145.4M = 72% of Q2 2025 net income of $202.8M**. The bargain gain alone ($110.6M) is more than twice the entire YoY decline in net income ($51.3M). The company says so itself: the release attributes the GAAP EPS decrease "primarily" to the prior-year bargain purchase gain (Ex-99.1, second bullet).

Arithmetic on the alternatives:

| Basis | Q2 2025 | Q2 2026 | YoY |
|---|---|---|---|
| GAAP diluted EPS (as yfinance scores it) | $1.49 | $1.10 | **−26.2%** (net income −25.3%) |
| Ex bargain gain only: (202.846 − 110.561) ÷ 136.499 | $0.676 | $1.10 | **+62.7%** |
| Ex bargain gain and tax benefit: (202.846 − 110.561 − 34.815) ÷ 136.499 | $0.421 | $1.10 | +161% |
| Company non-GAAP diluted EPS (Ex-99.1) | $0.86 | $1.34 | **+55.8%** |
| GAAP operating income (no non-operating items) | $76.1M | $182.4M | +139.7% |
| GAAP pre-tax income | $188.2M | $189.8M | +0.9% |

**Why none of the ex-item numbers is a clean substitute:** the prior-year quarter is polluted in *both* directions. Besides the two gains, Q2 2025 also carried $40.4M of merger costs and $19.9M of restructuring (Ex-99.1 reconciliation) that depressed operating income. Removing only the gains leaves a base that is artificially low, so +63% and especially +161% overstate underlying growth. The company's own non-GAAP figure (+55.8%) removes both sides but also strips recurring amortization of intangibles, which this project does not normally accept. The honest range for underlying EPS growth is "strongly positive, roughly +50–60%," not a single defensible number.
