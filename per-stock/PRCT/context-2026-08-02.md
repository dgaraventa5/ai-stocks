## 1. Pre-research mental model (written BEFORE searching)

- PROCEPT is a single-indication surgical robotics company: Aquablation waterjet ablation for BPH, delivered by the AquaBeam and newer HYDROS robotic systems.
- Classic razor-and-blade: place a capital system, then sell a single-use handpiece per procedure. Installed base growth leads procedure growth leads revenue.
- My prior on growth was **too high** — I carried a "+40–50% grower" impression from the 2023–24 period.
- I believed it was still loss-making but with a clear path to breakeven, and that the balance sheet was fine.
- I assumed competitive pressure was modest (UroLift/Rezum are different modalities with weaker durability data).
- I had **no** awareness of any securities litigation, and I assumed the share price was roughly flat-to-up.
- On AI, I vaguely recalled "First Assist" but could not have said what it does.
