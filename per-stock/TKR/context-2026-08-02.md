## 5. Rating implications

- **D1** — Robotics is **~3–6% estimated and entirely undisclosed**; the 10-K never says "humanoid." Cone Drive is described as primarily worm gears and slew drives for solar/oil-and-gas/aerial platforms; the robotics-serving lines (Spinea, CGI, Rollon 7th-axis, thin-section bearings) are real and shipping but subscale inside a 65.5%-bearings company. The estimate straddles the 5% band line. → **2**, and this is the **most DEBATABLE rating in the block** (see below).
- **D2** — "Top 2 strain wave North America" is a **regional** claim in a market where Japan holds ~80–85% globally; the core 65.5% is bearings, a four-player global oligopoly (SKF, Schaeffler, NSK/NTN, JTEKT) where Q1 volumes were flat. → **3**.
- **D3** — Timken brand, 100+ years of bearing metallurgy and application engineering, and a 40% distribution/aftermarket channel are a genuine moat in bearings; **the robotics gearing assets carry no demonstrated moat or share gain**. Blended → **3**.
- **D4** — Capex down three consecutive years against rising sales; the 2028 plan contains no robotics capacity program; capital is going to bolt-on M&A and margin. → **3** (DEBATABLE at 2).
- **D5 (L11 lens, proposed)** — Deck-only claims ("25–30% of humanoid BOM," "designs and prototypes with several U.S. companies") with **no order, no revenue, no named customer, and zero SEC-filing mention**. → **2**.
- **M1** — Q1 adj. EPS beat by **10.6%**; FY2026 guidance **raised** (13% growth at midpoint vs. a prior 8%); **Citi raised its target to $160 from $140**. → **5**.
- **M2** — ~**+29.1% over six months** (from $106.52 on 2026-02-06, an actual Form 4 transaction price, to $137.55 on 2026-07-31) vs **XLI +20%** in 1H 2026 → **~+9pp outperformance**. This is the best-grounded M2 in the block because the six-month-ago price is anchored to a real trade.
- **M3** — See §2. **Zero buys, 12 discretionary (non-10b5-1) sales, $16.8M, three insiders at 21–38% of holdings, into a +98% run, two days before an earnings print.** → **2**.
- **R1** — "No single customer >5%." → **5**.
- **R2** — Worst plausible shock: **a US–China rupture**, which would strand **$255.8M of net PP&E in China (18.8% of the total)** and impair the China portion of **20.0% Asia-Pacific revenue**. Currently absorbed and then some — tariffs are a **net +$0.15 EPS tailwind** and pricing added $32M. Two legs (demand ~20%, policy absorbed) plus a partial asset leg. → **3**, DEBATABLE at 2.
- **R3** — Net debt $1,725.4M, **2.15x** net leverage, 8.18x coverage, spread maturities, $670.3M undrawn. Squarely "average leverage for sector, ND/EBITDA 2–4x." → **3**.
- **R4** — Ordinary-course claims only; management states no material adverse effect; no material change to risk factors. → **4**.
- **R5** — Bearings and gearing are physical goods; agentic AI is demand, not substitution. → **5**.
