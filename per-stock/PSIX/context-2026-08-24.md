### Q2 2026 results — sequential recovery, still down year over year
| Metric | Q2 2026 | Q1 2026 | Q2 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net sales | **$152.5M** | $128.6M | ~$193M |
| | | **+18.6% sequential** | **−21% YoY** |
| Gross profit | **$41.4M** | $29.4M | — |
| Gross margin | **27.1%** | 22.9% | 28.2% |
| Operating income | **$23.9M** | $11.4M | — |
| Net income | **$16.9M** | $7.3M | — |

(Revenue/GP/OI/NI from yfinance quarterly statements, quarter ended 2026-06-30; margins and YoY per
the Q2 release and call.)

- **Gross margin recovered 420bps sequentially** (22.9% → 27.1%), which management attributed to
  "early benefits of operational improvement initiatives." Still **−110bps YoY** (28.2% → 27.1%) on
  a lower oil-and-gas mix and elevated Wisconsin production costs.
- **The YoY decline is attributed to shipment timing for data-center products and oil-and-gas
  softness** — the same "lumpiness, not demand loss" framing as Q1.
- **Net income more than doubled sequentially** ($7.3M → $16.9M). Q2 topped consensus.
- **Debt reduced** during the quarter (per the Q2 call summary).

### Guidance — still no formal full-year number
Management **continues to decline formal FY2026 guidance**, citing "ongoing variability in order
timing and market conditions." Directional guidance only: **H2 2026 sales are expected to exceed H1
and to be broadly in line with H2 2025**, as larger power-systems orders move into production.

**This is the third consecutive quarter with no formal guidance.** For a name whose entire thesis is
a data-center ramp, and which is defending a securities class action alleging it overstated its
ability to meet demand, the refusal to quantify is itself information — it is consistent, cautious,
and it caps how much the "H2 recovery" claim can be relied on.

### ⚠️ NEW CEO — 8-K filed 2026-07-27 (Item 5.02)
- On **2026-07-23** the Board appointed **Nan (Richard) Hu, age 52, as Chief Executive Officer,
  effective on commencement of employment 2026-08-17**, at which point he becomes principal executive
  officer, reporting directly to the Board.
- **Xun (Kenneth) Li ceased serving as Interim CEO** on that date and **continues as CFO** — i.e. the
  combined interim-CEO/CFO role is being unwound, which is a governance improvement.
- Hu was identified through the previously disclosed CEO succession search run with **Spencer Stuart**.
- Background: **25+ years across Asia, Europe and the Americas; six years at BorgWarner (2020–2026)**,
  most recently VP & GM of the Americas Region for the Turbo & Thermal Technology business unit, with
  full P&L accountability for a multi-billion-dollar operation spanning multiple plants and technical
  centers.

**Read:** a permanent, externally recruited, operations-heavy CEO with large-scale manufacturing P&L
experience — arriving precisely when the company's stated problem is manufacturing capacity and cost
at the Wisconsin facility. That is a well-matched hire on paper. It is also a change of principal
executive officer one week before this briefing, so there is no track record yet.

is still deeply negative, guidance is still withheld for a third quarter, and the whole thesis still
rests on data-center order timing the company will not quantify. The CEO change is the genuine new
fact: an outside operator with multi-billion-dollar manufacturing P&L experience arriving into a
manufacturing-cost problem.
