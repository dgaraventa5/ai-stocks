My prior: FY26 Q4 revenue ~$87-90B, Azure ~+38-40%, capex ~$35-40B in the quarter."

## 6. Implications for ratings (flag only — no ratings decided here)

- **D1 (AI revenue %)** — Azure at $100B+ with 43% growth and 30M Copilot seats is the strongest AI-revenue evidence MSFT has produced. Revisit.
- **D5 (AI-buildout tie)** — unchanged in substance. **Do not mark D5 down on the $175B headline**; the underlying commitment did not move. This is the single most likely error in the next rating pass, for MSFT *and* for every supplier whose D5 keys off hyperscaler capex.
- **R5 (disruption risk)** — MSFT is Layer 9, so rule 16 defaults it to 5 and nothing here disturbs that.
- **R1 (customer concentration)** — the end of Azure exclusivity slightly loosens the OpenAI dependency in both directions. Marginal, worth a look.
- **Quality inputs** — the 15→25 year useful-life extension will flatter forward margins and ROIC mechanically. Rule-9 objective refresh should note it so a margin improvement isn't read as operating leverage.
- **Momentum** — objective 50DMA% refresh due alongside the rule-9 pass (rule 11).

**Thesis-break signals: none.** Accelerating Azure, near-doubling RPO, and a bottom-third multiple. The only thing to argue about is the earnings-quality effect of the depreciation-life change.
