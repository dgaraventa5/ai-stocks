## 2. Latest filing highlights

**10-K FY2026 (filed 2026-07-29, cached `filings/10-K_2026-07-29_0001193125-26-323660.htm`):**
- **Revenue and segments.** Revenue was $331.8B, up from $281.7B. Operating income was $155.2B (Item 8, segment note). Intelligent Cloud revenue rose $31.5B (+30%), and "Azure and other cloud services revenue grew 41%" (Item 7 MD&A, Intelligent Cloud). Microsoft Cloud revenue was $214.4B (+27%). Commercial RPO rose 84% to $678B (Item 7 highlights).
- **Cloud gross margin.** Microsoft Cloud gross margin fell to **66%**, "driven by continued investments in AI infrastructure and growing AI product usage" (Item 7).
- **Capex and leases.** Additions to property and equipment were **$115.9B**, against $64.6B in FY25 and $44.5B in FY24 (cash-flow statement). The 10-K also discloses **$329.1B of additional leases, primarily for datacenters, that had not yet commenced**. These commence between FY2027 and FY2033 with lease terms of 1 to 20 years (Item 8, Leases note, just before Note 14). This is a contracted, multi-year buildout commitment. It is the most concrete D4/D5 evidence in the filing.
- **Customer concentration.** "No sales to an individual customer or country other than the United States accounted for more than 10% of revenue for fiscal years 2026, 2025, or 2024." US revenue was $170.8B (51%) and other countries $161.0B (Item 8, segment note).
- **OpenAI.** The partnership was extended in October 2025 and April 2026. Microsoft "will continue to receive revenue-sharing payments" and holds "rights to OpenAI's intellectual property, including models and infrastructure" (Item 7 overview). New risk-factor language says that because of capacity constraints, Microsoft "may modify capacity allocations… Strategic partners and other customers may likewise adjust their purchasing decisions" (Item 1A).
- **Cash.** Cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments were $76.8B, down from $94.6B. Equity and other investments were $36.3B, up from $15.4B (Item 7 liquidity).
- **Contingencies (Note 14).** The note covers the Irish DPC/LinkedIn GDPR fine appeal plus "other claims and suits" in the ordinary course. None is called material.

**8-K 2026-09-02 (Item 7.01, cached):** "Beginning in fiscal year 2027… two reportable segments: (1) Agents and Infra and (2) Devices and Consumer." The investor deck (Ex. 99.1) was not cached; details come from secondary sources in §3.
