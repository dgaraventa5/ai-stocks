## Latest results highlights

**Most recent quarterly: Q1 FY2026, quarter ended June 30, 2026 — announced 2026-07-29.** All figures JPY as reported (USD sense-checks at ~¥150/USD are approximate, not sourced).

- **Consolidated revenue ¥2,709.5B, +20% YoY** — record first quarter; all four sectors (DSS, Energy, Mobility, Connective Industries) grew double digits (Investing.com Q1 FY2026 slides, 2026-07-29; Hitachi 2026_1Q.pdf).
- **Adjusted EBITA margin +140bps YoY to 11.9%**, also a record Q1 (Investing.com Q1 FY2026 slides, 2026-07-29).
- **Guidance RAISED across the board:** full-year revenue lifted **+¥600B to ¥11,700B** (~+11% YoY, ~9% organic); adjusted EBITA lifted **+¥100B to ¥1,520B** (+¥208.5B YoY), adj EBITA margin outlook ~13%; net income, core FCF and ROIC outlooks all raised despite higher capex. Cited drivers: stronger-than-planned Q1, order trends, revised FX (Investing.com "revenue jumps 20% as outlook rises" transcript, 2026-07-29; TipRanks, 2026-07-29).
- **Energy segment: Power Grids business +37% revenue growth**, led by large-scale **HVDC projects in Europe**, pushing **group order backlog above ¥10 trillion** (~$67B) (BigGo Finance, 2026-07-29).
- **Shareholder returns:** ¥147.9B buyback executed in Q1 (30.1M shares, +18.2M YoY) = 27% of the ¥550B FY buyback target (Investing.com Q1 FY2026 slides, 2026-07-29).

**Prior April print resolved:** the June briefing's unverified "EPS-miss/revenue-beat" headline was on the FY2025 full-year release; the subsequent Q1 print is an unambiguous beat + guidance raise, so that soft watch item is closed as immaterial.
