## 3. What happened since 2026-09-08

**Cursor**
1. **OpenAI is pulling its models from Cursor.** Announced 2026-08-28; proposed shut-off 2026-11-12; future models (including "Astra") withheld. OpenAI's stated reason: it "cannot be confident that SpaceX will use our technology within our terms of service" ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/our-decision-on-cursor-following-its-acquisition-by-spacex/); [CNBC, 2026-08-29](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/29/openai-cursor-spacex-model-access.html)). Cursor's CEO says OpenAI models are about 5% of user traffic (secondary coverage).
2. **Anthropic is staying**, says it will add compute behind Claude in Cursor, and listed Cursor in its Claude Marketplace on 2026-09-09 ([Cryptopolitan](https://www.cryptopolitan.com/anthropic-keeps-cursor-openai-pulls-out/); [Runtime Wire](https://runtimewire.com/article/anthropic-claude-marketplace-spacex-cursor-vercel-crowdstrike)). Anthropic is also reported as a compute customer on SpaceX's Colossus cluster ([Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/anthropic-taps-spacexs-colossus-supercomputer-to-solve-claude-compute-crunch/)). Net: Cursor's product now leans on one outside lab that is also a SpaceX customer.
3. **OpenAI is hiring Cursor's sales leadership.** Cursor's EMEA head joined OpenAI on 2026-10-08, the second sales executive taken from SpaceX in a month; Cursor's former president of global revenue is now OpenAI's VP of worldwide sales ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-ismail-elmas-emea-sales-spacex-cursor)).
4. No post-close Cursor revenue figure exists; it is now inside the AI segment. First consolidated quarter is Q3 (about half a quarter, from 2026-08-14).

**Grok Bot**
5. Launched in beta mid-August 2026 as an agent that runs on a dedicated cloud computer and operates apps, inboxes and websites; priced around $120 a month ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/spacexais-grok-bot-turns-agents-into-persistent-digital-coworkers-that-can-operate-your-apps-for-120-per-month); [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/grok-bot-agent/)).

## 6. Implications for ratings (flags only)

- **D1 (4):** holds on the corrected 28.1%. Cursor at about $1B a quarter would lift it toward 36–40% in Q4. The September rationale needs its numbers corrected (ads excluded; Cursor size). Under the 2026-10-10 ad-platform convention the X advertising line is not AI revenue — already excluded above.
- **D2 (4) / D3 (4):** mixed. For: Cursor is larger than the briefing assumed; Grok Bot has traction. Against: Cursor depends on outside labs' models, one has left and is recruiting its sales team; Grok Bot's economics are undisclosed. The September text said Cursor "meaningfully strengthens" the moat on a figure a quarter of the real one, and without the supplier risk. Hold both; revisit after Q3.
- **D4 (5) / D5 (5):** reinforced by the $40B chip financing and the capacity target.
- **R3 (5): the rating most out of line.** Band 5 is "net cash, strong FCF generation, no debt concerns". FCF is deeply negative and the credit market is pricing concern (CDS at a record). The GOOGL ruling of 2026-10-06/10 turned on exactly this clause. Band 4 fits while net cash holds; the Q3 balance sheet may argue for 3.
- **M2 (2):** since listing the stock is +1.5% vs IGV +22.7% = about −21pp, which is band 1 by a point; against QQQ it is in line (3). Fewer than 126 trading days exist, so the standard window cannot be measured yet.
- **M1 (4), M3 (3):** no new print; lockup runs to about 2026-12-15.
- **R1 (3):** the AI-infrastructure line's buyers are undisclosed; if a single lab is most of $2.2B a quarter, that is concentration the current rating does not see. Check the Q3 10-Q.
- **R4 (3):** OpenAI's public statement about contract compliance by Musk companies is reputational, not legal. FCC approval pending on spectrum. No securities case found.
- **R5 (4):** unchanged.
- **Rule 35:** a $60B all-stock acquisition closed after the last reported quarter, plus $8B of spectrum and a $40B debt plan — "transformative transaction since the last reported quarter". Entry hold recorded to 2026-11-10 (after Q3 results). SPCX ranks about 104, so the hold is precautionary.
