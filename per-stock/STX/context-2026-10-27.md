## 4. Balance sheet and capital return

- Retired about $1.2B of debt principal in the quarter (gross debt $2.4B), then another $750M after quarter end, which brings **gross debt to $1.6B**. Cash at quarter end was $1.3B.
- Returned $568M through dividends and buybacks. The dividend rises about 3% to $0.76 per quarter (payable 2027-01-07).
- 230M shares outstanding.
- The release still references quarterly payments under the BIS settlement agreement (an existing item, not new).

## 5. What changed vs the prior briefing (2026-08-17)

- The margin path keeps beating the prior model. 2026-08-17 recorded 52.7% as "13th straight quarter of gains"; this is the 14th, and the step is the largest yet.
- CEO language moved from "sold out" to "long term customer demand visibility … a multi-year growth phase". HAMR (Mozaic) is again credited for areal-density growth with supply discipline.
- Deleveraging is close to finished. Net debt is now small relative to about $6B/yr of FCF at the current run rate.
