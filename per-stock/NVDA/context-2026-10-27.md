## 5. DIFF vs mental model, and the capitulation argument

| Prior belief | Fresh evidence | Δ |
|---|---|---|
| Q3 guide $108B is the forward anchor | FY28 is guided to about +70% against ~45% consensus; FY28 EPS consensus up about 24–29% | **Large.** The forward base moved, not just the quarter. |
| Gross margin dips 100bps in Q3 | The path runs to **71–72% in Q4** and 72–73% in FY28 (secondary source) | **Moderate.** This is a multi-quarter memory-cost absorption, not one step. |
| Top customer ~13–16% | Q2 single direct customer 16%; H1 16/15/13% (10-Q) | Small. In line. |
| Stock lagging all year | It made a record close of $239.24 on 2026-10-06, then fell about 10% | Moderate. The lag is no longer one-directional. |
| Litigation = 2018 crypto case + DOJ Groq probe | A new 2026 securities case (*Hu*, 5:26-cv-05496) and a cluster of patent and copyright suits | Small-moderate. Allegations only. |

**Why the market is this pessimistic.** P/S at the 3.9th percentile of the 3-year range is mostly arithmetic: revenue roughly doubled (Q2 +106% YoY) while the price went sideways. The question is why investors refuse to capitalize that revenue. I see four reasons, and each is a real argument, not noise:

1. **Peak-cycle discounting.** The market is pricing FY27–28 revenue as a capex-cycle peak, not a run-rate. The open question is whether hyperscaler capex funded with debt and thin FCF can keep compounding (the Fool AMZN example).
2. **Financing perimeter.** Of the commitments, $108.5B of guarantees, $279B of purchase commitments, 60-day DSO, terms of up to a year, and equity stakes in customers make part of the revenue look vendor-financed. A low multiple is the market's discount for revenue quality. TTM FCF/NI at 66% is the first hard datapoint behind that worry.
3. **Margin has peaked.** The guided glide from 75% to 71–72% says pricing power is being shared with the memory suppliers.
4. **Share erosion at the margin** from TPU, Trainium and AMD as the second source. This caps the terminal multiple even if units grow.

**Against the bear case:** growth is *accelerating* on a $96B base, and the FY28 guide is supply-constrained. Revisions are running 42 up and 0 down. FCF is still enormous ($70B in H1). None of the four concerns shows up yet in reported revenue. My read: the flag is a legitimate trough-multiple-on-intact-fundamentals setup, *not* a value trap of the secular-decline type. But the pessimism has identifiable, falsifiable causes. The tells are Q3 DSO/FCF conversion and whether the Q4 margin bottom holds at 71–72%. Under rule 32-A this stays qualitative. It does not support any rating change.
