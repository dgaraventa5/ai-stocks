## 3. What changed vs the 2026-11-12 briefing

- **Demand question answered for now:** the 11-12 briefing's first tell ("is demand still supply-constrained?") — the beat, the $130B guide, inventory up $31.6B → $39.7B "to support our Rubin product ramp" and supply commitments up $279B → **$390B** ("primarily related to the multi-year procurement of memory supply") all point to demand still ahead of supply. Kress (CNBC): chips, packaging and memory are "all... coming together".
- **Commitments keep growing:** total future commitments $518B (supply $390B, cloud service $28B, DC leases not commenced $74B, equity investments $20B, capex $6B); neocloud agreements $32B; guarantees $118.0B max gross exposure (SB Energy $105.0B, neocloud land/power/shell $3.5B, new "power and systems credit support" $9.5B). No guarantee payments made to date.
- **Cash flow recovered:** OCF $48.3B (Q2 $24.1B), FCF $43.8B (Q2 $21.3B). DSO 64 days (from 60), attributed to Rubin shipment linearity and "extended payment terms on large, multi-quarter agreements with certain investment-grade customers" — the extended-terms language is new and worth watching. Q3 FCF ÷ GAAP net income = 67% (single quarter; GAAP NI includes $1.77B equity gains). TTM FCF-conversion re-test deferred to the re-score with full statements.
- **Capital return:** $28.1B returned in Q3; buyback authorization +$150B (September), $227.3B remaining. Cash and marketable securities $53.5B (from $56.6B).
- **Leadership (CNBC, not in the filing):** board chair Harvey Jones said the board does not intend to reinstate Huang and that he resigned after disclosing personal conduct inconsistent with the code of conduct; Heidrick & Struggles hired for the CEO search (on 11-13 the board had told CNBC no firm was picked). Huang said he would be "honored" to return if the board decides so. The board formed a committee overseeing national-security and export-control compliance.
- **Megaspeed (CNBC):** DOJ and Commerce are investigating Megaspeed; NVIDIA put it on its "no sell" list in June 2025 (company account). No agency action naming NVIDIA itself is reported.
- **Hugging Face** acquisition still pending (listed in the press release highlights).
- After hours: shares up ~3–4% (CNBC; TradingKey).
