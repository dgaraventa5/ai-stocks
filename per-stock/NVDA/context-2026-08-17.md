## 1. Pre-research mental model (verbatim, written before any data pull)

> "NVIDIA is the merchant AI accelerator monopoly, ~87-90% of revenue from Data Center, riding the Blackwell/Blackwell-Ultra cycle with Vera Rubin as the next platform. Customer concentration is extreme — four hyperscalers plus the neoclouds are the bulk of the book, and all four hyperscalers are simultaneously building competing custom silicon (TPU, Trainium, Maia, MTIA) with Broadcom/Marvell as their design partners. China is structurally impaired: H20 licensing, a $4.5B charge in FY26 Q1, and Blackwell-class parts barred. My prior: Q1 FY27 revenue somewhere in the $75-85B range with Data Center ~87% of it, gross margin low-to-mid 70s, and the debate being not 'is demand real' but 'how much of the demand is circular vendor-financed neocloud/OpenAI capacity that NVIDIA is itself underwriting.' The moat is CUDA plus systems-level integration (NVLink, networking), not the die."

## 6. Implications for ratings (flag only — no ratings decided here)

- **R1 (customer concentration)** — the sharpest question on the name. The $105B RVG converts an *equity-style* exposure to OpenAI into a *credit-style* one: NVIDIA now eats a shortfall if OpenAI can't pay rent on 4.25 GW starting 2028. That is concentration risk of a different kind than "40% of revenue from four buyers." Revisit.
- **R3 (balance sheet)** — a $105B contingent obligation is not debt and won't show in ND/EBITDA, which is exactly the sort of thing rule 23 warns R3 exists to catch ("holistic maturities/dilution vs the raw ratio"). Revisit whether NVDA's R3 should still read as pristine.
- **D3 (moat)** — custom-ASIC share at 15-20% growing 44.6% is the first datapoint I'd call genuinely moat-relevant rather than narrative. Revisit against the absolute lens, not relative to peers.
- **D5 (AI-buildout tie)** — unambiguously reinforced; NVIDIA is now literally underwriting the buildout's real estate.
- **R4 (regulatory)** — direction of travel is loosening (H200 licensing) but with a new 25% tariff; net probably unchanged, worth an explicit look.
- **M-dimensions** — hold until the 2026-08-26 print; re-rating momentum now would be scoring into an event.

**Thesis-break signals: none.** The RVG is a large new risk *disclosure*, not a break — demand, share, and margin are all intact and the multiple is at a 3-year-low percentile. But it is the kind of disclosure that should be argued about explicitly rather than absorbed as "more AI good news."
