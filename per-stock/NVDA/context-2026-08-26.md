## 2. Guidance

**Q3 FY2027:** revenue **$108.0B ±2%** (+12% q/q off a $96.2B base), GAAP and non-GAAP gross margin **74.0% ±50bps**, GAAP opex ~$9.2B / non-GAAP ~$9.0B. FY27 tax rate 16.0–18.0%.

Two things carry the signal:
1. **"NVIDIA is not assuming any Data Center compute revenue from China in its outlook"** — the $108B is a zero-China number. China is now an option, not a line item.
2. **Gross margin guided DOWN 100bps** (75.0% → 74.0%), the first guided step-down in the Blackwell cycle. Read against the commitments disclosure below ("primarily related to the procurement of memory"), this looks like HBM/memory cost inflation passing through, not mix or pricing weakness.

Management framing (Huang, EX-99.1): "AI has reached its inflection point… Now, compute is revenue… This time last year, one lab alone was driving the buildout; today, we have a golden age of new AI labs and startups, multiple frontier labs scaling in parallel." Vera Rubin described as "now in full production."

## 4. Implications for ratings — flag only, nothing changed here

- **R1 (customer concentration)** — genuinely two-sided now, and the sharpest item. Diversifying: ACIE is 45% of Data Center and outgrowing Hyperscale 2:1, so the "four buyers" framing is weakening on its own terms. Concentrating: those ACIE buyers are AI natives and sovereigns, i.e. weaker credit than hyperscalers, and NVDA is extending them 60-day terms while guaranteeing $105B of one customer's leases. Revisit with both directions explicit — do not net them out silently.
- **R3 (balance sheet)** — the item rule 23 says R3 exists to catch. ND/EBITDA remains trivially clean and will keep reading "pristine," while off it sit **$366B of future commitments, $108.5B of guarantees, and $32.4B of new long-term debt**. Whether R3 should still read pristine is now a real question, not a formality. Note the guarantee genuinely is capped, phased, conditional on ready-for-service, and reimbursable — it is not $105B of expected loss.
- **D1 (AI revenue %)** — Data Center at 92.5% of revenue, up from ~87%. Mechanically stronger.
- **D2 (supply-chain position) / D3 (moat)** — the $279B commitment book, "primarily memory," is a two-edged datapoint: locking multi-year HBM supply is a bottleneck-control move (D2 positive), but the simultaneous 100bps gross-margin guide-down says NVDA is absorbing memory cost rather than passing it through, which is a pricing-power datapoint against the maximal moat reading. Also fold in the Groq absorption. Revisit against the absolute lens.
- **D5 (AI-buildout tie)** — reinforced again, and now quantified from the company's own mouth: $150–200B of NVIDIA revenue per infrastructure generation at one campus. Already at the top of the scale; the question is only whether anything could displace it.
- **R4 (regulatory)** — China now guided at zero for Q3 and ran <1% of Data Center in Q2. The regulatory risk has largely been *realized*, which paradoxically lowers forward sensitivity: there is no China revenue left to lose, only optionality to regain.
- **M-dimensions (M1 EPS revisions, M2 relative strength, M3 insider activity)** — the event has now passed, so the 2026-08-17 "hold until the print" freeze lifts. A $108B guide against a $100B-ish street number should move M1 hard. Correct sequencing is still the sentinel's mechanical re-score on the first post-reaction close (rule 31), not a re-rate tonight.
- **R5** — unchanged at 5; NVDA is not Layer 10 (rule 16 scope).
- **Rule 15 (EPS YoY blanking) — does NOT trigger.** GAAP EPS +128% y/y is inflated by $7.8B of equity-securities gains, but non-GAAP EPS grew +120% y/y on the same base. The YoY change is therefore operating-dominated, not one-time-dominated; the rule-15 test is dominance, not presence. Recording the check so it is not re-litigated at the rescore.

## 5. Watch items for the next pass

1. **DSO 60 days and the AR/FCF gap.** AR $63.1B on $96.2B of quarterly revenue, FCF down 56% q/q. Benign if it is investment-grade terms as stated; the tell is whether DSO stabilizes or keeps climbing in Q3 alongside the ACIE mix shift.
2. **Gross margin at 74.0%.** Whether the memory pass-through is one step or the start of a glide path.
3. **The financing perimeter.** $500B third-party platforms are "subject to definitive agreements" — unsigned as of this filing. Track whether they convert.
4. **Equity portfolio mark sensitivity.** $94B combined marketable + non-marketable equity securities now sits inside GAAP net income via fair-value gains ($23.7B YTD). GAAP EPS is becoming a partly mark-driven number; prefer non-GAAP for scoring continuity.
5. **10-Q (acc 0001045810-26-000075)** filed today, not yet read. Worth a pass for the guarantee accounting treatment and any related-party detail on OpenAI/Groq.
