## 1. Pre-research mental model (written BEFORE searching — honest)

I knew UBTech as a Shenzhen consumer/education robot maker that IPO'd in Hong Kong in Dec 2023 and has been pivoting toward industrial humanoids (Walker series). My working assumption was that humanoid revenue was still a small slice of a ~RMB 1–2B revenue base dominated by education robots and "smart logistics solutions," that the widely-reported order announcements were largely state-linked demonstration deals rather than ROI-driven purchases, and that losses were large and equity-funded. I had no view on any US regulatory action against Chinese humanoids, and I assumed UBTech was the clear listed leader in humanoid units.

**Two of those priors turned out wrong** (humanoids are now the *largest* revenue line; UBTech is #3 by units, not #1) and one turned out newly and materially more severe (US regulatory action landed four days ago).

## 4. DIFF

**vs. the universe appendix** (`11-robotics/robotics-universe-2026-08-02.md`): the appendix's framing — "only listed pure-play industrial humanoid maker with real order flow… >RMB 800M orders, 5,000-unit 2026 capacity plan" — is accurate but incomplete in three ways that matter for ratings:
- It omits that UBTech is **third by units** behind two private Chinese competitors, at 7.5% share.
- It flags "PRC geopolitical/export risk" generically; that risk **crystallised on 2026-07-28** into a named FCC Covered-List action. The appendix predates it by days.
- It does not carry the consumer-U1 launch, its 13,361 pre-orders, or the immediate low-cost clone response.
The appendix's "heavily loss-making; state-linked buyer concentration" caution is confirmed and now quantified (State Grid RMB 2.5B humanoid tranche).

**vs. my pre-research model:** I was too dismissive. Humanoids are genuinely the largest revenue line (RMB 821M, 41%, up 22x) and 1,079 delivered units is a real number, not a demo. My state-linked-demand suspicion held. My assumption that UBTech led the field did not.
