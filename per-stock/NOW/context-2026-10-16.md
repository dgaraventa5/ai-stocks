## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before pulling data)

NOW is the leading enterprise-workflow platform (ITSM/HRSD/CSM/ITOM), growing subscription revenue ~20–22% with ~97% renewal. The AI angle is Now Assist / Pro Plus and AI Agents converting the installed base into incremental ACV, with a stated $1B+ (stretch $1.5B) Now Assist ACV target for 2026 — still well under 10% of revenue. Most recent quarter I know well is Q1 FY26 (sub rev +22%, cRPO +23%, stock −17% on the beat amid "AI eats SaaS" fears); Armis ($7.75B) and Veza were being acquired, funded by a $4B term loan. Open questions: whether agentic AI erodes seat pricing (R5), whether the stock is still at a trough multiple, and whether insiders have done anything other than routine selling.

## 6. Implications for ratings (flags only — proposals delivered separately)

- **D1 (AI Rev %):** (a) AI-revenue evidence: AI ACV >$1B vs ~$15.8B annual sub revenue ≈ 6–7% now, ~9.5% if the $1.5B YE target lands → still the 5–10% band. (b) Forward optionality (2030 ~30% of ACV, Action Fabric consumption metering) is real but is optionality, not mix. D1 = 2 holds.
- **D2/D3:** 98% renewal and AI-SKU price uplift support the moat; no change signal. D3 bias check (would I rate it 5 if the stock were down 40%? — it was, and renewal held) passes.
- **D4:** own capex is *falling* (6M $255M vs $395M) as hosting shifts toward hyperscalers; expansion is via ~$10B+ of acquisitions, not capacity. Literal rubric reads steady-state.
- **D5:** supplier lens (Layer 10, Dom 2026-10-16). Customers are enterprises; hyperscalers/model labs are partners. Cohort convention ("intermediated chain") = 3 for CRM/SNOW/PLTR/NOW.
- **M1:** net negative (FY26 −1.5% over 90d, concentrated right after Q2); M2: sharply positive vs IGV; M3: CEO $3M open-market buy.
- **R1:** 13% top customer (<15% band) but rising and USG-linked; **R2:** ~25% EMEA, no China leg; **R3:** net cash → modest net debt; **R4:** active DOJ investigation (existing, not new); **R5:** contested-but-moated — no erosion visible (98% renewal, price uplift), core seat model still a direct agentic target.
- **Rule 15 (EPS YoY):** Q2 GAAP EPS $0.29 vs $0.37 includes a $273M unrealized gain on strategic investments (≈$0.26/share pre-tax on ~1.034B diluted shares) and $219M of new acquisition amortization + $75M deal costs + $62M severance. The non-operating gain masks most of the operating decline; ex-gain EPS falls far more than −22%. Candidate for a Dom ruling (blank or ex-item value) — the Q3 print on 10-28 will roll the quarter anyway.
- **Entry hold (rule 35):** no new enforcement action, no securities case found in the last 90 days (docket check skipped — re-run), no guidance cut (FY guide raised), no transformative deal since Q2 was reported (Armis closed in Q2, inside the reported quarter). → none.
