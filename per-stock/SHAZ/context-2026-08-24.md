## 5. Implications for ratings (flag only — NOT pre-decided)

- **D1 (AI revenue %)** — 100% AI by construction; not a live question. Note the base is trivially small.
- **D5 (strength of AI-buildout tie)** — the clearest upward pressure in this rotation. Under the
  rule-27/rule-23 "quantified and contracted, not deck-only" standard, SHAZ now has a **delivered and
  accepted** deployment plus $8.8B of disclosed TCV — a materially different evidentiary class than
  the June "announced collaboration."
- **R3 (balance-sheet risk)** — genuinely two-sided and the most debatable call here. Cash went from
  nothing to $1.86B (strongly better), but a **$1.0B convertible note liability** that grows with the
  share price, plus $438M of pre-funded warrants, is a large and reflexive claim on equity holders.
  Do not rate R3 on the cash balance alone.
- **R1 (customer concentration)** — extreme and unquantified. Revenue is about to come from a handful
  of named mega-contracts (NVIDIA-linked, one $950M take-or-pay, one $1.32B AI Lab). The 10-Q has **no
  customer-concentration note** (zero hits on "concentration") — a disclosure gap worth flagging.
- **R4 (regulatory/geographic)** — new geographic spread: Australia, **New Zealand**, ESDS/India-linked
  capex. Not previously scoped.
