## Implications for ratings (flags only — no numeric ratings set)

- **AI-Thesis D1 (AI-revenue directness / demand):** Reinforced positive. Backlog rebuilt to ~$15B, TTM organic orders ~+81%, liquid cooling in the majority of AI engagements, second FY raise. No change to the directness story.
- **AI-Thesis D2 (supply-chain position / demand magnitude):** Reinforced. The UIG deal moves VRT upstream into the grid-interconnection bottleneck ("time to power"), the single scarcest input to AI datacenter deployment — widens the value captured per site. Positive if it closes and integrates.
- **AI-Thesis D3 (moat):** Mildly reinforced — microgrid controls/orchestration + behind-the-meter switchgear broaden the integrated power-cooling-controls stack and NVIDIA-roadmap alignment (800VDC H2 26). Watch integration/execution risk on a $1.45B+ deal.
- **AI-Thesis D5 (AI-buildout tie strength):** Reinforced — explicitly positioned to solve grid-constrained AI capacity; still supplier-side 5-scale evidence.
- **Momentum:** Mixed signal to watch. Fundamentals strong (EPS beat, second raise) supports EPS-revisions/relative-strength, BUT the Q2 **revenue miss + growth-rate deceleration (30%→24%)** and the stock reaction are the countervailing data. Refresh objective **50DMA %** (`scripts/momentum_50dma.py`) at scoring time before trusting the Momentum inputs — the price trend may have wobbled around the print.
- **Risk R1–R4:** No downgrade signal; balance sheet still strong (adj FCF guide $2.4–2.6B, dividend maintained). **New watch-items, not downgrades:** (a) execution/integration + leverage from the $1.45B cash + $1.15B earnout UIG deal; (b) supply-chain congestion / project-timing slippage that caused the Q2 revenue miss — monitor whether it persists into Q3 (guide $3.65–3.85B). R5 (Disruption) defaults to 5 (non-Layer-10 physical infra, rule 16) — no change.
- **Rule 14 (expectations / what's-priced-in):** Still the central risk. Crowded consensus long on a high multiple; a revenue miss did not break the thesis but shows the downside sensitivity when execution slips against priced-in perfection. Run `scripts/expectations_flag.py VRT` at scoring time.

**Thesis-break scan:** NONE. No customer loss, no contract cancellation, no severe regulatory action, no going-concern, no guidance cut — guidance was RAISED for the second straight quarter and backlog re-accelerated to ~$15B. The only yellow flags are (1) a Q2 revenue **miss on project/supply-chain timing** with growth decelerating 30%→24%, and (2) integration/leverage risk on the pending $1.45B+ UIG acquisition — both watch-items, neither a break.
