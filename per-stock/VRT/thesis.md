## 1. One-line thesis

> Near-pure-play supplier of the power and cooling gear every AI data center needs (liquid cooling, UPS, busway, switchgear): Q2 2026 sales grew +24% (+18% organic) with organic orders +152% and backlog back to ~$15B, gross margin expanded to 37.7%, and FY2026 guidance was raised again to ~$14.0B sales; the pending ~$2.6B UtilityInnovation Group deal extends it from cooling racks into on-site power, the constraint now gating AI build-outs. Main caution is price — it is a crowded, richly valued consensus AI long.

---

## 2. Position in the AI supply chain

- **What they do:** Designs, builds and services the power and thermal systems inside data centers — liquid and air cooling, uninterruptible power supplies, busway, switchgear — plus the service contracts around them.
- **Where they sit:** Layer 03, between the grid/power layer and the servers. Every GPU rack that gets installed needs power delivery and heat removal sized to it, so Vertiv's demand follows AI capacity additions directly.
- **Unique vs. commoditized:** Breadth (power + thermal + service from one vendor) and co-design with NVIDIA on 800VDC and liquid-cooling reference architectures; management says liquid cooling is now in the majority of AI engagements (Q2 2026 call coverage). Individual product lines do have competitors (Schneider, Eaton, others).
- **AI revenue mix:** Not disclosed as a single percentage — flagged gap. Evidence of directness: organic orders +152% YoY "driven by demand for AI-focused data centers" (Yahoo Finance, retrieved 2026-09-21); Americas $2,070.8M of $3,274.3M Q2 sales (10-Q filed 2026-07-29, Business Segments).
