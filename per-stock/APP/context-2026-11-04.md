## What changed since the last briefing (2026-10-16)

- **The Q3 uplift the company promised showed up in revenue.** The Q2 call had blamed the miss on a thin model-upgrade pipeline and promised "pretty material uplift" in Q3 (`context-2026-10-06.md`). Q3 revenue grew 13.6% QoQ and beat its own guide by about 5%. The 10-16 thesis-break watch listed "a Q3 print without the promised model uplift"; that condition did not occur. What drove it (installs vs revenue per install, e-commerce share) needs the 10-Q or the call.
- **Nevada reincorporation, approved without a meeting.** Item 5.07: on 2026-10-29 holders of a majority of the voting power approved converting from Delaware to Nevada by written consent. The company will file an information statement with the SEC. This is a governance change (Nevada gives controlling holders and directors more room); it is relevant to R4 and is not a rule-35 event.
- **No word in the release on the San Diego County enforcement action or the Talbot securities class action.** Both remain unresolved as far as this briefing can tell.
- **Price into the print:** not pulled for this briefing. The stock hit a 52-week low of $266.41 on 2026-10-14 (news-log).
