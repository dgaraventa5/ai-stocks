## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before reading the 10-Q or searching, verbatim)

> AppLovin is a near-pure ad platform whose revenue runs through Axon; gaming dominates, e-commerce (self-serve opened mid-2026) is the optionality. Q3 2026 (reported 11-04) beat its own guide by ~5.5% ($2,184.8M, +55.5% YoY, +13.6% QoQ) with Q4 guided to $2,365–2,395M, which I read as the promised "material model uplift" arriving. I expect the 10-Q to show growth still almost entirely from revenue per install, nothing new on San Diego (company silent since 10-05), Talbot unchanged ahead of the 11-16 lead-plaintiff deadline, and the Nevada reincorporation by majority written consent as the only new governance item. I expect the stock rallied hard and estimates moved up, which would turn M1 but not yet repair the 6-month relative strength.

## 4. Earnings transcript highlights (Q3 2026 call, 2026-11-04)

Source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/app/transcripts/725385-q3-2026/ (fetched via summarizer, quotes as returned); corroborated by https://www.tradingkey.com/news/transcripts/262279481- . **Primary transcript not saved to `transcripts/`** (fetch tool returns extracts only).

- **Driver = model improvements in gaming.** CFO Stumpf: growth "primarily driven by the core mobile gaming business due to model improvements during the quarter." Mobile gaming revenue grew nearly 50% YoY (TradingKey summary).
- **Model rollout is partial.** Foroughi: "This was a partial rollout. The transition is still in front of us." He would not commit to a date for full rollout and declined to call it "Axon 3.0," saying the complexity is comparable to Axon 2.0.
- **Consumer/e-commerce:** spend "up double digits quarter-over-quarter and growing triple digits from the same quarter last year" (Stumpf). **No dollar figure.** Pixel counts rising, "especially in APAC" — the same region Wells Fargo flagged on 2026-09-30 for no-traffic storefronts (news-log); the call does not address that critique.
- **Self-serve:** sophisticated advertisers succeed on self-serve; small businesses with limited data need help; full SMB self-serve is a roadmap item. Referral/partnerships "still a work in progress."
- **Creative/generative AI:** "The ad format hasn't evolved that much yet"; dynamic product ads a focus. No generative-video GA claim. No agentic/autonomous-buying launch mentioned.
- **CTV:** pilots only; "I wouldn't bank on it at all for being accretive to numbers anytime soon."
- **Competition:** Unity not mentioned; "this is not zero sum." Foroughi: market "more competitive" than three years ago but advertiser ROI higher (TradingKey). Pricing: better targeting raises conversion and therefore CPM.
- **Q4 guide build:** normal seasonality, model updates already launched, consumer scaling, higher data-center/training compute. "We're guiding to what we know at that point in time."
- **Buybacks:** $1.4B / 4.1M shares in Q3 vs $551M in Q2, stepped up on the price "dislocation versus the actual intrinsic value"; authorization raised $3.2B to $3.6B. Since inception 104M shares for $9.4B.
- **Not mentioned on the call:** any lawsuit, San Diego County, the class actions, regulators, privacy, children, Nevada.

## 5. DIFF: what changed vs my mental model / the 11-04 briefing

1. **Confirmed:** growth is still yield (rev/install +55%); the promised Q3 model uplift arrived and beat consensus, not just the guide; estimates and price both reset up.
2. **New vs model — installs turned positive (+2% YoY)** after −2% in Q2 and −7% for 9M. Small, but it cuts against the "losing share to Unity" channel checks for this quarter.
3. **New vs model — the uplift is explicitly partial.** Management says the new architecture is not fully rolled out and won't date it. That is upside optionality, and also the same kind of model-pace claim *Talbot* is about.
4. **New vs model — a third piggyback suit** (*Patel* derivative, 2026-10-07) built on *Talbot*.
5. **New vs model — the 10-Q is silent on San Diego** while describing an equally post-period derivative suit. Management treats the County action as immaterial; the market has no company statement on it at all.
6. **New detail on San Diego** — BLB&G as County counsel; an advertiser-billing (inflated clicks) allegation alongside the child-ads claims.
7. **Unchanged:** San Diego, *Talbot*, *Brownback* MTD, Unity arbitration, FTC/state-AG status — all unresolved; no insider buying; balance sheet trivially levered; 2b/2c clean, 2e not firing.
