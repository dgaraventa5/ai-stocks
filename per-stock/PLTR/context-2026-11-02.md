## What changed since the last briefing

- **The October worry did not happen.** The guided step-down (93% → 83%) came in at 96%, and US commercial held at +148%.
- **Bookings slowed sharply, and this is the number to read.** TCV was $2.918B, +6% YoY, against $3.373B, +49%, in Q2. US commercial TCV was $1.463B, +11% YoY, against $2.132B, +153%. US commercial RDV was $6.517B, +79% YoY and +4% QoQ, against $6.238B, +124%, in Q2. Revenue is growing far faster than new contract value. One quarter of TCV is lumpy, and it compares with a strong Q3 2025, but RDV growth nearly halving is the first soft spot in this run.
- **Deals:** 212 of at least $1M, 107 of at least $5M, 64 of at least $10M.
- **Gross margin:** down about 160bps. Hosting cost was the line flagged in `context-2026-10-06.md`; the release gives no breakdown, so the cause is unknown until the 10-Q.
- **Price:** closes $196.42 (10-29), $184.11 (10-30), $178.34 (11-02, before the print) from yfinance. **The after-hours reaction was not found.**
