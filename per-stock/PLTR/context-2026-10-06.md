## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before pulling data — verbatim)

> Palantir sells Gotham (government), Foundry (commercial) and AIP, an LLM orchestration layer on top of its "ontology"; AIP bootcamps have driven a US commercial boom. My last firm numbers are Q1 2026 (reported May 2026): revenue around $1.6B growing roughly 85% YoY, US commercial more than doubling, US government growing 40-50%+, adjusted operating margin in the 50s, Rule of 40 above 100, net dollar retention around 135-140%, and FY2026 revenue guidance raised to roughly $7.2-7.6B. I expect Q2 2026 (reported early August) to show continued acceleration or a plateau at very high growth, heavy SBC with share count creeping up 2-4%/yr, and a price/sales multiple that is far off its late-2025 peak after the software sell-off — I do not know where the stock sits today.
