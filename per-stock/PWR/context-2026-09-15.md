## Pre-research mental model

Quanta is the largest US specialty electric-power / grid construction & EPC contractor (transmission, substations, grid hardening, underground utility, renewables/gas generation) and the leading labor + project-execution "picks-and-shovels" play on the AI-datacenter power buildout. Coming out of Q1 2026 I expected: record and still-growing backlog, ~17% FY revenue growth on a raised $35B guide, expanding adj-EBITDA margin (~8.7%), strong FCF, AI/data-center large-load as the explicit guidance-raise driver, and a premium multiple that already prices the AI-power narrative. Standing cautions were valuation/expectations and transformer supply-chain — the latter being actively internalized via vertical integration into transformer manufacturing.

## Implications for ratings (FLAGS ONLY — no numeric ratings set)

- **D1 (AI demand directness/exposure):** Strengthened further. Technology/large-load (data-center) is the named growth engine; record $53.4B backlog + four data-center-oriented acquisitions concretize the exposure. Revisit upward-bias.
- **D2 (revenue tied to AI / customer pull):** Strengthened — hyperscaler capex still the explicit demand source; acquisitions specifically bought to serve data-center/mission-critical customers. Revisit.
- **D3 (moat / supply-chain position):** Strengthened — vertical integration (transformer mfg + fab) now compounded by M&A that nearly doubles fabrication/logistics footprint, attacking the industry's #1 bottleneck (transformers/skilled electrical labor) at scale. Revisit.
- **D5 (strength of AI-buildout tie, rule-23 parallel scale):** Strengthened — backlog/RPO growth and named large-load demand deepen the buildout tie for the supplier role. Revisit.
- **Momentum (M):** Positive — second beat-and-raise, record backlog, stock +~18.8% on print, above-Street guidance. Refresh objective **50DMA %** (rule 11) and EPS-revisions subjective before trusting. Direction: up.
- **R1 (customer concentration):** Neutral-to-positive — demand described as broad-based across utility/generation/technology-load; two-segment diversification unchanged. No concentration red flag.
- **R3 (balance-sheet risk):** Mild watch — $2.0B new senior notes raise gross debt to fund M&A/capex; leverage rising but within the stated 1.5–2x target and against record backlog/rising FCF guide ($2.0–2.5B). Recheck ND/EBITDA at the objective refresh (rule 9); not currently a risk deterioration, just a delta to verify.
- **R5 (disruption risk):** DEFAULT 5 — physical power-infrastructure EPC (non-Layer-10); AI is a tailwind, not a disruption threat. No change.
- **Rule-14 expectations flag:** Multiple elevated; run `expectations_flag.py PWR` when scoring. Growth is **accelerating** (rev +26%→+41%, two guide raises), so this is crowded-but-growing, NOT the rule-14 peak-multiple-plus-decelerating-growth pattern. Note, don't treat as a break.
- **Objective inputs (rule 9):** PWR reported Q2 (6/30/26) on 7/30/26 — well outside the 1-week window; any prior scoring row is stale to TWO prints now (Q1 raise + Q2 raise). A full objective refresh (revenue, EBITDA/FCF margins, EPS YoY per rule-15 check, ROIC per rule 34, ND/EBITDA post-debt-raise) is due whenever next scored. Q2 was a clean beat (no >15% miss on the downside; ~+12% revenue beat), so no immediate-tier-urgency trigger, but inputs are materially stale.

**THESIS-BREAK CHECK:** **None.** Backlog at a new record ($53.4B), guidance raised (not cut), margins expanding, revenue growth accelerating, no project cancellation (broad-based demand), no adverse regulatory action, no customer loss, no going-concern signal. The only standing cautions are valuation/expectations (a sizing question) and a modest debt-funded leverage step-up (verify at refresh) — neither is a thesis break.
