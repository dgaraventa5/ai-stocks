## Pre-research mental model

Coming in from the 2026-06-22 briefing: Quanta is the largest US specialty electric-power / grid-construction EPC and the "picks-and-shovels" labor + execution play on the AI-datacenter power buildout. As of Q1 2026 it had a record $48.5B backlog, was growing revenue ~26% YoY, had just RAISED FY26 guidance (to $34.7–35.2B rev / $13.55–14.25 adj EPS) with AI-datacenter/large-load named as the explicit driver (large-load segment guided >100% YoY), and was internalizing the transformer supply-chain bottleneck ($500–700M into own transformer mfg + 6.7M sq ft fab). The standing caution was valuation/expectations (sell-side "already rewarded"), not a fundamentals break; growth was accelerating, not decelerating. Two reportable segments now (Electric ~82%, Underground & Infrastructure ~18%). thesis.md is still the untouched template.

## Implications for ratings (FLAGS ONLY — no numbers pre-decided)

- **D1 (AI-revenue directness):** Strengthened. Data-center/tech is now a named ~15–20%-of-business vertical and the Cupertino Electric platform takes Quanta directly to hyperscalers on data-center electrical work. Revisit whether current rating still reads as "indirect/emerging."
- **D2 (supply-chain position / pull):** Strengthened. Guidance raised on hyperscaler-driven demand; CEI adds a direct-to-buyer position; large transmission awards still queued to enter backlog. Revisit.
- **D3 (moat):** Possibly strengthened. Two reinforcing moat vectors now — transformer/fab vertical integration (prior) + CEI self-perform / direct-hyperscaler relationships + scale/execution-speed reputation. Revisit.
- **D4 (capacity / runway):** Confirmed strong. Record backlog, 12-mo backlog $32.3B, and the largest 765/345 kV transmission programs not yet in backlog (H2'26+) — long visible runway. Already at 5; no downgrade signal.
- **D5 (hyperscaler tie / AI-buildout tie):** Strengthened. CEI explicitly positions Quanta as a direct hyperscaler data-center contractor; tech end-market a stated strategic pillar. Revisit on the rule-23 parallel scale.
- **M1 (EPS revisions):** Clearly positive — second consecutive above-Street guidance raise; adj EPS beat ~29%. Supports a top rating.
- **M2 (relative strength):** Refresh objective 50DMA % (rule 11) before trusting; stock has been a strong performer ("+36% in a month" headline). Objective refresh needed, not assumed.
- **M3 (insider):** NOT refreshed in this pass (no Form 4 pull). Flag for a proper Form-4 check before any rating change; share issuance in the 10-Q is acquisition consideration, not selling.
- **R1 (customer concentration):** Positive support — 10-Q states no customer ≥10% of revenue or receivables; backlog broad-based; two-segment diversification. Revisit if scored on any stale single-customer assumption.
- **R3 (balance-sheet risk):** Watch, not alarm. $2.0B new debt + ~$957M H1 acquisition cash raises gross leverage, but IG-rated, within the stated 1.5–2x target, $2.77B liquidity, and H1 OCF +176%/FCF ~$1.04B. Confirm ND/EBITDA on the objective refresh (rule 9) hasn't crossed a band.
- **R4 (regulatory risk):** Unchanged. Silverado wildfire contingency and Canada renewable-transmission claims persist as disclosed items; permitting/policy is the standing management-flagged risk, not a current adverse event.
- **R5 (disruption risk):** DEFAULT 5 — physical power-infrastructure EPC (Layer 02, not Layer 10). AI is a demand tailwind, not a disruption threat. No change.
- **Rule-9 objective refresh:** PWR reported Q2 (6/30/26) on 7/30/26 — well outside the 1-week window; a full objective refresh (revenue, gross/FCF margin, EBITDA, EPS YoY, ND/EBITDA, ROIC, 50DMA%) is DUE and inputs in any prior scoring row are stale to this print. Not a >15% miss (it was a beat), so no immediate-tier emergency, but data is stale.
- **Rule-14 expectations flag:** Elevated multiple persists and the stock has run hard, but the rule-14 pattern is *peak multiple + decelerating growth*. Growth is **accelerating** (double raise, +41% Q2). Run `expectations_flag.py PWR` to note where P/S sits vs its 3-yr range — crowded-but-growing, not the rule-14 red flag.
- **Rule-15 (EPS YoY):** Q2 GAAP diluted EPS +94.7% is operationally driven (revenue +41%, margin expansion), NOT one-time-dominated — no rule-15 blank warranted on this print (decision belongs to the scoring pass, flagged here for the file).

**THESIS-BREAK CHECK:** None. Backlog at a new record, guidance raised (twice this year), margins expanding, growth largely organic, no customer concentration, no cancellation/impairment, no adverse regulatory action. The only standing caution is valuation/expectations — a sizing question, not a thesis break. Secondary watch: rising leverage from the CEI-funding debt (monitor ND/EBITDA), and integration risk on a $1.54B acquisition.
