## 3. WebSearch findings (Muse + AI developments, Sep 2026)

### 3a. Muse personal AI agent (launched September 8, 2026)

Meta launched **Muse**, a personal AI agent, on September 8, 2026. Key facts:

- **What it is:** a secure, personal AI agent that connects to a user's email, calendar, payments,
  health, shopping, and smart home, then executes multi-step tasks with minimal supervision. Runs
  on a dedicated "Muse Secure VM" (per-user isolated virtual machine).
  — [about.fb.com](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/),
  [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/meta-launches-ai-agent-that-can-access-other-apps-send-emails-make-payments-2026-09-08/)

- **Pricing:** free tier (up to 100M tokens/week per user), Power at $20/mo (500M tokens/week),
  Maximum at $100/mo (3B tokens/week). Payment card required at signup even for free tier. US-only,
  18+.
  — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/meta-personal-ai-agents-public-reckoning-privacy-safety.html),
  [tech-insider.org](https://tech-insider.org/meta-muse-personal-ai-agent-launch-2026/)

- **Underlying model:** Muse Spark 1.3 (released Sep 2), a multimodal reasoning model built for
  agentic tasks. Ranks #6 on Artificial Analysis. Reviewers note it's fast and cheap but trails
  frontier competitors (Opus 5) on some agent benchmarks; "max" reasoning tier still gated at launch.
  — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-02/meta-releases-more-powerful-ai-model-edging-closer-to-rivals),
  [eesel.ai review](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/meta-muse-spark-13-review)

- **Internal name:** "Hatch." Zuckerberg framed it as "personal superintelligence" for billions of
  users. "Everyone will have an exceptionally capable personal agent... Your agent will work 24/7
  on your behalf to improve your relationships, health, career, finances, home management, hobbies."
  — [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/meta-launches-ai-agent-that-can-access-other-apps-send-emails-make-payments-2026-09-08/)

- **Meta Connect 2026 (Sep 23-25):** new Muse features, connectors, and state-of-the-art model
  updates announced. Rolling out to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Messenger.
  — [Meta Connect blog](https://www.meta.com/blog/meta-connect-2026-everything-we-announced/),
  [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/23/everything-new-coming-to-metas-ai-agent-muse/)

**Rating implication:** Muse is Meta's first **direct consumer AI revenue line**. Previously Meta's
AI revenue was ad-derived (indirect — AI improves ad targeting/feed ranking, D1=3). Muse creates
actual subscription revenue ($20/$100/mo) for AI-native product. Revenue is almost certainly
immaterial vs $60B+ ad revenue today, but the strategic vector is real: Meta is now an AI
**seller**, not just an AI **buyer**.
