## 2. Why it matters

- Meta is now funding AI capex with **equity** as well as debt: Q3 FCF was −$2.12B on $34.9B capex (`context-2026-10-23.md`). Last month's briefing logged $24.9B of new debt.
- Dilution: 40M common shares now (6M more if the option is used), plus the shares issued when the preferred converts in 2029. The conversion share count is **not computed here**: secondary sources quote the 1.3620–1.6000 ratio without saying whether it is per depositary share or per preferred share, and the prospectus supplement (424B5, acc. 0001193125-26-436405) was not read in this pass.
- Net effect on balance-sheet risk is mixed: equity lowers leverage, but issuing it at all says the capex plan outruns internal cash.
