## 3. New since the 2026-09-28 briefing

1. **New non-voting Class C stock (8-K Item 1.01).** Board approved 2026-10-19, shareholders by written consent 2026-10-20, effective 2027-01-19. A one-time dividend of one Class C share per Class A/B share; the company expects Class A to trade as if split 2-for-1. Stated purposes: "capital raising, employee compensation, potential acquisitions" and keeping Zuckerberg in control. A Founder Alignment Agreement lets him sell only 20% of his Class C freely; the other 80% must be "stapled" to sales of voting shares.
   - **What this means for us:** (a) Meta now has a share class designed to issue stock for financing without diluting control, at the same time FCF has turned negative. That is a dilution path the Balance Sheet rating (R3) should watch. (b) **Rule-27 market-cap check needed after 2027-01-19:** yfinance may report Class A-only share counts or mis-handle the dividend, as it did for MBLY and HSAI. Every cap-based ratio (P/S, EV/EBITDA, FCF yield) must be cross-checked after the effective date.
2. **FCF went negative for the first time in this record:** −$2.12B for the quarter vs ~+$1.7B in Q2 (09-28 briefing). OCF is still strong ($32.8B); capex is the driver.
3. **Legal:** the Q2 $2.4B charge is now joined by the $9.89B Q3 charge. The company warns of "a number of youth-related trials scheduled in the coming months in the U.S." (Ex-99.1).
