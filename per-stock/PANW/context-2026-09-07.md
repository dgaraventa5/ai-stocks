## Pre-research mental model

Palo Alto is the platform consolidator of enterprise security: a network-firewall incumbent using "platformization" — bundling Network Security, Cortex and Prisma Cloud onto single contracts, sometimes giving away product to displace point vendors — to convert a hardware base into Next-Generation Security ARR. My prior model held that NGS ARR was the metric that mattered, that the platformization giveaways were compressing near-term billings in exchange for larger multi-year commitments, and that the pending CyberArk acquisition would push it into identity security at a large price. Most recent period I believed reported: fiscal Q3 2026. Product cycle: Cortex XSIAM and AI-security (Prisma AIRS).

## DIFF vs prior mental model

- **CyberArk is closed and integrating, not pending** — the single biggest change since June, and my model still carried it as a forward event.
- **The acquisition programme is bigger than one deal.** CyberArk *plus* Chronosphere *plus* Console means PANW is buying identity, observability and agentic workflow simultaneously; the "platformization" story is now substantially an M&A roll-up story financed partly in stock.
- **Dilution is material and quantified** — ~112M new shares and ~13.5% EPS dilution, with share count guided to 844–847M for FY27.
- **The +63% NGS ARR headline is not the growth rate.** FY27 guides to +22–23%; I would have carried the headline forward incorrectly without the M&A-contribution disclosure.
- **GAAP profitability went backwards while non-GAAP improved** — a swing to a $(282)M quarterly GAAP net loss that my model did not contain.
- **Free-cash-flow quality is the durable good news:** FY26 adjusted FCF margin 38.4%, guided to 38.0% in FY27 with a 40% FY28 target — the cash engine is intact through the acquisition programme.
