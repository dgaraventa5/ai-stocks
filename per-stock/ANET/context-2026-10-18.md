## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before any search)

Arista is the premier high-end data-center switching vendor, winning AI back-end networks with Ethernet (Etherlink 800G) + EOS, anchored by Meta (~26% of FY25 revenue) and Microsoft (~16%). Last quarter I hold is Q1 2026 (rev $2.71B, +35%; FY26 guide raised to $11.5B; AI fabric target $3.5B), punished ~14% on a 1–2 year supply-shortage warning and GM compression. Live risk vectors: NVIDIA Spectrum-X having overtaken Arista on DC-Ethernet share (~25.9% claim, June), $8.9B purchase commitments cutting both ways, and rising single-account concentration. Roadmap: 1.6T/XPO liquid-cooled optics (2027), scale-up Ethernet as a 2027+ opportunity. I expect Q2 2026 (reported ~early Aug) to have been another beat, with the open question being whether share vs NVIDIA kept slipping.

- **Growth re-accelerated, not just sustained:** Q2 +37.7% and FY guide $11.5B → $12.6B (+40%), Q3 guide $3.3B (implies ~+43% YoY vs Q3'25 revenue of $2.31B per yfinance quarterly statements — my arithmetic, not company-stated). I had the FY at $11.5B.

- **Share vs NVIDIA: the gap widened but the June "25.9%" figure was overstated.** IDC 2Q26 has NVIDIA 20.4% vs Arista 18.7% vs Cisco 18.2% of DC Ethernet — three near-equal vendors, with Arista growing slower than the segment. Cisco is a real DC competitor in this data (contradicts the 2026-05-18 D2 rationale "Cisco doesn't compete here").
- **Margin give-up goes to the biggest customers:** 10-Q explicitly attributes the 230bp YoY GM decline to large end customers' higher discounts — evidence about pricing power with the anchor accounts.
- **Purchase commitments $8.9B → $9.7B** and deferred revenue $6.2B → $6.9B; buyback paused in 1H26 (cash going to supply).
- **Scale-up became a concrete product (SU-144 / ESUN)** with Meta and Microsoft as ESUN partners — but no customer or ship date.
- **Geography swung back:** Non-Americas 15.5% (Q1) → 23.0% (Q2) — customer-mix noise, not a trend.
