- **The share worry did not show up in revenue.** The 2026-10-18 briefing cited IDC 2Q26 showing Arista growing slower than the DC Ethernet market (+37.6% vs +64.5%). Q3 revenue grew 58.5%. IDC's 3Q26 data is needed to say whether share held. This bears on the pending D2 5→4 proposal and the D3 question.
- **Gross margin held.** Q3 63.6% is above the H1 average (62.4%), so the large-customer discounting the 10-Q described did not deepen this quarter.
- **Rack-scale and scale-up:** Etherlink SU-144 (ESUN) with AMD, Arm, Broadcom, d-Matrix, Meta, Microsoft and Qualcomm. Already in the news-log (2026-10-07); still no named ship date.
- **Supply build:** inventory +43% and deferred revenue +27% year to date, consistent with the "two-year supply problem" in the prior briefing.
- **Price into the print:** closes $184.22 (10-29), $168.86 (10-30), $164.14 (11-02), $169.91 (11-03). **The after-hours reaction was not found.**
