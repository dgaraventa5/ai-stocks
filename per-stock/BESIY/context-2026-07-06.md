## 1. Pre-research mental model (verbatim, written before any searches)

> Besi is the leading (duopoly-with-ASMPT) supplier of hybrid bonding die-attach equipment — the critical tool for sub-10-micron-pitch bumpless copper-to-copper 3D packaging used in HBM stacking and logic-on-logic chiplet integration. Key customers TSMC (anchor for the hybrid-bonding ramp), ASE, AMD, Intel, Apple and the HBM memory makers. As of my last clear read (~Q4 2025 / early 2026) the cyclical trough was over and orders had inflected sharply upward, with hybrid bonding going from a small slice toward ~1/3 of revenue by 2026 on TSMC's CoWoS/SoIC 3-4x capacity expansion. High gross margins (~63-66%), strong FCF, net cash, Dutch domicile, extreme valuation re-rate (fwd P/E ~50s). Roadmap: 2026 logic hybrid-bonding ramp is the sure near-term driver; HBM4/HBM4e memory transition is the 2026-2027 next leg (with some risk it stays on microbumps longer).
