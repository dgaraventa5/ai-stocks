## 6. Implications for ratings (flags, not decisions)

- **D5 (new L10 lens).**
  - What clears the bar: a disclosed AI ARR and AI-ACV share, plus disclosed organic usage (5,500 customers). That clears band 3 and touches band 4.
  - Against band 4: the ARR definition is undisclosed and folds in acquired businesses (guard b). And company-level results are moving the **opposite** direction: subscription growth is decelerating to a guided 11% with no AI-attributed margin gain (the 10-Q credits efficiency). Band 4 requires company results "moving the same direction."
  - Band 5 is clearly not met.
- **D1:** AI ARR ~$600M ÷ ~$10.3B annualized revenue ≈ 5.8%, which is band 2.
- **D3:** GRR of 97% and stable share argue for "modest moat," but the 10-Q's flexible terms and new pricing models point to pricing that is competitive rather than price-taking.
- **D4:** Restructuring in Product and Technology, buybacks far larger than reinvestment, and FY28 margin expansion all read as harvesting rather than expanding. C1.ai is the offset.
- **M1:** Estimates are rising mechanically.
- **M2:** Strong outperformance, though inflated by the rumor.
- **M3:** No buys; all sales 10b5-1, so this is the routine default.
- **R3:** The net cash cushion is gone after buybacks, and a $999M maturity is within 12 months.
- **R4:** *Mobley* claims have widened.
- **R5:** The 10-Q now documents seat-commitment pressure. GRR is still 97%, so this is not yet active erosion.
- **Entry hold (rule 35):** C1.ai is not transformative at ~2.6% of market cap (terms undisclosed). The Silver Lake talks are a press report, not a signed transaction, and they predate the last reported quarter. No enforcement action and no securities case in RECAP. Guidance was reiterated, not cut. Nothing meets the hold criteria. **Watch item:** a signed take-private would cap upside and would warrant a hold.
- **Rule 15:** the ruling for quarter-end 2026-07-31 (blank; $374M deferred-tax benefit) is **current**. yfinance `mostRecentQuarter` = 2026-07-31. It goes STALE when Q3 FY27 (q/e 2026-10-31) prints, expected ~2026-12-01 per yfinance (date unconfirmed by the company). Q3 GAAP will carry the $55–70M restructuring charge, and the Q4 FY26 base included prior restructuring, so the next ruling needs the primary 10-Q.
