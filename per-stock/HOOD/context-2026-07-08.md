## 1. Pre-research mental model (verbatim, written before any data pull)

> - **Business**: Robinhood is a commission-free retail brokerage app — equities,
>   options, and crypto trading, plus Robinhood Gold subscription, cash management,
>   and retirement accounts. Revenue is transaction-based (PFOF), net interest income
>   (margin lending, cash sweep), and subscriptions.
> - **AI thesis**: Weak/indirect. Robinhood is a *fintech*, not an AI infrastructure
>   name. Any AI angle would be "AI adopter" — using AI for customer service, product
>   features (Cortex assistant?), not supplying the AI buildout.
> - **Most recent quarter (per training)**: Fuzzy — returned to GAAP profitability in
>   2024, crypto revenue volatile, expanding into retirement, credit card, and
>   international (UK/EU). Possibly acquired Bitstamp.
> - **Competitive position**: Competes with Schwab, Fidelity, Coinbase (crypto),
>   Webull. Strong brand with younger retail demographic.

**AI language in the 10-Q (this is the whole AI story):**
- *"We currently use machine learning and AI to improve our products and processes
  in certain circumstances, such as to increase the efficiency of our in-app…"* →
  **AI as an adopter/tooling**, not a revenue line.
- *"Robinhood Gold Subscribers have access to Robinhood Cortex, which is an
  AI-powered investing assistant that enhances Robinhood product offerings with
  tailored insights…"* → **AI enhances the Gold subscription**; no separately
  disclosed AI revenue.

**Conclusion from the filing:** There is **no AI revenue** at HOOD. AI is an internal
efficiency tool and a Gold-subscription feature. Materiality to the return ≈ nil in
supply-chain terms; the growth engine is prediction markets + margin lending + equities.

## 5. DIFF — what changed vs my training model

- **Prediction markets / event contracts** are now a *material, top-growth* line
  ($104M/qtr transaction revenue, from ~nil) — this was not in my mental model.
- **Crypto revenue collapsed −47% YoY** — I knew crypto was volatile; I did not have
  the magnitude of the 2026 air-pocket.
- **Net interest revenue is large and growing** ($359M/qtr, margin interest +75% YoY)
  — margin lending is a bigger earnings pillar than I'd modeled.
- **Cortex is real and shipping** to Gold — but it is an *adopter feature*, confirming
  (not overturning) the "no AI revenue" read.
- **Agentic finance is the real AI story, and I under-covered it first pass** (see 3b):
  MCP-server access for third-party agents + Agentic Trading/Credit Card (launched
  2026-05-27). Upgrades the *forward* thesis from "chatbot" to "agentic-finance rails,"
  but monetization is unchanged (no AI revenue line) and Robinhood remains demand-side.
- **Valuation re-rated hard**: P/S ~21.9x, fwd P/E ~37.8x — extreme for a brokerage.

## 6. Implications for ratings (flag only — do NOT pre-decide)

- **AI-materiality GATE (the decisive one):** By the exact test that dropped HOOD on
  2026-06-22 — *judge AI materiality to the return, not "uses AI"* — HOOD still fails on
  **AI revenue**: monetization is unchanged (transaction/PFOF, interchange, net interest),
  no paid AI tier or model-provider revenue share, so there is no AI revenue line. The
  agentic-finance launch (3b) is real strategy but doesn't create AI revenue and keeps
  HOOD **demand-side** (destination for others' agents), so a **RDDT-style AI-revenue
  hook is still absent** (RDDT sells training data; HOOD monetizes brokerage activity).
- **AI-Thesis dims — NOT uniformly low (this is the correction from Dom's flag):** don't
  reflexively set T/U/V/W/X to the floor. D1 (AI-Rev %) ≈ 1 (still true — no AI revenue).
  But the agentic-finance rails give a genuine *optionality/positioning* argument for the
  moat/position dims — comparable to how the map carries **TSLA** ("AI rev ≈ 0% of P&L,
  scored as optionality"), not to a pure non-AI name. Rate the agentic story on its
  merits at the session; it is a legitimate forward-thesis input, just not a revenue one.
- **Momentum** strong on fundamentals (record accounts, Gold +36%, prediction markets)
  **but** the objective 50DMA%=40.8 (→band 60) is soft — price lagged the story post-miss.
- **Risk:** R4 (Reg) **elevated** — prediction-markets gambling-law overhang hits the top
  growth driver. R5 Disruption: brokerage core is fairly durable AND Robinhood is
  *embracing* agentic disruption (building the rails) rather than being a victim of it —
  a point *for* durability, so R5 likely 4 (not lower).
- **Valuation**: expectations_flag came back **clean** (rev YoY above 3y median) but
  P/S sits at the **78th percentile of its own 3-yr range** — near the high end; a rich
  multiple that the rubric's absolute Value bands will punish (low FCF yield 2.95%).
- **Data-quality caveats for scoring:** yfinance "gross margin 92%" / "FCF margin 64%"
  are financial-company artifacts (a brokerage has no COGS in the usual sense; FCF is
  distorted by balance-sheet/customer-cash flows) — treat Quality margins skeptically.
  ROIC and ND/EBITDA not exposed by yfinance (financials/leverage structure). EPS YoY
  (+4.2%) is a clean operational number — **not** rule-15-blank material.
