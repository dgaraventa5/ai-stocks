# HOOD — Research Context Briefing (2026-10-16)

Robinhood Markets, Inc. | Layer 10 / Consumer fintech (AI adopter) | refresh-context pass for the
"AI-adopter progress" rescore. Public sources only (no Robinhood MCP tools used).

> **Bottom line up front.** Since the 2026-07-08 briefing, Robinhood's agentic-finance push has
> gone from a beta to a product line: ~100,000 Agentic accounts holding >$100M by the Q2 call
> (late July), crypto added in July, and at HOOD Summit (2026-09-29) **Robinhood Agents** — an
> in-app agent built on OpenAI/Anthropic models — plus "Agent Apps". **It still produces no AI
> revenue line.** The one new money flow, token billing for Robinhood-hosted agents, is charged
> at the model's *public* token cost, which makes it a pass-through rather than a margin. The
> AI story is forward optionality on trading volume, not AI revenue. Separately, the stock is
> down ~22% from its 2026-09-03 high and has lagged software (IGV) by ~14 points over six months,
> and Q2's GAAP EPS growth is inflated by a $129M non-operating gain (rule-15 candidate).

## 1. Pre-research mental model (verbatim, written before any data pull)

> - **Business:** Commission-free retail broker. Revenue = transaction-based (options, crypto,
>   equities via PFOF, and since 2025 prediction markets/event contracts), net interest (margin
>   lending, cash sweep), and Gold subscriptions. Bitstamp (crypto exchange) acquired 2025;
>   WonderFi (Canada) closing mid-2026; prediction-markets JV with Susquehanna (Rothera/MIAXdx).
> - **AI thesis:** Adopter, not supplier. Cortex = AI research assistant bundled in Gold.
>   Agentic Trading + an MCP server for third-party agents launched 2026-05-27. Expect no
>   disclosed AI revenue; any monetization is indirect (more trading volume, Gold retention).
> - **Latest quarter (my belief):** Q2 2026 reported ~late July; Q1 2026 was a miss on a
>   crypto air-pocket offset by prediction markets and margin interest.
> - **Risk:** Prediction-markets state-gambling litigation is the main regulatory overhang;
>   valuation rich (P/S ~20x).
