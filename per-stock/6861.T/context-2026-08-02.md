## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before searching)

What I believed coming in: Keyence is the highest-quality industrial technology franchise on earth — ~50% operating margins from a direct, no-distributor, consultative sales model, a fortress balance sheet, and a permanently expensive multiple. I expected a steady ~5–10% grower whose entire investment debate is valuation, with machine vision as one pillar of a broad sensing catalogue and no meaningful robotics programme. I expected the deep pass's flagged "revenue conflict" (FY release ¥1.17T vs yfinance ¥1,254.8B) to be a genuine data problem requiring a blank or a manual override.

## 2. Filing / results highlights (cited)

### ⚠️ THE FLAGGED DATA CONFLICT IS RESOLVED — AND yfinance IS THE CORRECT NUMBER

**FY3/2026 full year** (ended 2026-03-20, announced 2026-04-24): net sales **¥1,169,289M (+10.4%)**; operating income **¥595,759M (+8.4%)**; ordinary income ¥635,756M (+13.3%); net income attributable to owners **¥445,185M (+11.7%)**. Operating margin 50.9%. [japanir.jp FY2026 summary](https://japanir.jp/en/company/company-6861/ir/6861-20260424-02_wp_financial_summary/)

**Q1 FY3/2027** — primary source: 2027年3月期 第1四半期決算短信〔日本基準〕（連結）, announced **2026-07-28**, period **2026-03-21 → 2026-06-20**. [keyence.co.jp tanshin PDF](https://www.keyence.co.jp/pdf/EarningsRelease_202607_ja.pdf)

| | Q1 FY3/2027 | Q1 FY3/2026 | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net sales | **¥346,621M** | ¥261,076M | **+32.8%** |
| Operating income | **¥187,076M** | ¥129,301M | **+44.7%** |
| Ordinary income | ¥196,793M | ¥131,532M | +49.6% |
| Net income (owners) | **¥139,133M** | ¥92,116M | **+51.0%** |
| EPS | ¥573.69 | ¥379.82 | +51.0% |

Operating margin **54.0%** (187,076 / 346,621).

**The reconciliation, exactly:**
`FY3/2026 ¥1,169,289M − Q1 FY3/2026 ¥261,076M + Q1 FY3/2027 ¥346,621M = ¥1,254,834M`
yfinance TTM = **¥1,254.8B**. Match to the last reported digit. Same for earnings: `445,185 − 92,116 + 139,133 = ¥492,202M` vs stockanalysis TTM net income **¥492.20B**. Match.

**Conclusion: there was never a conflict.** The ¥1.17T figure is the *completed fiscal year*; yfinance's ¥1,254.8B is a *current TTM* that correctly rolls in Q1 FY3/2027. **Use the TTM.** Keyence's fiscal year ends **March 20**, not March 31 — several English secondary sources say March 31 and are wrong; the tanshin cover page states the period as 2026年3月21日～2026年6月20日.

**Organic, not accounting.** The tanshin's 注記事項 explicitly state: 連結範囲の重要な変更 **無** (no material change in scope of consolidation); 会計基準等の改正に伴う会計方針の変更 **無** and その他の会計方針の変更 **無** (no accounting-policy changes); 会計上の見積りの変更 **無**; 修正再表示 **無**. The +32.8% is clean.

**Management commentary (tanshin p.2, 定性的情報):** global capex continued, led by manufacturing; **the Americas broadly solid across industries; Asia growth continued centred on the semiconductor and electrical-precision industries (半導体・電気精密業界)**; Europe recovering; Japanese capex on a recovery trend.

**Balance sheet (tanshin p.1):** total assets ¥3,724,891M; net assets ¥3,548,740M; **equity ratio 95.3%**; total liabilities ¥176,150M (down ¥23,033M q/q, mostly on a ¥42,385M fall in income taxes payable). Investment securities rose ¥83,973M in the quarter. Essentially debt-free.

**No guidance.** The tanshin publishes only a dividend forecast (¥550/share annual for FY3/2027, unchanged). Keyence does not issue earnings guidance — this matters for how M1 must be read.

**Disclosure gap (rule 3):** Keyence reports as a **single segment** with **no product-line and no geographic revenue split**. Therefore:
- The "vision ≈ 30–40% of revenue" figure carried in the universe file is a **derived estimate with wide error bars**, not a cited disclosure. It cannot be upgraded from this filing.
- There is no disclosed customer concentration and no disclosed regional revenue split — which directly constrains how R1 and R2 can be evidenced (see §5).

## 5. Rating implications

- **D1 stays low (2) despite the acceleration.** The vision share is an unconfirmable estimate; robotics/physical-AI revenue is effectively zero and undisclosed; the AI-tied slice (semiconductor/electrical-precision) is real but unsized. Rating the theme rather than the evidence here would be exactly the "quality masquerading as AI exposure" failure.
- **D2/D3 both 5.** The 54.0% operating margin against Omron's 7.8% on overlapping products is the strongest pricing-power evidence in the entire watchlist, and the moat is a *business model* (direct sales engineers, same-day availability, no distributors) rather than a product — far harder to copy and demonstrably 30 years durable.
- **D4 = 3.** Asset-light by design; the balance sheet is accumulating investment securities (+¥84.0B in the quarter), not plant.
- **M1 = 5** on the print and the Goldman upgrade, with the caveat that Keyence issues no guidance, so revisions are entirely analyst-driven.
- **R1 = 5, R3 = 5** with high conviction. **R2 = 3 is the weak rating** — Keyence publishes no geographic revenue split, so the required "% of revenue exposed" cannot be cited (see the DEBATABLE note in the rating sheet).
- **M3 = 3, flagged default** — foreign filer, no SEC Form 4 regime.
