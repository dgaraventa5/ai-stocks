## 1. One-line thesis

> The quality anchor of physical AI: Keyence sells the eyes and sensors of factory automation at an 83.7% gross margin and 51–54% operating margin — economics no industrial peer has replicated in 30 years — and its Q1 FY3/27 quarter (+32.8% sales, +44.7% OP) is being driven by exactly the Asia semiconductor/electronics capex that the rest of this watchlist tracks from the other side.

---

## 2. Position in the AI supply chain

- **What they do:** Machine vision systems, measurement sensors, laser markers, microscopes — the perception layer of industrial automation, sold direct (no distributors) by consultative sales engineers with same-day availability.
- **Where they sit:** Upstream supplier of "eyes" to every automation line; vision is ~30–40% of revenue (**estimate — Keyence discloses no product split; flagged per rule 3**).
- **Unique vs. commoditized:** Near-duopoly with Cognex in high-end vision; the direct-sales model is the unreplicated part — Omron runs a 7.8% operating margin on overlapping products vs Keyence's 51–54%.
- **AI revenue mix:** Management names semiconductor/electrical-precision demand in Asia as the Q1 growth engine (tanshin p.2) — genuine AI-capex pull-through, unsized by disclosure. D1 = 2 held deliberately low for that reason; the quality is not allowed to masquerade as AI exposure.

---

## 3. Customers

Extremely fragmented industrial base via direct sales force; no concentration disclosed and structurally implausible under the model → R1 = 5. No hyperscaler customer; no humanoid program — this is picks-and-shovels breadth, not a robot-program bet.

## 4. Pricing & demand

- Q1 FY3/27 (Mar 21–Jun 20, 2026): sales **+32.8%**, operating profit **+44.7%**, net income +51.0%, OPM **54.0%** (tanshin; no scope/accounting change). Note the weak prior-year comp (+5.6%) — do not annualize the +32.8%.
- FY3/26: sales +10.4%, NI ¥445.2B, OPM 51.0%.
- Pricing power is structural (direct model, catalogue breadth, availability), evidenced by margins holding through cycles — D2 = 5 / D3 = 5, the only 5/5 pair in the Layer-11 batch.

## 6. Moat

D3 = 5. The moat is a *business model* — direct sales engineers, consultative spec-in, same-day shipping, ~70% of products new within 7 years — which is harder to copy than IP. Thirty years of 40%+ operating margins across cycles is the demonstration. Goldman upgraded to Buy + APAC Conviction List on the Q1 print.
