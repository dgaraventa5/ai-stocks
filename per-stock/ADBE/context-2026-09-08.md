## 1. Pre-research mental model (verbatim, written before any data pull)

> Adobe is the incumbent creative-software franchise (Creative Cloud: Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere; plus Document Cloud/Acrobat and the Digital Experience enterprise segment). Its AI thesis is two-sided: Firefly generative models embedded across the Creative Cloud apps as an upsell/retention lever, and GenStudio/Adobe Experience Platform as the enterprise content-supply-chain play. The bull case is that Adobe monetizes AI on top of a locked-in professional installed base with commercially-safe (indemnified) training data; the bear case — which has weighed on the stock for over a year — is that generative image/video models (OpenAI, Midjourney, Google, Black Forest Labs) commoditize the core creative act and that seat-based Creative Cloud pricing is a direct agentic-AI disruption target. Most recent quarter per my training is roughly Q1 FY2026 (reported ~March 2026); I recall total ARR in the mid-$20B area growing low-double-digits and management leaning hard on "AI ARR" disclosure. Latest product cycle I remember is Firefly video GA and the Firefly standalone app/mobile push.
