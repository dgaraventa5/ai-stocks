## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before any data pull, verbatim)

> Shopify is the commerce operating system for ~millions of independent merchants; ~75% of revenue is merchant solutions (mostly Shopify Payments take on GMV), ~25% subscriptions. FY2025 revenue roughly $11–12B growing high-20s/low-30s, GMV ~$300B+, FCF margin high-teens. AI story: Sidekick/Magic merchant tools (free), Shopify Catalog + Agentic Storefronts syndicating merchant products into ChatGPT/Gemini/Copilot, the Universal Commerce Protocol co-built with Google (Jan 2026), and AI-driven orders reportedly ~13x in Q1 2026 — but ChatGPT Instant Checkout was rolled back in March 2026. Most recent quarter I believe is Q2 2026 (reported early August). Shopify holds big equity stakes (Affirm, Global-E, Klaviyo) whose mark-to-market swings dominate GAAP EPS. My prior: AI is a distribution channel and a defensive question (do agents disintermediate the storefront?), not an AI-revenue line — likely a case of quality masquerading as AI exposure.

## 5. DIFF: what changed vs. my pre-research model

- **Confirmed:** AI is a distribution channel + merchant tooling, not a revenue line. The only AI disclosures in filings are *costs* (10-Q p.32–33).
- **New:** AI-order growth decelerated from ~13x (Q1, vs Jan-2025 base) to ~3x YoY (Q2) — the multiples are on a tiny base and management itself calls agentic volume small vs GMV.
- **New:** Shopify is being adopted as the *checkout rail* by agent platforms that lack merchant networks (Meta Muse via Shop Pay; UCP with Google). This is the strongest evidence against the disintermediation bear case so far — but no agent platform has yet extracted a fee from Shopify's take.
- **New:** Sezzle antitrust monopolization claims survived MTD (May 2026); Briskin CIPA privacy class action survived MTD (2026-07-30). Neither disclosed as material.
- **New:** Q2 GAAP EPS +68% is two-thirds MTM gains; operating income also +68% but pre-tax ex-investment income only +30% (interest income fell $106M → $66M as cash was spent on buybacks).
- **New:** fast-rising transaction & loan losses (+76% YoY) as Shopify Capital originations scale.
- **New:** stock round-tripped: ~$113 (2026-07-24) → $168.8 today; ~+29% over 6 months vs IGV +22%.
- Unchanged: net-cash balance sheet, no customer concentration, US 63% of revenue.
