## 4. Earnings transcript highlights (Q2 FY27 call, 2026-08-27)

Source: [Motley Fool Q2 FY27 transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/31/autodesk-adsk-q2-2027-earnings-call-transcript/) (third-party transcript; cross-checked to Ex-99.1 where numeric).
- **No AI usage, Assistant user, or AI revenue/ARR figures given.**
- AI monetization framework: task-based automation embedded in subscriptions as an "accelerant"; workflow- and systems-level automation to bring consumption revenue (including via Flex). Machine-based and API usage will be charged, timelines adjusted to customer readiness.
- AI workloads may compress gross margin over time but are "gross profit dollars accretive"; FY27 gross margin expected flat.
- Models: mix of frontier third-party models and Autodesk's own (NeuralCAD).
- Flex minimum purchase cut from $300 to $99 — accelerated small-customer adoption, called net accretive.
- Construction grew >20%; Fusion "growing robustly" with more multi-seat purchases.
- Sales reorganization: Americas/APAC/E. Europe/Middle East normalizing faster than Western Europe; renewal productivity improved. FY27 has the largest EBA renewal cohort, concentrated in Q4.
- SBC guided to ~9% of revenue (from ~11%). FY29 non-GAAP margin target 41% maintained.
- MaintainX revenue to be disclosed for four quarters; Design/Make split to be discontinued.
- Amy Bunszel (EVP AEC Solutions) to retire after 23 years.

Band 3 needs disclosed usage numbers; band 2 = "launches, pilots, partnerships and narrative only; no adoption numbers." Points to **2**.
- **D1:** No AI revenue disclosed; consumption revenue (~17%) is Flex, not AI. Stays at the bottom band.
- **D2/D3:** Unchanged facts. D3 was deliberately re-rated 4→3 on 2026-06-17 (absolute-lens pass). New evidence (NR3 at top of range, multi-year-discount removal improving price realization) argues the moat is holding, but doesn't overturn that rationale.

- **M1:** Q3 guide below consensus → current-quarter and next-quarter EPS estimates cut ~3% over 90 days; FY27 flat, FY28 −0.5% (yfinance eps_trend 2026-10-16). Neutral-to-negative.
