## 1. Pre-research mental model (written BEFORE searching)

SPAC'd in 2022 (SVF Investment Corp 3 / SoftBank). Builds full-warehouse case-handling
automation — bots on a structure, no fixed conveyor — sold as multi-year installation
projects. Walmart is the anchor and, in my model, roughly 85–90% of revenue. GreenBox JV
with SoftBank (2023) was meant to be the diversification vehicle. Revenue ~$1.8–2.5B, gross
margin low (high-teens to ~20%) because it's a systems-integration P&L; adjusted EBITDA only
recently positive. Backlog ~$22B, many years of revenue but converting slowly. Restated
three FY2024 quarters in late 2024 on revenue recognition; securities class actions
followed. Bought Walmart's Advanced Systems & Robotics unit in early 2025 for ~$200M plus a
Master Automation Agreement. Fiscal year ends late September, so Q3 FY2026 covers the June
quarter and should print in early August 2026.

**What I expected to be stale:** the Q3 print; the exact litigation status (dismissed?
settled? in discovery?); whether the SEC subpoena disclosed in FY2025 Q3 was closed; and the
stock's 2026 move.

## 6. Implications for ratings

- **The bull and bear both got sharper.** Bull: GAAP profitability arrived, gross margin is
  expanding, software revenue nearly doubled, $2.0B net cash, $22.7B contracted.
  Bear: one customer is 85% of revenue, the internal-control failure that caused the
  restatement is **still not fixed**, and the market has taken half the price out since
  December.
- **The expectations picture inverted.** The decision record framed SYM as the rule-14
  "peak multiple, crowded" profile. On 2026 data it is the opposite: a −49% drawdown from
  the high, a Goldman target cut, and consensus EPS flat. Momentum ratings must reflect
  that, and the Value inputs will look very different from the research snapshot.
- **R4 is the rating that moved most on fresh evidence.** An unremediated material weakness
  plus an undecided securities class action is squarely band 2, and it is not a stale
  2024 story — it is a March 2026 management conclusion.
- **Rule-9 refresh is mandatory by 2026-08-12** (Q3 prints Aug 5). If revenue or EPS beats
  or misses by >15%, that is a same-session refresh under rule 9 priority 1.
