## 1. Pre-research mental model

SNOW is the consumption-priced cloud data platform; growth had re-accelerated to ~34% product revenue in Q1 FY27 with NRR 126%, and AI (Cortex Code, Snowflake Intelligence) was framed by management as the main driver of a 27%→31% FY guide raise. My understanding of the AI claims came from secondary recaps: Cortex Code at 7,100+ accounts, ">9,100 accounts using AI weekly", and the CFO calling Cortex Code "the largest driver" of the raise. I expected Q2 FY27 (late Aug/early Sep 2026) to show ~30–35% growth, still GAAP losses on heavy SBC, Databricks as the main competitive watch, and no AI revenue dollar figure beyond the old ~$100M run-rate. Litigation: I knew of the 2024 data-breach MDL and nothing else material.

## 6. Implications for ratings (flags only — proposals go to the rating session)

- **D5 (new Layer-10 lens):** measured AI usage KPIs growing fast (CoCo 4,400 → 7,100 → 9,100+ in two quarters) AND visible in company results (three consecutive accelerations; ~half of the acceleration attributed to AI; two guide raises; op margin +400 bps). Guard checks: Observe is ~1 pt (organic still ~35%); the AI gross-margin drag is real but company operating margin still expanded. Argues for the top band.
- **D1:** AI-product revenue still undisclosed beyond the old $100M run-rate; a strict estimate stays under 10–25% — no basis to move the Dom-approved compromise of 3.
- **M1 / M2:** both mechanically at the top band (estimates +15% in 90d; +90 pp vs IGV over 6 months).
- **M3:** no buys; all large sales 10b5-1 or exercise-and-sell → routine default.
- **R1/R2:** literal rubric reads higher than current 4s (no ≥10% customer; no China/asset leg; EMEA 16.7%).
- **R3:** net cash and 23% adj FCF guide, but convert stack more than doubled and SBC dilution ~4%/yr.
- **R4:** the "Clean" rationale behind the current 4 predates or ignores two securities class actions, an MDL past dismissal and an LLM copyright class action.
- **R5:** AI = consumption tailwind confirmed by numbers; residual risks (AI margin drag, Databricks) unchanged.
- **Rule 15:** Q2 FY27 GAAP loss both periods; existing ruling (null, quarter_end 2026-07-31) is current. Q2 contains no dominant one-time item (strategic-investment gains ~$34.8M in 6M — minor).
- **Rule 35:** no new enforcement action, no securities case filed in the last 90 days (newest district case 2026-05-22; the 2026-10-14 filing is an appeal), no guidance cut, no transformative transaction. Convert financing is not transformative.
- **Thesis-break watch:** none triggered. Watch RPO deceleration and AI gross-margin drag at Q3 (≈2026-12-02).
