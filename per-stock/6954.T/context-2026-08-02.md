## 4. DIFF

**vs. the universe appendix:** the appendix's FANUC numbers are **one quarter and one fiscal year stale** — it cites the January 2026 revision to FY26 revenue of ¥840.7B (+5.5%) and robot division +11.1%. Since then FANUC printed FY3/26 at ¥857.8B (+7.6%) and has guided FY3/27 to ¥948.1B with a **+19% profit raise**, on a Q1 that grew revenue 18%. The appendix's qualitative points (NVIDIA Isaac/Omniverse, Google Gemini/Intrinsic on 1.1M robots, net cash, "China = growth driver AND biggest threat; no humanoid entry") all hold. **New:** the explicit management rationale for abstaining from humanoids (two-arm systems at ~1/10th the cost), and China at 30% of Q1 revenue.

**vs. my pre-research model:** I expected a slow grind and depressed margins. The guided FY3/27 operating margin is ~23% and profit is guided +19% — the cycle has turned harder than I assumed. My "structural share loss to Chinese domestics" prior is intact and quantified (40% domestic unit share).
