## 1. Pre-research mental model

PPL is a regulated utility holding company — PPL Electric (Pennsylvania, T&D only; PA generation is deregulated), LG&E/KU (vertically integrated Kentucky), and Rhode Island Energy. The AI angle: one of the largest disclosed data-center interconnection queues in the country in PA (advanced-stage pipeline 28.3 GW at Q1 2026, ~10x since early 2024; ~10 GW under Electric Service Agreements, ~5 GW under construction), plus Kentucky's own 11.9 GW pipeline — driving a $23B 2026–2029 capital plan and 10.3% rate-base CAGR (to $42.9B by 2029), with ongoing EPS guided to the top end of 6–8%. Structurally, PA monetizes DC load as *wires rate base at a capped regulated ROE* (no energy margin — that goes to merchant generators), Kentucky captures it via GCR/Excess-Facilities riders at ~9.775% ROE, and the only uncapped/merchant-like leg is the 51/49 Blackstone JV building PA gas generation under long-term ESAs — which as of the last briefing had signed ZERO ESAs ten months post-announcement. Most recent reported quarter with solid knowledge: Q1 2026 (GAAP EPS $0.60, ongoing $0.63, 2026 guide reaffirmed $1.90–$1.98).
