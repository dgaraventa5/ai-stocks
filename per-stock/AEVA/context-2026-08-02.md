## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before searching)

What I believed coming in: Aeva is a venture-stage FMCW lidar company with genuine technical differentiation (per-point instantaneous velocity, which time-of-flight lidar physically cannot produce) trading at a venture-stage multiple on almost no revenue. I expected a Daimler Truck design win that is real but years from volume, a cash-burn clock, and a "robotics now exceeds automotive" narrative that would turn out to be unit-count framing rather than revenue. I expected the balance sheet to be the binding constraint.

The 10-Q states plainly: "revenue recognized over time was from non-recurring engineering services" (Note 2), and NRE was **61% of Q1 2026 revenue** versus 26% a year earlier. ## 5. Rating implications

- **D1 = 5 on mix, not on scale.** ~100% perception revenue, no legacy business — but the base is $21.0M TTM and product revenue is shrinking. D1 measures percentage; the scale problem belongs to Value/Growth (P/S 57.0).
- **D2 = 2.** Component supplier two layers from the end customer (inside Nikon's, SICK's and Bendix's products), no volume, no cost position, 31% GM mostly on NRE, in a segment with a documented bankruptcy graveyard and collapsing Chinese ASPs.
- **D3 = 3.** FMCW-on-silicon-photonics is genuinely hard and the Nikon + SICK design-ins are third-party validation. Bias check passes: the stock is *down* 10% over a year, so this is not a halo rating.
- **D4 = 4** — expanding capacity (Asia PP&E +26% q/q), multi-year Nikon production agreement, funded by the June raise; not 5 without disclosed volume commitments (RPO only $31.4M).
- **D5 = 3** (L11 lens, proposed) — strong named counterparties for the size, but pre-volume and indirect.
- **R1 = 2** (47% customer). **R2 = 3** (two legs: 62% EMEA single-programme demand + 45% of PP&E in Asia). **R3 = 2**.
- **M2 = 1** and it is benchmark-insensitive (−30pp vs XLI, −114pp vs SOXX).
- **M3 = 3** — the rubric procedure applied literally, with the aggregate-optics counterargument flagged rather than silently split.
