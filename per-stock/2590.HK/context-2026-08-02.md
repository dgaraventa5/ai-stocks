## 1. Pre-research mental model (written BEFORE searching)

Beijing-based AMR warehouse robotics (goods-to-person shelf-carrying robots, tote-to-person,
sorting, forklift AMRs) — the archetypal Kiva-style AMR vendor selling to 3PLs and
retailers globally. Listed on HKEX in July 2025; before that a heavily VC-funded unicorn
burning cash. Revenue around CNY 3B with roughly half from outside China — Nike, Decathlon,
Boots, Japanese 3PLs. Gross margin mid-30s. The structural overhang is US/EU
procurement-restriction risk on Chinese robotics.

**What I expected to be stale:** FY2025 results and the first-profitability claim, H1 2026,
any US restriction developments, and post-IPO lockup/share-price dynamics.

## 6. Implications for ratings

- **On fundamentals this is the most attractive-looking name in the bucket:** #1 global AMR
  share for seven years, 31.6% revenue growth, 31.7% order growth, first positive operating
  cash flow, 46.6% overseas gross margin, 950 customers, and a valuation the market has cut
  by ~72% from the high.
- **On risk it is the most exposed.** 75% of revenue is exported from China into markets —
  above all the US — where an active Section 232 robotics-tariff investigation is running,
  and where Western warehouse buyers face growing procurement restrictions on Chinese
  robotics. That is a policy leg *and* an asset leg (all production is Chinese). The
  discount is not mispricing; it is the market pricing a binary.
- **Momentum is the worst in my five** — at the 52-week low, −35% in three months, with a
  fresh lock-up expiry.
- **The humanoid/embodied-intelligence narrative should earn no rating credit yet.** It is a
  stated 2026 ambition with no disclosed revenue. Judge AI materiality, per the
  `project_ai_adopter_bucket3` lesson.
