**The synthesis that matters:** the June briefing read Q1 as a genuine inflection. Q2 says otherwise —
revenue grew but the company fell back into operating and adjusted-EBITDA losses, cash halved, and
inventory and grant receivables absorbed over $170M of working capital. The AI thesis advanced on
paper (KORE closed, T1 NRI branded) with nothing contracted behind it yet. And the financing question
got sharper rather than softer: management itself calls the $120M convert a *bridge* to a larger,
debt-heavy package still to be arranged for G2_Austin Phase 1. TE is a funding story with a solar
manufacturing business attached and an AI option that is currently a brand, not a backlog.

## 5. Implications for ratings (flag only — NOT pre-decided)

- **D1 (AI revenue %)** — no evidence supports lifting it. Q2 AI/data-center revenue is, as far as the
  filings show, **zero**; T1 NRI closed on 2026-07-01 and its market entry is prospective.
- **D5 (strength of AI-buildout tie)** — the debatable one. KORE/T1 NRI is a real closed acquisition
  with an explicit data-center-infrastructure purpose, which is more than the June "announced" status;
  but per the rule-27 precedent that deck-only/unquantified claims cap low, **an entry point with no
  contracted MW or customer is not a quantified tie.**
- **R1 (customer concentration)** — the strongest case for a *downgrade* anywhere in this rotation.
  ~100% of sales and 100% of receivables from one related-party customer, and the customer base
  narrowed year over year. If R1 currently reflects the FY25 two-customer picture, it is stale.
- **R3 (balance-sheet risk)** — downward pressure (worse). Cash −$114.3M in six months, adjusted EBITDA
  back negative, and **management's own language says Phase 1 of the flagship fab is not yet funded.**
  Going concern is clean, which is the offsetting fact and should be stated alongside.
- **R4 (regulatory)** — 45X credit eligibility remains load-bearing; $95.4M of grants receivable is
  now a material asset dependent on it.
- **Rule-9 objective refresh: DUE.** Note yfinance had **not** ingested the Q2 statements as of
  2026-08-24 (its latest quarter is 2026-03-31) — a refresh run before Yahoo catches up will silently
  use stale Q1 data. Use the 10-Q figures in §2.
- **Rule-15 check:** the Q2 loss is *operating*, not one-time-dominated — no EPS-YoY blanking case here.
