## Pre-research mental model

PLUG is a green-hydrogen and PEM fuel cell company whose real revenue base is material handling (GenDrive forklift power for Amazon/Walmart warehouses), electrolyzers (GenEco), delivered hydrogen fuel and PPAs — not data-center power. It has a long history of deeply negative gross margins, ~$150M+/quarter cash burn, chronic equity dilution (share count roughly doubled in two years), a going-concern warning in the FY2023/FY2024 filings, and a $1.66B DOE loan guarantee whose associated hydrogen buildout it suspended in Nov 2025. Customer concentration is severe (Amazon + Walmart). The data-center angle going in was assumed weak-to-inverted: as of the June briefing, PLUG's only material "data center" activity was *selling its own hydrogen-plant sites and interconnect assets to* Stream Data Centers for liquidity, plus a non-binding LOI to "explore" backup power — no deployed, revenue-generating data-center power.

## Implications for ratings

Flagging which dimensions the new evidence bears on and why. **No numbers proposed** — per rule 12 the rating decision stays with Dom.

- **D1 (AI/data-center revenue % today)** — evidence is unchanged and, if anything, more firmly documented: no data-center revenue line, no contracted MW, and the July 8-K's own forward-looking language treats data-center deployment as something to "pursue"/"explore." Nothing here supports movement upward. The $50M Graham + $142M Gateway proceeds are one-time asset disposals, not product revenue.
- **D2 (supply-chain position / pricing power)** — no data-center pricing exists because no data-center sales exist. New evidence is neutral-to-negative: PLUG accepted a *fixed* $142M (bottom-of-range timing) with a nine-month extension and had to structure an interim closing and a repurchase right — that is the negotiating posture of the liquidity-constrained side of the table.
- **D3 (moat)** — one small positive data point: the Walmart Release Event License Agreement monetizes GenKey IP with recurring fees while PLUG retains ownership, which is a (minor) evidence of switching-cost/IP value. Offsetting: PEM-for-data-centers is a contested field where competitors already hold contracts PLUG does not.
- **D4 (secular tailwind)** — hydrogen policy tailwind is deteriorating, not improving: DOE loan suspended with self-disclosed termination risk, the flagship DOE project site sold off, and the pivot is explicitly away from self-built hydrogen. Worth re-examining whether D4=3 still reflects the funding environment.
- **D5 (strength of AI-buildout tie, rule-23 parallel scale)** — the counterparty (Stream US Data Centers) is named, the terms are disclosed, and the relationship has now *expanded* to a second transaction with an explicit 164 MW capacity reference. But the tie is as an **asset seller**, and the power-solutions leg remains non-binding exploration. This is the dimension where the evidence is most genuinely two-sided and most deserves discussion.
- **M1 (EPS revisions)** — Q2 prints 2026-08-10; guidance (13–15% FY revenue growth, H1 ≈40% of the year, EBITDAS-positive exiting Q4) is a hard, checkable near-term test. Do not re-rate M1 before 08-10.
- **M2 (relative strength)** — clearly affected: −50.2% from the 2026-05-27 peak, ~flat YTD, trading at $2.06 vs a $4.58 52-week high. Any M2 rating set during the spring narrative rally is stale. The rule-11 50DMA % should be refreshed for this name.
- **M3 (insider activity)** — mechanically: zero open-market buys YTD 2026, two open-market sells, everything else grants. Per the rubric's asymmetry principle (buying is the reliable signal; selling is ambiguous), the absence of any insider buying with the stock halved from its peak is the more informative half.
- **R1 (customer concentration)** — unchanged structurally (Amazon + Walmart dominate). Mild positive: management cites second/new sites, Stellantis and other European OEMs, Southwire. Mild new consideration: the Walmart license agreement deepens the relationship on non-equity terms.
- **R3 (balance sheet / liquidity risk)** — the dimension most clearly affected.
