## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before searching)

NBIS is the ex-Yandex N.V. Dutch holdco turned pure-play AI GPU cloud (neocloud). As of the June briefing: Q1 2026 revenue $399M, ARR $1.92B, FY26 guidance ARR $7–9B / revenue $3.0–3.4B, capex $20–25B; anchors Microsoft (~$17.4–19.4B, Sept 2025) and Meta (up to $27B, March 2026), giving extreme two-customer concentration; ~3.5 GW "contracted power" but only ~170 MW energized at YE2025; funded by converts, prefunded warrants and asset-backed debt; non-core stakes in ClickHouse, Toloka and Avride. Since then (from the news log): Meta launching "Meta Compute" (July, −17% day), a Palantir sovereign-AI partnership (Sept) and a $3.5B Citi bridge loan (Oct). I expected Q2 (Aug) and Q3 (Nov) prints to show revenue ramping toward $3B+ for the year, with the debate centred on funding stress, dilution and whether the ARR target is reachable. I did not know the Q3 numbers or whether guidance held.

## 5. DIFF: what changed since the 2026-06-22 briefing

1. **Guidance cut, first since the AI-cloud pivot.** FY26 revenue to $3.0–3.2B; the year-end ARR target slips to February and ARR guidance is dropped. Cause is third-party capacity timing, not demand — but it is the Microsoft-linked site.
2. **Quality of the headline numbers fell.** ~33% of Q3 revenue ($325M) is self-described non-recurring; September ARR likely includes it (arithmetic above). ARR is being retired as a metric the quarter it would have been tested.
3. **Capital intensity stepped up again:** 2026 capex $20–25B → ~$27B; 2027 $40–45B against $12–13B revenue.
4. **Balance sheet much heavier:** debt ~$8.4B (3/31) → $14.2B (9/30) + $3.5B Citi bridge (Oct, due Oct 2027); cash down $9.3B → $7.4B; Q3 operating cash flow negative; shares +15.4% in two quarters.
5. **Customer base broadened:** >$12B new contract value in Q3–early Q4 from existing customers, mainly AI natives; enterprise names added. RPO $38.7B. Concentration on the backlog is still heavy (Microsoft + Meta dedicated ≈ much of RPO; not disclosed).
6. **Pricing firmed:** ACV/MW $20M → >$25M; GPU-hour +20% QoQ; prepayments >100% of capex on new deals. This is new evidence for D2 pricing power.
7. **Contracted power 3.5 GW → >4 GW** (5 GW YE target). Energized MW still undisclosed.
8. **Price collapse:** −59% since 2026-06-22 and −60% from the June 18 peak.
9. **New litigation exposure:** Alabama zoning suits against an owned 2027 site.
10. Meta Compute (July) threat: not addressed on either call; Meta deal "on track" for early 2027 per the Q2 letter.
