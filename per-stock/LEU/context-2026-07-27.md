## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before searching)

Centrus is the only US-owned/US-technology enricher and the only company to have produced and delivered HALEU in the US. Two segments: LEU (commodity SWU/uranium to existing-fleet utilities — the revenue majority, lumpy) and Technical Solutions (the DOE-funded HALEU demo cascade at Piketon, ~900 kg/yr). As of the 2026-06-10 briefing, direct AI-tied revenue is ~0% — the AI link is forward-looking (post-2029 commercial HALEU) and intermediated (SMR developers like Oklo/X-energy/TerraPower stand between Centrus and the hyperscalers). The $900M January DOE commercial-scale award was still "subject to a definitive agreement" and DOE had funded a second HALEU entrant (General Matter), making the monopoly explicitly time-limited by policy. Open questions I carried in: was the $900M definitive contract signed (prior Flag #3), and had any advanced-reactor/hyperscaler-linked offtake progressed past "engagement"?

## 5. DIFF vs prior mental model

- **Resolved (prior Flag #3): the $900M award is now a SIGNED, definitive firm-fixed-price contract** (June 30). It moves commercial HALEU from "funded-but-pre-definitive" to contracted, with a concrete deliverable (1 MTU HALEU by March 2032), milestone-payment structure, and — importantly — Centrus takes **title to the deployed enrichment capacity** on completion. The build-out is explicitly framed as transitioning the sole US HALEU cascade to **commercial** operation, targeting 12 MT/yr plus LEU-backlog capacity.
- **New commercial linkage: the Oklo LOI (June 18) is a SUPPLY-direction agreement**, and broader than the bare 8-K implies — HALEU to fuel up to five Aurora powerhouses on Oklo's 1.2 GW campus, deliveries from 2029, with possible Oklo prepayments. This is the most direct AI-datacenter-adjacent commercial development to date (Oklo's campus is oriented to datacenter/AI power demand), and a progression from the March deconversion-JV discussions. Still **non-binding**, definitive agreement pending — so it is not yet revenue or bankable backlog.
- **Confirmed / unchanged:** Direct AI-tied revenue today is still ~0%; the AI link remains forward-looking (post-2029) and intermediated through SMR developers. Prior demo contract fully delivered (>1,900 kg cumulative), so the demo phase is complete and the story is now execution on the commercial build-out. General Matter as a second funded HALEU entrant (from the January round) is unchanged — the moat remains time-limited by policy, but Centrus retains the only operating cascade and now the only signed commercial contract.
- **Stale-but-not-wrong:** Prior Flag #1 (Q1 stock drop on consensus miss) — Q1 was indeed a ~22% EPS miss / ~6% revenue miss vs estimates; next data point is Aug 5 Q2. `thesis.md` is still the empty template (prior Flag #5) — unchanged, no populated per-name thesis exists.

## 6. Implications for ratings (dimensions affected — values NOT pre-decided)

- **D1 (% revenue genuinely AI-tied today):** No change in substance — still ~0% direct AI revenue. The Oklo LOI and $900M contract are forward (2029+) and, for Oklo, non-binding. Do not let the signed contract inflate *current* AI-revenue mix; DOE funding is national-security policy, not datacenter demand.
- **D2 / moat (HALEU position & pricing power):** Strengthened on the durability axis — signed commercial contract, cascade transitioning to commercial operation, title to enrichment capacity on completion, demo obligations fully delivered early. Still tempered by General Matter as a policy-funded competitor and by the fact that near-term HALEU output is DOE-contracted (not auctioned at market prices).
- **D5 (hyperscaler/AI-buildout linkage via SMR customers):** Most-affected dimension. The Oklo supply LOI (up to five Aurora powerhouses, 1.2 GW datacenter-oriented campus, possible prepayments) is a genuine step-up from "engagement" and from the March discussions — but it is a **non-binding LOI**, not a definitive offtake. Weigh as improved-but-still-pre-definitive; the tie to AI power demand runs through Oklo's campus, not a direct Centrus–hyperscaler contract.
- **Risk (R3 balance sheet / R4 regulatory / R5):** Milestone-payment structure and DOE cost-share reduce financing risk on the build-out versus a fully self-funded expansion; NOL protection (Section 382 amendment) extended to 2029. Non-Layer-10 name → R5 defaults to 5 (no AI-disruption to the fuel-cycle model). Execution risk on a 2029 first-capacity / March-2032 delivery schedule is the live concern, not obsolescence.
