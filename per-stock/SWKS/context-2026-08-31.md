## 1. Pre-research mental model

Skyworks is the RF front-end supplier whose economics are dominated by Apple — historically ~65-70% of revenue — with a smaller broad-markets business in automotive, IoT and infrastructure. My model had SWKS facing a structural content-loss risk at Apple (dual-sourcing and Apple silicon internalization), a declining-to-flat revenue base, and a Qorvo merger agreed in late 2025 that was in regulatory review with an uncertain timeline. I did not have the merger financing or the current Apple content outcome in the model.

## 5. DIFF vs prior mental model

- **The Apple risk stopped being a risk and became a result.** 20–25% content loss on a dual-sourced iPhone 17 RF socket; Apple down to ~57% of revenue from ~63%.
- **Concentration is still the dominant fact.** Even after the loss, ~57% of revenue is one customer — this remains the most concentrated name in the rotation by a wide margin.
- **New and material: $2.0B of senior notes issued 2026-08-10** to fund the $3.0B cash portion of the Qorvo merger. My model had the merger but not the leverage.
- **New: the close accelerated to end-2026** — but an **FTC Second Request (2026-02-05)** is outstanding, so the accelerated timeline is a company expectation, not a cleared path.
- **New: a pending securities class action** (*Nunez*, 8:25-cv-00411) and a **pending Denso patent suit** aimed at the automotive business SWKS is using as its diversification story.
- **Fundamentals are the weakest in this rotation** — the only name here with negative revenue growth *and* deeply negative earnings growth (−68.6% TTM), while six of the eight are in a strong upcycle.

## 6. Implications for ratings (flag only — do not pre-decide)

- **⚠️ R1 (customer concentration)** — **highest-priority revisit.** ~57% single-customer revenue after a realized 20–25% content loss, with Apple's internalization path unresolved.
- **⚠️ R3 (balance sheet)** — $2.0B of new senior notes at 5.00–6.25% against a ~$10.0B market cap, to fund a $3.0B cash consideration. Revisit; note rule 23's +0.77 collinearity with Quality ND/EBITDA and avoid double-counting.
- **⚠️ R4 (regulatory/legal)** — three items stack: a pending Exchange Act class action (*Nunez*), an outstanding **FTC Second Request** on a merger of the two largest US RF suppliers, and a pending Denso patent action. The current rating predates all three.
- **D3 (moat)** — the Denso suit lands specifically on the automotive diversification story. Weigh it there, not as generic NPE noise.
- **D1 / D5** — data-center and automotive growth exist but are not scaled; do not let cohort-wide AI enthusiasm lift SWKS on peers' evidence.
- **M1 (EPS revisions)** — TTM earnings −68.6%, stock fell on a beat. Direction down.
- **Merger caveat for the rating session:** if the Qorvo deal closes by end-2026, essentially every input on this name changes. Consider whether it is worth rating the standalone entity at all, or explicitly rating "SWKS pre-close" and revisiting on close.
