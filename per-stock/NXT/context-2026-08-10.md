## 1. Pre-research mental model (written BEFORE pulling data)

Nextracker is the #1 global supplier of utility-scale solar trackers — the steel-and-motors systems
that tilt PV panels to follow the sun. Its AI-supply-chain relevance in this project is indirect:
solar is a fast-to-deploy generation source for behind-the-meter and grid-adjacent data-center power.
My model was of a single-product hardware company with strong share, exposure to US solar policy
(IRA 45X credits, tariffs) and interest rates, competing mainly with Array Technologies and GameChange.
I did not have any acquisitions, product diversification, or a corporate rebrand in the model.

## 6. Implications for ratings (flagging only — not pre-deciding)

- **D1 (AI-revenue %)** — revisit, carefully. NXT's revenue is utility-scale solar, most of which
  serves the grid generally, **not** data centers specifically. The company's AI framing is a
  positioning claim; I found no disclosed data-center revenue share. Per
  [[feedback_adopter_ai_angle_depth]] and the memory note on judging AI materiality, D1 should be
  rated on disclosed AI-driven revenue, not on the rebrand narrative. **Resist the temptation to lift
  D1 on the strength of the name change.**
- **D5 (strength of AI-buildout tie)** — same discipline. $5.5B backlog is large but is *solar*
  backlog; a contracted tie to the AI buildout specifically is not evidenced in what I retrieved.
- **D2 (supply-chain position)** — revisit **upward-leaning**: #1 US and global tracker share
  (Wood Mackenzie), now extending into eBOS, inverters and storage.
- **R4 (regulatory)** — **the dimension most in need of review.** ~$99M of IRA 45X credits and
  tariffs net is ~42% of adjusted EBITDA. Any change to 45X materially changes this company's
  earnings power. This is a concrete, quantified regulatory dependency — exactly what R4 measures.
- **R3 (balance sheet)** — check post-M&A: four deals in roughly one quarter, one still pending
  (Zimmermann). Integration and consideration terms should be read from the 10-Q.
- **Growth inputs** — flag for the objective refresh: headline +8% YoY revenue vs a record backlog.
  Whichever the Watchlist picks up, note the divergence.
- **Rule-9 objective refresh:** NXT reported **2026-07-30 — overdue by ~11 days.** Adjusted EPS beat
  by ~14%, just under the >15% priority-1 bar, and no >500bps sequential GM move (GM +210bps QoQ) →
