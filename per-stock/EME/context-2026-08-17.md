## 1. Pre-research mental model (verbatim, written before any data pull)

> "EMCOR is a specialty mechanical and electrical contractor — the people who physically install the electrical distribution, switchgear, piping, and HVAC inside data centers, hospitals, and industrial plants. It is a picks-and-shovels AI play with no technology risk and no product cycle: the moat is skilled tradespeople, bonding capacity, and local density, all of which are genuinely scarce. Growth comes from data-center construction plus bolt-on acquisitions of regional contractors (Miller Electric in early 2025 being the big one). Margins are contractor-thin but have been expanding as mix shifts to complex high-value work. My prior: revenue ~$16-17B annualized, RPO/backlog low-teens billions, data centers a large and growing share of the electrical segment but not yet a majority."

That is sector concentration, which is a different (and for a contractor, more cyclical) risk than single-customer dependence.
