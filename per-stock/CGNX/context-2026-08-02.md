## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before searching)

What I believed coming in: Cognex is the US pure-play machine vision company, a quality industrial franchise with ~70% gross margins that has been range-bound for years on a stagnant logistics/consumer-electronics cycle. I expected a mid-single-digit grower at a premium multiple, with the "AI" angle being mostly deep-learning marketing layered onto a barcode and inspection business. I expected robotics exposure to be a marketing adjacency rather than a revenue line, and I expected the balance sheet to be clean net cash. I did *not* have a view that the business had inflected.

## 5. Rating implications

- **D1 low (2).** The evidence, not the theme: no disclosed robotics revenue, no robotics language in the filings, 85% of revenue in logistics/packaging/consumer-electronics/automotive inspection. Genuine but unsized AI pull-through via semiconductor and consumer-electronics customers.
- **D2/D3 at 4.** The 71.1% gross margin is the pricing-power evidence; near-duopoly with Keyence in vision. Capped below 5 by barcode competition (Zebra, Honeywell, Sick, Datalogic) and by merchant edge-AI silicon being available to every competitor.
- **D4 at 3.** Asset-light: $2.8M capex against $99M of buybacks. Capital is returning, not expanding.
- **R1 to 4** on the 15% customer. **R2 at 4** — China 14% + Other Asia 17%.
- **M1/M2 both 5**, and both are the ratings most exposed to the 08-05 print.
- **M3 = 3** — the mechanical rubric outcome (no buys; all size selling is option-exercise/10b5-1 → routine). The CEO and CFO not selling at all is the offsetting positive; the CLO going to zero shares is the offsetting negative.
