## 1. Pre-research mental model

onsemi is the power-semiconductor and image-sensor franchise whose thesis was built on **silicon carbide for EV traction inverters**, with long-term supply agreements underwriting a big capacity build. My model had that thesis under real pressure: EV demand growth decelerating in the West, SiC pricing under aggressive attack from Chinese suppliers, revenue down double digits, and gross margin compressed from the 47%+ peak into the high 30s. I viewed onsemi as the most cyclically damaged and most China-exposed name in the cohort, with modest AI/data-center content.

## 5. DIFF vs prior mental model

- **Partly right, and the part I was right about got rerouted.** EV/SiC pressure in the West was real, but onsemi redirected SiC into AI data center (+~60% in 2026) and gained China EV share (+60–70%) into a declining market.
- **Wrong on margin direction.** I modelled continued compression; onsemi has expanded gross margin four consecutive quarters to 39.3%.
- **Missed a $7B acquisition entirely.** The Synaptics deal is transformational and repositions onsemi from "power semis" toward "physical AI" edge systems. It also introduces ~18 months of integration and regulatory risk before a mid-2027 close.
- **Missed that the main securities class action (*Lew*) terminated 2026-07-23** — a de-risking event since the last rating.
- **Missed the China anti-dumping proceeding**, which names onsemi and decides within two weeks — a sharper risk here than for TXN/ADI given the China SiC concentration.

## 6. Implications for ratings (flag only — do not pre-decide)

- **D1 (AI revenue %)** — AI data-center revenue more than doubling, with NVIDIA MGX / AWS / Great Wall design wins. Revisit.
- **D5 (AI-buildout tie)** — named design wins across the power tree are the contracted/quantified evidence D5 asks for. Revisit.
- **D2 (supply-chain position)** — SiC share gains in China against a shrinking market suggest better competitive position than I credited. But check whether share was bought with price.
- **D3 (moat)** — Synaptics adds edge compute, connectivity and HMI, but the deal will not close until mid-2027. **Do not rate a moat the company does not yet own**; note it as pending.
- **⚠️ R2 (geography)** — **highest-priority revisit on this name.** onsemi carries both a named China anti-dumping exposure (decision ~2026-09-13) *and* an unusually high China SiC revenue dependence. These compound; the current rating likely reflects neither.
- **R4 (regulatory/legal)** — genuinely two-sided: *Lew* terminated 2026-07-23 (improvement) while the derivative matter (2:24-cv-00552) remains pending and HSR review of a $7B deal is open. Check the derivative case's posture before scoring.
- **R3 (balance sheet)** — the Synaptics deal is **all-stock**, so it does not lever the balance sheet; the cost is dilution, not leverage. Do not mis-file this as a solvency risk.
- **Value context, not a rating:** P/S at the 87.8th percentile of its own 3-year range on revenue still well below peak — the recovery is substantially priced.
