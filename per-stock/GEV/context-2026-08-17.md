## 2. Latest filing highlights

Filings pulled 2026-08-17 via `scripts/sec_edgar.py`: **10-Q filed 2026-07-22** and 8-K same date (Q2 results). Direct from the 10-Q:

- **"As of June 30, 2026, the aggregate amount of the contracted revenues allocated to our unsatisfied (or partially unsatisfied) performance obligations were $176,284 million."** Split: **equipment RPO $87,821M** (36% / 65% / 97% recognized within 1, 2 and 5 years) and **services RPO $88,463M** (16% / 54% / 79% / 92% within 1, 5, 10 and 15 years).
- **Contract liabilities and deferred income increased $14,088 million in the six months ended June 30, 2026**, "primarily due to new collections received in excess of revenue recognition." Total contract liabilities and deferred income **$25,950M**.

That second line is the one to sit with. **Customers pre-paid GE Vernova ~$14.1 billion, net, in six months** to hold equipment slots. That is not a backlog number — it is cash in the door ahead of delivery, and it is the most direct available measure of how scarce turbine capacity has become. It also flatters operating cash flow, which is worth naming before anyone reads the FCF as pure operating performance.

**FCF-conversion check (rule 2b / §2b):** FY-0 76% (FCF $3.71B / NI $4.88B), FY-1 110%. **Clean** — no two consecutive years <60%. AR +25% and inventory +23% against revenue +9% is elevated; in context it is production build-out against the prepaid backlog, and the $14B customer-prepayment inflow more than offsets it. Not the accrual-games signature (which requires *low* conversion plus ballooning working capital).

**Expectations flag (rule 14 / §2c):** `expectations_flag.py GEV` → **clean**. P/S 7.39 = 93.8th percentile of its own 3-year range; revenue YoY 38.2% vs 3-year median 20.3%. High multiple, accelerating growth — both conditions do not hold.
