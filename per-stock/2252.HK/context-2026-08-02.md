## 1. Pre-research mental model (written BEFORE searching)

- MicroPort MedBot is the surgical-robotics spin-out of the MicroPort Scientific group, listed in Hong Kong under Chapter 18A (the "-B" suffix = pre-profit biotech listing rules).
- Toumai is a da Vinci-analogue laparoscopic system; the group also has orthopedic, bronchoscopic and endovascular robots.
- My prior: **a heavily loss-making domestic-substitution play**, sustained by parent-company funding and Chinese hospital procurement policy, with revenue in the low hundreds of millions of RMB and losses far exceeding revenue.
- I expected it to be a China-only story with negligible exports and no Western regulatory access.
- I assumed the US market was closed and would stay closed, and that it was not a competitive threat to Intuitive in any market that mattered.
- I had no view on telesurgery as a differentiator.

### Commercial metrics
- **Toumai cumulative global orders surpassed 300 (June 2026), with more than one-third of those secured in H1 2026 alone**; new orders in H1 2026 exceeded the whole of the prior year. (MedBot corporate news, June 2026.)
- **>200 laparoscopic Toumai orders across nearly 50 countries with ~130 systems installed** as of February 2026 (MedBot corporate news).
- Toumai accounts for **more than half of all laparoscopic remote (tele-) robotic surgeries performed globally** — a genuinely leading position in a distinct product category.
- **EU CE mark achieved mid-2026**, opening the European market and enabling teleoperated applications.

### ★ The competitive datapoint that matters most to this whole cohort
**Over January–May 2026, Toumai became the best-selling robot-assisted surgery system in China, displacing da Vinci** — ending roughly two decades of Intuitive leadership in that market. This is **independently corroborated by Intuitive's own SEC filing**: ISRG's Q2 2026 10-Q (filed 2026-07-21, MD&A "System Demand") states that China system demand "has been adversely affected by increasing competition from domestic robotic-assisted surgical system manufacturers," that this contributed to "**fewer systems being placed in China than we anticipated**," and it **names "Shanghai Microport Medbot (Group) Co., Ltd." in its competitor list.** On the ISRG Q2 call (2026-07-16) David Rosa cited "lower tender activity, increased domestic robotic competition, and policy-driven pricing pressure" in China.

That is the strongest available evidence that MedBot's product is competitive on merit rather than only on procurement policy.

## 5. Rating implications

- **The assigned question, answered plainly:** *no*, H1 2026 audited/reviewed results have **not** confirmed the profit guide. The **2026-07-22 positive profit alert** (RMB 28–40M net profit, revenue +200–230%, GM +15pp, overseas Toumai +450%) is a **preliminary unaudited management estimate**. The **interim report lands 2026-08-28** — that is a hard rule-9 refresh trigger, and the scores set today are explicitly provisional.
- **D1 = 5.** ~100% surgical robotics (Toumai laparoscopic/endoscopic plus orthopedic, bronchoscopic and endovascular platforms). Filing-equivalent confidence is lower than for the US names but the business has no non-robotics segment.
- **D2 = 3, D3 = 3.** Genuine positives — China's #1 RAS system by units Jan–May 2026, >300 global orders across ~50 countries, CE mark, global leadership in laparoscopic telesurgery, and 48% gross margin rising. Genuine negatives — no bottleneck position, five-plus domestic Chinese rivals (Edge Medical, Weigao, Surgerii, Sizherui), policy-driven pricing pressure in the same market ISRG complains about, gross margin still 20 points below Intuitive's 67.8%, and Western IP exposure. Neither dimension earns a 4.
- **D4 = 5.** New orders in H1 2026 exceeded the entire prior year; revenue guided +200–230%; export capacity and CE market access being added simultaneously. This is a major, underway, demand-backed buildout.
- **D5 = 3 (L11 lens, proposed).** Telesurgery at global-leading volume is a real physical-AI-adjacent capability with a hard latency/reliability engineering moat, and there are AI-imaging and "AI-assisted autonomy" claims — but nothing is quantified in results. Mid-score is the honest read; it is not an AI-infrastructure story.
- **M1 = 4, Medium confidence.** No direct EPS-revision feed exists for an HK 18A name. Inferred from a **positive** profit alert (which mechanically forces estimates up) plus a 10-analyst "Strong Buy" consensus at a +80% target. Flagged as inference, not measurement.
- **M2 = 3.** −9.48% over 12 months vs IHI −14.11% → within ±5%, i.e. in line. **Flagged:** IHI is a US medical-device ETF and a poor benchmark for an HK-listed China medtech; and on a shorter window the stock sits below both its 50DMA (23.71) and 200DMA (25.72). If a China-healthcare benchmark is preferred, this rating should be revisited.
- **M3 = 3 (default, flagged).** Foreign issuer — **no SEC Form 4 data exists.** This is the rubric's stated default for absence of data, not a positive read.
- **R1 = 4, Low-Medium confidence, flagged.** Customer-concentration data is **not disclosed** in any source I could reach. >300 orders across ~50 countries argues against severe single-customer dependence, but export sales run through dealers/distributors, and the MicroPort parent is a related-party channel. I am rating on inference and saying so.
- **R2 = 2.** Named worst plausible shock: **US/EU procurement restrictions or tariffs on Chinese surgical robots would impair the export line that just produced the first profit** (overseas Toumai +450% is the entire inflection). The US market is already effectively $0 — a policy leg currently capping material revenue — and **100% of production assets are in Shanghai** (asset leg). Not a 1 because there is no active, existential impairment underway.
- **R3 = 2.** Net cash appears to be roughly one year's loss cover, with a history of group/strategic recapitalisation, offset by an inflecting P&L that may end the burn outright. **Verify at the 2026-08-28 interim.** If the H1 profit is confirmed and cash is stable, this is a candidate to move to 3.
- **R4 = 3, Low-Medium confidence, flagged.** No disclosed material litigation found, CE obtained, NMPA-regulated in the home market. But I could not reach the annual report's legal-proceedings section, and IP-infringement exposure in Western markets is a live category risk for Chinese RAS exporters expanding into Europe. Revisit with the interim report.
- **R5 = 5** (non-Layer-10 default).
- **Rule-19 handling note for the scoring pass:** HKD-trade / CNY-report → general FX branch. FX-convert market cap into CNY, build EV as market cap + debt − cash, and do **not** use yfinance's enterprise value. P/S ≈ 35x on that basis, which is the most expensive multiple in the surgical-medical sub-layer by a wide margin — the rule-14 expectations concern applies here more than to any other name in this batch.
