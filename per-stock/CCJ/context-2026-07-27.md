## 1. Pre-research mental model

Cameco is the largest Western uranium producer (McArthur River/Key Lake and Cigar Lake in Saskatchewan, plus JV Inkai in Kazakhstan), selling mainly through a long-term utility contract book (~230M lb committed, >28M lb/yr deliveries next five years) rather than at spot, with a smaller fuel-services segment. It owns 49% of Westinghouse (Brookfield 51%), whose AP1000 franchise sits at the center of an Oct 2025 US-government term sheet targeting ≥US$80B of new reactor orders — explicitly framed by the term sheet as nuclear underpinning the AI/AGI buildout, but government-intermediated, unvested, and zero current revenue. The AI link is third-order for the uranium book (AI → utility nuclear demand → term contracting → realized price, which lags badly: Q1 2026 realized US$66.21/lb vs ~US$94 term). Coming in, my open questions were: did the Westinghouse definitive agreement get signed, where are uranium prices now, and is production on track.
