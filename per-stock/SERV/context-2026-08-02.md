## 1. Pre-research mental model (written BEFORE searching — honest)

Uber/Postmates sidewalk-delivery spinout, NVIDIA-backed, revenue in the low single-digit millions, scaling toward 2,000 robots with **Uber Eats as the dominant customer (I assumed >50% of revenue)**, funded by repeated equity raises, valued absurdly on sales. ## 4. DIFF

**vs. the universe appendix:** the appendix said "2,000+ robots/44 cities, Q1 revenue +578%, DoorDash added, Diligent acquired. Speculative: 78x sales, losses through ≥2028. Q2 prints Aug 6." All confirmed, and the multiple is now ~71x because the stock fell further. **Not in the appendix:** (a) the customer de-concentration from 57% → 17% top customer — a genuine positive; (b) the Q1 loss stepping up to $49M/quarter against $3M of revenue; (c) the guidance's extreme back-end loading combined with a deliberate H1 deployment pause; (d) Coco's $80M raise and its Uber Eats LA placement — direct evidence the anchor customer multi-sources; (e) the Bear Cave report.

**vs. my pre-research model:** I was wrong that Uber dominates revenue — the top customer is 17%. I was right on dilution, valuation and the de-rating. I under-weighted how quickly a well-funded competitor could land inside Serve's own anchor channel.
