## 6. Implications for ratings (flags only)

- **D5 (new L10 lens, "is AI demonstrably working for this business?"):** the evidence that exists is demand for Zscaler's security-for-AI products from customers adopting AI. Guard (c) and the CrowdStrike anchor (2026-10-16) place that in D1, not D5. Zscaler has disclosed nothing that measures its own AI use: no AI-driven cost ratio, automation rate, sales-productivity attribution, or AI SOC throughput for Red Canary. Record margin and productivity are not attributed to AI, and the 3% RIF "to support AI" is intent, not a result. Company-level growth is decelerating organically (ex-RC +20% -> FY27 ~17%) with flat 115% NRR, so even a generous reading fails the "results moving the same direction" test for a 4. Points to **2**; at most 3 if the AI Protect TCV is counted as adoption, which guard (c) does not allow.
- **D1:** Security-for-AI ARR is undisclosed. Rough bound: >$100M TCV in AI Protect's first year (May) plus +50% QoQ bookings gives an ARR order of magnitude of ~$0.1-0.2B against $3.77B, i.e. ~3-5%. That sits on the 1/2 boundary; the trend is up.
- **D2/D3/D4:** no structural change. Moat: RPO $7.37B and Fortune-500 penetration at 50% vs NRR flat at 115%. Capacity: capex step-up plus four acquisitions = active expansion.
- **M1/M2:** both flipped from deeply negative to strongly positive. Mechanical.
- **M3:** no buys; all sales are 10b5-1 or mandated sell-to-cover. Rich's departure followed only mandated sell-to-cover, which is not discretionary selling, so the band-1 "resignation after sales" trigger does not apply.
- **R1-R5:** no change. R1: no customer ≥10%. R2: EMEA 28% / APAC 15%, no risk-geography leg. R3: net cash ~$1.75B, converts due 2028. R4: routine NPE patent and employment suits. R5: security software, rubric band 4.
- **Rule 35:** the guidance reset happened in May and was reaffirmed on 2026-10-06; there is **no new cut since the last reported quarter**. The CRO change is an execution watch item, not a listed hold trigger.
- **Rule 15:** the existing `eps-yoy-overrides.json` ZS entry (quarter_end 2026-07-31, blank, GAAP loss both periods) matches the latest quarter. No new one-time item. The FY27 restructuring charge ($30-33M, H1 FY27) will need a fresh ruling at the Q1 FY27 print.
