2. **OBBBA tax-credit sunset — a concrete, dated policy factor for the renewables engine.** The One Big Beautiful Bill Act terminates the §45Y (PTC) and §48E (ITC) clean-energy credits for wind/solar that **begin construction after July 4, 2026** and are **placed in service after Dec 31, 2027**; no credits at all for wind/solar in service after 2030. BUT the **5% "safe harbor" was restored by a court ruling** (a project locks in credit eligibility by incurring ≥5% of cost + continuous construction), and NEE publicly says there is **"no earnings cliff"** without the credits; budget legislation supports cash flow via credit monetization through ~2030. NEE targets **$1.6–1.8B of transferable tax-credit sales by 2026.** — Utility Dive; Hart Energy; CLA; 2026 [https://www.utilitydive.com/news/republican-introduce-bill-renewable-tax-credits/818536/] , [https://www.hartenergy.com/alternative-energies/renewables/he-nextera-electricity-demand-battery-renewables/]
