## Pre-research mental model

Prior model (from 2026-06-22): Eaton is one of the cleanest large-cap AI-datacenter electrification plays — sells switchgear, busways, UPS, transformers and power-distribution gear into data centers, riding an electrification/reshoring super-cycle with a large, fast-growing backlog. Q1 2026 confirmed acceleration (DC orders +~240% YoY, Electrical Americas backlog $13.2B +31%, guidance raised) but with one soft spot: **near-term margin compression** (total segment margin 22.7%, −120bps YoY; Electrical Americas 25.6% below plan) that management framed as transitory factory-ramp/input-cost investment, promising a 2H exit rate >30%. The open question into Q2 was whether that sequential margin recovery would actually show up. Also in flight: Mobility/Dana Reverse Morris Trust separation (becoming an Electrical+Aerospace pure-play) and ~$11B M&A wave (Boyd Thermal cooling).

**Key point on the GAAP EPS decline:** GAAP diluted EPS fell −16% YoY even though *operating profit rose in every ongoing segment*. The gap is driven by (a) interest expense nearly tripling ($201M vs $71M) on the ~$11B M&A debt (Boyd Thermal etc.) and (b) a higher effective tax rate (28.1% vs 17.1%). Adjusted EPS was a record $3.15 and *up*. **Interest expense is recurring (not one-time)**, so this is not a clean rule-15 "one-time-dominated" blank — flag for the scorer to examine, do not assume.

## Implications for ratings (flags only — no numeric ratings set)

- **AI-Thesis D1 (AI-revenue directness):** Strongly reinforced. Data-center organic revenue +65% (vs ~23% market) = direct, accelerating, share-gaining AI exposure. Bias check: exposure is genuine and growing, not decelerating.
- **AI-Thesis D2 (supply-chain position / magnitude):** Reinforced — EA backlog $15.2B (+33%), orders +41%, book-to-bill 1.3, $24.1B total firm backlog; 307 GW industry backlog framing. Eaton sits at a pricing-power bottleneck (switchgear/UPS/transformers) and is out-growing its own end-market.
- **AI-Thesis D3 (moat):** Supported — taking share vs a 23% market, expanding into DC cooling (Boyd) alongside power; multi-year (2028+) backlog visibility implies durable position.
- **AI-Thesis D5 (strength of AI-buildout tie / catalysts):** Active and strengthening — DC revenue +65%, 307 GW framing, Boyd ahead of plan, Mobility/Dana RMT (Q1 2027 close, accretive). Multiple near-term catalysts.
- **Momentum:** Fundamentals/orders strongly positive with a second beat-and-raise. Refresh objective **50DMA %** (rule 11, `scripts/momentum_50dma.py`) before trusting Momentum — check whether price action confirmed the beat or repeated Q1's beat-then-selloff pattern. Cannot assert direction without the price series.
- **Risk R1 (customer/end-market concentration):** Watch — growing dependence on data-center/hyperscaler capex as the demand driver; a DC-capex wobble is the concentrated risk. No customer loss surfaced.
- **Risk R3 (balance sheet):** **New negative signal** — interest expense tripled ($201M vs $71M) on ~$11B M&A debt; effective tax rate up to 28%. Leverage rose meaningfully to fund the M&A wave. Examine ND/EBITDA and maturities at scoring; this is the item most changed since June.
- **Risk R2/R4 (geo/regulatory):** No new red flags. Ireland-domiciled (foreign filer note: SEC financials in USD, US-listed — standard). Integration risk from ~$11B M&A persists but Boyd is tracking ahead.
- **Risk R5 (disruption):** Defaults to **5** — Layer 02 physical electrical/power infrastructure, no AI/agentic disruption to core model (rule 16, unchanged).
- **Rule-15 (EPS-YoY one-time distortion):** **Flag for examination, do not blank mechanically.** GAAP EPS −16% while adjusted EPS and operating profit rose — but the driver (interest expense on M&A debt) is *recurring*, not a one-time item; only the tax-rate delta is arguably non-operating. Do NOT assume a clean blank; verify against the 10-Q before any rule-15 ruling (memory: yfinance mislabels recurring items as one-offs).
- **Rule-14 (expectations red flag):** Still elevated — premium multiple, second consecutive beat-and-raise into crowded consensus. Run `python3 scripts/expectations_flag.py ETN` at scoring; qualitative red flag, not a scored metric.

### THESIS-BREAK scan
**None.** Orders accelerating (+41% EA rolling, book-to-bill 1.3), DC revenue +65%, backlog at record $24.1B, guidance raised (twice), margin watch-item recovering as guided, no customer loss / cancellation / regulatory action / going-concern. The GAAP EPS decline is a leverage/tax artifact, not a demand or operating break. Watch items (not breaks): (1) Electrical Americas margin still −200bps YoY — confirm continued 2H recovery toward the 28.8–29.2% FY guide; (2) rising interest expense / leverage from the M&A wave (R3); (3) elevated expectations bar.
