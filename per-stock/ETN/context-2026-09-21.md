## Pre-research mental model

Coming out of the 2026-06-22 briefing: Eaton is one of the cleanest large-cap AI-datacenter electrification plays (switchgear, busways, UPS, transformers, power distribution) riding the electrification/reshoring capex super-cycle with a fast-growing multi-year backlog. Q1 2026 confirmed the demand thesis was *stronger* than assumed (DC orders +~240% YoY, 228 GW / "12-year" industry backlog framing) while surfacing one genuine soft spot — **near-term margin compression** (Q1 segment margin 22.7%, −120bps YoY; Electrical Americas 25.6% below plan) that management framed as transitory factory-ramp + input-cost investment with a 2H exit rate >30%. The open question for Q2 was whether that **sequential margin recovery would actually show up**, plus watching order momentum and the rich multiple (rule-14 crowded-consensus angle). Becoming a cleaner Electrical+Aerospace pure-play via the Mobility/Dana Reverse Morris Trust.
