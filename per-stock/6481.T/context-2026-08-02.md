- FY-Dec-2026 guidance was ¥260B sales / ¥26B OP; P/S 2.94x, P/E ~51x.

The guidance was stale (too low), the −18.7% flag is confirmed *and* I found where it comes from, and I discovered a much bigger accounting issue nobody flagged. See §4.

### Data flags (rule 3) — three of them, one material

1. ⚠️ **The −18.7% revenue print is confirmed and located.** kabutan shows Q1 FY2026 revenue YoY as **−18.7%** while the same period grew **+27.4%** on a like-for-like basis. This is the fiscal-year-transition stub the roster warned about (THK moved to a December year-end; the comparison base is a different-length period). **Do not let −18.7% enter the Growth inputs.** Use +27.4% (quarter) / +14.8% (guided FY).
2. ⚠️ **Rule-15 candidate — EPS YoY should be blanked.** FY2025 net income was **−¥69,891M**, and the transportation-equipment (automotive) business was **classified as a discontinued operation from the fiscal year ended December 2025**. Any FY2025→FY2026 EPS YoY is a swing off a divestiture-driven loss, i.e. **non-operating-dominated**. This is a documented rule-15 blank, and it is **not in the roster's data-flag list** — add it.
3. ⚠️ **Forward P/E conflict.** [stockanalysis](https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tyo/6481/) shows **19.61x**. Company guidance implies ¥6,700 ÷ ¥202.6 = **33.1x** (equivalently ¥750.53B ÷ ¥22.7B). The street is running roughly **68% above company guidance**. Resolve before the Value inputs are trusted.

## 5. Rating implications

- **D1** — THK discloses **no robotics split**. LM guides and ball screws serve machine tools, semiconductor/LCD equipment, general industrial machinery, electronics, and industrial robots. Robotics is plausibly 10–25% but is an **estimate with no disclosure** — the weakest D1 evidence base of the three Japanese names. → **3**, Low confidence, flagged.
- **D2** — Global #1 in LM guides and a high-end ball-screw near-duopoly with NSK, but the competitive set (NSK, Hiwin, IKO, Bosch Rexroth, PMI, Chinese entrants) is 4+ deep and linear rails are more standardized/substitutable than a joint reducer. → **4** (upstream with 2–4 viable competitors), not 5.
- **D3** — Inventor's patent estate and global #1 share, but switching costs on a linear rail are lower than on a cycloidal joint reducer, and pricing is competitive. → **3**.
- **D4** — Recovering from a trough with structural reform underway and a portfolio divestiture just completed; no disclosed capacity program. **Capex figure not obtained — flag.** → **3**.
- **D5 (L11 lens, proposed)** — Own FRED humanoid demonstrator; architecture is right; **zero disclosed humanoid revenue, orders or customers**. Inferred exposure only. → **2**.
- **M1** — Q1 OP +364%; FY2026 guidance set well above what the market carried; consensus running ~68% above company guidance; target ¥7,625 (+13.8%). Positive but **no explicit 90-day revision counts sourced**. → **4**, Medium confidence.
- **M2** — +60.10% over one year vs XLI +20% (1H 2026) → clear >20pp outperformance, tempered by a −18.7% drawdown from the ¥8,244 high.
- **R2** — Worst plausible shock: **US–China escalation**, which would hit both the China demand engine (**+44.1% in Q1**, share of revenue **not sourced — data gap**) and THK's China plants, on top of already-named **U.S. tariff** costs. Two legs → **3**, Low confidence pending the Aug 5 tanshin.
- **R3** — **Net debt ¥44,479M, ND/EBITDA ≈0.9x** (estimated), equity ¥252.7B, dividend ¥184 at an 8% DOE target. → **4**.
- **R5** — Physical component. Default **5**.
