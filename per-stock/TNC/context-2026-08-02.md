## 4. DIFF

**vs. the universe appendix:** the appendix described TNC as "the cheapest real service-robot franchise… robotics ~5% → targeted $250M (~20%) by 2028, on a profitable 13x-forward industrial base. Brain Corp dependency." Three corrections:
- **Robotics is 9% of Q1 2026 revenue (+85% YoY), not ~5%** — better than stated, and the Brain Corp relationship is now locked to 2029.
- **"Profitable industrial base" understates near-term fragility.** Q4 2025 was a net loss; Q1 2026 net income was $0.2M. The ERP failure cost ~$30M of sales and >$20M of remediation.
- **The appendix omits the litigation and management-churn overhang entirely**: open securities-fraud investigations (July 2026) and a **CFO retirement notice five days ago (2026-07-27)**.

**vs. my pre-research model:** wrong that robotics was ~5% (it is 9%), and wrong that this was a drama-free compounder. What I did not anticipate at all — and what is the most decision-relevant fact here — is that the **CEO and two directors put ~$1.26M of their own money into the stock in the open market within days of the crash**.
