## 1. Pre-research mental model (Step 1 — articulated BEFORE pulling fresh data)

> Amkor is the largest US-listed OSAT — back-end contract packaging & test, #2 globally behind ASE. Since the June briefing I expect the AI-advanced-packaging ramp (HDFO / 2.5D for accelerators + the 2H-2026 CPU programs) to have kept accelerating: Q2 2026 should be another record, and the news-log already flags a new 10-year TSMC Arizona partnership on 2026-07-27, which upgrades Arizona from a pure ~2028 option toward a contracted anchor. My model still holds Apple ~30% concentration, thin ~14% gross margin / heavy capex, net-cash balance sheet, and a peak-valuation (P/S near top of its 3-yr range) rule-14 risk that requires the AI mix-shift to persist. Watch items: whether the AI/compute mix is now large enough to move D1 from 3 toward 4, whether the TSMC 10-year deal materially de-risks Arizona, and whether Q3 guidance shows the communications/mobile seasonal step-up masking or supporting the AI story.

**What the diff actually found (see §5):** the mental model was directionally right but understated the magnitude. Q2 was not just "another record" — it was a **gross-margin breakthrough** (16.8%, guided to 18.5-19.5% in Q3, well above the sub-15% band the model held as structural). And the Arizona story got two contracted AI anchors, not one: the 10-year TSMC procurement agreement (June 16) **plus a new multi-year NVIDIA partnership backed by a ~$1.5B prepayment (July 23)** — a direct merchant-AI-accelerator tie that did not exist in the June briefing. Funding: a **$1.15B 0% convertible note** (July 2031) was issued in Q2 and capex tripled, so the net-cash cushion is now roughly neutral and H1 FCF is negative. The rule-14 peak-multiple flag no longer fires (expectations_flag is now clean).

## 6. Implications for ratings (FLAGGING ONLY — not pre-deciding)

- **D1 (AI revenue %):** June set the AI-tied slice at ~10-25% (→D1≈3) because communications/mobile is still the plurality (42%) and "advanced" ≠ "AI." Q2 doesn't overturn that mix, BUT the *direction and contracts* strengthened: record Computing, Computing guided +30% QoQ, and now a direct NVIDIA AI-infrastructure partnership + TSMC CoWoS overflow. Worth re-examining whether the genuinely AI-infra-tied revenue is now toward the top of that band (supports a 3→4 debate) — but show the math; communications is still #1 and Arizona AI revenue is ~2028, so a mechanical 4 may be premature. **Revisit.**
- **D2 (supply-chain position) / D3 (moat):** Meaningfully stronger. Being TSMC's contracted domestic packaging partner, NVIDIA's US-capacity partner (with prepayment), and an Intel EMIB alt-source lifts Amkor from "mid-chain price-taker" toward a scarce, anchored advanced-packaging node in a capacity-short market (10-20% CoWoS shortfall). The June D2≈3 / D3≈2-3 reads low against this. **Revisit both upward — carefully** (still a thin-margin service model with Apple concentration; the margin breakout is one year old).
- **D4 (capacity expansion):** Already strong in June; now *contracted*. $7B AZ build + TSMC 10-yr + $1.5B NVIDIA prepayment + advanced capacity ramp → clearly 4-5.
- **D5 (AI-buildout tie, rule-23 parallel scale):** The biggest single upgrade. June = "indirect, leans 2-3." A direct NVIDIA next-gen-AI-infrastructure partnership backed by a ~$1.5B prepayment is a quantified, contracted tie to the AI buildout. **Revisit upward** (likely 4; weigh that AZ revenue is ~2028 and Apple/comms is still the bulk of *today's* revenue).
- **M (momentum):** M1 EPS revisions strongly positive (beat-and-raise, margin guide up); M2 relative strength still high but the stock *slipped on the print* despite the beat (crowding/profit-taking) — refresh the objective 50DMA% (rule 11) before finalizing. M3 insider unchanged (Kim-family overhang; no new open-market buying surfaced). **Revisit M1/M2 with fresh price + revision data.**
- **R1 (customer concentration):** Apple last disclosed ~29.8% (FY25 10-K; no new % in the Q2 10-Q). Squarely band 3. The TSMC/NVIDIA anchors *may* broaden the advanced-customer book over time, but Apple is still the single largest and the concentration risk is unchanged for now. Hold at 3 unless a fresh disclosure moves it.
- **R3 (balance sheet):** Weaker than June. The clean net-cash position is now ~neutral after the $1.15B convertible, and FCF is negative during the AZ build (partly offset by the $1.5B NVIDIA prepayment, which is customer cash, and a 0% coupon on the convertible). Still investment-grade-ish and manageable, but the "strong net cash → 4" call deserves a re-look; convertible dilution is a new overhang. **Revisit (possibly 3-4).**
- **R4 (regulatory/litigation):** No new litigation (litigation_check clean). CHIPS-incentive clawback conditions and export-control overhang unchanged. Hold ~4.
- **R5 (disruption risk):** Physical back-end manufacturing, non-Layer-10 → **defaults to 5** (rule 16). AI is a tailwind, not a disruptor of the packaging service model. The TSMC/Intel in-housing dynamic is a D2/D3 competitive question, not an R5 business-model-obsolescence one. **No change — 5.**

**No THESIS-BREAK.** Every June thesis pillar held or strengthened; the only genuine deteriorations are (i) the net-cash cushion converting to ~neutral via the convertible and (ii) negative FCF during the build — both expected, financed, and customer-anchored, not thesis-breaking.
