## 1. Pre-research mental model

TI is the largest broad-line analog franchise, with a structural cost advantage from 300mm internal manufacturing (SM1/SM2/LFAB) bought with a multi-year capex program that crushed FCF/share. My model had TI grinding through a long industrial and automotive downcycle with flat-to-down revenue, defending share via price, and treating AI/data center as a rounding-error side market rather than a growth driver. China is a large revenue geography and a live policy risk. I expected the analog cycle to be bottoming but not yet inflecting.

## 5. DIFF vs prior mental model

- **Wrong on the cycle.** I modeled a grinding downcycle; TI is printing +23% revenue with analog operating profit +50% and guiding above seasonal. The inflection already happened.
- **Wrong on data center materiality.** I treated it as a rounding error. It doubled YoY, after +90% in Q1. TI is a real beneficiary of the AI *power-delivery* bottleneck, not just a general-industrial name.
- **Wrong on pricing.** I assumed continued price defense. TI took 15–85% increases in April 2026.
- **New and not in my model at all:** the China anti-dumping proceeding, with a decision deadline 13 days out.
