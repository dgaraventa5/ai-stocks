## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before searching)

What I believed coming in: Ouster is a surviving digital-lidar company in an industry that has mostly gone bankrupt (Luminar, Velodyne, Quanergy, Innoviz on life support). I expected a sub-scale sensor maker growing off a small base with structural gross-margin pressure from Chinese competitors (Hesai, RoboSense) who ship 10–30x the units at a fraction of the ASP, funded by dilution and hoping to reach breakeven before the cash runs out. I expected the StereoLabs acquisition to be the main driver of reported growth and the "robotics platform" framing to be repositioning rather than substance. I did not expect named robot-platform design wins or a genuine US-content regulatory moat.

### The StereoLabs organic-vs-inorganic question — ANSWERED from the filing

10-Q **Note 3** provides unaudited supplemental pro forma revenue as if StereoLabs had been acquired 2025-01-01:

| | Q1 2026 | Q1 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Pro forma revenue | **$50,216K** | **$34,371K** |
| Pro forma net loss | $(24,245)K | $(31,848)K |

**→ Pro-forma combined growth = +46.1%** (50,216 / 34,371 − 1), versus the **+49%** reported headline.

Deriving the split:
- StereoLabs' pre-close stub (Jan 1 – Feb 3, ~5 weeks) = 50,216 − 48,578 = **$1,638K**
- StereoLabs' full Q1 2025 quarter = 34,371 − 32,632 = **$1,739K**
- Extrapolating the stub run-rate to the ~8 post-close weeks implies ≈ **$2.6M** of inorganic revenue inside the reported $48,578K
- **→ Ouster-standalone organic growth ≈ +41%**

**So: the headline +49% is inflated by roughly 8 points, and the underlying business still grew ~41–46%.** That is the honest answer and it is a good one.

**🚩 Rule-3 flag on StereoLabs sizing.** The pro forma implies StereoLabs was running ~$1.7M/quarter (~$7M annualised) in Q1 2025 — materially below the **"$16M unaudited 2025 revenue"** figure cited at deal announcement and carried in the deep-pass research. Either StereoLabs' 2025 was extremely H2-weighted or the $16M is not a comparable measure. **Do not model $16M.** Consideration paid: $32,392K cash + 1,847,677 shares (1,187,672 valued at $19.18 = $22,780K), total $55,172K; 660,005 shares are compensatory retention subject to 4-year staggered lock-up.
