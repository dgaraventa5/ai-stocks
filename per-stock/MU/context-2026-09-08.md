**Read-through:** a settlement at the union's FY27 ask (15% of operating profit) would be a ~$20B/yr cost at the Q4 run-rate (~$43B quarterly op income × 4 × 15%) — that arithmetic is the reason to expect Micron to settle nearer the Samsung/SK Hynix 10–10.5% benchmark, still ~$13–17B/yr, i.e. several points of operating margin on a business guided to ~86% GM. The one-off FY26 ask is the bigger tail. A strike that actually stops Taichung HBM lines would hit committed, sold-out volume with no substitute site until Hiroshima/Idaho (2028). This is a **new, unmodelled cost line** whether or not anyone strikes.
