### 2.2 Strategic Customer Agreements: the largest change in the filing

- **Remaining performance obligations ~$134B at 2026-09-03**, of which $13B is already contract liabilities; about one-fourth is expected within twelve months and all within five years (10-K Note 16). The Q3 10-Q showed ~$5B (prior briefing §2.1). RPO counts committed volumes at minimum pricing and excludes agreements with no fixed price or band (10-K Note 16).
- **Deposits actually received: $12.75B in FY26**, nearly all repayable 2029–2031 and booked in financing cash flow, not operating (10-K Item 7 Liquidity; cash-flow statement). Letters of credit issued or committed: $7B (10-K Note 16). Noncurrent customer contract liabilities $12,895M (10-K balance sheet). This answers the September question: the money is arriving. It remains a liability, not permanent capital.
- Floor language: gross margins from banded agreements, "even at floor pricing levels," are expected to be "meaningfully above our peak quarterly margins in any past cycle" (10-K Item 7).
- Scale check (my arithmetic): one-fourth of $134B is about $33.5B over twelve months, against the FQ1 guide annualized at about $246B. The contracted floor covers roughly 14% of current run-rate revenue. Floors sit far under today's prices, so most of the current revenue is still market-priced.
- Risk factor: a labor stoppage "could also impair our ability to meet delivery schedules under strategic customer agreements, which could result in contractual liability" (10-K Item 1A).

### 2.3 Customers and geography

- China: the May 2023 CAC decision remains in the risk factors (10-K Item 1A). Micron names CXMT and YMTC as competitors and flags oversupply from state-backed investment (10-K Item 1).
