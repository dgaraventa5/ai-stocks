## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before searching)

Melexis is the global #1 in automotive magnetic-position sensor ICs — a ~€2.7–3.1B Belgian fab-lite semiconductor company, ~85–90% automotive, running high dividend payout and historically net cash. Its robotics angle is the most concrete of any sensing name: the **Arcminaxis** 18-bit joint encoder and **Tactaxis** tactile force sensor, and a CEO statement on the Q2 2026 call that humanoid business is booking *today* with a US customer. At 17.9x forward and a ~5.4% yield, the deep pass argued the robotics option is effectively free. The blockers are scope (it's a semiconductor, not a motion component) and access (Brussels only).

**The structural read held up. What changed is that the price and the 1-year performance are meaningfully worse than "the option is free" implied — and the 2025 fundamentals were worse than I carried.**

## 4. DIFF — pre-research model vs. fresh reality

1. **"The option is free" needs a footnote: the stock is flat-to-down over a year while its benchmark nearly doubled.** −1.80% over 52 weeks against **SMH +83.02%**. That is not a cheap option on a stable base; it is a de-rating automotive semi with a robotics kicker. The 5.42% dividend yield is doing a lot of the work in the "cheap" argument, and a yield that high on a semiconductor usually means the market disagrees about the earnings base.
2. **FY2025 was a decline year I had not internalised: revenue −10.0%, earnings −34.4%.** The Q2 2026 beat is a recovery off a depressed base, not continuation of growth.
3. **The valuation numbers differ from the deep pass.** Deep pass: "17.9x forward, ~5.4% yield, ~$3.1B" (and a $2.7B figure in the components pass). Current: **forward P/E 19.80, yield 5.42%, market cap €2.69B**. The yield checks out; the forward multiple is ~2 turns higher and the two research passes disagreed with each other on market cap. Use the current figures.
4. **China is the named Q2 growth driver** — which cuts both ways for an automotive semi in 2026, and is directly relevant to R2.
5. **A sell-side downgrade landed in late July** (Deutsche Bank Buy→Hold) against a beat. Same pattern as ALGM: good print, sceptical sell side.
6. Unchanged: the robotics products are real and specific (Arcminaxis, Tactaxis), the humanoid revenue is booking but immaterial, and Brussels is the only usable line.
