## 4. DIFF — where fresh reality departs from the prior model

### 4a. ★ PDYN is no longer an autonomy-software pure play. It is a small vertically integrated defense manufacturer with an autonomy software effort attached.
The Q1 2026 disaggregation is unambiguous: **$1.7M precision machined parts + $1.8M avionics engineering services + $0.1M autonomy development contracts + $0 software.** The characterization in `research/2026-08-02-defense-autonomy-perception.md` ("the only listed pure-play autonomy software company… the software-margin layer the $54.6B Autonomous Warfare Group must buy") and in the universe file ("Only listed pure-play autonomy software (SwarmOS)") describes the *company as of early 2025*, before the 2025-11-14 acquisitions. Management's own language has already moved: **"vertically integrated, autonomy-based defense technology company … advanced avionics, precision-manufactured components, UAVs, and advanced aerospace engineering services."**

**This is exactly the failure mode logged in `feedback_per_name_research` and the Layer-11-adopter lesson, in reverse:** the ticker was included for a software-margin thesis, and the actual P&L is machining and services. It does not disqualify the name — the *strategic* direction is genuinely autonomy — but the D1 rating and any "software margins" narrative must be corrected. **The prompt's rating guidance "PDYN 100% autonomy software" cannot be applied as written.**

### 4b. ★ Rule-15 blank required — FY2025 GAAP net income was non-operating
**FY2025: operating loss −$32.404M, GAAP net income +$10.039M** → **~$42.4M of non-operating gain.** (H1 2025: operating loss −$15.025M, net income +$15.272M → ~$30.3M.) A company with $5.2M of revenue and a $32M operating loss reported a GAAP *profit*. This is the ONDS pattern (rule 15's own precedent: "+$389.5M warrant fair-value gain… adjusted EBITDA −$10.9M").
**Rule-3 honesty:** PDYN carries 2024 private-placement warrants and remeasures both warrant and contingent-consideration liabilities to fair value each period (10-Q Note 2, Level-3 rollforward: warrants $1,514k and contingent consideration $1,382k at 2025-12-31). **Warrant fair-value remeasurement is the most likely driver, but I did not isolate the exact line item and did not verify it against the FY2025 10-K.** What *is* certain and sufficient: **the FY2025 GAAP profit is entirely below the operating line.**
> **RECOMMENDATION: BLANK PDYN's EPS YoY input (rule 15).** GAAP EPS flips sign purely on the measurement window (+$10.0M FY2025 vs −$25.3M TTM-through-Q1-2026), which is the definition of a garbage input. Growth should average the revenue metrics. Cite: Palladyne AI FY2025 operating loss −$32.404M vs GAAP net income +$10.039M (XBRL, CIK 0001826681).

### 4c. The IAI loitering-munition deal is a bigger strategic event than the research pass conveyed
The 2026-06-08 partnership gives PDYN **exclusive U.S. rights to HARPY / HAROP / Mini HARPY** — combat-proven SEAD/DEAD loitering munitions with, per management, no domestic equivalent despite "hundreds of millions of dollars invested by others." That is a *product franchise*, not a software licence, and it points the company further away from the software thesis and toward munitions manufacturing. It is also the single largest potential revenue driver in the story — and it contributes **$0** today.

### 4d. Cash was flat in Q2 despite a $10.2M quarterly operating burn — almost certainly the ATM
Q1 operating cash flow was **−$10.239M**; cash + securities went $43.678M (3/31) → ~$44.0M (6/30). Something funded roughly a quarter's burn. The ATM sold $6.7M in Q1 alone with ~$35.8M of capacity left, and PDYNW warrants are outstanding. **Inference, not disclosure — the preliminary release does not explain it, and the Q2 10-Q will. Flagged.** If it *was* ATM issuance, share count has grown again and the dilution run-rate continues.

### 4e. Runway is ~4 quarters at the Q1 burn rate
$44.0M cash and securities against a **−$10.2M** quarterly operating burn. Management asserts 12 months of resources. Revenue is ramping fast, which helps, but cost of revenue rose 601% YoY and G&A 64% — the acquired businesses bring cost with them. There is no debt and no maturity wall, so this is a *dilution* risk, not a solvency-covenant risk.

### 4f. M3 is a clean rubric application with an uncomfortable result
**Two insiders made three open-market discretionary buys in the last 12 months** (CEO twice, a director once). The rubric's decision procedure step 1 is explicit and was set as precedent on 2026-05-28 after CEVA was wrongly marked down: *"If ≥2 insiders made open-market discretionary buys in the last 12 months → 5, full stop. **Dollar size does NOT downgrade this** … Multiple buyers = 5 regardless of dollar amounts."* The buys here total **~$37,000**. I am following the rubric and flagging the tension rather than quietly re-deciding it (see DEBATABLE).

### 4g. Short interest is 19.52% of shares outstanding
Roughly one share in five is sold short, with a beta of 3.63 and a $247M cap. Not a rating input, but it means the name will move violently on the 8/5–8/6 print in either direction, and it argues strongly for waiting on the full Q2 before sizing anything.
