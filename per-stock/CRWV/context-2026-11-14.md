- **D1 5 / D4 5 / D5 5:** reinforced (≈100% AI compute revenue; contracted power 4.6 GW, capex $37–39B; Layer-9 buyer lens at $30B+). Expect holds.
- **D2 4 / D3 3:** pricing evidence strengthened (prices up ~25% + ~10% in Q3; older GPUs repricing above contract). That is market tightness rather than a moat; customers-as-competitors (Meta Compute) and commoditization critiques still stand. - **M1 5:** beat-and-raise, consensus EPS less negative vs 90 days (FY26 −5.20 → −4.75; FY27 −4.24 → −3.64), but 30-day counts skew down (pre-print cuts). Mixed but net up.
- **M2 5:** **the rubric reading is 1** — CRWV lags SMH by >30 pts on both 3- and 6-month windows.
