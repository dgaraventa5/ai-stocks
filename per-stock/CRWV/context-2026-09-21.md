## Pre-research mental model

Coming in (from the 2026-06-22 briefing + news-log through 2026-09-18): CoreWeave is the flagship neocloud — GPU-as-a-service, NVIDIA-backed, debt-financed buildout — with explosive contracted demand (Q1 2026 revenue $2.08B +112%, backlog $99.4B) set against mounting GAAP losses, doubling interest expense ($536M Q1), and a rising, increasingly *unsecured* and expensive debt stack (9.625% USD / 8.5% EUR notes closed June). The central tension is contracted demand + ~3.5 GW secured power vs. leverage/interest burden and concentration in a few (partly non-IG) AI-lab counterparties. The two watch-items I carried in: (1) customer concentration — improving on management framing but MSFT/OpenAI still dominant and exact % was a data gap; (2) the emerging competitive/counterparty wrinkle — "Meta Compute" (news-log 2026-07-03) and OpenAI's ability to pay.

- **OpenAI / Meta commitments (10-Q text):** OpenAI order form (Sept 2025) — committed to pay **up to ~$6.5B through May 31, 2031**; Meta (March 2026 order form) — initially committed **up to ~$21.0B**. Cash & equivalents **$5,524M** + restricted-current $873M + marketable $15M. Accounts payable $3,633M; deferred revenue current $2,686M.
- **Debt (Note 10) — total ≈ $35.1B:** recourse debt net **$31,405M** (up from $20,726M YE2025) + non-recourse **~$3,663M** (DDTL 4.0 $2,837M + OEM $882M − $56M). Effective rates run high and wide: DDTL 1.0 **15%**, DDTL 2.0 11%, the several senior-notes tranches **~10%**, OEM financing 11%, Magnetar 12%; the two convertibles are cheap (2%). New tranches vs. prior briefing: **2031 9.75% Senior Notes $2,750M** and **2032 Convertible Senior Notes $4,000M** (both new), plus the 2032 9.625% $1,250M / EUR $2,279M closed in June. **No going-concern language** anywhere in the 10-Q (grep-confirmed).

**8-K filed 2026-08-10** (acc 0001769628-26-000357, event 2026-08-07) — **NEW DDTL 5.5 facility, $2.6B** delayed-draw term loan (Borrower: CoreWeave Financing DDTL V-V, LLC; JPMorgan admin agent). Parent-guaranteed, secured by substantially all Borrower assets. **Covenant: Debt Service Coverage Ratio ≥ 1.35x** (from the earlier of commitments-to-zero or 2026-12-31); customary events of default incl. **cross-defaults and "adverse events with respect to certain material contracts."** (The material-contract EoD is the leverage/concentration linkage worth noting — a large customer stumble could trip financing.)

## Implications for ratings (FLAGS ONLY — no numbers assigned or recommended)

**AI Thesis (D1–D5):**
- **D1 (AI revenue %) = 5:** unambiguously reinforced — ~100% AI-infrastructure revenue, backlog $104.2B. No change indicated; confirm it stays 5.
- **D2 (supply-chain position) = 4** and **D3 (moat) = 3:** the **pricing-power** evidence (Citi; "contracting at higher prices," Sept 16) is a mild positive for D2/D3. But **counter-signal for D3**: customers building in-house ("Meta Compute") and the circular-financing critique argue *against* raising moat. Net: re-examine D2/D3 as a pair; evidence is mixed, not clearly directional — do not reflexively lift.
- **D4 (capacity expansion) = 5:** reinforced — contracted power 3.7→4.2 GW, capex raised to $35–39B, active-power target raised. Stays 5.
- **D5 (buyer/AI-tie, Layer-9 lens) = 5:** reinforced by $104.2B backlog + >$25B new early-Q3. Stays 5.

**Momentum (M1–M3):**
- **M1 (EPS revisions) = 5** and **M2 (rel strength) = 5:** guidance raised + beat + backlog surge + post-print +13% support both; refresh mechanically at scoring time (M1 via Yahoo/Zacks revisions; M2 vs SMH; 50DMA % via `scripts/momentum_50dma.py`). Likely still strong — confirm.
- **M3 (insider) = 2:** **revisit.** Large founder selling continues (>$2.3B since lockup; CEO under a Nov-2025 10b5-1 plan; CSO >$1.1B), and **no open-market buys** surfaced. Per rubric M3 step 2, 10b5-1 sales are "routine → default 3," and % of holdings (not absolute $) governs bands 2/1 — so the current **2** may be *stricter than the rubric supports* unless the sales are discretionary open-market and >15% of holdings. **Action for the rating session: verify Form-4 transaction codes (10b5-1 vs discretionary) and % of holdings on EDGAR before confirming 2 vs 3.** Direction genuinely ambiguous.

**Risk (R1–R5):**
- **R1 (customer concentration) = 1:** now backed by hard disclosure — top-2 ≈62%, top-3 ≈72% of Q2 revenue; single largest 36%. That is *below* the >60% single-customer line for band 1 on the *top-1* metric (36% would map toward band 3 on top-1 alone), **but** the top-2/top-3 aggregate and the OpenAI-payment tail keep this firmly high-risk. Re-examine whether **1 vs 2** is right given top-1 has fallen to 36% while top-2 stays ~62% — a genuine calibration question for the session (don't auto-hold at 1 without deciding which metric governs).
- **R2 (geo/export) = 5:** ~93% US revenue, US-located assets; no China/export leg. Confirm stays 5.
- **R3 (balance-sheet risk) = 2:** **the live risk, re-examine.** Total debt ~$35.1B (+~$14B YoY), thin equity (~$5.0B vs $77B assets), interest $640M/qtr, marginal secured cost SOFR+5.5%, several tranches ~10–15%, H1 capex $14.1B, new 1.35x DSCR covenant with material-contract cross-default. Offsetting: $5.5B cash, no going-concern, deferred-revenue/committed backlog, no near-term maturity wall (earliest sizable 2028–2029). Leverage trajectory is *worse* than at last review; whether that argues 2→1 is a judgment call — surface it, don't pre-decide.
- **R4 (reg/litigation) = 4:** no new material regulatory/litigation item surfaced; confirm.
- **R5 (disruption) = 5:** Layer-9 default; GPU compute is physical infra, AI is the tailwind. The "customers-as-competitors" theme is a *demand-durability/counterparty* concern (belongs in D3/R1 thinking), not a rule-16 Layer-10-style model-layer disruption. Stays 5.
