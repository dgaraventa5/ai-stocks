## Pre-research mental model

Coming into this pass (prior briefing 2026-06-22 + news log through 2026-07-03): CoreWeave is the flagship NVIDIA-backed "neocloud," renting GPUs at scale on a debt-heavy, contract-backed model. Q1 2026 had revenue ~$2.1B (+112% YoY), a $740M GAAP loss, interest expense doubling to $536M, backlog ~$99.4B, and ~3.5 GW contracted power. The two genuinely new, scoring-relevant Q1-era facts were the raised FY capex ($31–35B) and a rising *unsecured* cost of capital (9.625% USD / 8.5% EUR notes closed 06-18). Customer concentration was said to be improving (non-IG labs <30% of backlog) but the exact MRQ customer mix was a data gap. The live risk was always R3 (leverage/funding-cost trajectory), not demand; the newest cloud was the 2026-07-03 report that Meta is building "Meta Compute" — a customer potentially becoming a competitor.

## Implications for ratings (FLAGS ONLY — no numeric ratings pre-decided)

**AI Thesis (D1/D2/D3/D5):**
- **D1 (AI-revenue %) / D2 (supply-chain position):** Reinforced — $104B backlog, >$25B new early-Q3, 4.2 GW contracted, price increase absorbed by demand. Among the most direct AI-demand exposures on the watchlist; confirm ratings already credit this (direction: stable-to-up).
- **D5 (AI-buildout tie):** Reinforced/stable — deep hyperscaler + frontier-lab commitments (Meta ~$35B, OpenAI ~$22B, Microsoft, Anthropic). No downgrade signal.
- **D3 (moat):** Watch the customers-as-competitors thread (Meta Compute, Microsoft/hyperscaler in-house). Not yet in the numbers, but it bears on long-run moat/durability if you weight terminal demand there.

**Momentum:**
- Positive narrative: Q2 beat, +14% AH, backlog + guidance + capex all raised, 25% price hike stuck. Offset by high volatility and the debt bear case. Refresh objective **50DMA %** (`scripts/momentum_50dma.py`) at scoring time; flag M for a fresh subjective look (direction: up).

**Risk:**
- **R1 (customer concentration):** **Improving and now filing-verified** — top customer 36% (was 71%), three 10%+ names. If a prior R1 penalized single-name (Microsoft) dominance, that input is now partly stale — revisit, but don't over-credit: top-3 ≈ 72% is still concentrated. (Direction: up/less risky.)
- **R3 (balance sheet / leverage):** The live risk. Interest expense $640M/qtr and rising; ~$35B debt; new DDTL 5.5 at SOFR+5.50%; 15% legacy tranche; capex $35–39B vs $12–13B revenue → continued reliance on expensive external funding. No going-concern or covenant-stress language in the 10-Q; positive FY adj-op-income guide. Not a near-term solvency flag, but R3 should reflect the still-worsening leverage/interest trajectory. **Revisit R3** (direction: watch, arguably slightly more stretched).
- **R5 (disruption, rule 16):** Layer 09 → defaults to **5**. GPU capacity is physical infra; confirm it sits at 5 (the customers-as-competitors thread is a demand-durability/moat point, not a rule-16 Layer-10-style disruption).

**Value — EV/MW (rule 13, this cohort's EV/EBITDA substitute):**
- **`00-master/capacity-mw.json` secured_gross_mw = 3,500 MW (as-of 2026-03-31) is STALE.** Q2 shows ~3.7 GW contracted, ~4.2 GW as of 2026-08-11. A stale (too-low) denominator *overstates* EV/MW (makes CRWV look more expensive). **Flag for a rule-13 MW refresh** at scoring time (the json itself notes "Next trigger: Q2 earnings" — that trigger has now fired). EV side is also rising fast via DDTL 5.5 + prior notes — ensure EV reflects the post-Aug debt stack.
