## 6. Implications for ratings

- **This is the AI-tied name in the warehouse bucket, and the tie runs through
  semiconductors, not warehouses.** 46% of Q1 orders are Electronics, growing 89%, which
  Daifuku itself attributes to AI-driven advanced-semiconductor investment. Cleanroom OHT is
  the same capex wave the portfolio already owns through AMAT/LRCX/TSM/MU — Daifuku is the
  *fab-logistics* leg of it. That justifies a top D5 under the rule-23 parallel lens, and it
  is a genuinely different exposure from SYM/AutoStore/Geek+.
- **Corollary the decision record missed: the warehouse leg is currently shrinking.**
  Commerce/retail orders −22%. Do not credit Daifuku with warehouse-automation momentum it
  does not have this quarter.
- **Quality is the strongest in the cohort** — 15.2% operating margin, 28% service revenue,
  net cash, record ¥700B backlog with orders guided +16–22%.
- **The geography that makes it valuable also makes it risky.** ~25% of sales and ~35% of
  orders ship into Taiwan and Korea, plus 12% China with a Suzhou plant. R2 must name that.
- **Momentum has broken down** despite the operating strength: −24% from the May 11 high
  while XLI compounded. Rate the read, not the story.
