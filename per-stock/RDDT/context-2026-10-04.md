## 5. DIFF — what changed versus my prior model and the June briefing

## 7. Addendum (same day): ChatGPT citation drop — raised by Dom, missed in the first pass

- **What is reported:** Reddit's share of ChatGPT Search citations fell from 3.83% (2026-07-18 to 08-07) to 0.52% (08-14 to 08-17), an 86% relative drop, in two steps: 08-08, when ChatGPT changed how it fans out queries (domain-scoped `site:` queries rose from 0.37% to 16.8% of fan-outs), and 08-14. Data: Promptwatch. [Promptwatch](https://promptwatch.com/data/reddit-citations-are-dropping-in-chatgpt), [Semrush 2026-08-26](https://www.semrush.com/blog/reddits-citations-in-chatgpt-fall/), [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/reddit-chatgpt-search-citations-fall-report-485473) (403 on fetch; search summary only).
- **Corroboration:** Petra Labs reported about −80% (per Search Engine Land summary). Across all engines Reddit's share slipped 2.00% → 1.66%; Google AI Overviews −11%, AI Mode −31% (Semrush/Promptwatch).
- **Statements:** OpenAI: it "doesn't set a fixed level of visibility for individual sites" and "ChatGPT still cites Reddit" (Semrush). Reddit (Adam Collins, CCO): "This has no meaningful impact on our business or on the value of Reddit to users, brands or AI companies" ([The Drum 2026-08-25](https://www.thedrum.com/news/did-the-brand-rush-to-reddit-kill-the-platform-s-chatgpt-citations)).
- **Caveats:** one vendor's measurement, which Promptwatch itself calls provisional and says may reflect a collection problem; sample size not published; cause unconfirmed; no September data found, so persistence is unknown. A similar drop in September 2025 recovered only partially and unevenly (The Drum).
- **Why it matters for ratings:** the 2026-06-22 D2 and D3 rationales leaned on Reddit being the most-cited AI source. The event shows an AI platform can cut its reliance on Reddit in days by changing retrieval, which bears on Reddit's pricing power as an AI-supply-chain input (D2), alongside the unresolved Google renewal. It is not evidence about ad revenue.
