## 2. The material part: search traffic (Ex-99.2, letter p.4–5)

- DAUq 130.3M (+12% YoY) but **US DAUq −2% YoY**; international +24%.
- The company says search referral traffic "was down meaningfully in Q3, with the decline accelerating later in the quarter", and the decline continued into Q4: search traffic dropped by more than 1 million referrals a week on average in October.
- **The company's own warning:** if the rate continues, total DAUq "could fall in Q4, by a low-double-digit percentage" from 130M.
- Offsets cited: logged-in and app DAUq grew sequentially and are "over 80% of the monetization"; new-user first-week retention up >50% YoY.

This is the first time the company has quantified a likely user decline. It is AI search (answers on the search page instead of a click to Reddit) shrinking Reddit's top of funnel — the risk the R5 rating of 2 was set for, now visible as erosion rather than a possibility.
