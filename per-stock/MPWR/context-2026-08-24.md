## 1. Pre-research mental model (verbatim, written before this pass)

> Carried from the 2026-06-12 briefing: MPWR sells the power-delivery silicon (multiphase VRMs,
> DC-DC, power modules) that steps rack voltage down to the sub-1V/hundreds-of-amps a GPU core
> needs. The bear fact I hold most firmly is that MPWR **lost most of the Blackwell (GB200/B200)
> power-module socket to Infineon and Renesas** in late 2025 on PMIC thermal issues, and the bull
> case rests on a **projected ~70% recapture on NVIDIA's next-gen Vera Rubin (VR200)** via 800V-capable
> parts. In June the stock was ~$1,550–1,600 (~$78–81B cap, ~22–24x sales, P/S at the 97th percentile
> of its own 3-year range) with heavy insider selling and zero buys. The unresolved question I wrote
> down last time and never answered: **is the Vera Rubin recapture confirmed (design-win locked and
> ramping) or still projected (analyst model, not company-stated)?** Everything about whether the
> peak multiple is justified hangs on that distinction. My expectation going in: Enterprise Data
> kept compounding, but the socket question is still open.

**The synthesis that matters:** the two halves of the story moved in *opposite* directions. The
business got materially better (Enterprise Data +164% YoY, FY outlook nearly doubled) while the
stock de-rated ~23%. The market is not disputing current earnings; it is discounting the *next*
socket. And on that question the news is genuinely worse than the June model assumed — not because
a recapture was lost, but because the 800V architecture the recapture depends on has been
positioned as optional for VR200 and deferred to Rubin Ultra (2H2027). The bull case now needs a
longer bridge than it did in June, funded by a Blackwell socket MPWR has largely lost.

## 5. Implications for ratings (flag only — NOT pre-decided)

- **D1 (AI revenue %)** — likely upward pressure. Enterprise Data is now 38.8% of revenue and the
  explicit driver of all growth; the June rating was set on a 32.7% mix.
- **D2 (supply-chain position) / D3 (moat)** — the most debatable pair this pass. Two independent
  sources now corroborate socket loss to Infineon/Renesas on the highest-volume AI platform. If D2/D3
  were set assuming MPWR is the incumbent power-delivery bottleneck for NVIDIA, that premise is
  weaker than it was. Counterweight: the "component → full solution" shift and high-current-density
  module capacity is a real position-building move.
- **D5 (strength of AI-buildout tie)** — unchanged to slightly up; the tie is undisputed, the
  question is share within it.
- **M (Momentum)** — genuinely two-sided: EPS revisions strongly positive (beat + raised guide),
  relative strength negative (−23% off the high, sector de-rating), Rosenblatt downgrade. The 50DMA %
  objective metric should be refreshed before the subjective M dims are touched.
- **R (Risk)** — no change indicated. The plaintiff-firm investigations do not meet any R-dimension
  bar (no filed case, no regulator, no disclosure). R1 distributor concentration was not re-verified
  this pass.
- **Rule-9 objective refresh: DUE.** Q2 revenue +47.6% YoY with a 130% FY Enterprise Data outlook is
  exactly the fast-ramp case where TTM inputs understate the run-rate (CLAUDE.md rule 9 TTM caveat).
- **Rule-14 expectations flag: re-run.** The June flag fired at P/S 27.25 (97.3rd pctile) on
  *decelerating* growth. Both inputs have inverted — P/S ~19.2x and growth re-accelerating — so the
  flag should be re-computed rather than assumed to still hold.
