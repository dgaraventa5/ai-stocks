## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before any data pull)

> Upstart runs an AI underwriting marketplace: banks and credit unions originate personal (and increasingly auto/HELOC) loans that Upstart's models price, and Upstart earns referral/platform and servicing fees; it does not want to hold credit risk but holds some loans on its balance sheet. Revenue recovered strongly in 2025 (~$1B) after the 2022–23 funding collapse, and it turned GAAP-profitable around mid-2025. The stock trades on funding-market appetite and rates far more than on model releases — 2022 showed that when loan buyers step back, volume collapses regardless of model quality. My last firm data point is Q1 2026 (originations +61%, revenue +44%, $4B+ of committed capital). I expect the AI to be genuinely the product, but the return driver to be credit/funding cycles.

### 2b. FCF conversion — not meaningful

Operating cash flow was **−$269.9M** in H1 2026 (10-Q cash-flow statement), and yfinance shows TTM OCF of −$284M. The cause is how lending works: loans bought for sale run through operating cash flow, while held-for-investment loans run through investing. So FCF conversion measures balance-sheet loan growth, not earnings quality. This is **not** the accrual-games signature. Flagged as a garbage input, not a red flag.

### AI-materiality verdict

The AI model *is* the product: 91% of loans are fully automated, and the take rate exists only because the model prices risk better than FICO. Model gains show up as higher approval/conversion at a given loss rate. But how much loan volume clears is set by loan buyers' appetite, and that depends on rates and credit spreads. 2022–23 proved that capital flight overwhelms model quality.

**Rough judgment: about one-third of the equity's return driver is AI-model progress (conversion, automation, take-rate durability, new products) and about two-thirds is rates, credit and funding.** The 2026 tape fits that split: record model metrics and +50% originations coincided with a −45% stock. This is a judgment call, not a regression.
