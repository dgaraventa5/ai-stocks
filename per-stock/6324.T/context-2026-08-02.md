## 5. Rating implications

- **D1** — Robotics/precision-motion is essentially the entire business, but the *AI-driven* (humanoid) slice is only **4.2%** of revenue. Score the layer-theme exposure, not the humanoid slice, or every Layer-11 component name floors at 1–2; but discount for AI-driven vs. automation-driven. → **4**, flagged DEBATABLE.
- **D2** — Near-monopoly share (80–85%) but a **documented, in-the-numbers** dissolution: −37.3% China revenue, competitor share 15%→38%, 20–40% price gap, SKF/Leaderdrive. A dissolving bottleneck is not a 5. → **4**.
- **D3** — Process/IP moat is real and decades-old, but a 60%+ global share earning a **4.3% operating margin** while losing a third of its China revenue fails the rubric's pricing-power test for a 5. → **4**.
- **D4** — The FY3/27 story is **operating leverage on existing capacity** ("marginal profit over 50%"), not a funded buildout. → **3**.
- **D5 (L11 lens, proposed)** — The **only** name in this five-name block with dated, quantified humanoid order figures (¥1.3B/qtr, ¥2.5B FY, guided to 2–3x) and >15 identified prospects. Held below 5 by the 4.2% base and unnamed customers. → **4**.
- **M1** — No sourceable 90-day revision counts for a Tokyo line; consensus sits ~32% above company guidance, and the Q1 print (8/7) is the resolver. → **3**, Low confidence, data gap flagged.
- **M2** — See DIFF. +115% 1-yr is far ahead of XLI (+20% 1H 2026) but −32.9% from the high breaks the near-term trend. **Needs `momentum_50dma.py` to settle.**
- **R2** — Worst plausible shock: **US tariff/trade action on Japanese-origin precision components**, hitting the **~18% North America** revenue base that is also the humanoid growth engine. China policy/localization overhang covers only ~9% and is already impaired.
- **R3** — Net cash ¥3,617M but thin trough FCF and ¥18.1B gross debt.
- **R5** — Physical component; agentic AI is demand, not substitution. Default **5**.
