## 2. Latest filing highlights

Filings pulled 2026-08-17 via `scripts/sec_edgar.py`: **10-Q filed 2026-07-30**, 8-K 2026-07-29 (Item 2.02, Q1 FY27 results), 8-K 2026-07-24 (Item 7.01, recast segment data).

**8-K 2026-07-24 (Item 7.01) — the structural change. Direct quote:**
> "Effective April 1, 2026, the Company reorganized its Climate Solutions segment and split it into two separate operating segments: 1) **Data Centers** and 2) **Commercial HVAC** … Beginning with its reporting for the first quarter of fiscal 2027, the Company will report the financial results of its three operating segments."

Data Centers is now a standalone reported segment with its own P&L. Per the 10-Q, it "sells data center cooling solutions, including chillers, dry coolers, precision air handling units, computer room air conditioning and air handler units, fan walls, **rear-door heat exchangers, coolant distribution units and immersion solutions**," plus modular data centers, parts, service and controls. FY2026 disaggregated revenue has been **recast** for comparability — so clean YoY segment comparisons now exist for the first time.

**Gentherm RMT, from the 10-Q:** the Company "will retain its Data Centers and Commercial HVAC segment businesses, **creating a pure-play climate solutions company**." Modine shareholders receive newly-issued Gentherm stock representing **~40% of the combined company**. Expected to close by end of calendar 2026, subject to Gentherm shareholder approval. Not classified as held-for-sale (a spin is not a sale under GAAP); will become a discontinued operation on completion.

**10-Q MD&A on the margin problem — direct quote:**
> "cost of sales increased 340 basis points to 79.2 percent, primarily due to the higher material costs and the operating inefficiencies … gross margin declined 340 basis points to **20.8 percent**"

MD&A attributes it to "the rapid expansion of manufacturing capacity for data center cooling solutions and **supplier capacity constraints that temporarily disrupted our production schedules**." SG&A +22%, including **$7.1M of Reverse Morris Trust transaction costs**.

**Contract balances:** contract liabilities $199.2M at 2026-06-30 vs $194.5M at 2026-03-31 (the $165M LTA deposit is the bulk); contract assets $78.3M vs $43.6M.

**FCF-conversion check (rule 2b / §2b):** FY-0 87% (FCF $0.11B / NI $0.12B), FY-1 70%. **Clean.** AR +53% and inventory +48% against revenue +23% is the most stretched working capital in this rotation — but conversion is high, not low, so the two-condition test does not fire. The correct read is a company building inventory and receivables to ramp capacity, which is also why Q1 FCF was a $5.0M *use* of cash.

## 6. Implications for ratings (flag only — no ratings decided here)

- **D1 (AI revenue %)** — a cleanly-disclosed 40% of consolidated revenue from a segment literally named Data Centers, growing 90%. After the spin closes it becomes a pure-play climate company with data centers as the dominant segment. Revisit.
- **D5 (AI-buildout tie)** — the $4B CY2027-29 LTA with a $165M prepay is exactly the contracted, quantified tie rule 23's parallel scale asks for.
- **D2 (supply-chain position)** — cuts against the name this quarter. Modine was on the *receiving* end of component scarcity: its own supplier constraints cost it 340bps. A company that cannot get parts is, by definition, not the bottleneck. Worth an honest look rather than crediting the demand and ignoring the constraint.
- **R1 (customer concentration)** — the FY26 10-K disclosed a first-ever >10% customer (~11%), and the $4B LTA concentrates further into one unnamed strategic customer. This is the sharpest risk question on the name and it has gotten sharper, not softer.
- **R3 (balance sheet)** — the $165M deposit is customer money inside contract liabilities ($199.2M), and Q1 FCF was negative on capacity investment. Not distress, but the funding profile is tightening as the ramp accelerates.
- **Quality inputs (rule 9)** — TTM will now blend pre- and post-shortage margins. Note the MRQ gross margin (20.8%, −340bps) explicitly so the cohort-percentile Quality score isn't scored off a misleading average in either direction.
- **Structural note:** the Gentherm RMT will make Performance Technologies a discontinued operation on close (expected end CY2026). Segment history and any margin/growth comparisons will break at that point — worth pre-registering so the discontinuity isn't later read as a fundamental change.

**Thesis-break signals: none.** The margin compression is real and should not be waved away, but it is a supply constraint inside a 90%-growth segment, with guidance reaffirmed.
