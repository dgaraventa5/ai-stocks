## Pre-research mental model

Tempus is an AI-native precision-medicine company with two engines: a sequencing/diagnostics business that generates clinical data, and a data-licensing business that monetizes that data to pharma — the second being the higher-margin reason to own it. My prior model held that it was still loss-making with reimbursement rates the central variable on the diagnostics side, that data licensing was the real operating leverage, and that the risks were payer coverage decisions, cash burn, and a persistent short thesis around revenue quality and related-party structure. Most recent quarter I believed reported: Q1 2026. Product cycle: MRD (molecular residual disease) scaling and foundation-model work with pharma partners.
