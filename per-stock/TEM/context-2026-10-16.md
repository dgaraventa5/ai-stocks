## 1. Pre-research mental model

Tempus is a precision-medicine company: a genomic-testing lab (oncology NGS plus Ambry hereditary, roughly three quarters of revenue) that feeds a de-identified clinical-molecular dataset licensed to pharma (Data & Applications, roughly a quarter). The AI story is mostly the data business plus algorithm attach on tests; the testing itself is AI-enabled, not AI-driven. Last quarter I know of is Q2 2026 (rev $382.5M, +22%; guidance $1.595–1.605B; adj. EBITDA ~$65M), with a pending all-stock Personalis acquisition and a $98.5M securities gain flattering GAAP net income. I believed the balance sheet was roughly net cash after the June 2026 convertible.
