## 1. One-line thesis

> Tempus is monetising a linked clinical-molecular dataset twice — once as reimbursed diagnostic tests and again as data and models licensed to pharma — and the second of those is now growing faster, at better margins, with named counterparties and a delivered oncology foundation model.

---

## 2. Position in the AI supply chain

- **What they do:** Comprehensive genomic profiling and MRD testing (Diagnostics), plus licensing of de-identified clinico-molecular data, analytics and now foundation models to pharmaceutical partners (Data and Applications).
- **Where they sit:** An AI-native vertical application — a *buyer* of AI infrastructure, not a supplier to it. Its tie to the AI economy is that its proprietary data is a scarce training and evidence asset.
- **Unique vs. commoditized:** Sequencing chemistry is commoditising; the linked longitudinal clinical-plus-molecular dataset is not, and neither is FDA companion-diagnostic approval.
- **AI revenue mix:** Rated D1=5 — **and flagged for scrutiny.** Data and Applications is $93.2M (24% of revenue, +28%), with Insights (data licensing and modelling) +36%, and the first oncology foundation model was delivered to AstraZeneca. The unresolved question is whether the ~76% of revenue that is Diagnostics counts as AI-*driven* or as a lab-services business with an AI overlay — that is the rubric's own "AI-adjacent vs AI-driven" failure mode and deserves a deliberate answer before the 5 is trusted further.

## 4. Pricing & demand

- **Pricing trend — the key event:** **FDA approved xT Tumor Only**, making Tempus the first lab holding companion-diagnostic approval for *both* tumor-only and tumor-normal comprehensive genomic profiling. Management expects this to migrate tissue testing to **ADLT pricing** — a reimbursement-rate event, which is the single biggest lever on diagnostics gross margin.
- **Lead times / backlog:** ~$200M of Data and Applications licences signed in the quarter.
- **Gross margin trend:** Gross profit $246.5M, **+26% YoY — growing faster than revenue**, led by Data and Applications.
- **Volume:** Oncology testing volume **+31% YoY** (up from +28% the prior quarter); **MRD volume 9,000 tests, up from 6,500 (+38% sequentially)**. Hereditary is the visible drag at **+5%**.

## 6. Moat

- **IP / process know-how:** The linked clinico-molecular dataset, accumulated through clinical operations and difficult to replicate retrospectively.
- **Scale advantage:** More tests → more data → better models → more pharma value. A genuine flywheel, if it compounds.
- **Switching costs:** Moderate for pharma partners (data integrated into research workflows); low for individual ordering physicians.
- **Network effects:** Indirect via the data flywheel.
- **Regulatory:** **The strongest leg.** Dual FDA companion-diagnostic approval (tumor-only *and* tumor-normal) is a real barrier and converts directly into pricing via ADLT.

**Moat rating:** Narrow, rated D3=4 (raised this pass). Two independent dimensions — regulatory approval and data scale — are now evidenced. Bias check applied (the stock is +33% over three months): both facts are price-independent. **Counterweight: a four-docket two-way patent war with Guardant Health, unresolved in both directions.**

## 9. Risks

- **Thesis-killer #1:** Reimbursement moves against comprehensive genomic profiling, or ADLT migration fails to materialise — the diagnostics line is a rate-taker from payors.
- **Thesis-killer #2:** Pharma data-licensing bookings prove lumpy rather than recurring, so the ~$200M quarter is a high-water mark rather than a run-rate.
- **Dilution / capital allocation:** New this pass. Tempus is now an acquirer financed with its own stock on a ~$1.5B enterprise-value deal, having just raised $460M of zero-coupon converts.
- **China / export controls:** Not material. Rated R2=5 (domestic diagnostics and reimbursement).
- **Cyclical risk:** Pharma R&D budgets are the demand driver for Data and Applications and are not immune to cuts.
- **Technology substitution:** Rated R5=4 — a lab-plus-data business is not a seat-based agentic target, and AI is a tailwind to the data asset.
- **Regulatory / litigation:** **The securities overhang cleared** — *Shouse v. Tempus AI* (1:25-cv-06534, N.D. Ill.) **terminated 2025-11-17**, and no pending federal securities docket appears in RECAP. What remains is the Guardant patent war plus two unread "other fraud" dockets (*Farrier* 1:26-cv-04246; *Barone* 1:26-cv-03516, both pending N.D. Ill.) — **flagged for a look, not characterised.** Rated R4=3 (raised from 2 this pass). A shareholder-rights firm "examining" the Personalis related-party structure is a **plaintiff-firm solicitation, not a filed case**, and is set aside per rule-2d discipline.

## 10. The "inverse the thesis" test

1. **If the data asset is the real business, Data and Applications keeps outgrowing Diagnostics and Insights holds above ~30% growth for the next four quarters.** If D&A decelerates below the diagnostics line, this is a lab company with an AI narrative and D1=5 is wrong.
2. **If the FDA approval is a genuine moat, realised diagnostics gross margin expands as ADLT pricing takes effect.** If gross margin is flat a year after approval, the regulatory win did not convert to economics and D3=4 is too high.
