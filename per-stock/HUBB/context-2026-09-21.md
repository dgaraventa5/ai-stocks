## Pre-research mental model

Coming in from the 2026-06-22 briefing: HUBB is a two-segment grid/electrical compounder (Utility Solutions ~larger/higher-margin; Electrical Solutions the more AI-direct data-center play) riding a grid-modernization + electrification + AI-datacenter-power tailwind. At Q1 2026 the story was accelerating (data center ~40% YoY, FY DC guide raised to >25%; Grid Infrastructure +12% organic; Aclara/AMI the lone soft spot at −7% organic). The single flagged watch-item was **leverage**, not demand: HUBB levered up ~$1.4B+ of new debt to close the ~$3.0B NSI Industries acquisition (2026-06-09, electrical fittings/connectors/wire-management, ~$570M revenue). Expectation was "quality at a full price" where the question is how much of the DC story is already priced.

**Consolidated (Q2, three months ended 2026-06-30):**
- Net sales **$1,711.8M** vs $1,484.3M = **+15.3%**; **+10.2% organic**, +4.8% acquisitions (NSI + DMC + Nicor), +0.3% FX (10-Q, p.39/42).
- Gross margin **35.8%** vs 37.2% — contracted 140bps; ~5pts price/productivity/volume/acq expansion more than offset by ~6pts material & tariff cost inflation + higher intangible amortization (10-Q, p.42).
- GAAP operating margin **20.4%** vs 22.7% — contracted **230bps** (NSI intangible amortization + transaction/integration costs). GAAP operating income $348.6M vs $336.3M (10-Q, p.39).
- **Adjusted operating margin 23.9%** — contracted 50bps (adj op income guided by underlying price/productivity gains offset by tariff/material inflation) (10-Q, p.39; call).
- GAAP net income attributable to HUBB **$240.4M** vs $244.2M (−1.6%); GAAP diluted EPS **$4.52** vs $4.56 (−0.9%) — depressed by NSI amort/transaction costs (10-Q, p.39).
- **Adjusted net income $293.7M, +11.1%**; adjusted diluted EPS **$5.52, +12%** (10-Q, p.43; call).
- Diluted shares 53.1M vs 53.5M (buyback: $29.3M repurchased in Q2, $298.2M remaining authorization) (10-Q, p.39/51).

**Segments (Q2):**
- **Utility Solutions:** net sales **$1,025.8M, +9.7%** (**+5.5% organic**, +3.9% acq, +0.3% FX). "Strong substation, transmission and distribution markets drove volume growth." GAAP op margin 22.8% (−50bps); adjusted op margin **25.6% (+10bps)** (10-Q, p.43). 1H text notes organic held back by "weak advanced metering infrastructure and meter project activity" (Grid Automation/AMI still soft) (10-Q, p.46).
- **Electrical Solutions:** net sales **$686.0M, +25.1%** (**+18.3% organic**, +6.5% acq, +0.3% FX). Volume growth "driven primarily by strength in the data center, light industrial and non-residential markets" (heavy industrial soft). GAAP op margin **16.7% (−480bps)**; adjusted op margin **21.2% (−130bps)** — ~8pts contraction from material/tariff inflation + restructuring, offset by ~7pts price/volume/productivity/acq (10-Q, p.44).

**Balance sheet / leverage (the flagged watch-item — now materialized):**
- **Long-term debt $4,803.9M at 2026-06-30 vs $2,036.3M at 2025-12-31** — more than doubled (~$2.77B increase) to fund NSI (10-Q, p.51). Short-term debt **$568.7M vs $289.1M** (10-Q, p.51).
- Q2 total other expense +$23.8M YoY to $44.9M, driven by **net interest expense +$24.6M YoY** on higher average debt, incl. **$7.2M NSI bridge-financing cost** (10-Q, p.42).
- 1H26 OCF **$336.4M** (vs $298.0M); 1H capex **$77.6M** → 1H FCF ≈ **$258.8M** (10-Q, p.49). Investing used $3,086.6M (the ~$3.0B NSI cash) (10-Q, p.49). $1.0B revolver fully available (10-Q, p.53).
- NSI contributed only ~$35.4M sales / immaterial operating income for the ~3 weeks post-close (10-Q, p.34); operating-income contribution masked by purchase-accounting.

**Tariffs / trade:** U.S. Supreme Court ruled (2026-02-20) IEEPA tariffs invalid; immediately replaced by tariffs under alternative statutory authority (scope/duration uncertain). HUBB submitted refund claims and, **post-quarter, received ~$30M of IEEPA refunds** (not recognized at 2026-06-30 under a gain-contingency model) (10-Q, p.39). Tariff/material inflation is the dominant margin headwind this quarter.

**Backlog:** No consolidated firm-backlog figure was surfaced in the Q2 10-Q MD&A in this pass (the "backlog" line items found are NSI purchase-accounting intangibles, not company backlog) — flag as a gap; use the call's book-to-bill instead.

## Implications for ratings (affected dims + why; NO numbers set here)

- **D1 (AI-Rev, now 3):** Up-bias review. Data center +65% Q2 / FY ~50% is a large, quantified, AI-direct acceleration; NSI adds DC/network content. Counter-consideration: DC is still a minority of total revenue, so the *directness/materiality* judgment (rule-12 absolute lens) must weigh mix, not just growth rate.
- **D2 (Position, now 3):** Up-review. "Orders accelerate whenever we add capacity" = a supply-constrained, pricing-power position in DC power distribution + substation/T&D — evidence of a bottleneck role rather than a commodity supplier.
- **D5 (AI-buildout tie, now 3):** Up-review on the rule-23 parallel scale. T&D order/quoting strength "driven by load growth and data center build-outs" ties the utility book directly to the hyperscaler buildout, not just the electrical segment.
- **D3 (Moat, now 3) / D4 (Capacity, now 4):** Neutral-to-review. Substation/T&D franchise strength + a raised $175–190M capex plan (capacity is the binding constraint) support the current reads; no clear mover.
- **M1 (EPS-Rev, now 4):** Supports holding. Beat + second consecutive FY raise + sell-side PT raises/initiations are positive revisions.
- **M2 (RelStr, now 3):** **Down-bias review.** Stock −12–13% over 1–3 months into a beat-and-raise = weakening relative strength/de-rating. Refresh objective **50DMA%** (`scripts/momentum_50dma.py`) before finalizing — the price action contradicts the fundamentals.
- **M3 (Insider, now 3):** Gap — no fresh Form-4 evidence pulled this pass. Check insider activity before trusting the rating (rule 3).
- **R3 (BSRisk, now 4):** **Down-bias review — the single biggest delta.** LT debt more than doubled to $4.8B for NSI; net interest +$24.6M YoY. Mitigants: healthy 1H FCF (~$259M), full $1.0B revolver, no LT maturities inside 12 months, strong EBITDA coverage — so this is a leverage step-up to digest, not a distress signal. Weigh whether it clears the R3 bands at a lower notch.
- **R2 (GeoRisk, now 4) / R4 (RegRisk, now 4):** Monitor, likely neutral. Tariff/material inflation is a real margin headwind and the IEEPA regime is unsettled (invalidated → replaced), but ~$30M refunds are inbound and price actions offset. No clear rating move.
- **R1 (CustConc, now 5):** Hold. Diversified utilities + electrical; no concentration flag surfaced.
- **R5 (Disrupt, now 5):** Hold — physical grid/electrical infrastructure, non-Layer-10, rule-16 default 5. No AI/agentic disruption to the revenue model.
- **Rule-14 expectations note:** Fundamentals accelerating while the multiple compresses is the *opposite* of the rule-14 peak-multiple/decelerating-growth trap — if anything a de-rating into acceleration. Still worth running `scripts/expectations_flag.py HUBB` at scoring time for the P/S-percentile check.

**Thesis-break signal: none.** Demand accelerating (DC ~50%, T&D book-to-bill ~1.2x), guidance raised twice, sell-side constructive. Two watch-items, both non-break: (1) **leverage** post-NSI (R3), and (2) **relative-strength weakness / multiple compression** despite the beat (M2) — the market is discounting a strong operator, which is a valuation/entry question, not a thesis question.
