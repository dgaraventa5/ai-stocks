## Pre-research mental model

(Carried from 2026-06-22.) Hubbell is a two-segment electrical/utility products company — **Utility Solutions** (grid T&D hardware, substation/connectors, plus Aclara AMI meters & grid-comms) and **Electrical Solutions** (connectors, enclosures, and the most data-center-direct power-distribution line). Bull case = multi-year grid-modernization + electrification + AI-datacenter-power tailwind; Q1 2026 confirmed and accelerated it (DC ~40% growth, FY DC outlook raised to >25%, book-to-bill ~1.2), with the lone soft spot (Aclara/grid-automation, −7% organic Q1) bottoming. The single watch-item coming in was **leverage**, not demand: the ~$3.0B NSI acquisition (closed 2026-06-09, ~15.5x EBITDA) was financed with new term-loan + senior-notes debt, pushing interest expense up materially. Premium "quality at a full price" name; question is how much of the grid/DC story is already priced (rule-14 angle live).
