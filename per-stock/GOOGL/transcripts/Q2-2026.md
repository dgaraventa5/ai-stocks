## CFO — Anat Ashkenazi

- **Capex guidance raised:** "we are updating our full-year 2026 CapEx guidance range to **$195 billion–$205 billion**, up from our previous estimate of $180 billion–$190 billion." Increase attributed to acceleration in delivery of capacity to meet demand. Mix per coverage: ~60% servers / ~40% data centers + networking (Investing.com slides recap, https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/alphabet-q2-2026-slides-24-revenue-growth-cloud-surges-despite-capex-93CH-4807148).
- **2027 capex:** "we continue to expect our CapEx to increase significantly in 2027 and will provide more details at a later date."
- **Negative FCF quarter:** "negative free cash flow of $5.9 billion in the second quarter, driven by our investments in CapEx"; TTM FCF $53.3B (matches press release reconciliation: Q2 OCF $39,069M − capex $44,924M = **−$5,855M**; TTM FCF $53,273M).
- **Third-party capacity bridge:** plan to "expand the use of third-party capacity in Q3 as a bridging strategy while we build out more internal capacity" given supply constraints; this "will create modest margin pressure in the near term." (Corroborated by Seeking Alpha news summary, https://seekingalpha.com/news/4617114-alphabet-signals-195b-205b-2026-capex-while-expanding-third-party-capacity-as-a-bridge; and by 10-Q disclosure of a ~$5.8B non-cancelable short-term lease commencing Q3 2026 — 10-Q filed 2026-07-23, MD&A Leases.)
- **Backlog:** Cloud backlog "$514 billion"; "just over 50% of the total backlog" expected to convert to revenue within 24 months. TPU system sale agreements are included in backlog; "the vast majority of the revenue from that agreement" lands in 2027. (10-Q Revenue Backlog note: total RPO $519.5B, of which $513.9B Google Cloud.)
- **P&L pressure:** infrastructure investment will "continue to put pressure on the P&L in the form of higher depreciation expense and related data center operations costs such as energy." (Q2 depreciation $7,104M vs $4,998M yr-ago, +42% — press release cash flow statement.)
- **Q3 color:** "in Q3, we will begin lapping an acceleration in Search performance that began in the third quarter last year"; slight FX headwind to consolidated revenue in Q3 vs ~1pt tailwind in Q2; Cloud margins face modest near-term pressure from third-party capacity and Wiz integration through 2026.

## CEO — Sundar Pichai

- **Gemini app:** **950M MAU**; DAU tripled over the past year. Omni feature: +40% daily active video creators since May launch.
- **Tokens:** model APIs "processing approximately **22 billion tokens per minute**... up from 16 billion just a quarter ago." 9M+ developers building monthly; Gemma downloads 900M+ cumulative.
- **Gemini Enterprise:** "Nearly 90% of the Fortune 100 are using it." Nearly 500 Cloud customers each processed >1T tokens in the past year; 2,000+ enterprises consumed >100B tokens over 12 months; existing customers exceeded commitments by >50%; new-customer velocity more than doubled YoY. Press release quote: "It's great to see wide adoption of Gemini Enterprise, with nearly 90% of the Fortune 100 using it."
- **AI Mode:** surpassed **1B MAU** since October global expansion; AI features "driving Search query growth"; "billions of clicks to websites every week."
- **TPUs:** customers "increasingly looking at opportunities to put TPUs in their data centers or in other data centers, like the project we are doing with Blackstone." New TPU generations "8t and 8i" cited for price-performance; Axion CPU +30% perf/$.
- **Compute allocation:** "our first priority is making sure we are allocating what we need to compete at the frontier in terms of AGI development. That is the foundation for everything we do." Still supply-constrained against demand.
- **Models:** Gemini 3.6 Flash shipped post-3.5 (coding "jumped over 10 points in DeepSuite"); "releasing models almost at a monthly cadence"; Gemini 4 in training, "very ambitious."

## CBO — Philipp Schindler

- Gemini-driven query understanding lets Search "find relevant ads for longer searches previously difficult to monetize"; shopping ads: "20% improvement in showing highly relevant ads."
- **AI Max:** 500,000 advertisers adopted; average "15% more conversions or value on Search at similar ROAS."
- New AI Mode ad formats: Direct Offers and Highlighted Answers ("early user traction").
- **YouTube:** ads +13% YoY; FIFA World Cup 2026: 1.7B unique viewers of related videos, 550M+ on TVs; Ask YouTube 140M+ users engaged in June 2026.

## Q&A highlights (as summarized by source)

- **Nowak (MS), ROIC/capex philosophy:** Pichai — "very early innings of what feels like secular shift"; Ashkenazi — invest "as long as we see an attractive return on that investment" while keeping "a resilient... healthy balance sheet."
- **Anmuth (JPM), model competitiveness:** coding "an example" of "where we've acknowledged we need to improve"; 3.6 Flash closed ground.
- **Sheridan (GS), TPU margins:** declined to break out TPU-specific margins; TPUs are "an expansion of our total addressable market."
- **Shmulik (Bernstein), capacity trade-offs:** allocation order = frontier AGI development → core products (Search/YouTube) → Cloud; third-party capacity near-term costs "very high" but "highly ROI positive" over multi-year horizons.
- **Sandler (Barclays), release cadence:** monthly-cadence model releases through Gemini 4 development.
