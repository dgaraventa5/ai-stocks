## 1. Pre-research mental model (verbatim, written before any data pull)

> "Coming into this pass (training + the 2026-06-12 briefing), my model of Alphabet: Google Cloud growing ~60%+ YoY (Q1 2026: +63% to $20.0B) against a $462B cloud backlog, compute-constrained, with TPU externalization ramping through three channels (direct hardware sales, the Blackstone JV, Broadcom LTA). Capex guided $180–190B for 2026 with 2027 'significantly higher,' and the funding model had flipped from buybacks to capital-raising (the June $84.75B equity package incl. Berkshire's $10B), but I still expected modestly positive quarterly FCF (~$10B/qtr) — not a negative-FCF quarter. Gemini held ~27% chatbot share vs ChatGPT ~55% and closing; Search grew +19% with AI Overviews monetizing at parity, so the 'AI kills search' bear case lacked evidence. Regulatory risk sat mainly in the US search-remedies appeal, the ad-tech remedy, the upheld €4.1B EU Android fine, and the 2026-07-16 DMA binding-specification orders — I did not know of a new standalone DMA fine or a Search redesign order, and I did not know Q2 2026 results."

## 5. DIFF vs training / prior briefing

1. **Cloud accelerated again — 63% → 82% YoY — while backlog still grew $52B QoQ to $513.9B.** My model said "sustain ~60%"; the second derivative is still positive. Cloud margin 35.6% despite the ramp.
2. **The negative-FCF quarter arrived (−$5.9B), a quarter or two earlier than my model implied**, and the mechanism is specific: capex $44.9B in one quarter plus a rent-the-bridge strategy (leases-not-commenced $85.2B; ~$5.8B short-term lease starting Q3). This is the "capex outruns returns" watch-item from the June briefing becoming measurable.
3. **Capex guide raised a second time this year: $180–190B → $195–205B.** April's raise was +$5B; July's is +$15B. Direction of revisions is consistently up; 2027 still unquantified.
4. **$707.0B of fixed/guaranteed purchase commitments** — a balance-sheet-adjacent number with no 2025 comparative in the filing; larger than the entire revenue backlog. New disclosure scale vs my model.
5. **TPU merchant business is now visible in the accounts, not just announcements:** inventory 4x to $10.0B in six months, "product revenues primarily from the sale of TPU systems" in the segment description, revenue mostly 2027.
6. **Regulatory regime shifted from fines-about-the-past to orders-about-the-product:** first DMA fine (€890M) with a 60-day **Search redesign** order + 5%-of-turnover periodic-penalty threat, Android €4.1B paid, PriceRunner $2.1B recognized, ad-tech €3.0B appeal pending, US search-remedies appeal pending. The new marginal risk is product-design intervention in the EU SERP, not the fine amounts.
7. **Earnings quality got worse, not better:** $99.0B unrealized equity gain = ~69% of Q2 pre-tax income (PR income statement). Two consecutive quarters where GAAP EPS is dominated by marks on private holdings.
8. **Gemini distribution kept compounding:** 950M app MAU, AI Mode 1B MAU, 22B tokens/min — all above the trajectory in my model; the ~27%-vs-55% chatbot-share frame from June is likely stale (no fresh share data pulled this pass — gap noted).

- **Quality (objective):** TTM FCF margin ~11.9% vs MRQ −4.9% — **state both anywhere Quality is discussed (rule 9); TTM currently flatters the run-rate.** Op margin 34% (+2pt YoY) is genuinely strong; depreciation +42% YoY is the coming drag.
- **AI Thesis (D-dims):** evidence strengthened again (D1/D2/D5-relevant): Cloud accel, TPU inventory/product revenue, allocation hierarchy, Gemini distribution. Whether that moves ratings is a rating-session question.
- **Momentum (M-dims + 50DMA):** mixed — fundamentals beat but the stock fell 5–7% on capex; EPS-revision and rel-strength reads may diverge. Refresh 50DMA % objectively.
- **Risk (R-dims):** the live debate.
  - **R4 Regulatory:** materially worse marginal news — first DMA fine with a structural **Search redesign order** (60-day clock to ~9/21/2026, periodic penalties up to 5% of worldwide daily turnover if non-compliant), on top of the 7/16 binding-specification orders, PriceRunner $2.1B, ad-tech appeal, US remedies appeal. Qualitatively: fine = noise; remedy = the first ongoing product intervention in the core asset.

  - **R5 Disruption:** Q2 evidence (Search +17%, AI Mode 1B MAU, AI Max data) continues to cut against the disruption bear case for Search.

**Thesis-break check: none triggered on demand or monetization — Q2 strengthens the demand side of the thesis.** The structural watch-item from June (funding flip → "capex outruns returns") is now measurable: track (a) quarterly FCF trajectory against the $195–205B guide, (b) TPU product revenue actually landing in 2027, (c) backlog conversion rate, (d) ATM usage / any buyback resumption, (e) EU Search-redesign implementation and any EMEA ads impact from Q4 2026.
