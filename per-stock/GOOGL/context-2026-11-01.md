- **Inventory** $13,308M vs $2,439M at 12/31/25, "primarily" TPU systems for sale plus consumer devices (10-Q balance sheet, Note 1).
- **Capex guide unchanged** at $195–205B, which my mental model got wrong (call). 9-month capex $132.0B (10-Q Liquidity) implies Q4 of ~$63–73B. **"2027 above $300B" is press framing, not guidance:** the CFO said only "increase significantly" (call).

Two disclosures are new to this project:
- **TPU repurchase obligations.** "Certain contracts require us to repurchase previously sold TPU hardware" under conditions; max $6.6B. The company says counterparties have no economic incentive to exercise, given the discount (Note 10).
- **Crux AI is a related party.** The Blackstone joint venture has a multi-year commercial agreement with Alphabet that "constitutes a related party transaction"; Alphabet holds a minority stake under the equity method (Note 3). Press reports a $22B bank loan secured on the hardware and customer agreements (https://aiweekly.co/alerts/ten-banks-line-up-22b-loan-for-crux-ai-to-buy-google-tpus). TPU sales are therefore partly funded through a vehicle Alphabet part-owns and backstops. How much Cloud revenue runs through Crux is not disclosed in what I read; the Note 2 cross-reference was not followed to a figure.

## 4. Earnings transcript highlights (Q3 2026 call, 2026-10-28)

Fetched from https://singjupost.com/alphabet-googl-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/ through a summarising fetch tool, so quotations are as extracted and not checked against audio or a second transcript.

- **Search slowdown, Schindler:** FX "was the single largest driver of Search deceleration relative to Q2"; also APAC advertisers hit by the EU ending its de minimis exemption, spend normalising after the World Cup, and "the lower monetization we were seeing in AI Mode." **None quantified.** Ashkenazi added lapping of about three points in Q3 and a couple more in Q4, and a somewhat larger FX headwind in Q4.
- **Agentic search:** "clearly additive" (Schindler), answering a question on headwinds; cannibalisation not addressed by name. AI Mode queries ~3x longer (Pichai).
- **AI revenue:** Cloud "has generated tens of billions of dollars in AI revenues year to date" (Ashkenazi). The first company-level size marker found by this project, but not a figure.
- **TPU:** "a meaningful contributor"; no size; "nearly 100 customers use TPUs" (Kurian, which includes rented capacity, not only systems sold); scaling in Q4, more through 2027. Growth ex-TPU "incredibly strong", with no number.
- **Capacity:** "supply constrained"; third-party compute began late Q3; "we expect margin headwinds in the near term as we utilize this capacity" (Ashkenazi). Capex mix ~65% servers and networking, 35% data centers.
- **Models:** Gemini 4 Argon usage ramped 3.5x faster than Gemini 3 "even with capacity constraints"; **Carbon** announced, claimed #1 on the Artificial Analysis index at 59 (vendor-cited), rolling first to cyber defenders. Gemini app 1B users; ~32B tokens per minute through first-party APIs. Pichai gave two different Argon timeframes on the call.
- **Financing:** no statement on ATM, equity or debt plans; no analyst asked directly about FCF. Whether Q2's "no further equity raises planned" still stands was not restated.
