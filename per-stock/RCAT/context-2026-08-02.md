## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before searching)

Red Cat is the cleanest "program won, now manufacture" story in listed defense drones: Teal Drones won the US Army Short Range Reconnaissance (SRR) production selection with Black Widow, is scaling toward ~1,000 units/month, exported 173 systems to the Japan GSDF, and put the Variant 7 USV into full-rate production via Blue Ops. My prior: TTM revenue ~$54.6M growing at a four-digit rate, net loss ~$75.5M (loses more than it sells), extreme US Army concentration, ~21x sales, and execution on a ~25x ramp is the entire thesis. I expected no material litigation.

The revenue trajectory and the litigation prior both turned out to need correcting.

## 4. DIFF — pre-research model vs. fresh reality

**① The Chief Revenue Officer was terminated *for cause* on 2026-07-17 and has sued the company for retaliatory termination.** Neither fact appears in the roster research. For a company whose entire equity story is "we won the program, now we execute the manufacturing ramp," losing the officer responsible for revenue — with cause, no severance, no equity acceleration — and then being sued by him is a first-order governance and execution signal. It also creates a disclosure-risk overhang independent of the dollar value of the claim.

**② The ramp is not linear, and the TTM growth headline hides it.** Q4 2025 revenue was ~$28.3M; **Q1 2026 was $15.47M — down ~45% sequentially.** Meanwhile 2026 consensus is $155.05M, which requires **~$139.6M across Q2–Q4** — roughly 3x the entire trailing twelve months, in nine months. That gap, not the program win, is the underwriting question. Inventory building from $23.5M to $50.5M in one quarter is consistent with management preparing for that ramp; cash falling $167.9M → $131.9M is the cost of preparing for it.

**③ Concentration is severe but the disclosed number is 56%, not ~100%.** Customer A = 56% of Q1 2026 revenue (and 50% of AR, down from 88% at year-end); Customer B = 19%. The top two are 75%. The concentration is real, but the *disclosure* lands in band 2; what pushes R1 to 1 is the market structure, not the percentage — see §5 R1.

**④ July's contract flow is validating but immaterial.** The $2.49M USAF order is a **technical and operational assessment** to consider Black Widow as a *successor* to the incumbent Teal 2 fleet — i.e. Black Widow is still being evaluated against alternatives, as is the Gauntlet II shoot-off. Real progress; not revenue.

**⑤ Two outside directors exited or nearly exited, discretionary, and nobody bought.** Paul Funk II sold his **entire** 165,028-share common position to zero on 2026-06-15 at ~$11.50 with no 10b5-1 footnote; the stock is $7.53 six weeks later. See §5 M3.

**⑥ Cash runway is the constraint nobody is pricing.** $131.9M of cash against a Q1 net cash decrease of $35.9M is ~3.7 quarters at that rate. Much of the Q1 burn is a working-capital inventory build that should reverse into deliveries — but only if the deliveries happen. If the ramp slips, RCAT raises equity.

## 5. Rating implications — proposed 13 subjective ratings

### AI Thesis (D1–D5)

**D1. AI/Robotics Revenue % — 5**
- *Rationale:* **100%** of revenue is defense robotics — Teal Black Widow and Hellcat small UAS (air) plus Blue Ops Variant 7 uncrewed surface vessels (sea). There is no legacy or non-robotics line.
- *Source:* 10-Q Q1 2026 (business description and revenue disaggregation); company product releases.
- *Confidence:* High.

**D2. Supply Chain Position — 3**
- *Rationale:* Mid-chain with differentiation. RCAT is a small-UAS **integrator/assembler**: motors, ESCs, flight controllers, radios and cameras are bought in (from suppliers such as UMAC). The SRR production selection is genuinely valuable and creates switching cost at the *procurement* level, but there are no widening lead times, no demonstrated pricing power, and the Army explicitly runs recompetes (Teal is in Gauntlet II now, and the USAF order is framed as an assessment). Not 2: the NDAA/Blue-UAS qualification and the program win are real differentiation.
- *Source:* 10-Q Q1 2026; Red Cat IR releases 2026-07-02 and 2026-07-30.
- *Confidence:* Medium.

**D3. Moat Strength — 3**
- *Rationale:* Modest moat. What exists: the SRR production selection (a multi-year qualification barrier a competitor must re-run), the Blue-UAS/NDAA listing, and the Army's sunk training and logistics. Not 4: **a program of record is a contract, not a moat** — the Air Force is currently *assessing* whether Black Widow should replace Teal 2, and Gauntlet II is an active shoot-off. Share is not yet stable and pricing is competitive.
- *Source:* Red Cat IR 2026-07-30 (assessment framing), 2026-07-02 (Gauntlet II).
- *Confidence:* Medium. *Bias check:* would I rate this 3 if the stock were at $18? Yes — the assessment/shoot-off language is in the July releases regardless of price.

**D4. Capacity Expansion — 4**
- *Rationale:* Active expansion in progress: capacity stated at **>500 Black Widow units/month** with a 1,000/month target, **Variant 7 in full-rate production since 2026-05-28**, Quaze Technologies acquired 2026-05-20, and inventory more than doubled ($23.5M → $50.5M) in Q1 2026 to support deliveries. Not 5: there is no disclosed committed multi-year capex program, and the expansion is being funded out of losses (−$26.6M net loss in Q1 on $15.5M of revenue).
- *Source:* 10-Q Q1 2026 balance sheet (inventory); Red Cat IR 2026-05-28; CEO capacity statement via Defense Daily/Aviation Today.
- *Confidence:* Medium.

**D5. AI-buildout tie — 5 · L11 lens (proposed)**
- *Rationale:* The strongest *direct* tie in the cohort for a company of this size: **100% of revenue comes from the drone/autonomy budget**, and RCAT holds a named **US Army SRR production selection** — a program of record — plus a Japan GSDF export order (173 systems) and a new USAF Black Widow evaluation. On the rule-23 parallel scale, that is "as AI-buildout-tied as this kind of company gets."
- *Source:* Red Cat SRR production-selection release; 10-Q Q1 2026; `research/2026-08-02-defense-autonomy-perception.md`.
- *Confidence:* Medium.
- **DEBATABLE:** a case exists for **4** — the SRR full-rate production decision has *not* occurred (RCAT is in TD3/LRIP, ~$35M awarded), and the July USAF order is an assessment. If Dom prefers D5 to reward *converted* dollars rather than program status, this is a 4 and it lines up with KTOS/AVAV at 4.

### Risk (R1–R5) — 5 = lowest risk

**R1. Customer Concentration — 1**
- *Rationale:* Disclosed: **Customer A = 56% of Q1 2026 revenue** (and 50% of accounts receivable, down from 88% of AR at 2025-12-31); Customer B = 19%; top two = **75%**. On the number alone that is band 2 (40–60%). What drives band 1 is the rubric's second clause — **"near-single-buyer market structure."** RCAT sells a US Army program-of-record reconnaissance drone and allied FMS-style variants; there is no commercial channel, no second civilian end market, and a single Army program decision determines the company's revenue.
- *Source:* 10-Q Q1 2026, Concentration of Credit Risk note (verbatim in §2).
- *Confidence:* High on the facts; Medium on the band, because 56% alone reads as a 2.
- **DEBATABLE:** if Dom scores R1 strictly on the disclosed percentage, RCAT is a **2**.

**R2. Geographic / Export Risk — 5**
- *Rationale (mandatory named shock + % exposed):* The worst plausible shock for RCAT is a **US appropriations freeze or an SRR program restructuring** — but that is customer/budget risk and is scored in R1, not here. On the three R2 legs: **no demand leg** (revenue is US Government plus allied FMS-style orders to Japan; no risk-geography shipment concentration above 25% is disclosed), **no policy leg** (no China revenue, no BIS/entity-list exposure — RCAT's positioning is the *opposite*, as an NDAA-compliant alternative to Chinese platforms), **no asset leg** (operations are in South Salt Lake, Utah).
- *Source:* 10-Q Q1 2026 cover page and concentration note.
- *Confidence:* Medium — **RCAT does not publish a geographic revenue breakout**, so the absence of a demand leg is inferred from the customer table rather than disclosed. Flagged.

**R3. Balance Sheet Risk — 2**
- *Rationale:* Elevated risk driven by burn and dilution rather than leverage. **Q1 2026 net loss −$26.55M on $15.47M of revenue** — the company loses roughly 1.7x what it sells — with TTM net income **−$75.51M** on TTM revenue $54.57M. Cash fell from **$167.9M to $131.9M** in one quarter (operating −$31.9M, investing −$6.8M, financing +$2.8M); at that rate the balance is ~3.7 quarters of runway. Equity is $238.7M against an accumulated deficit of $223.4M. Not 1: **no going-concern language appears in the Q1 2026 10-Q**, there is no debt maturity wall (convertible notes are being converted to equity, not refinanced), and a large part of the Q1 burn is an inventory build ($23.5M → $50.5M) that should convert to receivables if deliveries occur. Dilution — not default — is the defining risk: weighted shares went from 85.5M to 120.8M year over year.
- *Source:* 10-Q Q1 2026 balance sheet, statement of operations and cash-flow statement.
- *Confidence:* High.

**R4. Regulatory / Litigation Risk — 2**
- *Rationale:* Material, current and management-level. On 2026-07-17 the Board terminated **CRO Geoffrey Hitchcock for cause** (no severance, no equity acceleration), and on 2026-07-21 Hitchcock **filed a civil complaint alleging retaliatory termination** under New York and Oregon law plus breach of contract and breach of the implied covenant, seeking unspecified damages; the Company calls the claims meritless and is evaluating counterclaims. A retaliation claim brought by a just-removed C-suite officer carries disclosure risk well beyond its dollar value. Context that compounds it: a **"CORRECTION"** press release on 2026-05-21. Band 2 ("material litigation"); not 1 — no regulatory investigation, class action or agency proceeding is disclosed.
- *Source:* 8-K filed 2026-07-22, Items 5.02 and 8.01 (verbatim in §2).
- *Confidence:* High.

**R5. Disruption Risk — 5**
- *Rationale:* Rule-16 default for all non-Layer-10 names. Physical drone hardware; no credible AI/structural disruption to the revenue model on a ~5-year view.
- *Source:* CLAUDE.md rule 16 / rubric R5 scope note.
