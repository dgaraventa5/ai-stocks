## 1. Pre-research mental model (verbatim)

- As of the June briefing: a high-quality franchise (~80% GM, ~37-40% FCF margin) de-rated to ~11x forward after a disappointing 2026 tax season in which TurboTax "lost on price" at the DIY low end; a 17% workforce cut; FY26 guide of +13-14%.
- AI story: GenOS + Intuit Assist + "done-for-you" QuickBooks agents, plus a $100M+/yr OpenAI deal that puts Intuit apps inside ChatGPT. No disclosed AI revenue line; my working assumption was that adoption metrics exist (e.g., "~2-3M customers using agents") but no monetization numbers.
- Fiscal Q4 FY26 (reported late Aug 2026) I had not seen. I half-expected the "Big Bets +34%" headline to be an AI line — I suspected it was not.
- Open litigation: plaintiff-firm "investigation" stage only on the pricing disclosures.

**Q4 FY26 (ended 2026-07-31) — 8-K EX-99.01:** revenue $4.4B, +14%; GBS $3.4B +14%; QBO Accounting +20% "driven by higher effective prices, customer growth, and mix shift"; Consumer $930M +14%; Credit Karma $743M +16%.

**FY27 guidance (8-K EX-99.01) — a deceleration:**
- Revenue **$23.279-23.512B, +9-10%** (vs +14% FY26). GBS +13-14%; **TurboTax +2-3%**; Credit Karma +11-13%; Consumer +4-6%; Mailchimp −1% to 0% (new separate segment from 2026-08-01).
- Non-GAAP EPS $22.88-23.12 — **now includes SBC** (non-GAAP definition change effective 2026-08-01; $5.81/sh SBC impact). Ex-SBC this is ~$28.7-28.9. Every "EPS guide vs consensus" comparison in the press is distorted by this change (see §3).
- Q1 FY27 revenue guide $4.294-4.313B, +11%.

**Capital/balance sheet (8-K EX-99.01):** cash & investments $7.2B vs total debt **$7.7B** at 2026-07-31 (now modestly net-debt, ~$0.5B); $1.75B senior notes issued June 2026 to pre-fund FY27 maturities; FY26 buybacks **$5.5B (+96% YoY)**, $7.9B authorization left; dividend $1.38/qtr (+15%).

**Restructuring:** FY26 restructuring charges up $278M YoY in opex (10-K Item 7, Operating Expenses); ~18,600 employees at 2026-07-31 (10-K Human Capital).

**10-K strategy language on AI (Item 1):** "virtual team of AI agents and AI-enabled human experts"; GenOS described as the enabling platform; Big Bet #1 is "Deliver done-for-you experiences". Narrative only — no AI KPIs in the 10-K.

## 6. Implications for ratings (flags only)

- **D5 (new L10 lens):** evidence is adoption/usage with customer-outcome numbers; no AI-attributable revenue, ARR, conversion, or cost-ratio KPI; company-level results are decelerating, not accelerating. Fits band 3 territory; the case for 4 rests on QBO price increases after the agent launch, which Intuit does not attribute to AI.
- **D1:** still no discrete AI revenue; Big Bets are not AI.
- **D3:** deliberate DIY price cuts and unit loss reinforce price-taker behaviour in Consumer; QBO pricing power offsets partly.
- **M1:** confounded by the SBC redefinition; revenue estimates were cut and two downgrades landed.
- **M2:** −39 pts vs IGV over 6 months.
- **M3:** no new buys; Prabhu's May buy still inside 12 months.
- **R3:** now small net debt (not net cash).
- **R4:** two filed securities class actions plus Ontario certification; FTC order vacated.
- **R5:** visible price concession and unit loss at the DIY entry; management blames low-cost providers, not AI.
- **Rule 35:** *Bruce v. Intuit* (filed 2026-08-17) is a securities case filed within the last 90 days — qualifies on the rule's letter (follow-on to *Baldwin*, already disclosed in the 10-K). Hold not written by this pass (00-master out of scope); flagged for the orchestrator/Dom.
- **Rule 15:** latest quarter is still Q4 FY26 (q/e 2026-07-31), already ruled 2026-09-07 (blank; tax swing $177M provision vs $15M benefit, $293M restructuring secondary). No new ruling needed until Q1 FY27 (2026-11-19).
