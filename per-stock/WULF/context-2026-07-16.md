## 2. Latest filing highlights

**8-K filed 2026-07-06, acc 0001104659-26-080583 (Items 8.01/9.01) + Ex-99.1** — read in full; saved to `filings/8-K_2026-07-06_0001104659-26-080583.htm` and `..._ex99-1.htm`.

**Anthropic / Justified Data Campus lease (Item 8.01):**
- Landlord is subsidiary **Raylan Data LLC**; tenant **Anthropic PBC**. 20-year term, **~401 MW critical IT load**, Hawesville KY.
- **Phased delivery: initial capacity in service H2 2027, ramping to full 401 MW by early 2028.** Rent commences **upon delivery of the applicable leased premises** (per-phase rent commencement) and runs 20 years from each delivery.
- **Two successive five-year renewal options** (up to +10 years at Anthropic's option).
- Payment obligations "**expected to be supported by an investment-grade credit**" — the credit-support provider is **NOT named** in the 8-K or PR. Anthropic itself carries no public IG rating; whether this is a Google-style third-party backstop, an LC, or something else is **undisclosed — flag, do not assume** (rule 3).
- ~$19B initial-term contracted revenue (Ex-99.1). Arithmetic: $19B / 401 MW / 20 yr ≈ **$2.37M per IT-MW-year**.
- Ex-99.1 says the lease "creates a framework for future expansion" — **no expansion-option terms, milestones, delay LDs, or termination rights disclosed**. The lease agreement itself was NOT filed as an exhibit (Item 8.01 narrative only) — terms beyond the above are unverified until a 10-Q exhibit appears. Flagged gap.
- CEO Prager: February guidance was "a major customer commitment by around the end of Q2 2026" — delivered roughly on time (Ex-99.1).

**Abernathy sale (Item 8.01):**
- Big Country Wulf LLC (TeraWulf sub) sells **all** of its equity in FS CS I LLC (the 50.1% Abernathy JV interest, 168 MW critical IT campus in Abernathy TX) to **Fluidstack CS I Inc. + other purchasers**.
- Consideration **~$530M in three installments**: (i) **$250M within 14 days of execution** (i.e., ~mid-July 2026), (ii) **$150M by 2026-12-31**, (iii) **~$130M, subject to adjustments, by 2027-04-30**. Mutual releases of claims.
- Monetizes ~$450M invested capital "at a premium" (Ex-99.1); ATB pegs the premium at ~17.8% (see §3). Eliminates JV accounting. Stated use of proceeds: **redeploy into wholly owned AI infrastructure** (i.e., Justified).
- **No financing for the Justified buildout is disclosed in this 8-K** — but see §3 finding 2 (planned ~$3.5B debt raise, press reports).

**FCF-conversion red-flag check (rule 11 companion / refresh-context Step 2b):** **Not meaningful — GAAP losses / buildout capex.** yfinance quarterly statements through 2026-03-31: TTM OCF **−$197.3M**, TTM capex **$1,489.5M**, TTM statement FCF **−$1,686.7M** vs TTM net income **−$1,027.6M**. Both numerator and denominator deeply negative; ratio conveys nothing. (yfinance, pulled 2026-07-16.)

**Expectations red-flag (rule 14, Step 2c):** `python3 scripts/expectations_flag.py WULF` → **⚠ FLAG FIRES: P/S 53.47 = 90.2th pctile of own 3-yr range; rev YoY 25.7% vs 3-yr median 78.3%** (peak multiple + decelerating growth). ## 6. Implications for ratings (FLAGS ONLY — no numbers; rating session separate per rules 2/12)

- **D1 (AI revenue %) / D2 (supply-chain position) / D5 (AI-buildout tie):** The Anthropic lease is the strongest single datapoint yet for the converter thesis — a third marquee AI counterparty, ~$19B/20-yr contracted, secured *before* construction, validating WULF's power-first origination model (8-K acc 0001104659-26-080583; KBW 18.3% yield-on-cost). Evidence supports the high end of these dims; the offset is that **none of the Anthropic revenue arrives before H2 2027**.
- **R1 (customer concentration):** Now **three named counterparties** (Fluidstack, Core42, Anthropic) — but near-term revenue is still **two-dominated**: 2026–H1 2027 cash revenue is Core42 (60 MW energized) + Fluidstack CB-3/4/5 only; Anthropic rent begins per-phase from H2 2027; and the Abernathy sale *removes* a Fluidstack JV revenue stream. Diversification is real but **back-loaded ~18 months**. Also note Fluidstack is simultaneously customer and Abernathy buyer — relationship concentration cuts both ways. Mitigants: Google backstop (~$3.1B) on Fluidstack, unnamed IG credit support on Anthropic (**verify before crediting it in R1**).
- **R3 (balance sheet):** Two-sided change. Better: +$530M staged Abernathy proceeds ($250M ~now), ~$3.1B cash, $250M revolver, and the Anthropic lease makes project-level debt financeable. Worse: press-reported **~$3.5B new debt** for Justified → pro-forma ~$9B+ total debt while burn ran ~$630M/qtr and consolidated FCF is −$1.7B TTM. The model is deliberately *more* levered, now against 20-yr contracted revenue. R3 evidence is genuinely contested — do not let the headline $19B mask the financing execution risk between here and 2028.
- **M1 (EPS revisions / sell-side):** Strongly positive raw input — KBW $33, Rosenblatt $30, Needham $33, Morgan Stanley $72, ATB $51, 19/19 Buy (§3.1). Counterweight: the stock *fell* through the upgrades — see M2.
