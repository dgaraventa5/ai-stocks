## 1. Pre-research mental model (quoted verbatim)

> Lemonade is an AI-native P&C insurer (renters, homeowners, pet, car, life; US plus a small EU book). Customers ~2.5–3M, in-force premium (IFP) somewhere around $1.2–1.4B growing ~25–30%. Revenue growth looks huge in 2025–26 because they cut the main quota-share cession from ~55% to ~20% on 2025-07-01, so they keep more premium on their own books — that is an accounting/capital choice, not demand. The AI story is claims and service automation (bots "Maya"/"AI Jim", ~half of claims handled without a human) and flat headcount while IFP grows. Gross loss ratio was in the high 60s/low 70s in 2025 (LA wildfires hit Q1 2025). Management targets adjusted-EBITDA breakeven around end-2026 and GAAP profitability in 2027. Stock ran hard in 2025.

## 5. DIFF — what changed vs my mental model, and the AI-vs-accounting split

**Revenue growth decomposition (Q2 2026, my math from 10-Q figures):**
- Revenue +$130.3M YoY.
- Had cession stayed at Q2 2025's rate (ceded 139.8 / GEP 252.3 = 55.4%), Q2 2026 NEP would have been 332.4 × 44.6% = $148.2M, i.e. +$35.7M from volume. The other **+$103.8M of NEP came from retaining more premium**, partly offset by **−$10.0M ceding commission**.
- **≈ $94M of the $130M revenue increase (~72%) is reinsurance accounting; ~$36M (~28%) is underlying volume.** The economically real growth rate is **~32% (GEP and IFP)**, with customers +23% and premium/customer +8%.
- From Q3 2026 the comparison laps: Q3 guide revenue +66% at midpoint vs consensus FY2027 +35%.

**Where the AI does show up (efficiency, not revenue):**
- LAE ratio 5% vs ~9% industry (Q2 letter) — roughly a 4-point cost advantage on premium; at $1.43B IFP that is about $55–60M a year.
- IFP per employee: >$1M (Q1 letter); yfinance lists 1,282 full-time employees → ~$1.12M IFP and ~2,580 customers per employee on Q2 figures (employee count is yfinance, unverified against the 10-K).
- Technology development +34% and S&M +30% grew slower than GEP (+32%) only marginally; G&A +85% (including $6.5M of executive multi-year SBC grants, Q2 letter). **Operating leverage is not yet visible outside claims handling.**

**Where it does NOT show cleanly:**
- The gross loss ratio improvement (67%→60%) includes a 7-point favorable reserve release; on a current-accident-year basis Q2 is ~67%. H1 current-AY net LR improved 80.9%→65.4%, but H1 2025 carried the LA-wildfire cat. Rate increases, mix (pet/renters), and the new cat reinsurance are at least as plausible drivers as AI underwriting. I cannot attribute the loss-ratio gain to AI from disclosed data.

**Other diffs:**
- Stock is **down ~56% from its $99.90 high** and at a 52-week low — not "ran hard". The 2026 de-rating is post-Q2 (IFP guide light, CFO change, wider Q3 EBITDA loss).
- New: Tesla FSD autonomous car insurance (Jan 2026) — a genuine AI/data product, but Car is ~17% of IFP and FSD a slice of that.
- New: Hannover Re growth financing at ~9.8% (cheaper growth capital).
- Profit timeline intact: first positive adj. EBITDA quarter guided for Q4 2026.

## 6. Implications for ratings (flags only — not decided here)

- **D1:** revenue is insurance premium; AI-infrastructure-tied revenue is nil. Same position as HOOD/TSLA (D1=1). The ad-platform convention (AI-*enabled* baseline 2) is scoped to ad platforms and does not apply literally.
- **D2:** downstream consumer insurance, low switching cost (85% dollar retention).
- **D3:** a demonstrable claims-cost edge (LAE 5% vs 9%), but no pricing power and small scale against Progressive/GEICO/State Farm, which are also adopting AI.
- **D4:** active, funded expansion (IFP +32%, Car into new states, Hannover facility to 2028).
- **D5 (supplier lens):** no hyperscaler revenue.
- **M1:** consensus cut over 90 days (FY2027 EPS −0.82 → −1.10).
- **M2:** −38% over 6 months vs IGV +22% / KIE +8%.
- **M3:** no buys in the 6.5 months pulled; routine selling.
- **R3:** ~$1.2B cash & investments vs $226M debt; FCF near zero; SBC $95M/yr.
- **R4:** immaterial settled privacy cases; docket check skipped; AI-in-underwriting regulation is a live theme (state AI/insurance rules).
- **R5:** Layer-10 scope applies. AI is a tailwind to Lemonade's own model; autonomous driving shrinks the auto risk pool over the long run, which touches the Car line only.
- **Entry hold (rule 35):** none of the four triggers — no new enforcement action, no securities case found in the last 90 days (docket check skipped — re-run), no guidance cut (revenue guide raised; IFP guide only below Street), no transformative transaction.
- **Rule 15:** GAAP EPS is negative in both periods, so EPS YoY is blank in any case. Q2 2025 net loss was flattered by an $11.7M ERC tax refund (10-Q Non-GAAP reconciliation, footnote 2), so the flat −$43.4M vs −$43.9M understates underlying improvement.
