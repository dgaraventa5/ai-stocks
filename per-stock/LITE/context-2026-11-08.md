## 1. Pre-research mental model

LITE is a direct AI-datacenter optical play: EML/CW/pump lasers, narrow-linewidth assemblies, cloud transceivers and optical circuit switches (OCS) sold into hyperscaler and NVIDIA AI clusters, with a shrinking industrial tail. As of the June briefing it was undershipping demand by >30%, had a $2B NVIDIA preferred investment plus a multi-billion purchase commitment, a fifth InP fab (Greensboro) coming ~2028, and a convertible stack being equitized. I expected two more strong quarters (FQ4 FY26 and FQ1 FY27) of record revenue, some GAAP noise from the converts, and rising customer concentration. I did not know why the stock fell ~49% in a month and had no model for a merger.

## 6. Implications for ratings (FLAGS ONLY — not decisions; rule 12)

- **Overarching:** the price now reflects 4.5 × MRVL less a completion-risk spread, not standalone LITE. Price-based inputs (P/S, EV/EBITDA, FCF yield, 50DMA %, M2) now largely measure MRVL and deal odds. The Total Score should be read with that caveat until the deal closes or breaks.
- **D1 (5):** components and systems growth is all intra-DC/DCI/OCS/cloud transceivers. Still >50% AI-tied. No change indicated.
- **D2 (5):** booked through early 2029, select price increases, pump lasers sold out. Bottleneck intact.
- **D3 (4):** CPO/ELS wins and the >$5B commitment support the IP moat. Chinese InP capacity has no impact yet. No change indicated.
- **D4 (5):** capex tripled; customer commitments (NVIDIA plus >$5B CPO) back the buildout. Intact.
- **D5 (5):** two 10%+ customers were 48% of FQ1 revenue and 41.6% of FY26, but both are **unnamed "end customers"**. Whether they are hyperscalers or NVIDIA (not a hyperscaler) is unverified, so this band read rests on inference.
- **M1 (5):** results beat and guidance stepped up sharply, which should drive upward estimate revisions. **Revision data not verified.** Analyst actions are rating/PT cuts on MRVL tied to the deal, not EPS cuts.
- **M2 (5):** LITE −39.7% 6m, −33.8% 3m, −48.6% 1m against SMH −23.6% 1m. **Strongly indicates a move down.** The SMH 3m/6m figure is needed to set the band.
- **M3 (3):** only RSU-tax and 10b5-1 sales seen; no buys. Default 3 holds. September–October Form 4s unchecked.
- **R1 (4):** top customer 37% (FQ1) and 26.6% (FY26), both inside the **25–40% band = 3**.
- **R2 (4):** HK + China ship-to = 26.6% of FY26 revenue. Assets sit in Thailand and China. A BIS/DOJ export inquiry is open. The FCC rule is a two-way risk. A strict three-leg read could reach 3; review.
- **R3 (4):** net cash ~$580M and strong EBITDA argue for 5. Against that: thin FCF ($37.9M) as capex rises, $780M of cash-settled conversion requests, all converts current, and +30% diluted shares. 4 is defensible.
- **R4 (4):** the open BIS/DOJ inquiry is a disclosed, pending regulatory matter, which literally fits 3 at minimum. Merger antitrust review and the plaintiff-firm "investigation" add deal risk, not company liability.
- **R5 (5):** Layer-07 default; no change.
- **Rule 35:** the Marvell merger is a **transformative transaction since the last reported quarter** (signed 2026-11-01, after the quarter ended 2026-09-26). It qualifies for an entry hold. **Not written here; flagged to the orchestrator/Dom.** No guidance cut and no filed securities case found.
