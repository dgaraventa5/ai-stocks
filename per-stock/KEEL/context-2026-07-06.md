## 1. Pre-research mental model (verbatim)

Keel Infrastructure Corp. is the rebranded Bitfarms Ltd. (BITF), a Canadian bitcoin miner pivoting toward AI/HPC hosting — the rebrand itself signals the pivot from pure mining to "infrastructure." As of my training, Bitfarms had been executing an aggressive strategy shift under new leadership (Ben Gagnon as CEO), acquiring Stronghold Digital Mining for its powered-land/energy assets in PA, and targeting HPC/AI hosting conversion of its ~1 GW-plus energy pipeline; it also fended off a Riot Platforms hostile stake. Most recent quarter per training is likely Q4 2025 / early 2026, with the company likely still pre-revenue on AI hosting (no signed hyperscaler contract that I can confirm) and reliant on equity/ATM issuance — dilution risk is high for this cohort. Secured MW / powered land is the key value driver (Layer-9 EV/MW), and the AI-hosting pivot is still early-stage with execution/financing risk dominating; balance sheet likely leans on ATM dilution rather than debt.

## 2. Latest filing highlights (SEC EDGAR, primary)

**Q1 2026 10-Q (filed 2026-05-11):**
- Revenue **$37M, −22% YoY** (Q1'25 ~$47M); legacy mining shrinking (Q1 2026 press release, StockTitan/Globe & Mail 2026-05-11).
- **Net loss $145.4M** vs $55.6M loss Q1'25; operating loss $98M (incl. $28M non-cash D&A). Driver: **$41M loss on change in fair value of digital assets** (vs $23M loss Q1'25) — a mark-to-market BTC swing, largely non-cash/non-operating.
- **Adjusted EBITDA −$17M (−45% of revenue)** vs +$7M / +14% Q1'25 — the operating business is now EBITDA-negative as mining economics deteriorate and HPC build spend ramps ahead of revenue.
- Liquidity **~$533M as of May 8** ($336M unrestricted cash + $197M unencumbered BTC). Sold 269 BTC for $20M YTD to de-risk BTC exposure (BTC position ~$197M remaining).
- Exited Latin America (sold 70 MW Paso Pe, Paraguay).

**8-K 2026-06-05 / 2026-06-10 — convertible notes financing (Item 8.01 / Item 1.01):**
- Priced then closed **$458M of 1.250% Convertible Senior Notes due 2032** ($400M base + full $58M option; upsized from $350M). Senior unsecured; **guaranteed by Bitfarms Ltd.** Ranks pari passu with the **existing 1.375% convertible notes due 2031** (a second convert already on the balance sheet).
- Entered **capped call transactions** (conversion price ~$7.41, a 25% premium; cap ~$11.86) to blunt dilution; ~$41.7M of proceeds used for the caps (per Yahoo/TipRanks 2026-06 coverage).
- Use of proceeds: general corporate + deposits for long-lead equipment + collateralizing letters of credit for data-center development. Management states existing liquidity was *already* sufficient to develop Panther Creek/Sharon/Moses Lake through leasing; the convert is for flexibility/acceleration.

**8-K 2026-06-16 (Item 4.01):** Appointed **PwC (US)** as new auditor for FY2026 (consistent with US redomiciliation).

**8-K 2026-07-06 (Item 5.02, event 2026-07-05):** Appointed **Ganesh Aiyer as President** (eff. 2026-07-06), reporting to CEO. Aiyer most recently **Chief Business Officer at Digital Realty Trust** (a top-3 global data-center REIT) — a credible data-center commercial/leasing hire, directly relevant to converting pipeline into signed leases.
