## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before searching)

What I believed coming in: Basler is a small German industrial camera maker — a component supplier in a fragmented market (Teledyne DALSA, Allied Vision, IDS, Hikrobot), structurally below Cognex and Keyence on margin because cameras are closer to a commodity than a system. I expected a cyclical recovery story off a depressed 2024 base, with the machine-vision "AI" angle being end-market pull rather than anything proprietary, and liquidity being the practical blocker on any position. I did not have a robotics angle in mind at all.

### 🚩 The internal tension a rater must not skip

**Q1 delivered a 22.7% EBIT margin, but the full-year guidance targets only a 9.5–13% pre-tax margin.** Either management is guiding very conservatively or it expects a severe H2 mix/cost step-down. Order growth of +64% against revenue growth of +30% also means a backlog build — and machine-vision cameras are short-cycle with cancellable orders. **The 2026-08-05 H1 report is where this resolves and it is the single most important number to check on this name.**

## 5. Rating implications

- **D1 = 2**, on the same standard applied to CGNX. 100% machine vision ≠ robotics/physical AI. No robotics revenue is disclosed; the growth is China + semiconductor/electronics/logistics — board and factory inspection riding electronics/AI capex. The Orbbec AMR product is the only genuine robot-perception vector and it is five months old with no revenue.
- **D4 = 5** is the standout. Doubling assembly capacity, physically installed, *into* a +64% order book, with guidance raised in the same release — that is precisely the D4 rubric's top band ("major buildout funded and underway… customer commitments backing it"), scaled to a €770M company.
- **D2/D3 both 3.** Merchant camera supplier in a fragmented market, winning in China on price/performance — the opposite of a bottleneck. Deliberately not letting a +105% share price lift these (rubric bias check).
- **D5 = 2** (L11 lens, proposed) — indirect exposure through equipment makers and integrators; no named anchor, no disclosed concentration, no hyperscaler or robot-platform relationship.
- **M1 = 5** on the guidance raise, but confidence Medium: only 3 analysts cover the name, so the guidance raise is doing the work, not a revision count.
- **M2 = 5** (+105% vs XLI +19.84%), with two flags: the stock is ~21% below its €31.90 high, and a EUR return is being compared to a USD benchmark.
- **R2 = 3 is rated without a disclosed % exposed**, which the revised R2 rubric explicitly requires. Flagged, not papered over — resolve on 08-05 if a regional split appears.
- **R3 = 4 with unverified net cash** — the weakest-evidence rating in this file. If the H1 balance sheet shows net cash, it is a 5.
- **Practical constraint, not a rating:** ~€0.75M/day of turnover (down from the ~€1.07M/day measured in the spring). Position size is bound by liquidity before it is bound by conviction.
