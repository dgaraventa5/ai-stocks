## Pre-research mental model

(Written from the 2026-06-10 briefing, thesis.md — still an unfilled template — and news-log.md, before this pass's filings/searches.) Oklo is a pre-revenue advanced-fission developer building the ~75 MWe Aurora sodium-cooled fast "powerhouse" on a build-own-operate model, with Aurora-INL under construction at Idaho National Laboratory on a **DOE Reactor Pilot Program** authorization path rather than an NRC license — the 2022-denied COLA had still not been resubmitted as of the Q1 10-Q. Customer pipeline is ~14 GW but almost entirely non-binding: Switch 12 GW master agreement (non-binding framework), Equinix 500 MW LOI backed by a $25M right-of-first-refusal payment sitting on the balance sheet, Diamondback and Prometheus Hyperscale LOIs, and the strongest item — a Meta Prepayment Agreement (2026-01-05) for a 1.2 GW Pike County, OH campus with power "as early as 2030." Fuel is the second leg: A3F fuel fab at INL, EBR-II recovered HALEU for the first core, Centrus deconversion JV. Balance sheet was flush at $2.537B after a $1.2B Q1 ATM that grew shares ~8.3% in one quarter; first revenue was expected to come from radioisotopes (Atomic Alchemy / Abundantia), not power. Open questions I expected to resolve: whether the COLA was finally filed, whether the Meta prepayment has any disclosed dollar amount, and whether Aurora-INL cleared its DOE safety gates.

## Implications for ratings

No numbers proposed (rule 12 / task constraint). Dimensions where the evidence moved, and why:

- **D1 (AI Revenue % of Total)** — unchanged in substance and still the hard floor: revenue is $0 and the first dollar, when it arrives, is **isotopes from a radiochemistry lab, not AI-datacenter power**. Anything that reads AI revenue into OKLO today is reading the pipeline, not the income statement. The one genuinely new fact — Groves startup authorization — moves the *isotope* line, which is not AI revenue. If anything this pass argues the first revenue that shows up will make D1 look *worse*, not better, because it will be non-AI revenue.

- **D2 (Supply Chain Position)** — modestly strengthened. Groves went groundbreaking→startup authorization in ~10 months on privately owned land with commercially sourced fuel; Aurora-INL cleared PDSA (3 of 4). Oklo is demonstrably faster through the DOE gate than peers are through the NRC one, and the Creative Engineers tuck-in plus the A3F/Centrus fuel work continue the vertical integration. Counterweight: the DOE path authorizes *these* units, not the commercial fleet the customers are notionally buying, and the NRC COLA — the thing that unlocks commercial deployment — has still not been filed four years after the denial.

- **D4 (Capacity Expansion)** — strengthened on evidence and now measurable: FY2026 capex guidance of **$350–450M** against $32.8M spent in Q1 is a real, funded, disclosed build ramp, and two DOE gates cleared in two months. This is the dimension with the cleanest positive movement this pass.

- **D5 (Hyperscaler Exposure / AI-buildout tie)** — **this is the dimension the pass most calls into question, and it should move down or at minimum be re-justified on stricter evidence.** Under rule 23's "strength of the tie" framing, the tie got no stronger and arguably weaker in disclosure quality: (a) the 10-Q states there were **no material commitments or contractual obligations** at all; (b) **Meta has no disclosed dollar amount and no balance-sheet contract liability** — the prepayment mechanism has not been drawn; (c) the Equinix $25M ROFR has now sat unchanged for two straight quarters with no PPA; (d) Switch remains a framework whose own filing language contemplates "converting such preliminary agreements into power purchase agreements"; (e) **no new customer 8-K since January**. Seven months of elapsed time with zero LOI→PPA conversions is itself information. The June briefing rated the tie prospective; it is still prospective, with one more quarter of non-conversion on the clock.

- **M1 (EPS Revisions)** — low-signal for a pre-revenue name (consensus is a ~$0.17 loss), but the one analyst datapoint found is a **cut**: Barclays $82 → $76 while maintaining Buy. Directionally negative, thin evidence.

- **M2 (Relative Strength)** — **materially negative and the largest single change this pass.** ~$69.66 → $41.22, roughly -41% since the last briefing, -35.7% y/y market cap, and trading near a 52-week low of $36.61 against a $193.84 high. June -21.8%; sector-wide nuclear selloffs on the DOE large-reactor loan news and again on 7/16. Whether OKLO is weak *relative to its sector* needs a sector check at rating time (SMR peers fell together), but on an absolute basis this is a broken chart. **The rule-11 objective 50DMA % input will almost certainly collapse** — refresh `scripts/momentum_50dma.py` before any Momentum rating.

- **M3 (Insider Activity)** — **negative and worth an explicit judgment call rather than a mechanical pass.** Two founder-executives (both 10% holders) each filed to sell 400,000 shares (~$15.5M) on 2026-08-03, against a prior cadence of ~60,000/month, at a price ~79% below the high; the CFO is also selling; **no insider buying anywhere in 2026**. But the rubric's M3 edge-case precedents (rule 26) exist precisely for judgment calls like this one, and the *acceleration* of size into a collapsing price is a different fact pattern than routine diversification into strength. Flag for Dom rather than auto-resolve.

- **R3 (Balance Sheet Risk)** — the two-sided one. **Positive:** $2.537B cash, no debt, total liabilities $64.9M, clean one-year going-concern language, ~5 years of runway at guided burn. **Negative:** shares +8.3% in a single quarter, a $1.5B ATM fully exhausted, a fresh $1.0B ATM opened at depressed prices, and the *real* consumption rate is ~$430–550M/yr once capex is counted — not the ~$18M/quarter operating figure the "30 years of runway" commentary keys off.
