# DLR (Digital Realty Trust) — Context Briefing

**Date:** 2026-11-08
**Purpose:** Rule-12 rotation refresh (`/refresh-context`) for DLR, Layer 03 (data center REITs). The previous briefing (`context-2026-06-22.md`) was 139 days old. This is a research briefing only: no workbook edits and no rating changes. Rating proposals are in Section 6 and go to Dom.
**Lens:** DLR is a REIT, so the right earnings measures are Core FFO and AFFO per share, bookings, backlog, renewal spreads, churn, same-capital cash NOI, and net debt / adjusted EBITDA. GAAP EPS is not the right measure (see the 2026-06-22 caveat, still open).
**Price context (Yahoo, supplied by the orchestrator):** $174.20 close on 2026-11-06. 1 month −1.1%, 3 months −8.3%, 6 months −9.4%. SPY: 1 month −5.0%, 3 months −4.6%. SMH: 1 month −23.6%.

## 1. Pre-research mental model

> Digital Realty is the largest global data-center REIT, leasing hyperscale blocks to cloud and AI customers and retail colocation/interconnection to enterprises. As of the June briefing: Q1 2026 Core FFO $2.04, record-ish bookings of $707M (100% share), a 200 MW AI-inference lease in Charlotte, backlog of $1.8B (100% share), net debt/EBITDA 4.7x, 2026 Core FFO guide $8.00–8.10, and a new ATM equity program. I expect Q3 2026 to show continued strong bookings and another guide raise, funded by heavy equity issuance. The stock has drifted lower over 3–6 months despite this, so I suspect either dilution worries or rotation out of AI infrastructure. Open questions: how concentrated the hyperscale book has become, how much leverage moved as development spending ramped, and whether AI is actually a disclosed share of revenue (the D1 rating rests on it).

## 6. Implications for ratings (flags for the rating session; nothing decided here)

- **D1:** The absolute lens is the issue, not a new event. No AI revenue share is disclosed. The original rationale's own 21%-of-signings figure fits band 3–4, not band 5 (">50% … clearly and reportedly AI-tied"). AI is plainly the growth driver of new bookings, but the in-place revenue base is mostly cloud and enterprise colocation. This is the rubric's named failure mode ("counting data center revenue as AI revenue").
- **D2:** Pricing power is demonstrable (record >1 MW pricing, +26% renewals, record-low churn). But "limited substitutes" is debatable: many private developers compete (QTS, Vantage, Aligned, and others). The bottleneck is powered land, which DLR is aggressively banking.
- **D3:** Renewal spreads and 0.7% churn support the current rating. Nothing points to a moat change.
- **D4:** 1.5 GW / $22B, 63% pre-leased, $4.25–4.75B of 2026 development capex, with land banking in five-plus new GW-scale campuses. Squarely band 5.
- **D5 (supplier lens):** Two customers at 11% + 10% = 21% disclosed, and they are almost certainly hyperscalers. The rest of the top-20 hyperscale share was not retrieved. The combined >30% needed for band 5 is likely but not directly verified here.
- **M1:** Beat-and-raise in Q1, Q2 and Q3, and FY26 consensus up from ~$8.05 to $8.33. Counterpoints: dilution and a sequentially lower implied Q4. The 2026-05-18 rating of 2 rested on a then-negative revision tally, which no longer describes the trend.
- **M2:** Benchmark is XLRE over 180 days (2026-05-18 precedent). DLR is −9.4% over 6 months. I could not get XLRE's trailing 6-month return (gap). If XLRE is roughly flat, DLR lags by ~9–10 pts, which is band 2 territory.
- **M3:** No open-market buys found. Only awards and routine 2025 sales. Default 3.
- **R1:** The new 11%/10% disclosure is directional concentration, though still under the 15% line of band 5.
- **R2:** 45.7% of revenue is outside the US, spread across Europe, APAC, Africa (Teraco), LatAm (Ascenty) and Türkiye. No China or export leg.
- **R3:** 5.2x net debt/EBITDA (4.9x pro forma) is up from 4.7x but below the 5.9x on file when R3=3 was set. Moody's upgrade to Baa1, maturities termed out to 2036, and heavy but orderly equity funding.
- **R4:** The 10-Q reports no material proceedings. The litigation docket check was skipped, so this is not a clean bill.
- **R5:** Default 5. Physical infrastructure.

**Rule-35 screen:** no new enforcement action, no securities case found (docket check skipped), no guidance cut (guidance raised), and no transformative transaction (Columbia ~$690M and Teraco ~$688M are each ~1% of enterprise value). No hold warranted on this evidence.
