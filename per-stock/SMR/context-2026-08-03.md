## Pre-research mental model

NuScale is the only SMR developer with NRC-approved designs (US600 50 MWe certified 2023; uprated US460 77 MWe Standard Design Approval 2025-05-29), but it is effectively pre-revenue — Q1 2026 revenue was $565K — and has zero binding commercial orders. Commercialization is outsourced entirely to ENTRA1 Energy, which owns the customer relationship and which NuScale *pays* under the Partnership Milestones Agreement (MC1 of $507.4M already expensed, ~$1.18B MC2 implied on a binding PPA); the flagship TVA "up to 6 GW" program remains an explicitly nonbinding collaborative agreement with a definitive PPA promised "later this year." The balance sheet is the strength — ~$1.0B liquid at 3/31/26 with no debt, funded by a $1.0B ATM program — against an underlying opex burn of roughly $55M/quarter. Order book: none; the only near-term revenue path was RoPower/Doicești (Romania, 6 modules, 462 MWe), which took FID on 2026-02-12 and was expected to move into a 15-month pre-EPC stage.

**Where the prior mental model turned out to be wrong (detail in DIFF below):** (a) the RoPower FID did *not* clear the way to pre-EPC — its conditions have not been met and Nuclearelectrica publicly floated re-opening the technology choice to NuScale's competitors; (b) Fluor — founder, majority shareholder and FEED/engineering partner — has fully exited the equity; (c) Q2 dilution was materially larger than the "implied ~$200M April ATM issuance" I flagged as unconfirmed.

### 2. FCF conversion — DELIBERATELY NOT COMPUTED; cash burn and runway substituted

**FCF conversion (TTM FCF ÷ TTM net income) is meaningless for SMR and is not computed here.** Both the numerator and denominator are negative — NuScale is pre-revenue with a $565K quarterly top line and a $(46.7)M quarterly net loss, so the ratio would be a positive number produced by dividing one negative by another, which carries no information and would actively mislead. Per CLAUDE.md rule 3 (flag, don't assume), the substitute measures are:

**Cash burn:**
- Q1 2026 total operating cash burn: **$(314.7)M** (10-Q cash-flow statement).
- Of which the one-time ENTRA1 Milestone Contribution settlement: **$259.9M** (10-Q MD&A).

- This excludes investing outflows for long-lead material and vendor prepayments, which the 10-Q separately identifies as a driver — so the true cash consumption rate is somewhat higher than $54.8M/qtr and will rise as manufacturing scales.

**Runway:**
- Liquid resources at 3/31/26: cash $341.1M + ST investments $549.0M + LT investments $118.6M = **$1,008.8M**, no debt.
- Plus Q2 ATM proceeds ≈ **$216.8M** gross (22.36M shares; corroborated by the Class A share-count move 323.74M → 346.11M) → **≈ $1.23B**.
- **At ~$55M/quarter underlying burn: ~22 quarters, i.e. 5+ years of runway.** Even doubling the burn leaves >2.5 years.
- **At the Q1 headline rate including milestone payments ($314.7M/qtr): ~4 quarters.**
- ATM capacity remaining ≈ $745M (of the $1.0B 2026 program), a further funding source at the cost of dilution.

**Conclusion: liquidity is NOT the near-term risk; dilution and the PMA contingency are.** The single largest claim on this cash is contingent and off-balance-sheet: MC2 on a binding PPA, implied at ~$1.18B (computed in the prior briefing from $507.4M = 15%; **not disclosed by the company**). ## Implications for ratings

**No numbers proposed** (rule 12 — rating changes stay in the human loop; rule 26/rubric-driven). Below is which dimensions the new evidence touches and why.

### D1 — AI-tied revenue today
**Unchanged in substance, weaker in base.** AI-tied revenue remains **$0**. What changed is that the *non*-AI revenue base also collapsed: Q1 2026 revenue $565K (−95.8% YoY), Q2 consensus ~$1M, and the one revenue-producing relationship (RoPower) is now stalled at the pre-EPC gate. The FY2026 consensus of $35.9M implies an H2 step-up that depends on the very pre-EPC contract Nuclearelectrica says cannot advance until FID conditions are met. The prior briefing's finding — "AI-tied revenue today = $0 of an already de-minimis revenue base" — is now more acute, not less.

### D2 — Supply-chain position vs other SMR developers

- *Positive:* "components of 12 power modules already in production" (2026-06-17) is the most concrete manufacturing-progress datapoint we have, and the Paragon I&C final-design award moves the safety-systems critical path. NuScale retains the only NRC-approved SMR designs.
