## 1. Pre-research mental model (verbatim, written before searching)

> Navitas is a small-cap GaN/SiC power-semi pure-play whose ~$6-7B market cap is almost entirely an option on the NVIDIA 800V HVDC data-center power architecture (Rubin/Kyber, 2027+ inflection), not current fundamentals. Current revenue is tiny (~$8.6M Q1 2026, down ~39% YoY, guided ~$10M Q2) with the legacy mobile/consumer base eroding and the high-power/AI base growing off a trivial dollar base. The company burns ~$16M/quarter operating cash, funded by serial equity dilution (as of last briefing: $122M ATM done, $250M shelf + $125M ATM live, earn-out and settlement shares issued). No debt, ~$221M cash, so no near-term solvency risk — the risk is perpetual dilution and the 2027 NVIDIA ramp failing to materialize or Navitas getting designed out against TI/Infineon/Renesas (NVIDIA multi-sourced the 800V design). I expect the last month to add more 8-Ks (annual meeting, more earn-out/dilution) but no new hard revenue.

**Verdict on mental model:** Confirmed and slightly under-called on the dilution escalation. The one genuinely material new item is a **$500M automatic shelf + $500M ATM** (S-3ASR, filed 2026-06-08) — roughly 2x the prior dilution capacity, now explicitly earmarked for "working capital and general corporate purposes, **including potential acquisitions**." Everything else (annual meeting, final earn-out tranche, director resignation) is housekeeping / already-flagged.

**Thesis-break / speculative-risk candor:** No thesis-break trigger this month.
