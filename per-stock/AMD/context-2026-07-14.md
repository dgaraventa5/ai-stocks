## 1. Pre-research mental model (verbatim)

AMD is the only credible second-source data-center GPU vendor at hyperscaler scale, with Data Center now the primary revenue driver (~56% of revenue, $5.8B of $10.3B in Q1 2026). The AI thesis rests on multi-year, multi-generation hyperscaler commitments — Meta and OpenAI 6-GW deals plus an Oracle 50K-GPU MI450 supercluster — layered on EPYC server CPU share gains, with equity warrants (~320M shares) deepening customer entrenchment but adding dilution risk. Most recent reported quarter I know of is Q1 2026 (ended March 28, reported May 5): $10.3B revenue +38% YoY, non-GAAP EPS $1.37 +43%, Q2 guided to $11.2B. Most recent roadmap milestone is MI450 sampling to lead customers, with MI400-series/Helios rack production shipments on track for 2H 2026 and CUDA/ROCm software gap still the swing factor vs NVDA.

## 5. DIFF — what changed since prior mental model

- **Q1 driver was MI350, not MI450.** The 10-Q attributes the $5.8B/+57% Data Center quarter to 5th-gen EPYC + **MI350 Series** (current shipping product). MI450 is still pre-revenue (sampling) — the mega-deals are 2H-2026-and-later ramp, not yet in the numbers. The prior model blurred this.
- **Meta deal is a distinct SECOND 6-GW agreement** (Feb 24, 2026), custom MI450 + Venice EPYC — confirms and extends the "Meta $100B+/6GW" note in the thesis; both OpenAI and Meta are now 6-GW.
- **New near-term catalyst clarity:** Advancing AI 2026 on **July 22–23** (Zen 6 Venice + Helios launch) and **Q2 earnings Aug 4** — both land inside the coming weeks. Thesis catalyst table had these as "H2 2026 / Q2" placeholders.
- **ROCm advanced concretely:** 7.2.x shipping, PyTorch 2.10 first-class on ROCm 7.1 stable — the "ROCm-7 PyTorch first-class support" item from the 2026-05-18 rating session is now confirmed shipped, though real-world inference still trails NVIDIA ~15–25%.
- **China re-opened:** MI308 export licenses granted and a potential 40–50K Alibaba order — upside optionality vs the Q1 "not material" framing (and vs the $800M ban cost). Countervailing: Beijing domestic-chip directives.
- **July 8-K is non-substantive** (exec comp), not a thesis event.
- **Sentiment shift:** stock near ~$900B with fresh analyst caution ahead of the two July/Aug events — expectations elevated.
