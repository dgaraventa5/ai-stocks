intends to litigate, sitting on the same Swedish asset base it is trying to monetize with the Big
Boden colocation LOI. The pivot got materially more real and a specific jurisdictional risk got
materially more expensive, in the same quarter.

## 5. Implications for ratings (flag only — NOT pre-decided)

- **D1 (AI revenue %)** — upward pressure but keep it honest: BUZZ HPC is **$7.1M of $79.1M ≈ 9.0%**
  of *trailing* revenue. The ~$180M annualized figure is forward. D1 measures the genuine AI tie;
  the forward number belongs in D5, not D1, or it gets double-counted (rule-27 D1/D5 discipline).
- **D5 (strength of AI-buildout tie)** — the clearest upgrade candidate. A **$350M five-year contract
  with an investment-grade counterparty on GB300 NVL72 systems**, with HIVE retaining ownership, is
  contracted and quantified — a different evidentiary class from the June "$35M ARR + a target."
- **D3 (moat)** — mild upward: retaining ownership of the NVIDIA infrastructure rather than acting as
  a pass-through host is a stronger economic position than pure colocation.
- **⚠️ R4 (regulatory risk)** — **the dimension that most needs re-rating.** An $84.7M contested tax
  assessment in a jurisdiction where HIVE holds significant operations, which the company is refusing
  to pay and litigating potentially up to the EU level, is a concrete, quantified regulatory exposure
  that did not exist in the ratings backing this name. Note the amount is ~11% of the current ~$802M
  market cap.
- **R2 (geography)** — related: Sweden, Paraguay, Canada. The Swedish exposure just proved to carry
  real tax risk.
- **R3 (balance-sheet risk)** — two-sided: adjusted EBITDA back positive and ARR contracted, versus
  ~$185M of capex committed for the new cluster plus the $115M exchangeable notes. Not re-verified
  this pass (§3 item 5).
- **Rule-13 MW input:** the **32 MW Big Boden** colocation LOI is signed but an **LOI**, and the
  Merritt BC deployment is at a **Bell** facility. Per the `capacity-mw.json` convention (energized +
  committed only; speculative excluded), an LOI likely does **not** qualify as secured — decide
  deliberately and record the basis.
- **Rule-9 objective refresh: DUE, with a data caveat.** yfinance's latest quarter for HIVE is
  **2026-03-31** — the June-quarter results are not in Yahoo's statements. A refresh run now will
  silently use fiscal-Q4 data and miss the entire reversal. Use the §2 figures.
