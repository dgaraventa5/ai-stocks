## 3. WebSearch findings (since 2026-10-19)

1. **US "Affiliates Rule" suspension extended to 2027-01-10.** The rule extends Entity List limits to entities ≥50% owned by listed parties. Its suspension was due to lapse on 2026-11-10. BIS published a final rule (Federal Register, 2026-11-09) extending it through 2027-01-10. The substance is unchanged, and BIS can reinstate the rule without further notice. ASML's FY2025 annual report says the rule "would have affected a limited number of our business partners." The overhang is deferred, not removed. Sources: [Nixon Peabody 2026-11-06](https://www.nixonpeabody.com/insights/alerts/2026/11/06/bis-formally-suspends-the-affiliates-rule-until-january-10-2027), [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2026/11/bis-affiliates-rule-suspension.html), [Dechert](https://www.dechert.com/knowledge/onpoint/2026/11/a-reprieve--not-a-repeal--bis-extends-the-affiliates-rule-pause.html).
2. **MATCH Act (H.R. 8170 / S. 4281) still pending, with no floor vote found.** It cleared House Foreign Affairs 44–0 on 2026-04-22 after being narrowed, but it still bans immersion DUV exports and servicing for covered Chinese fabs (SMIC, Huawei, Hua Hong, CXMT, YMTC) and gives the Netherlands and Japan 150 days to align. *Het Financieele Dagblad*, via ioplus (2026-10-12), reports that the Dutch government wants the bill dropped. Estimated exposure is up to ~$9B of China sales, including $1–1.5B a year of China service revenue. **Gap:** congress.gov returned 403, so I could not confirm floor status after July. Sources: [ioplus 2026-10-12](https://ioplus.nl/en/posts/asml-caught-between-us-and-china-over-match-act), [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/semiconductors/us-lawmakers-amend-new-restrictions-on-chinese-chipmakers-match-acts-blanket-restrictions-removed-from-select-chipmaking-tools), [TrendForce 2026-08-25](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/08/25/news-u-s-may-push-near-total-asml-china-ban-on-duv-sales-and-servicing-amid-chinas-domestic-lithography-push/).
3. **Securities class action pending, never logged in ASML's research files.** *In re ASML Holding N.V. Securities Litigation*, **S.D.N.Y. No. 1:24-cv-08664 (NRB)**, filed November 2024. Class period 2024-01-24 to 2024-10-15. It alleges misstatements on demand, 2025 targets and the impact of export rules. The court denied most of the motion to dismiss on 2026-03-27, defendants answered on 2026-05-01, and the case is now in discovery. This is a filed case with a docket number, but the status comes from the plaintiff firm's case page; RECAP was not checked (CourtListener returned 403), so Dom's litigation-check run should confirm it. That is consistent with a materiality judgment, and the 20-F predates the dismissal ruling. Source: [Kessler Topaz case page](https://www.ktmc.com/new-cases/asml-holding-nv/).
4. **EUV-in-China allegation (Lutnick, June 2026): no public resolution or enforcement action found.** ASML says none of its 314 operating and 26 decommissioned EUV systems is in China. Sources: [Yahoo/Bloomberg](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/articles/asml-asserts-compliance-china-export-092214888.html), [Techzine](https://www.techzine.eu/news/infrastructure/142291/u-s-concerned-about-possible-asml-euv-leak-to-china/).

## 4. Earnings transcript highlights (Q3 2026 call, 2026-10-14)

The full transcript was paywalled (Benzinga 403). These points come from the MarketBeat call summary ([link](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/transcript-what-asml-asml-said-on-its-q3-earnings-call-2026-10-14/)), which paraphrases management rather than quoting it, plus press coverage:

## 5. DIFF: what changed since the prior briefing / my mental model

1. **No new company guidance.** The 2026-11-13 "guidance lift" headline is a recap of the 2026-10-14 guide (refuted; Section 2).
2. **Affiliates Rule suspension was extended to 2027-01-10.** It did not snap back on 2026-11-10, so the China-customer overhang is deferred another two months.
3. **MATCH Act is still the single largest policy threat.** Unchanged in status as far as I could verify, but the Dutch government is now reported to be lobbying against it.
4. **New to this file, though not new in time: the S.D.N.Y. securities class action (24-cv-08664) has survived a motion to dismiss and is in discovery.** My mental model, and the 2026-05-18 R4 rationale ("export control scrutiny … no enforcement"), did not include it.
5. **Consensus moved up sharply after Q3:** FY27 EPS +10.5% in 90 days, with zero cuts. My model had the beat but not the size of the revisions.
6. **Relative strength improved.** ASML has held up better than SMH through the October–November chip selloff, over 6 months and since the print. The 2026-05-18 rationale for M2 = 2 (−34pp vs SOXX) no longer describes the tape.
7. **China is about 20% of FY26 sales** (CFO), against 23% of Q3 system sales. Management expects China demand to hold in 2027.
8. **Pricing power:** price increases are expected to contribute from 2027, which strengthens the D2/D3 evidence.
