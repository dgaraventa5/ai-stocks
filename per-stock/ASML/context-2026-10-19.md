## 4. What changed vs the prior briefing (2026-06-22)

1. **Scale.** The FY26 guide went from €36–40B (Q1) to €44–45B now. Q3 revenue was +55% YoY (weekly scan 2026-10-17, vs €7.52B in Q3 2025).
2. **Demand now exceeds planned supply through 2028.** The Q1 language was "demand outpacing supply", tied to 2027. The constraint now explicitly extends to 2028, and customers are asking to order for 2029–30. This is the strongest visibility statement ASML has made in this cycle.
3. **China rebounded as a share of sales.** It fell from ~36% (Q4 2025) to ~19% (Q1 2026) and 14% (Q2), then rose back to 23% in Q3. The quarter was DUV-heavy (ArFi units 23 → 40). The prior briefing treated China's step-down as a done deal. That is not confirmed: a re-acceleration raises export-control exposure again.
4. **EUV share fell** (57% → 49%) even as EUV units edged up. Growth this quarter came from DUV immersion and installed-base upgrades, not EUV.
5. **Capital return.** ~€4.1B of buybacks in Q3, and an interim dividend of €1.88 payable 2026-11-04 (Ex. 99.1).
6. **TSMC partnership on 12-inch photomasks for High-NA** (announced 2026-09-07; Ex. 99.2 slide 6). The target is a pilot line by 2031, so it supports the long-run High-NA roadmap but is not a near-term revenue item.
