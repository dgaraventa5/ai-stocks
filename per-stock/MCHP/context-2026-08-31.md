## 1. Pre-research mental model

Microchip is the 8/16/32-bit MCU plus mixed-signal franchise serving a very long tail of industrial and automotive customers, distinguished by a direct-distribution model and historically high gross margins. My model had MCHP as the *worst-positioned* name in the analog cohort: the deepest and longest inventory correction of the group, revenue down ~40% peak-to-trough, factory under-utilization crushing gross margin, a levered balance sheet from the Microsemi era, and a dividend under strain. AI exposure: essentially none in my model.
