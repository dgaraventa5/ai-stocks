Duke is a fully regulated utility (DEC/DEP in the Carolinas, plus Florida, Indiana, Ohio, Kentucky) with one of the strongest Southeast datacenter pipelines — the Carolinas host major Apple/Google/Meta campuses historically, and I recalled a multi-GW datacenter ESA pipeline being built up through 2025 alongside a large (~$85-90B) five-year capital plan and 5-7% EPS growth guidance. NC rate case pending (filed 2025-11-20), a proposed mandatory ≥50 MW large-load tariff, and allowed ROE the cap on upside.

## 5. DIFF vs prior mental model (2026-06-09)

- **Rate-case ask REDUCED, not just contested.** June briefing had Duke asking 10.95% ROE with the AG contesting ~$1.4B. Since then Duke cut the overall residential ask from ~18% to ~11% and lowered its ROE request to **10.48%**. The contested band is now explicit: Duke 10.48% / Public Staff 9.45% / AG 7.4%. Net: the *upside cap* (allowed ROE) is being negotiated **down** from the June assumption, and the outcome lands by a hard Sept 20, 2026 deadline. This is the single most decision-relevant change.
- **Partial settlement reached (July 2/6 8-K) — but it de-risks the small stuff, not the swing variables.** Winter-storm-Fern costs, solar tax-credit use, some O&M/riders settled; ROE, capital structure, and the entire Multi-Year Rate Plan capital program remain litigated. So the $103B plan's regulated-return recovery is still open at the commission. ~$10M one-time Q2 charge (immaterial).
- **Large-load tariff terms are now concrete** (≥50 MW @80% LF or ≥100 MW; ~decade min-bill; 25% exit penalty; upfront security) vs the June "min monthly bill at ≥80% of requested load, exit fees" sketch. Still a proposal, still ratepayer-protection/capex-de-risking (not margin capture), and now drawing "light-touch/insufficient" criticism from intervenors.
- **SMR concretized to a site**: early site permit at Belews Creek, GE Hitachi vs Holtec, early-2030s COD — vs June's generic "nuclear/SMR interest." Optionality, not near-term earnings.
- **Data-center GW, capital plan, Q1 numbers, and guidance are all UNCHANGED** from the June briefing — no new quarter, no new signed-ESA figure. The thesis engine (contracted load → rate base → capped ROE, revenue starting H2 2027) is intact.

## 6. Implications for ratings (research only — no numbers pre-decided)

- **D1 (AI-revenue % today):** No change. Still no disclosed data-center revenue share; contracted ESA revenue ≈ $0 today (first power H2 2027). The exposure remains forward rate-base growth, not current mix. No new evidence to move this dimension.
- **D2 (regulated return capture vs pricing power):** Mild NEGATIVE pressure to examine. The mechanism is unchanged (growth flows through rate base at allowed ROE, no premium pricing), but the *level* of the cap is being negotiated down — Duke itself cut its ROE ask to 10.48%, with Public Staff (9.45%) and the AG (7.4%) below that. A lower allowed ROE trims the return on the $103B plan. The large-load tariff still de-risks capex rather than adding margin. Consider whether D2 already reflects "capped, low-variance return" or needs to register the downward ROE drift.
- **D5 (named hyperscaler commitments / contracted GW):** No change. GW figures (7.6 executed / 7.8 late-stage) unchanged; still no Duke-attributed hyperscaler name. Terms disclosed in aggregate only.
- **M (Momentum):** Q1 beat + reaffirmed guidance are supportive but already known; refresh 50DMA% mechanically per rule 11. No qualitative catalyst change.
- **R4 (regulatory risk) — the dimension most in play this cycle.** Active, contested NC rate case with a hard Sept 20, 2026 decision, a below-ask ROE range on the table, unsettled MYRP capital program, and intervenor pushback on both the rate hike and the large-load tariff design. The partial settlement removes some litigation risk (storm/solar) but leaves the return-determining items open. This is a live regulatory-outcome overhang, not a thesis break — worth reflecting in R4 rather than assuming the June posture.
- **R3 (balance sheet):** No new data; $103B plan implies continued heavy external financing (equity + debt) — no change signal this pass, refresh objectively per rule 9 if/when Q2 lands.

**THESIS-BREAK signal: none.** The engine (contracted large-load → rate-base growth → 5–7% EPS, first revenue H2 2027) is intact and reaffirmed. The rate case is a *magnitude-of-return* question (how high the allowed ROE, whether the full MYRP capex is approved), not an existence-of-thesis question. Watch the Sept 20 NCUC decision as the next real swing.
