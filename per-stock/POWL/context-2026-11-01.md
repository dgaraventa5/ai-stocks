## 1. Pre-research mental model

Formed from the prior briefing and the assignment before reading the new filings; written into this file afterwards.

Powell makes custom-engineered medium-voltage switchgear and power-control buildings, with oil and gas as the heritage market and data centers the fast-growing slice inside "commercial and other industrial". I believed data centers were carrying the story, and that D1 = 4 (25-50% of revenue) was plausible but unproven because the company has no AI line. I believed the $400M+ mega order was probably hyperscaler-linked, which is what D5 = 4 implies. I expected net cash, a gross margin plateau near 30%, and a stock drop tied to some company-specific bad news, most likely a margin or order problem at FQ3.

## 3. WebSearch findings

1. **No company-specific cause for the fall was found.** The stock went from a $328 52-week high to $156.26 on 2026-10-30. What the evidence shows:
   - Two straight EPS misses (table in Section 2), and revenue short of estimates: $311.7M vs $318.3M per [Zacks](https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/2967743/powell-industries-q3-earnings-revenues-miss-estimates).
   - [Trefis](https://www.trefis.com/data/companies/powl) attributes the drop from $286.23 (June 30) to $197.84 (Oct 5) mostly to a lower P/E, not earnings.
   - A sector-wide leg down: [TIKR](https://www.tikr.com/blog/ge-vernova-sinks-11-vertiv-14-bloom-energy-15-in-a-week-as-ai-power-stocks-slide) reports GEV −11%, VRT −14%, BE −15% in a week in late October.
   - POWL fell 10.9% midday on 2026-10-22 with no stated cause ([MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/price-powell-industries-nasdaq-powl-stock-price-down-109-heres-what-happened-2026-10-22/)) and 7.25% on 2026-10-30, again with none ([AAII](https://www.aaii.com/investingideas/article/550288-why-powell-industries-inc8217s-powl-stock-is-down-571)).
   - JPMorgan cut its target from $300 to $250 on 2026-10-13, keeping Overweight, with no reason in the coverage ([Ad-Hoc News](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/nebenwerte/powell-industries-stock-jpmorgan-cuts-target-to-usd-250/70431337)).
   - Linking these to the fall is my **inference**, not a sourced fact. The stock remains above its $101.39 September 2025 level (Trefis).

2. **The $400M+ order is a neocloud campus, not a hyperscaler order.** In the FQ2 call the CEO said of this award, "It is in the NeoCloud space as well" ([Motley Fool transcript, 2026-05-05](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/05/powell-powl-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)). All of it is outside the data-center walls, for the first phase of a planned multi-phase greenfield campus. The customer is not named, and no hyperscaler is named anywhere I found. The same call described two channels: an indirect OEM and partner model, and direct talks with the contractor or end client. It did not say which applied here.

3. **Data center share of backlog was low twenties before the big order.** The CFO said data center was "in the low twenties" at 2026-03-31, and the CEO described a path of 7%, then 15%, then low twenties (same transcript). Data-center revenue was about 14% as of January 2026, per an AI-generated [Quartr summary](https://quartr.com/events/powell-industries-inc-powl-15th-annual-midwest-ideas-investor-conference_ofpzplOB) of a conference talk. **Treat that 14% as low confidence.**

4. **Competitors are adding capacity.** [Siemens and Jabil](https://m4snews.com/article/siemens-jabil-partnership-targets-medium-voltage-switchgear-supply-gap-with-300-) are building a 300,000 sq ft US plant for medium-voltage switchgear. Eaton is reported to be adding US plants ([Switchgear Magazine](https://switchgear-magazine.com/tag/switchgear/); [EnkiAI roundup](https://enkiai.com/data-center/hitachi-switchgear-manufacturing-us/)). Lead times remain long at about 52-80 weeks for 15kV gear, per Terrapin data cited by [Industrial Sage](https://www.industrialsage.com/switchgear-lead-times-2026/). These are trade and market-research sources, and no Schneider or ABB medium-voltage data turned up.

5. **Customer concentration: secondary source only.** A [Stock Titan summary](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/POWL/page-4.html) of the FY2025 10-K says no customer exceeded 10% of revenue, with oil and gas 37%, utility 25%, commercial and other industrial 16%. I could not confirm this in the filing itself.

6. **Litigation web check.** Two searches found no securities class action, docket number or plaintiff-firm press release for POWL in 2026. Search absence is weak evidence, especially with 2d skipped.

## 4. Earnings transcript highlights

Source: [Motley Fool FQ3 2026 transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/10/powell-industries-powl-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/), call held 2026-08-04, read through a summarizing fetch rather than verbatim. A second copy (Nasdaq highlights) timed out. The transcript has known errors, including a "29.6%" year-to-date margin that matches the 10-Q but conflicts with how it is phrased in the call.

- **Growth:** the CFO said double-digit revenue growth is unlikely given backlog conversion rates. Management expects another strong year in FY2027 but gave no numeric guidance. This is the most likely stock-relevant comment: $934M of orders with single-digit-to-low-double-digit near-term growth.
- **The big order:** Phase 1 of a behind-the-meter "power island", spread across at least five North American facilities, burning over about 2 to 2.5 years. Future phases depend on execution.
- **Pipeline:** the CEO said little is in the near-term pipeline at $400M+ scale, though $100M+ commercial projects are getting more frequent.
- **Margins:** project closeouts added about 100bps year to date, down from about 130bps. Inflation in metals and engineered components is moderate, partly offset by hedging and pricing.
- **Pricing:** delivery speed drives value in commercial work; industrial markets are more price-sensitive.
- **Capacity:** Jacintoport (335,000 sq ft) is nearly done and initially serves LNG; well over $100M of annual revenue when full. A roughly 300,000 sq ft leased plant is due late FQ2 or early FQ3 2027. Footprint grows more than 20% by end of FY2026. The owned greenfield plant ($70-100M) is **still undecided**, "in the near future". Quarterly capex was $6.5M.
- **Labor:** craft labor is tightening and could become a challenge in FY2027-28.
- **M&A:** pipeline healthy, valuations rich.

A single unnamed neocloud campus now is about 17% of backlog ($400M / $2.4B) and its counterparty credit is unknown. That is the risk the old thesis did not contain.

**Data center backlog estimate (my math, not disclosed):** about 22% × $1.8B = about $400M at March, plus >$400M in FQ3 = about $800M+, or **about 33-36% of the $2.4B** after some burn and other wins. The 40% commercial and other industrial bucket caps it.

**Forward revenue estimate (my math):** $400M / 2-2.5 years = $160-200M a year, or about 14-17% of TTM revenue of about $1.16B (859.5 + about 298 for FQ4 2025; the FQ4 figure is from rounded data).
