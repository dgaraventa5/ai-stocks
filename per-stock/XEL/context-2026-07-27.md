## 1. Pre-research mental model (verbatim from reasoning before pulling new data)

XEL is a fully regulated multi-state utility (NSP-Minnesota, PSCo Colorado, SPS Texas/NM, NSP-Wisconsin) whose AI angle is a well-structured but forward-dated data-center-load story: >2 GW contracted/under construction, a 6-GW-by-2027 contracting target, a $60B five-year capital plan (+33%), one named hyperscaler (Google Pine Island MN, 15-yr ESA where Google funds all incremental cost), and GE Vernova / NextEra supply-side alliances.
