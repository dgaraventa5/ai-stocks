EBITDA +20% YoY; 2026 guide reaffirmed $6.8–7.6B), with the 5,500 MW Cogentrix gas acquisition still pending H2 2026 close and both Meta PPAs and Cogentrix excluded from guidance. Management is notably more measured than consensus on load growth (Burke: ERCOT 5–6%/yr through 2030). Pricing power evidenced by cap-clearing capacity prices and analyst-estimated ~2x-merchant PPA pricing ($85–115/MWh).
