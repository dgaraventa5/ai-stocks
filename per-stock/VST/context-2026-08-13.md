## 4. Implications for ratings (dimensions to examine — NOT changed here)

- **D1 (AI revenue %)** — no change in realized AI revenue this quarter; still ≈0 from PPAs. Helix is a capital commitment, not revenue. No mechanical case to move D1 yet.
- **D5 (strength of AI-buildout tie, rule 23)** — the strongest candidate to move. Vistra is now a named founding investor and *preferred power partner* to a >$10B NVIDIA/KKR-backed hyperscaler-infrastructure vehicle, on top of ~3,809 MW of signed hyperscaler nuclear PPAs. Argues for the top of the supplier scale at the next rating review.
- **D2 / D3 (position / moat)** — Helix plus the FERC co-location order marginally strengthen the "bottleneck owner with a preferred channel" read.
- **M1 (EPS revisions)** — **negative pressure**: 2027 trending to the low end of $7.4–7.8B is the kind of statement that pulls sell-side numbers down, independent of the in-line Q2. Watch the revision tape into the next review.
- **R4 (regulatory)** — two-sided this quarter: FERC co-location order favorable; the **ERCOT data-center queue audit** is a new, genuinely uncertain regulatory/market item that could delay the very demand the thesis rests on. Worth an explicit watch-item rather than a rating move on one quarter.
- **R3 (balance sheet)** — unaffected; >$10B available cash through 2027, two-agency IG, liquidity $6.3B.
