## 6. Implications for ratings (flags only, not decided here)

- **D1 vs D5 split (requested test, Layer-1 directness lens):** the two ratings cannot both be right under the Layers 1–8 bands. A literal D5 = 5 needs multiple hyperscalers above 30% of revenue combined, which would force D1 ≥ 4. Estimating at full delivery:
  - Meta: 2,176 MW operating capacity × 8,760 h × ~92% capacity factor ≈ 17.5 TWh/yr. At an undisclosed price, an assumed $70–90/MWh gives ~$1.2–1.6B/yr from 2028.
  - AWS: 1,200 MW ≈ 9.7 TWh/yr, ~$0.7–0.9B at full ramp (2032).
  - Microsoft/Pulaski: 405 MW solar ≈ $50M.
  - Against a 2025 revenue of $17.7B (yfinance), rising with Cogentrix, that is **~5–8% in 2028 and under 1% delivered in 2026**.
  - D1 = 2 (5–10%) is a forward-contracted reading that is defensible. D5's literal supplier band fits **3** ("selling to hyperscalers but not concentrated, 5–15% total").
  - The same construct question applies to CEG (D5 5), NEE (5) and TLN (4). Rule 12's absolute-lens test says to change these as a **Layer-1 cohort pass**, not one name at a time.
