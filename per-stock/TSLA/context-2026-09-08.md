## 1. Pre-research mental model (Step 1, quoted verbatim)

> TSLA pre-research mental model (2026-09-08, from training): Tesla's investable AI angle is FSD (Full Self-Driving)/autonomy, the robotaxi program (launched in Austin ~mid-2025 as a small geofenced supervised pilot, with Elon promising unsupervised/scaling), Optimus humanoid robot (Gen 2/Gen 3, targeting limited internal production), and the Dojo/Cortex AI training compute buildout. The core business is still auto — deliveries were roughly flat-to-down in 2024–2025 (~1.8M units) with margin compression from price cuts and rising competition (BYD, Chinese OEMs), and energy storage (Megapack) was the fastest-growing, highest-incremental-margin segment. FSD revenue is recognized partly upfront and partly deferred; there's a large deferred-revenue balance released as features ship. My most recent clearly-reported quarter is roughly Q4 2025 / Q1 2026; I'm uncertain about robotaxi scaling progress, Optimus production numbers, the status of Dojo (there were reports Tesla might wind down the Dojo custom-chip effort in favor of Nvidia/AI5), and whether auto deliveries reaccelerated in 2026.

**Composition / quality facts:**
- **Revenue +25.5% YoY but operating income −57% and EPS −3%.** The headline growth number is real but the profitability underneath deteriorated sharply. GAAP **gross margin 16.8%** ($4,751M/$28,236M) — down from ~21.1% in Q1'26 and 17.2% in Q2'25. **Operating margin just 1.4%.**
- **Cause is deliberate AI spend + the credit cliff:** opex +47% (R&D +49% to $2,371M) as management states it is "investing heavily in research and development to accelerate our AI, software and fleet-based profits... which will negatively impact our profitability during this phase." ## 5. DIFF — what changed vs my pre-research mental model (and vs the June briefing)

1. **Profitability collapsed while revenue grew.** Q2 op income −57% YoY, op margin 1.4%, adj EPS missed by −33% — explicitly because Tesla is spending AI/Optimus/robotaxi money ahead of revenue *by management's own statement*. My model had "mature but profitable auto"; the reported reality is a company deliberately trading near-term margin for AI optionality. **Q2 FCF went negative (−$1.1B)** and 2026 capex guided **>$25B, AI-driven** — a materially bigger AI-infrastructure commitment than I (or the June briefing) carried.
2. **Robotaxi is further along than my model** — 7 metros, 1M cumulative unsupervised miles, Cybercab now in the public fleet (Sep 4) — **but scale is still ~25 cars** and there is a **new NHTSA Audit Query into Cybercab FMVSS certification** plus a contested "9x-worse crash rate" narrative. Progress + overhang both increased.
3. **Dojo is dead (confirmed, absent from the 10-Q); the compute story is Cortex (Nvidia H100s) + AI5 inference silicon + a $25B SpaceX/Intel "Terafab."** My model still hedged on Dojo — it's resolved: cancelled, and the AI6 architecture converges training/inference.
4. **Tesla is moving from AI *consumer* toward AI *hardware vendor*** — the "Megapod" modular AI-datacenter product (Electrek, June 2026) and in-house semiconductor/solar fabrication are new supply-side angles relevant to D2/D5 (was purely demand-side in June).
5. **A securities-fraud class action naming Musk over Autopilot/FSD/Robotaxi claims is now live and disclosed**, and the **Benavides ~$329M Autopilot verdict** stands on appeal — the litigation backdrop to the autonomy thesis is heavier than my model held.
6. **The +25% revenue "acceleration" is partly an easy comp** (trough Q2'25); underlying US auto demand is soft post-EV-credit — so the strong top-line does not, by itself, signal auto re-acceleration.
