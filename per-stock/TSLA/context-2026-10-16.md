## 5. DIFF — what changed

1. **Last night's event was a car-and-SpaceX showcase, not an AI disclosure.** The Roadster has no FSD, ships in 2028 at the earliest, and its novelty is SpaceX propulsion. It feeds the SpaceX-merger narrative, which is a valuation and structure question, not AI revenue. **It does not move any AI-thesis dimension.**
2. **Robotaxi scale changed by an order of magnitude since September:** ~25 cars then, ~300 Cybercabs plus ~400 Model Ys registered in Austin now. This is the material AI development, and it happened over the last 30 days, not last night.
3. **Optimus may finally be ramping** (several hundred a week, reported), but it is unconfirmed and Musk himself calls the units data-collection, not productive.
4. **The capex is now debt-backstopped.** $30B of committed credit is a new fact for R3; Tesla remains net cash (~$43.5B cash and investments at Q2) and has drawn nothing.
5. **Q3 deliveries ~flat YoY** against the pull-forward comp, so the auto business is not re-accelerating.
