## 2. Guidance

**Q4 FY2026** (ending 2026-11-01), from Ex-99.1 "Fourth Quarter Fiscal Year 2026 Business Outlook":
- Revenue **~$34.8B**, **+93% YoY** — a *further acceleration* from Q3's +86%.
- Non-GAAP operating income **~66% of revenue** (flat vs the year-ago 66%).
- **AI semiconductor revenue ~$21.7B, +236% YoY** (Tan, call). Implies AI ≈ 62% of Q4 revenue.
- Gross margin guided to **~73%**, down from 78% a year ago, attributed on the call to "increasing mix of XPUs with their increasing memory content."

**Full-year FY2026 AI revenue ~$58B, +186% YoY — raised from the prior ~$56B guide.**

**Multi-year (call commentary, not a filed number):** "we have secured the supply to again double AI revenue to approximately **$115 billion**" in FY2027, with a further doubling to **~$230B** indicated for FY2028.

**Named XPU programs disclosed on the call** (six XPU customers total, four named):
- **Google** — Ironwood TPU v7 shipping in high volume; **TPU v8i in production**; "multi-tens of billions of dollars of TPUs annually over the next several years."
- **Anthropic** — 1 GW of Ironwood in 2026; **5 GW of TPU v8i in 2027; 10 GW in 2028.**
- **OpenAI** — Jalapeño shipping; **1.3 GW in 2027, >5 GW of Jalapeño + successors in 2028.**
- **Meta** — three MTIA generations through 2027; **3 GW visibility through 2028**; production shipments begin in Q4.

**Other changes vs the prior model:**
- **The >50%-of-revenue AI threshold event flagged in `context-2026-08-17.md` §6 has now fired:** AI semis are **56%** of Q3 revenue and guided to ~62% in Q4. AVGO is now, on the numbers, an AI-accelerator company with a software annuity attached.
- **Reported leverage improved, not worsened.** Cash $23,975M (vs $16,178M at FYE25); long-term debt $57,167M (vs $61,984M); short-term debt $2,252M. Net debt ≈ $35.4B against annualising EBITDA well north of $80B. The *on-balance-sheet* picture is stronger than the sheet's stale 1.08× ND/EBITDA implies. The R3 question is entirely about what is **off** the balance sheet.
- **Margin mix is now an explicit headwind, and management said so.** Gross margin steps down 75% → ~73% on XPU memory content while operating margin holds at 66%. Custom-ASIC revenue is structurally lower-margin than the legacy franchise; the mix shift is the price of the ramp.
