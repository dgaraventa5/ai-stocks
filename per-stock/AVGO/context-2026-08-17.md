## 1. Pre-research mental model (verbatim, written before any data pull)

> "Broadcom is the custom-ASIC (XPU) arm of the hyperscaler build — Google TPU is the anchor program, Meta MTIA second, with newer wins that management refers to only as 'customers' rather than by name. AI semiconductor revenue has been compounding at triple digits and is on a path to be the majority of semi revenue. The other half of the company is VMware/infrastructure software, a high-margin subscription conversion that has largely run its course as a growth driver and is now the deceleration risk. Legacy non-AI semis (wireless/Apple, broadband, storage) are a cyclical drag that has been bottoming. Balance sheet still carries meaningful VMware acquisition debt. My prior: Q2 FY26 revenue ~$20B, AI semi revenue ~$9-11B, FCF margin north of 40%, and the key uncertainty being how many XPU customers are real programs versus design engagements."

## 6. Implications for ratings (flag only — no ratings decided here)

- **D1 (AI revenue %)** — AI semi crossing >50% of quarterly revenue in Q3 FY26 guidance is a threshold event for this dimension. Revisit.
- **D2 (supply-chain position) / D3 (moat)** — six custom customers including Anthropic and OpenAI strengthens the "the labs come to Broadcom to escape NVIDIA" position. But rule 23 flags D2↔D3 correlation at +0.73; resist moving both on the same evidence.
- **D5 (AI-buildout tie)** — reinforced; >$100B FY27 AI guidance is about as quantified a tie as this dimension gets.
- **R1 (customer concentration)** — cuts both ways this quarter: the Apple extension diversifies *away* from AI concentration, while six XPU customers still means a handful of buyers dominate the growth. Worth an explicit re-argument rather than a nudge.
- **R3 (balance sheet)** — continued note tender offers; check net debt/EBITDA at the Q3 print.
- **R5** — AVGO is not Layer 10; rule 16 default of 5 stands.
- **Watch item for Q3 (2026-09-02):** infrastructure-software growth rate, and whether receivables growth (+62% vs +24% revenue) normalizes.

**Thesis-break signals: none.** The software softness is a real negative and was punished, but it is a mix issue inside a business whose AI half is guided to triple.
