## 1. Pre-research mental model (verbatim)

SO is the dominant Southeast regulated utility holdco (Georgia Power, Alabama Power, Mississippi Power, Southern Company Gas, plus mostly-contracted Southern Power). Georgia Power sits on one of the largest datacenter load pipelines in the country. As of Q1 2026 (reported 2026-04-30): adjusted EPS $1.32 (+$0.09 YoY, $0.12 above estimate), revenue $8.4B (+8%); datacenter power consumption +42% YoY; **>11 GW contracted large load across 28 projects** (18 broken ground, 10 pending), 6 GW more in final stages, >75 GW prospective pipeline. Capex plan stepped up to ~$78–81B for 2026–2030 (discrepancy flagged last session). DOE loan $26.5B at Treasury +37.5bps. Georgia regulatory core: retail ROE set point 10.50% (band 9.50–11.90%), 56% equity, base rates frozen through 2028; Dec 2025 PSC certified ~9.9 GW new generation (~80% for data centers, ~$16–19.5B). Shareholders backstop the new generation through 2031 if DC load under-materializes; upside above the ROE band is 80% given away. Vogtle 3&4 (~2.2 GW nuclear) in commercial operation since 2023/2024. Thesis = forward rate-base growth at a capped allowed return, not merchant pricing.
