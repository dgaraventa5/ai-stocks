## 1. Pre-research mental model (written BEFORE searching)

German. Two halves: Industrial Trucks & Services (Linde Material Handling, STILL, Baoli
forklifts — ~70% of revenue, cyclical, European-industrial multiple) and Supply Chain
Solutions (Dematic, acquired 2016 — warehouse automation integration). Dematic has been the
problem child: margin disappointments, project-execution issues, order volatility
post-COVID. ~EUR 11.5B revenue. Weichai Power (Chinese state-linked) is the largest
shareholder at ~45% of votes. Announced a partnership with NVIDIA and Accenture (GTC 2025)
on Omniverse/Mega warehouse digital twins. Leverage is meaningful (industrial + financial
services book).

**What I expected to be stale:** the FY2026 segment rename to Intelligent Automation
Solutions, H1 2026 numbers, order trends, restructuring programs, and any 2026 NVIDIA
follow-through.

## 5. Data gaps and flags (rule 3)

- **Geographic revenue split not verified this session.** My web-search budget was exhausted
  before I could retrieve KION's regional revenue table, and both the Q1 2026 press release
  page and the H1 2026 trade coverage failed to return the data (one returned HTTP 403).
  **The R2 rationale below therefore names a shock but estimates the % exposed** — the
  rubric requires a filing-sourced %, so R2 should be treated as provisional and confirmed
  from the FY2025 annual report or the Q2 2026 interim report.
- **Leverage definition.** The "0.6x" is KION's industrial net-financial-debt measure. KION
  also carries a leasing/financial-services book and material pension obligations that this
  metric excludes; an all-in adjusted leverage could be materially higher. Flagged; do not
  read 0.6x as the whole picture.
- **Legal proceedings not read** — R4 is a default-clean rating with low-medium confidence.
- **Not all of IAS is "robotics."** Dematic sells conveyors, sorters, AS/RS, software and
  AGVs; robotics proper is a subset. Treating the full 29.5% as the robotics numerator is
  the generous reading, and I have flagged it in the D1 rationale.

## 6. Implications for ratings

- **The cheapest way into the segment, and for a reason.** Forward P/E 9.3x at a 52-week low
  with IAS growing 23% is genuinely interesting; but IAS orders fell 40% and group orders
  20%, and 71% of the company is a cyclical European forklift business whose order value
  also fell 6%. The multiple is discounting a real deceleration, not just sentiment.
- **The NVIDIA/physical-AI content is the best in the bucket on a technology basis** — live
  Jetson-based autonomous trucks in a GXO warehouse, Omniverse/Mega digital twins, an AI
  Control Tower — but it is a *capability* story, not a revenue line. Under the rule-23
  parallel lens, D5 should credit the tie without pretending KION sells into the AI-
  infrastructure buildout.
- **D4 is where honesty costs the most.** KION is in cost-out and order-selectivity mode with
  a shrinking forward book and a small bolt-on. That is not a capacity expansion.
- **Governance is a real, unscored-elsewhere feature.** A 46.5% Chinese anchor shareholder
  with board seats sits behind a business selling warehouse automation into US customers at
  the exact moment Commerce is running a Section 232 robotics investigation. It belongs in
  the narrative and colours R2/R4 even if no single rubric band captures it cleanly.
