## 1. Pre-research mental model

ST is a European broad-line analog/MCU/power maker, heavily automotive (including silicon carbide for EVs) and industrial, with an internal fab base that becomes a fixed-cost problem in a downcycle. My model had ST deep in a severe trough — revenue down sharply, gross margin compressed into the mid-30s, restructuring underway to resize the manufacturing footprint, and essentially no AI/data-center story. I viewed it as the weakest of the Western analog cohort on margin structure.

## 6. Implications for ratings (flag only — do not pre-decide)

- **D1 (AI revenue %)** — a stated path to >$1B (2026) and >$2B (2027) of data-center revenue on ~$14B of revenue is material. Revisit; this is the priority dimension on this name.
- **D5 (AI-buildout tie)** — check whether the $2B 2027 figure rests on *contracted engagements* or on pipeline. Rule-27 precedent caps deck-only claims; apply the same discipline here.
- **D2 / D3** — gross margin at 34.8% is evidence *against* a strong pricing or moat position, and cuts the other way from the revenue recovery. Weigh both.
- **⚠️ R4 (regulatory/legal)** — two pending Exchange Act class actions (1:24-cv-06384 and 1:24-cv-06370, S.D.N.Y.) were not in view at last rating. Revisit, reading the 20-F legal-proceedings disclosure first.

- **R2 (geography)** — mild *relative* positive versus US peers on the China action.
- **M1 / M2** — beat with a raised sequential outlook, but the stock sold off on it. Mixed; do not read the print alone.
