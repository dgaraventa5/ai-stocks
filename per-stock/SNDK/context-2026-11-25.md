## 1. What the 10-Q says (quarter ended 2026-10-02, filed 2026-10-30, acc. 0001628280-26-068601)

**Growth is price, not volume** (MD&A, Net Revenue):
- Total revenue +378% YoY ($11,031M vs $2,308M); total exabytes sold up "almost 10%".
- Datacenter: revenue +1,685% ($4,802M, 43.5% of revenue); exabytes up almost 330%, revenue per GB up almost 320%. The only segment with real volume growth.
- Edge: revenue +320% ($5,832M); revenue per GB up more than 450%; exabytes **down 20%**.
- Consumer: revenue −39% ($397M); exabytes down more than 70%, revenue per GB up more than 110%.
- Gross margin up ~5,500bps YoY, "primarily due to a higher revenue per gigabyte and an increase in exabytes sold."

**Contract cover** (Note 4, Revenue):
- Remaining performance obligations $88.9B (FY26 year-end: $59.8B), entirely NBMs; ~20% (~$17.8B) to be recognized in the next 12 months.
- Q2 FY27 guide midpoint $12.4B annualizes to ~$49.6B, so contracted revenue covers roughly 36% of the next year (rough: the guide is one quarter, the RPO schedule is not quarterly).
- NBM pricing has "fixed and variable components" (MD&A). The 10-Q does not disclose floor levels relative to current prices. **This is the open question on the name.**
- Collateral held by third-party institutions for customers: $12.8B. Contract liabilities $1.5B; refund liabilities (security deposits) $1.6B.

**Customers** (Note 3): "two separate customers accounted for more than 10% of the Company's net revenue, amounting to 12% each" (prior-year quarter: none). Unnamed. Customer types listed include cloud service providers. The 10-K showed none above 10% for FY24–FY26.
