## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before pulling new data)

> SNDK is a NAND pure play sitting at a pricing peak. FY26 revenue was $20.25B, with Q4 at $8.97B and an 84.6% gross margin. Datacenter revenue grew 437%, driven by enterprise QLC for Stargate. It holds about $42B of contracted backlog under NBM agreements, and the Kioxia JV runs to 2034. FQ1'27 guidance is $10.3-10.8B. My belief is that datacenter is perhaps 25-40% of revenue, and that the main risk is the cycle turning as JV capex rises about 40%.

### 2b. FCF conversion: clean on the ratio, but two input flags
TTM OCF $11.67B, capex $0.18B, FCF $11.49B, NI $11.43B gives **1.01x**. FY26 1.01, FY25 0.07, FY24 0.71 (orchestrator yfinance pull). AR is +340% YoY against revenue +372%, and inventory growth is far below revenue growth, so the working-capital condition does not hold. **Not flagged** under the 2b definition. However:

- **⚠ Capex understates economic capex (flagging per orchestrator request).** The yfinance $0.18B matches the 10-K's "Purchases of property, plant and equipment (177)" exactly, so the number is correctly sourced. It is *not* the economic investment in NAND capacity. Fab equipment sits in the Kioxia Flash Ventures JV. Sandisk funds 49.9–50% of JV capital investment through JV-level lease financings, and pays for it through depreciation reimbursement and lease payments embedded in **wafer cost (COGS)**, plus loans and equity (10-K Item 1 "Flash Ventures"). FY26 cash also included **$275M net issuances to Flash Ventures** (10-K Item 7, Investing Activities). FY27 Flash Ventures commitments are **$2,627M** (10-K Material Cash Requirements). Reported JV capex is ~$4.5B, +~40% YoY ([TrendForce, 2026-06-01](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/06/01/news-japan-u-s-nand-alliance-steps-up-investment-as-kioxia-sandisk-capex-reportedly-rises-40-yoy/)), which implies a Sandisk share of roughly $2.2B. Management guides capex at ~6% of revenue for FY27 ([Blocks & Files](https://www.blocksandfiles.com/flash/2026/08/06/sandisk-maketh-mucha-moolah/5284021)), which is ~$2.5B+ on a ~$42B run-rate. Net effect: **statement FCF (OCF − PP&E) structurally overstates economic FCF** relative to vertically integrated memory peers (MU, Samsung, SK hynix), flattering FCF margin and FCF yield. Because the JV costs run through COGS, the overstatement is partly self-correcting (OCF is already net of the depreciation reimbursement). The size of the residual gap is not quantified here. This is a cross-name comparability issue, not an accounting concern.
- **⚠ OCF includes customer deposits.** NBM prepayments ($1,242M contract liabilities) and security deposits ($1,500M refund liabilities) of **~$2.7B** are inside FY26 OCF (10-K Item 7, Liquidity). The company's own Adjusted FCF strips NBM prepayments and deposits: "reduced Adjusted free cash flow by $2,476 million in FY26" (EX-99.1). The deposits are refundable and must be performed against. GAAP FCF is therefore flattered by ~$2.5B relative to the company's own adjusted measure.
