## Implications for ratings (flags only — no rating numbers decided here)

- **D1 (AI-Revenue directness):** Strengthened further. Two record quarters with data-center-driven Electrification orders (+58% YoY Q2, triple-digit DC growth) plus the Infinitus/NVIDIA DC-architecture launch make AI/data-center a *named, accelerating primary driver*. Whoever rates next should weigh whether a 4 still captures this vs. diffuse exposure — but apply Layer-2 per-name discipline (MEMORY: feedback_per_name_research), not sector pattern-match.
- **D2 / D3 (supply-chain position / moat):** The **NVIDIA 800VDC collaboration** and first-mover source-to-rack DC portfolio (solid-state transformers) suggest ABB is moving up the value chain into differentiated grid-stability + DC-power architecture, not just MV/LV commodity gear. Fresh look warranted on Position and Moat.
- **D4 (capacity):** Already 5; capacity capex ($200M EU MV manufacturing, $110M US) and sustained record backlog (book-to-bill >1 for multiple quarters) reinforce, nothing lowers it.
- **D5 (hyperscaler / AI-buildout tie):** Deepened — explicit NVIDIA collaboration and hyperscale DC order strength. Re-examine whether the tie is now strong enough to move above 4.
- **Momentum (M1 EPS-Rev / M2 RelStr / M3 Insider / 50DMA%):** Two guidance raises + BofA PT increase + accelerating buybacks are momentum-positive; refresh the objective `scripts/momentum_50dma.py ABBNY` and re-examine M1 (EPS revisions clearly upward). M2 already 5. M3 (insider) unchanged by news; foreign filer — no SEC Form 4 (flagged default per rule 27 convention).
- **Growth / Value (objective, not this pass):** Comparable revenue guide raised to low-double-digit/low-teens; Q2 rev +12%. Q1 EPS carried a real-estate gain — per rule 15, if a future EPS-YoY input is dominated by that non-operating gain, blank it (cite the item). Flag for the objective refresh.
- **R4 (regulatory risk):** The **Robotics→SoftBank close (EU/China/US approvals, mid-to-late 2026)** remains the near-term execution/regulatory watch item — not a thesis-break, but a deal-break or forced remedies would be material and the ~$4.8B proceeds are now earmarked to fund Rotork. **New R-watch:** Rotork ($5.5B) itself carries UK/antitrust deal-close and integration risk (largest acquisition in ABB history). Neither yet changes the balance sheet materially (~$5.8B cash on hand).
- **R5 (disruption risk):** Layer 02 physical infrastructure — **defaults to 5** (rule 16); AI is a tailwind not a threat. Nothing here changes that.
- **Structural-change note:** Post-Robotics-close the group is Electrification + Motion + Automation (Rotork-enlarged), more AI/power-weighted. Log the Rotork acquisition and Infinitus launch to news-log.md.
