Rotork adds flow-control breadth to Automation (post-2027 close).
- **AI-Thesis D5 (AI-buildout tie):** Confirmed strong — supplier tie to AI power buildout is explicit and quantified.
- **Momentum:** Mixed near-term, structurally positive. Positives: record orders, second guidance raise, BofA PT bump (Sep 2026), EPS-revisions likely up. Offsets: shares **slipped ~2.8% on the Q2 print** and margin optics softened. **Refresh objective 50DMA%** (`scripts/momentum_50dma.py ABBNY`) before setting Momentum; re-examine EPS-revisions / relative-strength subjective inputs.

If a future EPS-YoY input spans the gain quarter, check whether it is non-operating-dominated and blank per rule 15 (cite the item); record any ruling in `eps-yoy-overrides.json`.

- **Risk R4 (regulatory / execution):** Two-sided. **De-risked:** Robotics-sale EU clearance secured (China/US still pending — residual deal risk if remedies/blocks emerge). **Added:** Rotork = new M&A execution/integration risk and a large cash outlay (biggest-ever deal, close H1 2027).
