## 1. Pre-research mental model (written BEFORE searching)

What I believed walking in:
- ISRG is a ~20-year effective monopoly in soft-tissue robotic surgery. Revenue ~$9–10B, growing high-teens, gross margin ~67%, net cash, no debt.
- Razor-and-blade: instruments & accessories (~60% of revenue) compound off a growing installed base; procedure growth mid-teens is the real driver, not system placements.
- **Competition has repeatedly failed to arrive.** Medtronic's Hugo slipped for years; J&J's Verb/Ottava was serially delayed; CMR is sub-scale. My prior was that this pattern would continue.
- Moat = 5 in my head: surgeon training, hospital capital sunk cost, clinical evidence, instrument lock-in.
- I assumed the stock was strong (a perennial compounder), and that the AI angle was "millions of hours of surgical video → future autonomy," i.e. optionality rather than shipped product.
- I had no view on China as a competitive risk. I assumed China was a growth market for ISRG.

**Every one of the last three bullets turned out to be wrong or materially stale.**

Tariffs (10-Q, "Trade and Tariffs Update"):

### FY2026 guidance (8-K EX-99.1, 2026-07-16)
- Worldwide da Vinci procedure growth **13.5%–15.5%**, and "**expects to be closer to the midpoint**" — i.e. maintained, not raised, with a soft qualifier.
- Non-GAAP gross margin **68.0%–69.0%** (raised; includes ~1.0% of revenue tariff drag).
- Non-GAAP opex growth 11%–13%.

### Competition — from ISRG's own filing (10-Q MD&A, "System Demand")
ISRG now names as competitors: **Beijing Surgerii, CMR Surgical, Distalmotion, Harbin Sizhe Rui, Johnson & Johnson, Karl Storz, Medicaroid, meerecompany, Medtronic, Noah Medical, Shandong Weigao, Shanghai MicroPort MedBot, Shenzhen Edge Medical, and SS Innovations International.** Two of those are names in this same Layer-11 batch.

**China, verbatim substance (10-Q MD&A):** "system demand in China has been adversely affected by increasing competition from domestic robotic-assisted surgical system manufacturers as well as a broader central government focus on systematic governance… In the second quarter of 2026, the competitive dynamics in China, various measures related to industrial policy, and the effects of this campaign contributed to **fewer systems being placed in China than we anticipated**."

### New risk factor added this quarter (10-Q Item 1A)
The only updated risk factor is about **third-party remanufactured instruments and unauthorized service** — i.e. the razor-blade annuity being attacked at the consumable layer. That is a new disclosure, not boilerplate carried forward.

### Legal (10-Q Note 8 "Contingencies")
- **In Re: da Vinci Surgical Robot Antitrust Litigation** (N.D. Cal., Larkin/Franciscan): **class certified 2026-03-31; trial set for 2027-09-14.** In the 2024-03-31 summary-judgment order the Court ruled the robot and EndoWrist instruments are separate antitrust markets, that an aftermarket for EndoWrist repair/replacement exists, and that ISRG holds **monopoly power in that aftermarket** — portions of which were vacated on reconsideration 2024-07-30. ISRG "unable to make a reasonable estimate of loss."
- **Surgical Instrument Service (SIS)**: ISRG won at trial 2025-01-28 on all antitrust claims; SIS appealed; **9th Circuit oral argument held 2026-06-25** — decision pending.
- **Restore Robotics Repairs** (N.D. Fla.): dismissed in ISRG's favor 2025-11-07; on appeal to the 11th Circuit, reply brief filed 2026-07-06.
- Ongoing individual product-liability docket; "reasonable possibility of a loss in excess of the amount recognized," not estimable.

### Other filings
- 8-K filed 2026-07-27 (period 2026-07-23): bylaw amendments only (universal-proxy mechanics, majority-of-votes-cast standard). **Not material.**

## 5. Rating implications

- **D2 Supply Chain Position: 4, not 5.** Three authorized Western competitors plus a Chinese incumbent that now outsells ISRG in China; ASP +1.7% and a deliberate price-concession program. "Limited substitutes / demonstrable pricing power" no longer holds cleanly.
- **D3 Moat: 4, not 5.** Bias check applied: I would have said 5 twelve months ago and the stock was higher then. The evidence forcing 4 is dated and specific — Ottava authorization, China share loss, the aftermarket antitrust ruling, and a new remanufacturing risk factor. Switching costs, 11,710 installed systems and clinical evidence still make 4 (not 3) the right level.
- **D5: 4 on the proposed L11 lens** — ISRG has the only genuinely shipping AI product line in this cohort (Case Insights / My Intuitive Plus) plus the deepest proprietary surgical-data asset. It is still AI-*adoption*, not AI-infrastructure, so it caps below 5.
- **M2: 2.** Underperforming its own sector ETF by ~15 points over 12 months. Don't fight the read.
- **M3: 3.** No buying at all, but all selling is 10b5-1/tax-withholding — the rubric's default.
- **R4: 2.** A certified antitrust class with a trial date and an unestimable loss is material litigation by the rubric's own words.
- **R2: 3.** Two legs — China policy/competition overhang currently suppressing placements, plus manufacturing asset concentration (Mexicali instruments, German endoscopes, China sub-assemblies) with live tariff and rare-earth-export exposure.
- **Rule-14 / rule-23 note:** ISRG is *no longer* the crowded-consensus profile the roster flagged. After a 29% drawdown to a 52-week low with a maintained-not-raised guide, the P4 "dominant crowded AI winner" skepticism applies less than it did; the offsetting concern is that a value-quality screen will now *reward* the falling multiple. R5 does not apply (non-Layer-10 → 5), which is exactly the asymmetry rule 16 describes — worth watching if the competitive erosion continues.
- **Refresh trigger:** ISRG already reported (2026-07-16) and this briefing covers it; objective inputs should be refreshed in the same pass per rule 9. Next catalysts: 9th Cir. SIS ruling (argued 2026-06-25), Hugo general-surgery/gyn clearance, first Ottava commercial placements, and Q3 print (~2026-10-20).
