## 1. Pre-research mental model (before this pass)

Carrying into this session, from training priors plus the two robotics research deliverables:

- Chinese lidar #1 by volume; the only lidar maker at scale that makes money.
- Permanently impaired by the DoD Section 1260H "Chinese Military Company" listing — a reputational overhang with a lawsuit attached that Hesai was probably going to lose.
- Robotics units exploding (+138% YoY) but ASPs collapsing; revenue growth (+30%) far behind unit growth (+141%).
- **Valuation: genuinely unknown.** Two of our own research passes disagreed by 8x — the machine-vision pass called it "extreme… ≈47x sales" off a $21.2B yfinance cap; the defense pass used $2.64B and computed P/E ~38. I did not know which was right, and treated *both* as untrustworthy.
- Assumed a Cayman/**VIE** structure with HFCAA delisting risk (the defense pass asserted "Cayman/VIE, HFCAA").
- Assumed live IP litigation with Ouster.

Three of those five priors turn out to be wrong or materially incomplete.

## 4. DIFF — where fresh reality departs from the prior model

### 4a. ★ THE MARKET-CAP CONFLICT IS RESOLVED — it is a share-subdivision artifact, and the correct cap is ~$2.6B

**Mechanism, from primary filings:**
- 6-K 2026-07-10 (acc 0001104659-26-082435, ex-99.1): **"SHARE SUBDIVISION BECOMING EFFECTIVE ON JULY 10, 2026."** HK board lot changed from **20 existing Class B shares → 100 subdivided shares**, with a temporary counter trading in lots of **160 subdivided shares (in the form of Existing Share Certificates)**. 160 ÷ 20 = **subdivision ratio 1:8**.
- **F-6 POS filed 2026-07-10** (Reg. No. 333-269524): "…make a ratio change to the American Depositary Share so that **the new ratio shall be one (1) American Depositary Share to eight (8) Class B ordinary shares**," plus Amendment No. 1 to the Deposit Agreement dated 2026-07-10.
- Prior ratio was **1 ADS = 1 Class B ordinary share** (6-K 2026-05-20 ex-99.1 definitions: *"ADS(s) — American Depositary Share(s), each representing one Class B Ordinary Share"*; same filing prices HK$158.5/share = US$20.025/ADS on 2026-05-19/20 — a 1:1 tie confirmed by FX).

**So:** the ADS is economically unchanged and the Nasdaq price did not split, but the ordinary-share count went up 8x. Share count at 2026-06-30 (pre-subdivision, HKEX Monthly Return in 6-K acc 0001104659-26-080965): 26,998,861 Class A + 130,143,350 Class B = **157,142,211**. Post-subdivision ≈ **1.257 billion ordinary shares**.

**yfinance's ~$21.2B = post-subdivision ordinary-share count × the unchanged ADS price.** This is the exact MVIS failure mode flagged in `research/2026-08-02-machine-vision-deep-pass.md` §2 (post-split shares × pre-split price), applied to HSAI.

**Four independent confirmations of the true cap:** stockanalysis $2.64B / 156.44M sh (7/31); companiesmarketcap $2.57B; HK line HKD 18.55B ≈ $2.37B; and the defense pass's own arithmetic (P/E 38 × net income $68M = $2.58B).

> **RULE-3 DATA FLAG FOR THE SCORING PASS — HARD BLOCK.** Do **not** let yfinance supply HSAI's market cap, enterprise value, or any cap-derived ratio (P/S, EV/EBITDA, FCF-yield, reverse-DCF EV/FCF, or any P1 cohort percentile that consumes them). Every one of them is **~8x wrong**. Use: market cap **$2.64B**, shares **156.44M ADS-equivalents**, **EV $1.93B**, **P/S 5.73**, **EV/Sales 4.19**, **EV/EBITDA 67.41**, **P/B 2.04** (stockanalysis, 2026-07-31). Re-verify after 2026-08-18. Consider adding HSAI to a split-artifact denylist alongside MVIS.

**Consequence for the thesis:** the machine-vision pass's headline objection — *"Valuation is extreme… ≈47x sales… for a company earning RMB 18M in the quarter"* — **is an artifact and should be struck.** At 5.7x sales, 38x trailing earnings, and 4.2x EV/sales with **40% of the market cap in cash**, HSAI is the *cheapest* profitable name in the entire perception bucket (vs OUST ~14x sales, AEVA ~56x, RoboSense ~4.8x). That is a material upgrade to the setup, not a downgrade.
