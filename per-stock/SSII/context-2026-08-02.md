## 1. Pre-research mental model (written BEFORE searching)

- SSi Mantra is an India-built, India-sold surgical robot priced at a fraction of da Vinci, founded by Dr. Sudhir Srivastava (a cardiac surgeon who was an early da Vinci adopter).
- I believed the whole thesis was a **binary FDA 510(k) decision expected during 2026** — clearance would be a step-function repricing.
- I assumed it was a small, fast-growing, loss-making company; India revenue concentration obvious.
- I assumed — following the roster note — that as an India-operating company it was a **foreign private issuer with no Form 4 data** and no US insider signal.
- I had no view on its balance sheet, its financing structure, or its governance beyond a generic "US-listed small cap operating in India deserves scrutiny."
- I did not know it was Nasdaq-listed vs. OTC, and I did not know its history.

**Three of these turned out to be wrong.**

## 5. Rating implications

- **R3 = 1.** The rubric's band-1 text is "Stressed… Dilution risk. Going concern flags." SSII discloses **explicit substantial-doubt going-concern language**, dependence on related-party funding, a $28M affiliate convertible struck at $1.38 (vs $3.98 today), a $50M ATM, a $150M shelf and a 30M-share plan. This is not a judgment call.
- **R2 = 2.** Named worst plausible shock: any India-specific demand, currency or policy shock impairs **~99% of revenue** (10-Q), and **100% of production assets are in the same country**. Both revenue and asset legs sit in one geography, with US and EU market access still gated by regulators. Not a 1 only because India is not an export-control/conflict geography and the impairment is not currently underway.
- **R4 = 3.** No disclosed material litigation. The FDA 510(k) is properly a *gate on the opportunity*, not a threat to existing revenue (India CDSCO approval is in hand). But the regulatory posture is genuinely unusual — a first-time multi-specialty soft-tissue 510(k) **including cardiac**, from an India-manufactured device, by a company with a reverse-merger history and no US quality-system track record — and the timeline has already slipped once. 3 rather than 4 reflects that.
- **M3 = 4, flagged DEBATABLE.** A literal application of the rubric's step 1 ("≥2 insiders made open-market discretionary buys in the last 12 months → 5, full stop") gives **5**: three insiders, $5.197M, one buy well over $1M. I propose **4** for two documented reasons: (i) these were **private-placement subscriptions**, not open-market purchases — economically discretionary but not the same information event the rubric's evidence base (Lakonishok-Lee, Jeng-Metrick-Zeckhauser) is built on; and (ii) the CEO's holding vehicle was **simultaneously tendering shares to retire personal notes**, which is the opposite of the "I believe it's undervalued" demonstration the rubric asks for. If Dom prefers the literal reading, 5 is defensible and I will not defend 4 on process grounds — I'd rather surface the divergence than split the difference silently.
- **M1 = 3 by default, flagged (rule 3):** there is **no published consensus EPS or estimate-revision data** for SSII. This is an absence of information, not a neutral read.
- **M2 = 1.** −41.6% over 12 months vs IHI −14.1% — lags by ~28 points.
- **D-dimensions:** D1 = 5 (100% robotics, filing-confirmed). D2 = 2 and D3 = 2 — a low-cost downstream OEM with a real cost position and genuine telesurgery differentiation, but no bottleneck, no IP moat against cheaper Chinese entrants, and it is now explicitly named as a competitor in ISRG's 10-Q rather than the other way round. D4 = 4 (18 systems sold in one quarter, 103 installs in 2025, +119%; funded — but funded by dilution under going-concern doubt, which caps it below 5). D5 = 2 on the L11 lens: telesurgery at record distance is a real capability, but the "AI- and XR-driven" language is a deck claim with nothing quantified in the financials.
- **Liquidity flag for sizing:** observed turnover of ~$132K on 2026-07-31 is below the bar the roster used to *exclude* HSYDF. Verify average daily dollar volume before this name is treated as investable at any size.
- **Rule-9:** provisional pending the **2026-08-06** Q2 print, which is also the first report from the incoming CFO (start date 2026-08-03).
