## 4. DIFF vs prior mental model

| Dimension | 2026-06-12 model | 2026-08-24 reality |
|---|---|---|
| PROPWR contracted MW | ~240 MW | **~350 MW**, guided to **~450 MW near term**; >100 MW in advanced negotiation |
| Hyperscaler DC | 60 MW signed, being commissioned | **Deployed and operating at scale, providing prime power** |
| Cash | $156.6M | **$783.958M** |
| Debt/funding | $112.0M debt; $163.4M equity raise | **+$690M 0%-coupon converts due 2031** (net ~$668.5M) **+ $164.3M equity**; $723.1M net financing inflow in 1H26 |
| Consolidated result | Q1 net loss $(3.6)M | **Q2 net loss $(8.1)M** — wider, even as revenue grew 13% |
| Adjusted EBITDA | $36M (13% of rev) | **$45M (15% of rev), +23% sequential** |
| Multiple | P/S 1.52, 96.4th pctile (flag fired) | **P/S ~1.15**, price $10.85 vs Exxon's $16.66 May exit |

**The synthesis that matters:** the June briefing's judgment was that PUMP's multiple was an option
premium on a power business that hadn't executed. Two months later the option is meaningfully more
in-the-money — MW contracted up ~46%, the hyperscaler site actually running, and roughly $854M
raised to build the rest — **while the stock fell.** The premium the flag objected to has largely
deflated. What has *not* changed is that PROPWR still loses money at the segment level and the
consolidated loss widened; the legacy frac business is still the revenue base and still off-thesis.
The name has moved from "priced for execution that hasn't happened" toward "execution starting,
priced less for it" — which is a different and more interesting setup than in June.

## 5. Implications for ratings (flag only — NOT pre-decided)

- **D1 (AI revenue %)** — still the weak link. PROPWR revenue remains a small fraction of a ~$306M
  quarter, and much of the new 110 MW is **oil-and-gas and industrial**, not data center. Contracted
  MW growing is not the same as AI revenue growing; do not let the MW headline carry D1.
- **D2 (supply-chain position)** — mild upward pressure: being among the first behind-the-meter
  providers actually delivering prime power to a hyperscaler site is a real position marker, and the
  Caterpillar framework secures turbine supply. Counter: it is a single-OEM dependency.
- **D5 (AI-buildout tie)** — upward pressure. In June the tie was one signed contract; now it is a
  running deployment plus several hundred MW in advanced data-center discussions.
- **R3 (balance-sheet risk)** — clear upward (better) pressure: $784M cash, 0% coupon, 2031 maturity,
  capped calls limiting dilution. Offsetting consideration: the converts are still a claim, and the
  buildout will consume the cash.
- **R1 (customer concentration)** — worth re-examining. FY25 top-5 was 68.2% with ExxonMobil 24.9%,
  and Exxon has since exited the equity entirely. The new PROPWR contracts add different
  counterparties, which cuts both ways.
- **Rule-9 objective refresh: DUE.**
- **Rule-14 expectations flag: re-run.** The June flag fired at P/S 1.52 / 96.4th percentile; P/S is
  now ~1.15 with revenue growing sequentially, so the reading that drove the June framing is stale.
