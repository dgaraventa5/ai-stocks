## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before searching)

AeroVironment is the scaled, listed incumbent in small unmanned aircraft and loitering munitions — Switchblade, Puma, JUMP-20 — that bought BlueHalo in 2025 to add space, cyber and directed energy. My prior: closest public analogue to Anduril, ~85–90% of revenue autonomy-adjacent, FY26 revenue ~$2.0B with +30% organic growth and record $2.7B bookings, but a violent de-rating (−64%) triggered by an FY27 guide of only +7–11% with flat EPS. The last two priors were wrong.

## 4. DIFF — pre-research model vs. fresh reality

**① A securities class action over the Space Force SCAR program was filed 2026-05-26 — not in the roster research.** It names the sitting CEO and the former CFO and alleges materially misleading statements across a nine-month class period. That is a band-2 R4 event on its own, and it is a *disclosure-quality* signal at a company that just absorbed a large private acquisition.

**② BlueHalo carried material weaknesses in internal control over financial reporting** and the 10-K discloses remediation as an open obligation. For a name whose GAAP is already unreadable because of merger accounting, an unremediated ICFR weakness is a real caveat on every reported number.

**③ The balance sheet is more levered than "scaled profitable incumbent" implies.** $729M long-term debt against $377M cash (net debt ~$352M) — comfortable at ~1.1x FY27 guided EBITDA, but **capex is guided to jump from ~5% to 12–14% of revenue**, so FCF deteriorates in FY27 before it improves. This is not a net-cash story like KTOS.

**④ Arlington Capital Partners can "significantly influence all matters submitted to stockholders."** A dominant, non-strategic PE holder post-merger is a governance overhang that the roster did not capture.

**⑤ The de-rating has an explicit, management-stated cause, and it is timing.** The FY27 guide assumes **budget money does not reach AVAV programs until spring 2027**. So the FY27 ~$75B drone/autonomy line is, for AVAV, an **FY2028 revenue event**. That is the honest framing of the "underwrite the deceleration, not the past" instruction: the question is not whether the budget is real but whether one is paid to wait four quarters at 46x forward with flat EPS.

**⑥ Analyst behaviour has capitulated on price, not on rating.** BofA halving its PT ($450→$225) while maintaining Buy, alongside a Raymond James upgrade in the same week, is the signature of a group resetting to a lower multiple rather than abandoning the thesis.

## 5. Rating implications — proposed 13 subjective ratings

### AI Thesis (D1–D5)

**D1. AI/Robotics Revenue % — 5**
- *Rationale:* The Autonomous Systems franchise — Switchblade loitering munitions, Puma/JUMP-20 uncrewed aircraft, counter-UAS, and the new P550 eVTOL — is the core of the business; the Space, Cyber & Directed Energy segment (ex-BlueHalo) is the non-robotics remainder. **Estimated ~70–90% uncrewed/autonomy-tied — comfortably above the 50% band-5 threshold on any reasonable split.** AVAV does **not** publish an "autonomy revenue" line, so this is an estimate and is flagged.
- *Source:* FY2026 10-K segment descriptions (AS and SCDE); FY2026 results release.
- *Confidence:* Medium (estimate; the AS/SCDE revenue split was not extracted this session).

**D2. Supply Chain Position — 4**
- *Rationale:* AVAV is the scaled incumbent — the Switchblade family and Puma have installed bases, airworthiness qualification and sustainment contracts that a new entrant must re-earn program by program, and $1.2B of *funded* backlog is evidence of committed demand. Not 5: it is a systems integrator rather than an upstream bottleneck, it buys its components (from suppliers like UMAC), and loitering munitions is now the most contested category in defense.
- *Source:* FY2026 10-K; FY2026 bookings/backlog release.
- *Confidence:* Medium.

**D3. Moat Strength — 4**
- *Rationale:* Strong in two dimensions: DoD program qualification / Blue-UAS listing (a multi-year barrier) and 40 years of small-UAS flight and safety data plus training-and-sustainment entanglement with the user units. Not 5 — the FY27 deceleration to ~+10% *against an exploding budget* is itself evidence that pricing power is not translating, and the private cohort (Anduril at $61B) is attacking exactly this category.
- *Source:* FY2026 10-K; FY2027 guidance.
- *Confidence:* Medium. *Bias check:* the −64% drawdown did not move this rating; the FY27 guide did.

**D4. Capacity Expansion — 5**
- *Rationale:* Capex guided from ~5% to **12–14% of revenue in FY27** — a deliberate, disclosed, funded step-change built ahead of the budget flow, backed by $2.7B of FY26 bookings, 1.4 book-to-bill and $1.2B funded backlog. This is a committed multi-year program, not an announcement.
- *Source:* FY2027 guidance (8-K 2026-06-29); [TradingView/Quartr summary](https://www.tradingview.com/news/urn:summary_document_slides:quartr.com:3724183:0-avav-fy2027-guidance-targets-10-revenue-growth-backed-by-strong-backlog-and-major-contract-wins/).
- *Confidence:* High.

**D5. AI-buildout tie — 4 · L11 lens (proposed)**
- *Rationale:* Very strong contracted tie in absolute terms — $2.7B bookings, $1.2B funded backlog, book-to-bill 1.4, and a fresh **$117.3M Army P550 award**. Held at 4 rather than 5 because **management explicitly guided that FY27 budget money will not fund its programs until spring 2027**, i.e. the $75B FY27 drone/autonomy line has *not yet* converted into AVAV orders — the tie is to the prior budget cycle.
- *Source:* FY2027 guidance commentary; [stockanalysis.com/stocks/avav](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/avav/) (P550 award).
- *Confidence:* Medium.

### Risk (R1–R5) — 5 = lowest risk

**R1. Customer Concentration — 1**
- *Rationale:* Applied mechanically to the disclosed figures: **the DoD alone accounted for ~63% of FY2026 revenue**, above the band-1 ">60% single customer" threshold, and **U.S. government sales in total (prime + sub + FMS) were ~85%**. This is the highest disclosed concentration in the Layer-11 defense cohort.
- *Source:* FY2026 10-K, Item 1A ("We rely heavily on sales to certain customers, including the U.S. government, particularly to agencies of the DoD"), verbatim quoted in §2.
- *Confidence:* High.

**R2. Geographic / Export Risk — 4**
- *Rationale (mandatory named shock + % exposed):* Worst plausible shock is an **ITAR/export-licence restriction or an abrupt curtailment of European/Ukraine-theatre loitering-munition demand**, which is where the non-USG order flow has been concentrated. Exposed revenue: **≤15%** — the residual after the 85% US-government figure (note FMS is inside that 85%). Assets are US plus one facility in **Stuttgart, Germany** — not a risk geography. No China revenue, no BIS/entity-list impairment. One partial demand leg.
- *Source:* FY2026 10-K Item 1A (85% USG figure), Item 2 Properties, and the export-control risk factor (*"failure to comply with export laws could result in fines, export restrictions and other sanctions and penalties"*).
- *Confidence:* Medium.
- **DEBATABLE:** the ≤15% foreign exposure is **below** the rubric's 25% band-4 shipment threshold, so a strict read gives **5**. I held 4 because **AVAV does not publish a country-level revenue breakout** — unlike KTOS, which explicitly states no single foreign country exceeds 10% of revenue — so I cannot rule out theatre concentration. If Dom prefers to score only what is disclosed, AVAV goes to 5.

## 6. Data flags for the scoring pass (rule 3)

1. **RULE 15 — BLANK AVAV EPS YoY.** TTM **GAAP net income is −$265.12M** against FY2026 **non-GAAP EPS of +$3.31**; the GAAP figure is dominated by **BlueHalo merger accounting** (purchase-price amortisation, transaction and integration charges) rather than operations. Growth should average the revenue metrics. Cite: FY2026 10-K; FY2026 results release (8-K 2026-06-29).
2. **Revenue growth is merger-inflated.** TTM revenue "+140.9%" reflects the BlueHalo consolidation; the comparable figure management gives is **+30% organic** for FY2026 and **~+10% guided for FY2027**. Do not score the +140.9%.
3. **BlueHalo ICFR material weaknesses are unremediated** per the FY2026 10-K — a standing caveat on reported financials.
4. **AS vs SCDE segment revenue split not extracted this session** — D1's 70–90% is an estimate. Pull the segment table at the Q1 FY2027 10-Q (~September) to firm it up.
5. **Arlington Capital Partners** holds influence over "all matters submitted to stockholders" (10-K Item 1A) — governance concentration not currently captured by any rating dimension.
6. **Timing, not thesis:** management states FY27 budget dollars reach AVAV programs only in **spring 2027**. Any model that credits the FY27 $75B line to AVAV's FY2027 revenue is double-counting a year.
