# ORCL (Oracle Corporation) — Context Briefing

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Purpose:** Rule-12 rating-research refresh (rotation). The prior briefing (`context-2026-06-22.md`) is nearly five months old, so this is a full pass. Research only — no rating, workbook, or holds file changed.
**Status:** not held; rank 31. Layer 09 (Compute-as-a-Service / Hyperscalers). Not in the rule-13 EV/MW cohort (hyperscaler).
**Current ratings (sheet, 2026-11-14):** D1 3, D2 4, D3 4, D4 5, D5 5; M1 3, M2 2, M3 3; R1 4, R2 4, R3 2, R4 4, R5 5. 50DMA % = 35.

## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before searching)

> ORCL is a Layer-9 AI-capacity landlord: OCI is growing >100% on the OpenAI/Stargate contract (~$300B over five years) plus Meta, xAI and others, and RPO was $664B at Q1 FY27 (8-K 2026-09-10, per the news log). Legacy database, applications and support are still roughly half of revenue and grow single-to-low-double digits. Funding is the central risk: FY26 FCF was −$23.7B, the $20B ATM was done in Q1 FY27, the Oct 28 Investor Day set FY30 targets ($225B revenue, FCF positive 2H FY28) and then on Nov 2–4 Oracle raised another $10B equity + $10B notes at 7–8.65% coupons — coupons that look close to high-yield, so I suspect at least one rating agency has cut it toward BBB-. The stock appears to have fallen hard (fwd P/E ~13, M2 already 2). The next print is Q2 FY27, likely mid-December 2026. Biggest unknowns: OpenAI's share of RPO and its ability to pay, the current credit ratings, whether any securities litigation is pending, and whether the Stargate sites are on schedule.

## 6. Implications for ratings (flags only — the rating session decides)

- **D1 (AI revenue %):** CPU+GPU infrastructure ~34% of Q1 revenue; GPU-only is undisclosed. Estimated GPU/AI share ~20–26% — straddles the 3/4 line. S&P projects IaaS ~60% of revenue by FY28, so D1 is likely to cross 25% during FY27. Re-check at the December print.
- **D2 / D3:** Supply is tight (97.9% utilization, +20% renewal pricing), but large AI deals are cost-plus at ~35% gross margin, which caps pricing power on the AI line. The database/apps moat is intact (cloud DB +26%). Neither looks to have moved a band.
- **D4 / D5:** FY27 capex $90–95B, 850 MW delivered in a quarter, signed multi-year contracts — top band on both. Site delays (NM, WI) are execution risk, not a funding gap.
- **M1:** FY27 consensus $8.05 → $8.15 over 90 days (+1.2%); FY28 $10.90 → $11.06; 30-day revisions on FY27: 6 up / 7 down. Mixed and stable.
- **M2:** −34.6% vs SPY +2.6% over 126 trading days (the benchmark basis in the prior audit row) = **−37pp**. Band-1 territory.
- **M3:** One director bought ~$3.5M in the open market (2026-09-29) — the rubric's lone-buyer path to 5 is a single buy >$1M. Ellison's plan cancellation supports it.
- **R1 (key flag):** On trailing revenue the rubric reads 5 (no customer ≥10% in FY26). On the forward book it does not: if ~half of $664B RPO is OpenAI and the ~$300B / ~$60B-a-year contract ramps from 2027, OpenAI would be roughly 27–35% of FY29–FY30 revenue ($170–225B) by my arithmetic. That is band 3 (25–40%). The counterparty is a cash-burning private lab, and Oracle has committed $288B of leases against this book. S&P names concentration as a downgrade driver.
- **R2:** Americas 71%, EMEA 19%, APAC 10%; minimal China; no asset leg. Under the 2026-06-12 revised bands no single shock plausibly impairs >10% of revenue — the bands read 5. The existing 4 predates the revision ("Global; minimal China").
- **R3:** The headline 3.8x (Watchlist) sits in band 3, but the holistic view is band 2: S&P adjusted leverage mid-4x, TTM FCF −$28.7B, $40B of new equity + debt in five months, BBB- one notch from junk, 7–8.65% coupons, $288B of leases still to commence. Not band 1: IG from all three agencies, $37B cash before the Nov raise, no going-concern language, management targets self-funding from FY29. Watch the S&P triggers (>4.5x sustained; no FOCF path by FY29) for a move to 1.
- **R4:** The securities class action in D. Del. was missed by the 2026-05-18 rating ("Routine"). A disclosed, pending securities case at motion-to-dismiss stage reads band 3 ("pending matters disclosed but immaterial" — Oracle's own view), not 4.
- **R5:** Layer-9 default 5. No change.

**Thesis-break watch (none triggered):** (1) any OpenAI payment renegotiation or failed funding; (2) S&P cut to junk, or any capital raise beyond the now-exhausted $20B cap; (3) Wisconsin/New Mexico slipping into FY28 revenue guidance; (4) a guidance cut at the Q2 print (~Dec 8–14); (5) the D. Del. motion-to-dismiss ruling (briefing done 2026-12-18).
