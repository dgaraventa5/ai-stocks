## 3. ⚠️ Rule-15 flag — the EPS "beat" is one-time-gain distorted (KEY QUALITY-OF-EARNINGS ISSUE)

The ~80% headline EPS "beat" was **driven overwhelmingly by a ~$2.6B non-operating gain on Salesforce's strategic investment stake in Anthropic**, not by core operations. This is the same category of distortion flagged (at smaller scale) in Q1 — where a ~$558M strategic-investment gain inflated GAAP NI but did *not* dominate (operations grew +21%, so Q1's EPS-YoY was kept). **Q2 is different in degree and likely in kind:** a ~$2.6B gain against a revenue beat of only ~0.3% and a non-GAAP-EPS "beat" of ~80% means the beat is **plausibly non-operating-dominated** — precisely the rule-15 condition for blanking the EPS-YoY Growth input.

**Two things must be resolved against the primary Q2 8-K/10-Q before scoring (rule 1, rule 3):**
1. **Is the ~$5.90 a GAAP or non-GAAP figure, and does it include the Anthropic gain?** Non-GAAP EPS normally *excludes* strategic-investment mark-to-market. An ~80% non-GAAP beat is implausibly large for CRM absent a one-time item leaking in, so either (a) $5.90 is GAAP/mixed and the true comparable non-GAAP operating EPS is near consensus, or (b) the gain was realized/reclassified in a way that hit the non-GAAP line. **Do not score EPS-YoY until this is pinned to the filing.**
2. **Rule-15 disposition (proposed, not finalized):** if the primary filing confirms the YoY EPS change is non-operating-dominated, **blank the EPS-YoY input** (Growth then averages the revenue metrics) and cite the Anthropic gain in the Rating Audit — the exact precedent from rule 15 (GEV Prolec, the negative-EBITDA-blanking family). The clean operating signal to lean on instead: revenue +11%, cRPO +14%.

## 4. R5 Disruption Risk (rule 16) — the crux, updated: tripwire NOT triggered; 3→4 conversation now open

The Q1 briefing left R5 at **lean-3** with a specific tripwire. Grading Q2 against it:

- **Tripwire = "subscription/cRPO YoY decel below high-single-digits WITH seat/NRR compression." → NOT triggered.** cRPO held at **+14% YoY** (no deceleration vs Q1's +14%); revenue still +11%; guidance was *raised* again. No visible per-seat erosion in the disclosed numbers → **R5 = 1 remains ruled out** (rule 16's "1" requires *visible active erosion*).
- **The consumption-monetization offset is not merely landing — it is accelerating.** Agentforce ARR growth *rose* (200% → 240%), and **AWU consumption +97% QoQ** is the first hard *usage* signal (not just bookings) that the agentic layer is being consumed at scale and monetized *on top of* the seat base. This is direct evidence for the durability case that argued 3 in Q1.
- **This opens a genuine R5 = 3 → 4 conversation (do NOT finalize — collaborative, rule 12).** Rule 16's "4" = "durable; AI net-neutral/tailwind or threatens only a peripheral line." The Q2 evidence — accelerating consumption ARR + AWU +97% QoQ + no seat erosion + a second guide raise — is the strongest case yet that agentic AI is a *net tailwind* CRM is capturing via consumption pricing, not a headwind eroding it.
  - **Case to HOLD at 3 (don't over-rotate on one quarter):** (a) **no NRR figure was disclosed** again — the single most important seat-durability metric is still dark; (b) the seat base (~$40B+) still dwarfs Agentforce (~$1.5B), so consumption is additive-but-small; (c) one quarter of accelerating consumption is not yet a durable pattern, and the Feb-2026 SaaS-seat-selloff thesis has not been *disproven*, only deferred. Rule 16's "3" ("contested but a structural moat resists core disruption") still fits cleanly.
  - **Recommendation:** hold **R5 = 3** for now, but log Q2 as **evidence pointing toward 4**, and set the next tripwire *in both directions*: a disclosed **NRR ≥ ~100% with continued Agentforce/AWU acceleration** would justify **3→4**; a disclosed **NRR < ~100% or cRPO decel to high-single-digits** would justify **3→2**. NRR disclosure is the swing datum — press CRM's IR / the 10-Q supplement for it.
