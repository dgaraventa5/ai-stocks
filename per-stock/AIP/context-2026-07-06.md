## 1. Pre-research mental model [verbatim]

Arteris is a pure-play semiconductor interconnect-IP licensor — its FlexNoC / FlexGen network-on-chip IP is the on-chip data-transport backbone inside SoCs, sold on a license + per-chip royalty model. The AI thesis is that rising chip complexity (chiplets, AI accelerators, more IP blocks per die) makes NoC a bottleneck Arteris automates, and FlexGen (AI-driven topology generation) is the growth product; key customers span automotive (Arm/RISC-V SoCs), edge AI, and now AMD AI chiplets (licensed Aug 2025). Most recent reported quarter per training: Q1 2026 (revenue ~$22.9M, +39% YoY, non-GAAP loss narrowing, positive-FCF full-year guide), with royalties as the long-term compounding lever. Latest roadmap milestone I recall: FlexGen smart NoC (launched Feb 2025) plus the Cycuity hardware-security acquisition (Jan 2026); it remains a cash-burning small-cap scaling toward FCF breakeven.
