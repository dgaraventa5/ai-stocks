## 1. Pre-research mental model (verbatim, written BEFORE searching)

> Rambus is a memory-interface-chip + IP-licensing company. It sells DDR5 RCD (registering clock drivers) and companion chips (SPD hub, temp sensor, PMIC) for server RDIMMs — one of three RCD suppliers alongside Montage and Renesas/IDT — with a high-margin patent-licensing royalty base underneath. Per my last refresh (June), the business is bigger than my original mental model: product revenue ~$88M/quarter, total ~$180M/quarter, market cap ~$17B, DDR5 RCD share mid-40% and holding. The AI thesis is more DRAM content per server + MRDIMM for bandwidth (~$600M SAM, ~20% target share, revenue 2027+) + HBM4E controller IP (fastest in market, 16 Gbps/pin, tied to hyperscaler custom silicon, 100+ HBM design wins). Most recent reported quarter = Q1 FY2026 (period ended 2026-03-31); next print due ~late July. Net-cash balance sheet (~$786M liquidity), no debt. Key customers = distributors servicing Samsung/SK Hynix/Micron module ecosystem, Korea/Singapore-weighted geography.
