## 1. Pre-research mental model (written BEFORE pulling data)

Astera Labs sells connectivity silicon for AI racks: Aries PCIe retimers (the original franchise and
the bulk of revenue), Taurus Ethernet signal conditioners, Leo CXL memory controllers, and Scorpio
fabric switches as the newer, higher-value product line. My model was that Aries retimers still
dominated the mix, that Scorpio was early and not yet a major revenue contributor, that the company
was extremely hyperscaler-concentrated, and that its strategic position depended on open standards
(PCIe/UALink) winning share against NVIDIA's proprietary NVLink — making NVIDIA both the ecosystem
it benefits from and its principal strategic threat.

## 6. Implications for ratings (flagging only — not pre-deciding)

- **D1 (AI-revenue %)** — effectively 100% AI-driven; unlikely to move.
- **D2 (supply-chain position)** — revisit. Moving from retimers (a component) to fabric switches
  (rack-scale infrastructure) is a move *up* the value stack, but into a market where Broadcom and
  now Marvell/Celestial AI compete directly.
- **D3 (moat)** — the most interesting dimension this pass. The NVLink Fusion collaboration cuts both
  ways: it de-risks the "NVIDIA crushes open standards" scenario, but it also means ALAB's position
  is increasingly *permissioned* by NVIDIA rather than independent of it. Worth a deliberate judgment
  rather than carrying the prior rating.
- **D5 (strength of AI-buildout tie)** — 3 hyperscaler design wins on X-Series, +2 starting late
  2026, 10+ customers engaged. Quantified and contracted-ish; supportive.
- **R1 (customer concentration)** — revisit. Improving (10+ engaged) but management explicitly
  declined to quantify, and "uneven demand patterns" is their own language.
- **Quality inputs / margin trajectory** — flag for the objective refresh: the ~72% Q3 gross-margin
  guide is a *structural* mix effect that will continue as switching scales. TTM margin will overstate
  the forward rate — the inverse of the usual rule-9 TTM problem.
- **Rule-9 objective refresh:** ALAB reported **2026-08-04 — within the one-week window, now due.**
  Revenue +104% YoY and Q3 guided ~32% above consensus clears the >15% bar → **priority-1
  (immediate)**, alongside GNRC.
- **Cross-name note:** rate ALAB and MRVL in the same sitting — they now compete in optical scale-up
  interconnect, and rating them apart risks inconsistent competitive assumptions.

**No thesis-break signals.** The gross-margin step-down is a mix effect that was guided and
explained, not a break.
