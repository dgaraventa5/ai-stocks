## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before searching)

> Astera Labs is a ~100% AI-driven connectivity silicon vendor (Aries PCIe/CXL retimers, Taurus Ethernet AECs, Leo CXL, Scorpio P/X fabric switches). Q2 2026 (reported 2026-08-04) was $392M revenue with a $540-560M Q3 guide as Scorpio X became the largest product family a quarter early, ramping into Amazon Trainium3 racks; gross margin stepping down toward ~72% on switch/module mix. Customers are highly concentrated (top-5 ~90%, Amazon ~29%+), and Amazon holds warrants. Strategically it is hedged across UALink (Amazon/AMD switches for 2027) and NVLink Fusion (custom switch with NVIDIA + a hyperscaler for 2027), with Broadcom (Tomahawk/PCIe switches) and Marvell as the main competitors. Insider selling has been steady (M3 = 2). Q3 2026 report expected 2026-11-03.
