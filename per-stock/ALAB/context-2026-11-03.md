## What changed since the last briefing (2026-10-27)

- **Scorpio became the largest product family**, led by volume production of the Scorpio X-Series 320-lane fabric switch. The 2026-10-27 briefing expected this; it is now confirmed in the release.
- **Revenue stepped up 49% QoQ**, and the Q4 guide implies a further ~26%.
- **New products:** Aries 7 (PCIe 7 copper and optical signal conditioning, sampling Q4 2026), Leo X-Series and Leo 2 memory controllers, and Orion, a rack-scale reference design for neoclouds and enterprises (through OEM/ODM partners from Q1 2027). Orion is a move toward system-level design, so it may put Astera nearer to its own customers' turf. Watch it.
- **Working capital ballooned.** AR and inventory together absorbed $347M in nine months, against $341M of operating cash flow. That fits a ramp, but it also matches the 2026-10-27 note (AR +131%, inventory +93% at Q2).
- **Price into the print:** closes $290.43 (10-29), $258.70 (10-30), $229.67 (11-02), $233.62 (11-03) — down about 20% in three sessions before the report, against SMH −7.7% over the same closes (561.67 → 518.52). **The after-hours reaction was not found.**
