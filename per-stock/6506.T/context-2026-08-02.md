## 1. Pre-research mental model (written BEFORE searching — honest)

Global #1 in AC servo motors and a top-four industrial robot maker (Motoman); ~¥550B revenue at roughly a 10% operating margin; more cyclical and structurally lower-margin than FANUC; exposed to China and to semiconductor capex. I expected a slow recovery under Chinese competitive pressure and no distinctive AI angle beyond marketing.

The AI angle turned out to be more concrete than I assumed, and — more importantly — **the segment polarity is the opposite of what "Yaskawa = robot company" implies**.

## 3. Web findings (fresh, this session)

1. **MOTOMAN NEXT is the only industrial robot line shipping with NVIDIA GPUs as standard equipment**, with 200+ units under deployment, and it now runs an agentic robot system powered by **Google DeepMind Gemini Robotics ER 1.6** — the robot assesses on-site conditions, devises the work procedure and executes autonomously. [Yaskawa news release](https://www.yaskawa-global.com/newsrelease/news/179679) · [Yaskawa product release](https://www.yaskawa-global.com/newsrelease/product/170859)
2. **Tokyo Robotics acquired** (announced ~Oct 2025; all shares acquired) — a 2015 Waseda University spinout building wheeled two-armed humanoids. Yaskawa's **first acquisition of a humanoid maker**. President Masahiro Ogawa: humanoid robots "will generate business value in niche areas in 2–3 years." [Tokyo Updates](https://www.tokyoupdates.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/en/post-1783/)
3. **Order geography has shifted away from China.** For AC servo semiconductor applications, the recovery is strongest in the **United States**, then Korea and Taiwan, then Japan; management explicitly called the **China outlook weaker**, with local Chinese semi-equipment projects only in the evaluation stage and volume expected only in H2 if equipment reaches mass production. [FY2025 Q&A (PDF)](https://www.yaskawa-global.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/254Q_QA_EN.pdf)
4. **Prior-quarter order momentum corroborates the demand signal:** Q4 FY2/26 orders surged 20% YoY / 10% QoQ on AI and semiconductor demand plus a large steel-plant project, ahead of the +29% Q1 print. [BigGo Finance FY2025 call](https://finance.biggo.com/news/JP_6506.T_2026-04-10)
5. **Sector context:** Fujitsu, FANUC, Kawasaki Heavy and Yaskawa have formed a joint NVIDIA physical-AI coalition explicitly to counter Chinese competition, against a backdrop of Chinese-made robots taking ~40% of China's domestic market by units. [Taipei Times](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/biz/archives/2026/07/17/2003860865)

## 4. DIFF

**vs. the universe appendix:** the appendix captured the setup correctly (servo orders +65% with Americas +117%, MOTOMAN NEXT shipping with NVIDIA GPUs, Tokyo Robotics acquired, FY2/27 guided +33% net income, robot segment OP −82%, "self-inflicted ERP disruption makes the turnaround unproven"). **The new information is the Q1 FY2/27 print itself (2026-07-10), which the appendix predates in substance:** revenue +10.6% but operating profit −19.2% and net −22%, with orders +29%. In other words the ERP damage **carried into the new fiscal year** while the order book kept accelerating, and the full-year +26.8% operating-profit guide was nonetheless held. Also new: the explicit statement that the China servo outlook is weaker while the US/Korea/Taiwan lead the recovery.

**vs. my pre-research model:** I did not appreciate that **motion control (servos) is the AI-datacentre-levered part of Yaskawa (+50% operating profit) while the robot segment is the drag (−82%)**. Anyone rating this as "a robot company with an AI story" has the causality backwards: the AI capex cycle is currently reaching Yaskawa through servos into semiconductor equipment, not through robot arms.
