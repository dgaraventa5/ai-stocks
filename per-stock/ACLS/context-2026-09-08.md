## 1. Pre-research mental model (verbatim, written before this pass's data pull)

> Axcelis is the ion-implant (Purion) niche leader — #2 overall behind Applied Materials but the clear leader (~70-80% share) in SiC/power implant. Demand is skewed to power (SiC for EV/industrial) and mature-node logic, NOT leading-edge; AI exposure is indirect via the memory/DRAM/HBM capacity channel plus a nascent SiC-for-AI-datacenter-power angle. As of my last briefing (2026-06-20) the standalone business was in a two-year cyclical trough (FY2025 rev −17.6% to $839M, FY2026 guided ~flat, recovery framed as 2027), the balance sheet was net-cash / effectively debt-free (~$570M cash+investments), China was ~42% of sales (a real export-control/concentration risk, sharpened by a Dec-2024 BIS ECCN aimed specifically at 300mm implanters), and the stock had re-rated ~+109% YTD to a 52-wk high (~$160) far above the ~$95 median PT — the expectations flag fired at the 99.9th percentile. The dominant, thesis-defining fact is the transformative all-stock merger with Veeco (ACLS holders 58%), expected to close H2 2026 with China SAMR the only remaining approval. I expect the merger to still be pending SAMR, the standalone business still near trough but possibly showing the first signs of the memory/2nm recovery, and I need to check whether the stock is still at its extreme valuation.

**Verdict on the mental model:** Business model, competitive position, balance sheet, and the merger-as-dominant-fact are all still correct. **What I underestimated / got wrong for the current setup:** (a) the recovery arrived **earlier than "2027"** — Q2 beat and a raised FY2026 growth guide; (b) I was unaware of the **CFO transition** (Coogan departed for another company's CFO seat, Ryzhik interim since March); (c) leading-edge/2nm logic is now a **real revenue channel with follow-on orders**, not the single tool it was in June; (d) the **valuation extreme has substantially unwound** — the flags are clean now.
