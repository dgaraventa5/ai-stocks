- **AWS's AI and Chips businesses are now large and separately trackable** ($25B+ run-rate each) — worth requesting these as explicit sub-metrics in future earnings refreshes rather than only the blended AWS segment number.
- **A second, narrower regulatory thread (the 8/31 ad-surcharge suit) is running in parallel with the older monopoly case** — these are two distinct pieces of litigation and should not be conflated in the rating rationale.
