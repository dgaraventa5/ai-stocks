## 1. Pre-research mental model

> Amazon's AI story is AWS — re-accelerating (I believe +37% in Q2 2026 per press coverage), with a large contracted backlog, sold-out Trainium custom chips, and a deep Anthropic tie that is partly circular (investee and customer). The tension is cash: ~$220B of 2026 capex against thin free cash flow, funded increasingly by debt. Reported EPS is inflated by Anthropic mark-up gains. The last quarter I've actually read in the filing is Q1 2026; Q2 is second-hand.

## 2. Latest filing highlights

Primary source: **10-Q for quarter ended 2026-06-30, filed 2026-07-31** (acc. 0001018724-26-000026), read directly. All figures from this filing unless noted.

**AWS**
- Net sales **$42,232M vs $30,873M = +36.8% YoY** (Note 8, Segment Information).
- Operating income **$16,621M vs $10,160M = +63.6%**; margin **39.4% vs 32.9%** (computed), +645bps.
- Quality caveat: AWS operating income includes **$551M of unrealized gains on long-dated energy contracts** marked as derivatives (Note 1, Derivatives and Hedging). Ex-gain margin ≈ 38.1%, +~515bps — CFO gave "520 basis points" on the call (Investing.com transcript). Still a large expansion; a year ago the worry was compression.
- AWS is 21% of consolidated sales but **61% of operating income** (computed from Note 8).

**Consolidated**
- Net sales **$200,606M, +19.6%**; operating income **$27,461M, +43.2%**; operating margin 13.7% vs 11.4% (income statement).
- Diluted EPS **$5.75 vs $1.68**. Other income was **$53,415M**, of which **$50,486M is upward adjustments on private-company equity, "primarily from our nonvoting preferred stock in Anthropic"** (Note 1). Six-month tax provision includes $15.9B of discrete tax expense on those adjustments (Note 7). **GAAP EPS YoY is non-operating-dominated** — rule 15.

**Backlog and the two AI-lab contracts (new vs June briefing)**
- Unrecognized performance obligations on >1-year contracts, "primarily related to AWS": **~$496B at 2026-06-30**, weighted-average remaining life **6.4 years** (Note 1, Unearned Revenue). Q1 was $364B (call), FY-end not re-checked here.
- **OpenAI:** Q1 2026 expansion of the existing $38.0B commitment "by $100.0 billion over 8.0 years" (Note 1).
- **Anthropic:** Q2 2026 expansion "by more than $100.0 billion over 10.0 years" (Note 1).
- Both "include contractual obligations related to the performance of AWS chips" — i.e. Amazon carries delivery/performance obligations on Trainium.

**Amazon is now a major shareholder in both of its largest AI customers (the June briefing knew only about Anthropic)**
- **OpenAI:** invested $15.0B (Q1) + $13.7B (Q2) = **$28.7B** in Series C Preferred; the remaining **$21.3B** was funded after quarter-end → **$50.0B total** (Note 2, Non-Marketable Investments).
- **Anthropic:** $8.0B convertible notes (2023–2025) + $5.0B Series G + $5.0B Series H preferred in Q2 ($10.0B in the quarter); plus a financing facility of up to $20.0B, $15.0B remaining, unlocked as compute-delivery milestones are met (Note 2).
- Balance-sheet carrying values at 6/30: Anthropic preferred **$92.5B** + Anthropic notes at fair value **$97.9B** (unrealized gain of $92.0B sitting in AOCI, not yet through earnings) + OpenAI $28.7B. Roughly **$219B** of Level-3 private AI stakes, ~$240B after the July OpenAI funding — about 9% of the $2.71T market cap (yfinance, 2026-10-05).

**Cash, capex, debt**
- Purchases of property and equipment **$54,208M in Q2 (+68% YoY)**, $98,411M H1, **$173,028M TTM** (cash-flow statement).
- TTM operating cash flow $161,403M. **Company-defined TTM free cash flow: −$7,604M**, vs +$18,184M a year earlier (MD&A, Non-GAAP).
- Long-term debt face value **$68.8B (12/31/25) → $133.0B (6/30/26)**; $67.0B of long-term debt proceeds in six months (Note 5; cash-flow statement). Interest expense $1,314M vs $516M in Q2. Post-quarter: $24.9B notes (8-K 2026-07-09), £4.25B sterling notes (8-K 2026-09-14).
- Contractual commitments at 6/30: unconditional purchase obligations $130.1B; **leases not yet commenced $137.2B**; debt principal + interest $220.3B (Note 4).
- Cash + marketable securities $123.0B, unchanged from year-end despite the borrowing (MD&A).

**Legal (Note 4)** — FTC/state antitrust cases continue; BIPA privacy class actions; **Xockets**: patent suits (June 2025) plus a **May 2026 ITC complaint against Amazon, AWS, Annapurna, NVIDIA and Microsoft** alleging EC2 P6e-GB200 UltraServers and related services infringe, seeking injunctive relief; **ITC instituted an investigation in June 2026**.

## 6. Implications for ratings (flags only — nothing decided)

- **D1 AI revenue:** reinforced. AI run-rate >$25B is ~15% of AWS run-rate and growing triple digits.
- **D2 Position / D3 Moat:** reinforced by Trainium (>$25B chips run-rate, multi-GW commitments, external-sale talks). Counter: contracts carry chip-performance obligations — Trainium under-delivery would be a contractual problem, not just a competitive one.
- **D4 Capacity:** strong demand signal (2027 largely reserved). Constraint is supply and financing, not demand.
- **D5 AI-buildout tie:** already at the top of the buyer scale; ~$220B capex confirms.
- **R1 Customer concentration — the dimension most worth a discussion.** Historically AMZN is as diversified as it gets. But roughly half of the $496B backlog is two AI labs, and both are investees. Consolidated revenue concentration is still low; *incremental AWS growth* concentration is not. Does the rubric grade the book or the growth?
- **R3 Balance sheet:** debt doubled, FCF negative, $137B uncommenced leases, ~$240B of illiquid stakes. Still investment-grade with $123B liquidity and $161B TTM operating cash flow — but the direction is clearly worse than at the last rating. Note rule 23: R3 already double-counts ND/EBITDA.
- **R4 Regulatory:** FTC trial may be imminent (date unconfirmed); ITC/Xockets is new. Neither touches the AWS thesis directly except Xockets.
- **M1/M2:** fundamentals support M1; stock is flat since July and 50DMA% is drifting down (58 → 55) — M2 should follow the objective number.
- **Rule 15:** needs a formal EPS-YoY ruling entry for the 2026-06-30 quarter.
