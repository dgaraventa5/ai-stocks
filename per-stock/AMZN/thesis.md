## 1. One-line thesis

> The AI story is AWS, attached to a much larger retail and ads business: AWS grew +37% to $42.2B in Q2 2026 (fifth straight accelerating quarter) at a 39% operating margin, backed by a $364B contracted backlog at Q1 that excludes Anthropic's >$100B ten-year commitment, and its in-house Trainium chips are sold out a generation ahead. The tension is cash — capex guidance is ~$220B for 2026 and consumes most of operating cash flow, so the bet is that this spend converts to durable AWS growth.

---

## 2. Position in the AI supply chain

- **What they do:** Runs AWS, the largest cloud platform, alongside online retail, third-party seller services, advertising and subscriptions. AWS rents compute (NVIDIA GPUs and its own Trainium/Graviton chips) and model access (Bedrock) to enterprises and AI labs.
- **Where they sit:** Layer 09 — a buyer of nearly everything upstream in this project (chips, networking, power, data-center construction) and a seller of AI compute. FY2026 capex guidance ~$220B (Q2 2026 earnings coverage, CNBC, 2026-07-30).
- **Unique vs. commoditized:** Custom silicon is the differentiator — Trainium3 nearly fully subscribed and much of Trainium4 already reserved, >$225B of Trainium revenue commitments (Q1 2026 call coverage, 2026-04-29); Amazon began selling Trainium to external customers in July 2026 (news-log 2026-07-15). Raw GPU rental is closer to commodity.
- **AI revenue mix:** AWS AI revenue run-rate >$15B at Q1 2026 against AWS net sales of $37,587M that quarter (10-Q filed 2026-04-30; run-rate from call coverage). AWS was ~21% of consolidated Q1 net sales ($37,587M of $181,519M), so consolidated figures dilute the AI signal.
