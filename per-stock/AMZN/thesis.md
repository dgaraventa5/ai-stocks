## 1. One-line thesis

> The AI story is AWS, attached to a much larger retail and ads business: AWS grew +36.8% to $42.2B in Q2 2026 (fifth straight accelerating quarter) at a 39.4% operating margin, and holds a $496B contracted backlog — though roughly half of that comes from OpenAI and Anthropic, two customers Amazon has itself put about $68B of cash into. The tension is funding: ~$220B of 2026 capex has turned trailing free cash flow negative (−$7.6B) and doubled long-term debt to $133B in six months, so the bet is that contracted demand converts to cash before the financing gets uncomfortable.

---

## 2. Position in the AI supply chain

- **What they do:** Runs AWS, the largest cloud platform, alongside online retail, third-party seller services, advertising and subscriptions. AWS rents compute (NVIDIA GPUs and its own Trainium/Graviton chips) and model access (Bedrock) to enterprises and AI labs.
- **Where they sit:** Layer 09 — a buyer of nearly everything upstream in this project (chips, networking, power, data-center construction) and a seller of AI compute. FY2026 cash capex guidance ~$220B (Q2 2026 call, 2026-07-30); $173.0B spent in the trailing twelve months (10-Q filed 2026-07-31).
- **Unique vs. commoditized:** Custom silicon is the differentiator — Trainium3 nearly fully subscribed and much of Trainium4 already reserved, >$225B of Trainium revenue commitments (Q1 2026 call coverage, 2026-04-29); Amazon began selling Trainium to external customers in July 2026 (news-log 2026-07-15). Raw GPU rental is closer to commodity.
- **AI revenue mix:** AWS AI revenue run-rate >$25B at Q2 2026 (call figure, not in the 10-Q) against an AWS run-rate of ~$169B — roughly 15% of AWS. AWS was 21% of consolidated Q2 net sales ($42,232M of $200,606M) but 61% of operating income (10-Q filed 2026-07-31, Note 8), so consolidated revenue figures dilute the AI signal.
