# AMZN — Q2 2026 earnings call (2026-07-30) — call notes

**These are paraphrased notes, not a verbatim transcript.** Compiled 2026-10-05/06 from the Investing.com transcript page and call coverage; figures marked (10-Q) were checked against the 10-Q filed 2026-07-31. Full transcript: https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-amazon-tops-q2-2026-estimates-as-aws-growth-accelerates-93CH-4826442

## Results cited on the call
- Net sales $200.6B, +20% YoY; operating income $27.5B, +43% (10-Q: $200,606M; $27,461M).
- AWS revenue $42.2B, +36.7%, fifth straight quarter of acceleration; annualized run-rate ~$169B (10-Q: $42,232M).
- AWS operating margin up about 650bps YoY; CFO said about 520bps excluding a derivative accounting gain (10-Q: $551M unrealized gain on energy contracts, Note 1).

## AWS and AI (Andy Jassy, CEO)
- AI revenue run-rate above $25B, growing triple digits YoY (was above $15B a quarter earlier).
- Backlog $496B, growing triple digits YoY (10-Q: ~$496B, 6.4-year weighted life).
- Custom chips (Trainium + Graviton) above a $25B annual run-rate, triple-digit growth.
- Graviton is used by 98% of the top 1,000 EC2 customers; Graviton commitments nearly tripled QoQ.
- Trainium: multi-year, multi-gigawatt commitments from Anthropic and OpenAI; other adopters named include Uber and Pinterest.
- Bedrock customer spend rose sharply QoQ (coverage cites +170%).
- External Trainium sales: a growing number of customers want the chips outside AWS's cloud; management is in active talks.

## Capacity and capex
- 2027 capacity is largely reserved and some 2028 capacity is already spoken for; management expects to keep adding significant data-center capacity for two to three years.
- CFO Brian Olsavsky: 2026 cash capex guidance raised to ~$220B from ~$200B, attributed to higher memory costs.
- Asset lives defended: data-center shells 30+ years; servers five to six years.

## Guidance
- Q3 2026 net sales $197–202B; operating income $22.5–26.5B.
