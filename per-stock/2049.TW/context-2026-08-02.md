## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before searching)

What I believed going in:
- Hiwin is Taiwan's linear-motion champion, cost-positioned between Japanese incumbents (THK/NSK) and Chinese entrants.
- **Robotics rose to 12% of revenue in Q1 2026** — the roster asked me to verify this specific claim.
- Machine-tool cyclicality is the main risk; Taiwan asset concentration is a TSM-style geographic risk.
- "The best fundamentals in the bucket" per one research pass, but no US listing.

The 12% claim **verified from three independent sources**, and the business is inflecting harder than the roster implies. See §4.

> **Reconciliation note:** "doubled" is measured against the ~5–7% historical base (and ~10% for FY2025); the sequential step was 11% → 12%. Both statements are true at different baselines. Cite the base you mean.

### Humanoid / robotics positioning (chairman, Q1 call + Computex 2026)

- Hiwin is **already supplying reducers, rotary tables and joint modules to global AI-robot makers for testing**; 2026 remains a **small-batch development phase**.
- Management: humanoids must first prove reliability in controlled, high-safety factory environments; **volume production not expected until after 2027**.
- **Planetary roller screw launched 2026**, sampling with multiple Taiwanese makers — the humanoid linear-actuator component.
- Showed a full stack (precision transmission → actuator modules → system integration) at **Computex 2026**, including an **edge-AI Load Port and wafer-transfer system**.
- **Dexterity (US logistics firm) dual-arm logistics robot** partnership — initial **20–30 unit** shipment expected in 2026 ([Digitimes, 2026-06-03](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20260603PD230/robot-logistics-component-technology-equipment.html)).
- Also entering the **US robot-dog (quadruped) supply chain**.
- Main current driver is **wafer robots and wafer-transfer systems** on TW/US/JP fab expansion, plus cleanroom robots. Advanced packaging named as the next battleground.

Sources: [cnyes](https://news.cnyes.com/news/id/6451262), [Digitimes June sales](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20260709PD245/revenue-robotics-demand-2026-subsidiary.html).

## 5. Rating implications

- **D1** — **12% of Q1 2026 revenue is robotics, company-disclosed** (NT$747M / NT$6.38B), up from ~10% in FY2025. Squarely band 3 (10–25%). Best-evidenced D1 in the block after Nabtesco.
- **D2** — Not a bottleneck owner (THK/NSK sit above, Chinese entrants below), **but** demonstrable pricing power this cycle: 7–15% price increases stuck, GM at a six-quarter high, 4–5 month backlog, 4.5–5 month lead times. Vertical integration (self-built grinding machines "cost at least half" of purchased equipment) is a genuine cost position. → **4**.
- **D3** — Cost/scale moat via vertical integration and mid-to-high-end positioning, but Hiwin is neither the technology leader nor the low-cost producer; share is contested from both sides. → **3**.
- **D4** — **Capex declining** from NT$3–4B; new products (planetary roller screw) but no capacity program. → **3**.
- **D5 (L11 lens, proposed)** — **Shipping samples/small batches into identified humanoid and AI-robot programs** (reducers, rotary tables, joint modules; planetary roller screws; Dexterity dual-arm 20–30 units; US robot-dog chain), with **no quantified humanoid revenue** and management guiding volume only after 2027. → **3**.
- **M1** — **Brokers upgraded to Buy with raised targets on 2026-07-14**; Q1 EPS beat; H1 revenue +18.05%; March price increases flowing to margin from Q3; "EPS potential to double." → **5**, Medium-High confidence (⚠️ brokers unnamed in the source).
- **M2** — +41.96% over one year vs XLI +20% (1H 2026) → roughly +17–20pp outperformance, blunted by a **−28.5% drawdown** from the high.
- **R2** — Worst plausible shock: **a Taiwan-strait conflict or blockade.** ~**4,500 of 6,400 employees and the great majority of production capacity sit in Taichung, Taiwan** — so effectively **~100% of revenue is exposed through the asset base**, not merely the 8% shipped into Taiwan. This is the rubric's explicit **asset leg** and matches the TSM anchor. → **2**.
- **R3** — **Net debt NT$1,763M, ND/EBITDA ≈0.3x**, equity NT$37.6B, capex falling. → **4**.
- **R5** — Physical component. Default **5**.

**Rule-9 note:** Q2 2026 full financials (not just monthly revenue) are due mid-August. Refresh Value/Quality inputs then — the TTM base currently in use is stale.
