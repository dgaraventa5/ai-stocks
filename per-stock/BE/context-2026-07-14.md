## 1. Pre-research mental model

As of the 2026-06-10 briefing (basis Q1 2026, reported 2026-04-28): Bloom is a SOFC maker that pivoted hard into on-site AI datacenter power. Q1 2026 revenue $751.1M (+130% YoY), GAAP operating income $72.2M, FY2026 guide raised to $3.4–3.8B; the datacenter book is anchored by an Oracle master agreement (up to 2.8 GW, 1.2 GW contracted) plus sole-provider status on Oracle's 2.45 GW grid-independent "Project Jupiter" campus in New Mexico, a Nebius capacity-as-a-service deal (up to $2.6B), and an AEP utility channel (up to 1 GW). The defining structural feature — and the biggest flag — is that ~50% of Q1 revenue flowed through a **related-party Brookfield-affiliated project-finance JV** ($373.3M vs $2.8M a year earlier), which obscures end-user identity and adds a financing-structure dependency; secondary flags were an Oracle warrant booked as a revenue reduction, mixed margin texture (product GM strong ~34%, but installation/electricity GM soft), and heavy insider selling through May 2026. D1 (datacenter revenue %) is an inference (~50% floor), not a disclosure.

## 5. DIFF — what changed since the prior mental model

- **NEW / material — Brookfield framework 5x'd to $25B (2026-06-30).** The financing counterparty that already intermediates ~50% of revenue has massively enlarged its committed backing. Cuts both ways: validates the demand/financing runway (bullish for scale) but *deepens* the single-counterparty intermediation dependency the prior briefing flagged as the key risk.
- **NEW — Hunterbrook short report (2026-07-08) attacks two pillars at once:** (a) the scandium-oxide/China supply chain and 5 GW scaling feasibility, and (b) *exactly* the related-party revenue / backlog-quality / revenue-recognition concerns we independently flagged on 2026-06-10. An external adversarial party converging on our own flag is worth weighting. Bloom's rebuttal escalated the supply-chain claim to "visibility to 25 GW/yr" — note the number jumped from the 5 GW/yr manufacturing figure cited on the Q1 call (25 GW = supply-chain visibility, not current capacity; don't conflate).
- **NEW — CEO retention mega-grant (2026-06-15):** 271K PSUs at target / up to ~813K at max, revenue-target vesting through 2029. Incremental dilution and a governance/comp signal; also a management-confidence signal (revenue + FY2029 margin gates). Market cap disclosed ~$79B (from ~$5B end-2024) frames how much is now priced in.
- **Confirmed, not new:** Project Jupiter 2.45 GW, Oracle 2.8 GW MSA, Nebius $2.6B, AEP ~1 GW all still stand; AEP $2.65B/2026-01-08 now has more secondary corroboration but still no primary.
