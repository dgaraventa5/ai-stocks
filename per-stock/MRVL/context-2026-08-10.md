## 1. Pre-research mental model (written BEFORE pulling data)

Marvell is the #2 merchant custom-ASIC house behind Broadcom, designing XPUs and accelerators for
hyperscalers — AWS Trainium and Microsoft Maia are the two programs I associated with it — plus a
large optical DSP/electro-optics franchise and legacy carrier/enterprise networking that has been a
drag. My model was that custom silicon was ramping into volume production but that Marvell was
losing the perception battle to Broadcom, that Trainium program share was a live competitive
question, and that data center was roughly two-thirds of revenue. I did not have any 2026
acquisitions in the model.

**The GAAP/non-GAAP gap is enormous** ($0.04 vs $0.80 — a ~20× difference, ~$684M). That gap is

to the non-GAAP line.

**Two acquisitions closed in Q1 FY2027, both disclosed in the release:**
- **Celestial AI, Inc.** — closed **2026-02-02**
- **XConn Technologies Holdings, Ltd.** — closed **2026-02-10**

Results include both from their acquisition dates. Celestial AI is an optical-interconnect
(photonic fabric) company; XConn is a CXL/PCIe switching company. Together these are a deliberate
push into scale-up interconnect — the same contested ground as ALAB, Broadcom and NVLink.

**Guidance:** Q2 FY27 revenue **$2.7B at the midpoint, +35% YoY**. CEO Matt Murphy: "We expect
revenue growth to continue accelerating each quarter throughout fiscal 2027… We are seeing
exceptional AI-related bookings, and as a result, we are **significantly raising Marvell's revenue
outlook for both fiscal 2027 and fiscal 2028**." Drivers named: 800G and 1.6T scale-out optics,
51.2T Ethernet scale-out switches, and custom silicon.

## 6. Implications for ratings (flagging only — not pre-deciding)

- **D1 (AI-revenue %)** — revisit **upward-leaning**: data center is 76% of revenue and growing 27%.
- **D2 (supply-chain position)** — revisit. Celestial AI + XConn materially change Marvell's position
  in scale-up interconnect. Whether that's a stronger bottleneck or an expensive entry into a
  contested market is the judgment call.
- **D3 (moat)** — the key open question is custom-ASIC socket retention (a designed-in socket is
  sticky for a generation but re-competed at each node). 18 design wins is evidence of breadth; it
  is not evidence of retention.
- **D5 (strength of AI-buildout tie)** — likely already high; the raised FY27 *and* FY28 outlooks
  plus "exceptional AI bookings" support it.
- **R1 (customer concentration)** — hyperscaler-concentrated by construction; 18 design wins argues
  for some diversification within a concentrated customer set.
- **R3 (balance sheet)** — check post-acquisition: two deals closed in one month; verify cash/debt
  and the amortization load driving the GAAP/non-GAAP gap.
- **Quality inputs** — when the objective refresh runs, be deliberate about GAAP vs non-GAAP. TTM
  GAAP net income is depressed by acquisition amortization; margins will look worse than the
  operating business.
- **Rule-9 objective refresh:** Q2 FY27 not yet reported (expect late Aug / early Sep). **Queue for
  the reporting date.** Prior backing hits the 90-day rule-12 wall on ~2026-08-16; this briefing
  resets that clock.
- **Cross-name note for the rating session:** MRVL (Celestial AI) and ALAB now compete directly in
