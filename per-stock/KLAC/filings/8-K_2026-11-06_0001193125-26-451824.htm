Item 5.07

Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

KLA Corporation (the “Company”) held its 2026 annual meeting of stockholders (the “2026 Annual Meeting”) on November 4, 2026. Of the 1,305,006,492 shares of Company Common Stock entitled to vote at the 2026 Annual Meeting, 1,165,754,779.25659 shares, or 89.33%, were present in person or by proxy at the 2026 Annual Meeting. Three proposals were considered at the 2026 Annual Meeting:

Proposal One: At the 2026 Annual Meeting, the stockholders elected the nine candidates nominated by the Board of Directors to serve as directors for one-year terms, each until his or her successor is duly elected and qualified.

Item 8.01

Other Events.

As previously disclosed in the Company’s 2026 Proxy Statement filed with the SEC on September 22, 2026, Jason Conley was not standing for re-election at the 2026 Annual Meeting, effectively ending his term on November 4, 2026.

On November 5, 2026, the Company issued a press release announcing that its Board of Directors declared a cash dividend of $0.23 per share on the Company’s common stock. Such dividend shall be payable on December 1, 2026 to stockholders of record as of the close of business on November 16, 2026. A copy of the press release is attached as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.
