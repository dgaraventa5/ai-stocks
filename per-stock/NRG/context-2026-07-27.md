# NRG — pre-rating context briefing (2026-07-27)

Refresh pass ahead of any scoring/rating review. Prior briefing: `context-2026-06-09.md` (Layer 1 AI-Thesis "directness" re-rate). This pass looks for what has changed in the ~7 weeks since, with Q2 2026 earnings still pending (Aug 4).

## 1. Pre-research mental model

NRG is the most retail-weighted of the Layer 1 IPPs — ~6M retail electricity customers plus Vivint smart home, a mostly gas-fired fleet (no nuclear), now roughly doubled to ~25 GW after the LS Power gas portfolio closed 2026-01-30. Its AI angle is forward pipeline, not current P&L: signed but tiny data-center retail contracts (445 MW ramping from ~5 MW in 2026 to 445 MW by 2032 at >$80/MWh, undisclosed counterparties), a GE Vernova/Kiewit venture for 5.4 GW of new gas (first power 2029, anchor data-center agreement "expected in 2026" but unsigned), and developer LOIs (Menlo/PowLan, up to 6.5 GW) that remain LOIs. Q1 2026 (reported 2026-05-06) was a large EPS miss on mild Texas weather — the signature of a commodity price-taker on ~25 GW of merchant exposure vs. 445 MW of premium-priced contracts. By the "direct ownership of the AI bottleneck" lens, NRG screens below VST/TLN/CEG: real signed MW but anonymous counterparties and a mostly announced-but-unsigned pipeline.
