## 1. Pre-research mental model

> A diversified French electrical-equipment maker where data centers are the fastest-growing piece (power distribution, UPS, cooling via Motivair), not the whole business. It reports in euros twice a year with quarterly sales updates, trades in the US only as an over-the-counter ADR, and I believe the last full results were H1 2026 in late July. I expect AI/data-center exposure around a quarter of sales and a premium industrial multiple.

## 2. What changed since 2026-09-21

**The PTC acquisition (announced 2026-10-05 — today).**
- Definitive agreement to buy PTC Inc. (US industrial design/PLM software) for **$205 per share in cash, $22.6B equity value, ~$23.7B enterprise value**, a **42.3% premium** to the prior close. ([Euronews, 2026-10-05](https://www.euronews.com/2026/10/05/schneider-electric-shares-plunge-on-record-226bn-ptc-deal); [InsideArbitrage](https://www.insidearbitrage.com/2026/10/schneider-electric-acquires-ptc-for-23-7-billion-in-cash/))
- **Financing:** ~€22B total, via **€16–17B of new debt and €5–6B of new shares**, bridged by a Morgan Stanley / Société Générale facility. **Buybacks paused for 2027–2028.** Company expects to keep A-category credit ratings, "subject to confirmation by the ratings agencies." (same sources; [company deck, se.com](https://www.se.com/ww/en/assets/pdf/Schneider-Electric-PTC-Energy-Industrial-intelligence) — not read directly)
- **Price paid:** ~13.2x PTC EBITDA; management expects return on capital to exceed cost of capital **only by year five** after close. Cost savings €250M a year by year three; ~€800M revenue uplift claimed.
- **Timing:** close expected **Q3 2027**, subject to PTC shareholder and regulatory approval.
- **Market reaction:** shares down ~9–10% on the day; Santander and Zacks downgraded, Barclays / Morgan Stanley stayed positive. Jefferies flagged that AI-disruption fears about software could now weigh on Schneider itself. ([Yahoo Finance UK](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/schneider-electric-stocks-fall-10-174757302.html); [Breakingviews, "boldly disrupts own AI story"](https://www.breakingviews.com/columns/breaking-view/schneider-electric-boldly-disrupts-own-ai-story-2026-10-05/); [MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/price-schneider-electric-otcmkts-sbgsy-shares-gap-down-heres-why-2026-10-05/))
