## Pre-research mental model

Schneider Electric is a French electrical-equipment / energy-management major (LV/MV distribution, switchgear, UPS via APC, EcoStruxure software, Industrial Automation) and a leading AI-datacenter beneficiary on the power-and-cooling side (datacenter power architecture, UPS, busway, Motivair liquid cooling), sitting upstream of the racks. Reports in EUR on a half-year cadence (H1/FY full P&L; Q1/Q3 revenue-only trading updates); no US 10-Q, US line is an ADR. As of the prior briefing (2026-06-22) the picture was: Q1 2026 record revenue €9.77bn (+11.2% organic), FY guidance reaffirmed (adj. EBITA +10–15% organic), record €25.4bn backlog (+18% YoY, datacenter-skewed to 2027+), with the main risk being valuation/FX not demand. H1 2026 full results were NOT yet available that pass — that gap is the main thing this refresh closes.
