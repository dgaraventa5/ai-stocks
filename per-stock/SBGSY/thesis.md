## 1. One-line thesis

> Schneider is the broadest pure-play on electrifying and cooling the AI data center — power distribution, busway, UPS, and now white-space prefab and grid-edge software — with a data-center franchise growing triple digits inside a diversified, high-margin industrial that keeps raising guidance.

---

## 2. Position in the AI supply chain

- **What they do:** Electrical distribution, medium/low-voltage power management, secure power (UPS/APC), building/grid automation, and — via AVEVA + the 2026 Cognite deal — industrial and agentic-AI software (EcoStruxure/AVEVA CONNECT).
- **Where they sit:** Layer 2 supplier of the power/cooling backbone inside the data center and at the grid edge; a direct beneficiary of hyperscaler and colocation capex.
- **Unique vs. commoditized:** Full-stack "grid-to-chip" breadth (few competitors — Eaton, ABB, Vertiv, Legrand — span as much), reference-design lock-in with hyperscalers, and a widening software moat post-AVEVA/Cognite. Prefab EcoStruxure Pods bundle power+cooling+IT as an integrated white-space product, raising switching cost.
- **AI revenue mix:** Data Center & Networks is the standout end market, growing triple-digit % in H1 2026 (Utility Dive, 2026); data centers are a large and fast-rising slice but the group is diversified across buildings, industry and infrastructure, so AI-infra-tied revenue is a strong minority (D1=4, 25–50% band; not a >50% pure-play). *(se.com H1 2026 release)*

## 6. Moat

- **IP / process know-how:** Deep electrical-engineering + software stack (EcoStruxure, AVEVA, now Cognite agentic-AI).
- **Scale advantage:** Global #1/#2 in electrical distribution; volume + installed base.
- **Switching costs:** High for integrated power/cooling designs and software-attached installs; prefab pods deepen this.
- **Network effects:** Growing on the software side (AVEVA CONNECT ecosystem).
- **Regulatory / geographic:** Diversified geography; EU-domiciled, global manufacturing.

**Moat rating:** Wide (D3=4).

## 9. Risks

- **Thesis-killer #1:** AI/DC capex pause turning the triple-digit DC&N growth cyclical.
- **Thesis-killer #2:** Multiple/FX de-rating on a premium valuation.
- **China / export controls:** Some China exposure (industrial), not a primary overhang (R2=4).
- **Cyclical risk:** Buildings/industry segments are late-cycle; a European industrial slowdown would offset DC strength.
- **Technology substitution:** Low for power/cooling hardware.
- **Regulatory / political:** Routine (R4=4).
- **Execution / management:** M&A digestion (Cognite $3.1bn + AVEVA); mild leverage drift (net debt €15.4bn) — covered by record FCF (R3=4).

---

## 10. The "inverse the thesis" test

1. Data Center & Networks organic growth falls below ~15% for two consecutive halves with no offsetting margin gain — the AI pull was cyclical.
2. Group adj-EBITA margin declines YoY for a full year despite positive volume — pricing power / mix advantage is gone.
