## Implications for ratings

Flags only — no numbers pre-decided; the collaborative rating session decides actual values. Current ratings shown for reference.

- **AI Thesis D1 (AI-revenue driver, now 4):** Well-supported to possibly *stronger*. Triple-digit pure-DC growth and DC & Networks as the standout end market strengthen the direct-AI-demand read. Apply the rule-12 absolute "directness" lens: Schneider is still a broad industrial where datacenter is ~30% of revenue (not a pure-play) — its AI directness is real but diluted by Buildings / Industrial Automation. The current 4 looks defensible; the case for lifting hinges on whether ~30%-of-revenue-but-accelerating clears the "directness" bar vs. staying capped by the diversified base.
- **AI Thesis D2 (position/scale, now 4) & D3 (moat, now 4):** Reinforced. Integrated power+liquid-cooling (Compass EcoStruxure Pod, Switch SCA, 800V DC), full-lifecycle positioning, and the Cognite/AVEVA agentic-AI software layer deepen the bottleneck/pricing-power and durability story. Worth re-examining whether D2 or D3 merits a lift given the widening product+software moat.
- **AI Thesis D5 (hyperscaler/AI-buildout tie, now 4):** Supported. Explicit hyperscale + colo + sovereign exposure and named capacity agreements (Switch $1.9bn) evidence a quantified tie to the buildout. On the rule-23 parallel scale, re-check whether the contracted evidence supports the current 4.
- **Momentum M1 (EPS revisions, now 3):** **Likely stale/low.** A genuine FY guidance *upgrade* + H1 EBITA +22% organic + net income +30% is exactly the setup that drives positive consensus revisions. M1 is a candidate to revisit upward. (Confirm with an actual estimate-revision source before moving — rule 2.)
- **Momentum M2 (relative strength, now 3) / 50DMA % (rule 11):** Stock reported near record levels post-H1 (ad-hoc-news). The objective 50DMA% uses the **US ADR (SBGSY)** price history — verify the momentum script handles the ADR line (ADR action can differ from the Paris SU line). M2 worth re-checking against the ADR trend.
- **Risk R3 (balance-sheet risk, now 4):** Slight negative drift to note but not clearly rating-moving — net debt rose to €15.4bn and the $3.1bn all-cash Cognite deal adds leverage; offset by record €1.6bn H1 FCF and strong EBITA. Net leverage still moderate (roughly ~1.5–2x FY EBITA, approximate — no clean TTM EBITDA disclosed in the trading-update format). Flag, don't assume; keep at 4 absent a cleaner ND/EBITDA figure.
- **Risk R2 (geography, now 4):** Broad-based across all regions; the June "South Asia/Middle East Q2 wobble" flag did not materialize as a demand issue. No change indicated.
- **Risk R5 (disruption, now 5):** Physical electrical/power/cooling infrastructure → defaults to 5 (rule 16, non-Layer-10). No change.
- **R1/R4, D4:** No new signal. R1 diversified end-market (datacenter is an end-market, not a single customer); R4 regulatory unremarkable; D4 capacity/execution supported by delivery on record H1.

**Data gaps / flags (rule 3):** (1) No fresh point-in-time backlog figure at H1 2026 — June's €25.4bn (YE2025) is the last hard number. (2) Q3 2026 trading update not out until 29 Oct 2026. (3) Value objective inputs remain EUR/USD-currency-trapped and unreliable — a Value-column issue, not touched by this subjective pass. (4) Estimate-revision direction for M1 inferred from the guidance upgrade, not yet confirmed against a consensus-revision data source — verify before rating.
