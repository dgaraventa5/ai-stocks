Call participants: Brian Lane (CEO), Bill George (CFO), Trent McKenna (President), Julie Shaeff (CAO). Call held Friday 2026-07-24, 10:00 a.m. CT.

## Prepared remarks — key points

- **Records everywhere:** "This is the first time that our quarterly revenue has exceeded $3 billion." Revenue $3.27B +50.3% YoY (same-store +43.8%); EPS $12.53 +92%; operating cash flow $1.14B in the quarter ("remarkable and completely unprecedented quarterly cash flow of more than $1 billion" — Lane, also in Ex. 99.1).
- **Mechanical profitability step-up:** mechanical segment gross margin 25.6% (from 22.9%); electrical 26.4% (+~1pt). "Our mechanical business experienced a sharp increase in profitability." (Lane)
- **Backlog:** $14.06B total, +73% YoY, +12.9% sequential; same-store $13.70B vs $8.12B a year ago (+68.7%). Modular bookings $510M in Q2. Entered Q3 with same-store backlog ~69% higher YoY.
- **Tech mix:** technology (incl. data centers) 58% of H1 2026 revenue vs 40% a year prior (10-Q: Q2 58.7%, H1 57.6%).
- **Guidance RAISED:** FY2026 same-store revenue growth now **mid-to-high-30% range** (was mid-to-high-20s at the Q1 call). Gross margins expected to "continue in the strong ranges that we have averaged in recent quarters." Effective tax rate ~23%. Capex ~5% of revenue. Optimism extended "well into 2027" (Ex. 99.1).

## Demand / hyperscaler commentary

- Bill George on hyperscaler customers: "There is a very deep and calm certainty among these people that they're going to continue to build, that they absolutely need and have to do this building. We see no letdown whatsoever."
- Trent McKenna on data-center moratoriums (NY etc., analyst Qs from KeyBank/William Blair): most backlogged projects "already planned, permitted"; projects may relocate rather than disappear; "these data centers, as many as can be built, are going to get built." Modular work is less site-specific, reducing moratorium vulnerability.
- New **pilot programs with frontier labs and co-location providers** are underway — smaller initial contracts, incremental customer set beyond the incumbent hyperscalers.
- Early-stage **data-center service pilots with hyperscalers**: management sees eventual service opportunity as significant long-term, currently limited by OEM warranty periods.

## Modular capacity

- Capacity now >3.5M sq ft → >4M sq ft by end-2026 → **~5M sq ft targeted by late summer 2027** (new disclosure; prior plan stopped at 4M).
- McKenna: "We're not going to invest in buildings just on speculation. We expand when customers provide us meaningful multi-year commitments." Expansion is "overwhelmingly for existing customers and existing orders."
- George on modular facility economics: "Full paybacks within a year or two" — returns he called "embarrassing."

## Cash flow discussion (Q&A, Thompson Davis)

- Q2 FCF $999.3M ≈ 2.3x quarterly net income (Ex. 99.1 non-GAAP table: OCF $1,139.4M − capex $141.4M + asset sales $1.3M).
- George: driven by "advanced cash payments, favorable payment terms, exceptionally good project performance… It was broad-based." "There's definitely an element of advanced cash… extraordinarily good payment terms because we have extraordinarily good negotiating power."
- He cautioned cash flow will normalize toward "net income plus non-cash items" over time — i.e., Q2's level is not run-rate.

## Other Q&A

- **Hunt Electric** (closed 5/1/26, Utah, electrical, ~$250M annualized revenue, $206.0M preliminary purchase price per 10-Q Note on acquisitions): early integration "fantastic," joint projects with FIX mechanical operations in Utah already underway (Sidoti Q).
- **Backlog duration** (UBS Q): construction backlog typically converts within 18–24 months; modular extends further out. Lane: "We only take work that we know we can perform. We don't out-kick our coverage."
- **Labor:** ~25,000 employees; execution credited to "elite trades people"; disciplined booking practices reiterated.
