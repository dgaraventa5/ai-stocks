## 1. Management tone on AI/data-center demand

Unchanged in direction, **stronger in conviction**. Q1 framing was "labor, not demand, is the growth limiter"; Q2 adds CFO George's flat assertion of hyperscaler intent: "very deep and calm certainty… We see no letdown whatsoever," and optimism explicitly extended "well into 2027" (Ex. 99.1). New in Q2: management engaged directly with the data-center-moratorium question (NY) and dismissed it — backlog already permitted, modular less site-dependent, projects relocate rather than cancel.

## 2. Customer concentration changes

Concentration **increased again**: technology 58.7% of Q2 revenue vs 56.4% Q1 and 43.0% Q2'25 (10-Q customer table). New construction 75.1% of revenue vs 74.5% Q1. Same-store backlog growth is still concentrated in a handful of tech operations — Texas modular (+$1.87B YoY), Texas electrical (+$1.13B YoY, +$1.00B in Q2 alone), Texas mechanical (+$860.0M), North Carolina (+$671.9M), Indiana (+$540.3M) (10-Q Backlog section). **Partial offsets:** Hunt acquisition adds a non-tech-centric Utah electrical book, and Q2 disclosed *new customer types* — pilot programs with frontier labs and co-location providers, plus early hyperscaler service pilots — the first visible steps toward diversifying within tech beyond construction for incumbent hyperscalers. One NC operation's backlog fell $118.5M on completed tech project work — first visible large-project roll-off in the disclosure.

## 3. AI/tech revenue mix

Q2 58.7% / H1 57.6% ($3,533.9M of $6,131.0M) vs FY-prior-period 40.2% (10-Q Revenue by Type of Customer). Manufacturing (16.4%) and every institutional/commercial category shrank as a share. Backlog composition by customer type remains **undisclosed** (same gap as Q1); bookings commentary implies backlog is even more tech-weighted than revenue.

## 4. Capex / capacity / labor commentary

- Capex guide unchanged at ~5% of revenue, but H1 actual spend is way up: $288.8M H1 capex vs $53.5M H1'25; $188.9M of H1 building purchases for modular (10-Q Investing Activities).
- **New:** modular target extended to ~5M sq ft by late summer 2027 (from the prior 4M end-2026 endpoint) — a ~43% expansion from current >3.5M sq ft, still gated on multi-year customer commitments ("we're not going to invest in buildings just on speculation" — McKenna). George: facility paybacks "within a year or two."
- Labor tone unchanged: ~25,000 employees, "elite trades people," discipline framing ("we don't out-kick our coverage"). No new labor-shortage escalation flagged.

## 5. Forward guidance vs prior

The headline delta: FY2026 same-store revenue growth raised from **mid-to-high-20s% → mid-to-high-30s%** — roughly a 10-point raise half-way through the year, implying high-20s/low-30s H2 growth against much tougher comps. Margin guidance language unchanged ("continue in the strong ranges we have averaged in recent quarters"). Tax ~23%. 10-Q MD&A adds: "solid earnings for the remainder of 2026" and demand supportive "for the remainder of 2026" while explicitly noting preparation for "a wide range of challenges… including a potential recession."

## 6. New risks

- **Data-center moratoriums** (state-level, NY named) — new topic this quarter; management-minimized but now a live watch item.
- **Cash-flow normalization:** George explicitly warned Q2's $999M FCF (2.3x NI) is advance-payment-driven and will revert toward net income + non-cash items — expect optically weaker H2 cash comps. Contract liabilities $3,231.1M vs $2,120.3M at 12/31/25 (10-Q contract liabilities note) — customers are pre-funding, which reverses as projects burn.
- **Roll-off risk now visible:** NC operation −$118.5M backlog on project completion; backlog converts in 18–24 months, so 2027–28 depends on continuous mega-project re-booking.
- **Expectations risk:** stock fell ~3% on a 20% EPS beat + guidance raise — the multiple (~52.8x TTM P/E per [GuruFocus](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/8975853/comfort-systems-usa-fix-reports-strong-q2-earnings-exceeding-expectations), unverified) already prices a lot.
- Not new but continuing: insider selling (~$59.7M in 3 months per GuruFocus, unverified against Form 4s this pass).

## 7. Other thesis-relevant items

- Hunt Electric closed 5/1/26: $206.0M preliminary purchase price, ~$250M annualized revenue, Utah electrical (10-Q acquisitions note; call Q&A) — fourth electrical deal in ~14 months; electrical now ~30% of revenue ($969.0M Q2, +81.2% YoY).
- Cash $1,854.8M (10-Q balance sheet) vs negligible debt — net cash grew ~$800M in the quarter.
- Buyback essentially dormant: <0.1M shares / ~$8.2M repurchased in H1 at avg $1,438.19 (10-Q share repurchase note) — capital is going to modular capex + M&A + dividend instead.
- $29.8M R&D-credit tax refunds received July 2026; Q2 tax provision included a $5.3M benefit (10-Q income tax note) — small one-time EPS help, worth remembering for rule-15 checks (does not dominate the YoY change).
