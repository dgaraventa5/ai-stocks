## Pre-research mental model

(Written before pulling Q2 data, per process.) Antero is an Appalachian liquids-rich Marcellus producer whose edge is a firm-transportation portfolio that moves the majority of its gas out of basin to the Gulf Coast LNG fairway and its C3+ NGLs to the Marcus Hook export dock, so it realizes premiums to both NYMEX and Mont Belvieu rather than weak in-basin basis. It is the second-largest US NGL producer and the largest producer-exporter of LPG, so a meaningful slice of the P&L is levered to international propane/butane freight and arbitrage, not to US gas. It has been deleveraging after the ~$2.8B HG Energy acquisition closed Feb 2026 (Utica sold to part-fund it), runs an affiliate midstream (Antero Midstream) that takes a large gathering/processing/transport toll out of the revenue line, and carries essentially no signed AI/data-center exposure — the AI link is the second-derivative story that Appalachian power/DC buildout tightens local gas demand and gives its new dry-gas acreage optionality.

## Implications for ratings

No rating numbers are proposed. These are the dimensions where the Q2 evidence moved, and why.

**D1 — AI Revenue %.** Evidence is unchanged in direction and *stronger* in support of a floor-level read. AI-tied revenue remains ~0% of the $1,559.8M Q2 revenue line: 44.1% gas sold at NYMEX/LNG-corridor index points, 41.4% liquids clearing into international LPG/petchem export markets, and no revenue line, contract, or disclosed volume sold to a data center, DC-serving power plant, or hyperscaler. **Explicit distinction:** the ~9 Bcf/d of announced Appalachian power/DC projects and the resulting tighter regional basis are a *market* condition that could raise the price AR receives for undifferentiated molecules — that is commodity-price uplift, not AI revenue. The Q2 10-Q's zero hits on "data center"/"AI" is the same result as Q1.

**D2 — Supply Chain Position.** Two forces pull in opposite directions this quarter. Negative: the logistics-derived pricing advantage that justified any above-commodity read on D2 did not show up in Q2 — gas at −$0.24 to NYMEX, C3+ at −$0.93 to Mont Belvieu, and the FY gas-premium guide cut to $0.05–$0.15. Positive: AR demonstrated *optionality* rather than price-taking by walking away from a power project whose economics didn't clear its alternative sales points, and unit costs fell across five of seven cost lines. The honest read is that AR's position confers *choice among index points*, not pricing power over AI buyers — and the Q2 realizations are evidence that the choice is currently worth less than assumed.

**D5 — Hyperscaler / AI-infrastructure exposure.** This is where the largest new evidence landed, and it points down or at best sideways, not up. Prior read was "narrative-stage, not contract-stage." Q2 adds that AR **declined** an announced local project on pricing/timing/execution/counterparty-credit grounds and signed nothing. Zero material-definitive-agreement 8-Ks in the quarter — the only Item 1.01 filing (2026-06-17) is a commercial paper program, i.e. a financing event. The named contracted in-basin volumes belong to EQT. And the entity actually committing capital to the WV data-center corridor is Antero Midstream, which AR holds at equity method. Per rule 23's parallel-scale framing, AR's tie to the AI buildout is presently an unpriced option that management has explicitly chosen not to exercise on offered terms.

**D4 — Capacity Expansion.** Genuinely stronger. FY2026 production guidance raised to 4.15–4.2 Bcfe/d with a Q4 exit of 4.4–4.5 Bcfe/d; $315M of July bolt-ons adding 125 MMcfe/d plus 15 dry-gas locations; capital budget $1.1–1.3B including up to $200M of price-contingent discretionary growth capital; 70–80 net wells planned. This is funded, disclosed capex backing disclosed volume growth, not a press release — but note it is *commodity* capacity expansion with no customer commitments behind it, which is the D4-vs-D5 distinction to keep clean.

**M1 — EPS Revisions.** Mixed signal to re-check against Yahoo/Zacks revision data (not pulled this pass — flagged gap): production and cost guidance both moved favorably, but the FY gas-premium guide was cut and adjusted EPS came in at/just under consensus ($0.90 vs ~$0.91). Do not assume a clean beat-and-raise read.

**M2 — Relative Strength.** Deteriorated. AR +8.6% over 6 months vs XLE +19.1% / XOP +29.5%, and −7.7% over 3 months vs XLE −0.3% / XOP −3.2% (yfinance, to 2026-08-03). AR is lagging both benchmarks on both windows despite record production — management itself noted the share price is roughly flat YoY.

**M3 — Insider Activity.** Not examined this pass; no Form 4 review was performed. **Flagged as a gap** — per rule-12 discipline this should be checked before the rating is trusted, especially given the price underperformance (the rubric's key question is whether anyone is buying into weakness).

Total debt +87% to $2,614.3M in six months; funding mix shifted toward floating and short-dated paper ($1.1B term loan at 5.2%, $182M CP at 4.4% under a new $1.65B program) — CP is revolver-backstopped, so the refinancing risk is mitigated but not zero.
