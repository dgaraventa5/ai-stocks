## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before searching)

Lam is the etch/deposition leader, memory-heavy (NAND + DRAM roughly half of systems), with AI demand flowing through HBM, gate-all-around logic and advanced packaging. Last reported quarter was Sep-2026 (fiscal Q1 2027, $8.22B revenue, China 35%), with the December-quarter guide at $8.60B. Main customers are Samsung, TSMC, SK hynix and Micron; there is no direct hyperscaler revenue. The main overhang is China export controls; the next print is late January 2027.
