# WDC — Context Briefing 2026-10-06

## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before any data pull)

> WDC since the February 2025 SanDisk spin is a pure-play hard-drive maker in an effective duopoly with Seagate (Toshiba a distant third). I believe roughly 85–90% of revenue comes from cloud/data-center customers buying high-capacity nearline drives, and that demand is being pulled by AI data-lake storage with supply deliberately held tight (no unit-capacity adds; growth via areal density). Customer concentration is high — a handful of hyperscalers, with multi-year purchase agreements running into calendar 2027–2028.
> The most recent quarter I hold from training is fiscal Q3 2026 (March 2026 quarter): revenue a little over $3B, gross margin in the high 40s and rising each quarter. I expect fiscal Q4 (June/early-July 2026) to have continued that path, i.e. revenue around $3.3–3.8B and gross margin near or above 50%.
> Technology: WDC is behind Seagate on HAMR; it bridges with ePMR/UltraSMR drives and planned HAMR hyperscaler qualification in 2026 with volume ramp in 2027.
> Balance sheet: WDC kept a ~19.9% SanDisk stake at the spin and has been monetizing it (debt-for-equity exchanges). Because SanDisk shares rose enormously, I expect large non-operating mark-to-market or disposal gains in GAAP net income, which would make GAAP EPS and anything built on it (EPS YoY, trailing P/E) unrepresentative of the HDD business. I do not know why the stock fell ~28% over the last three months; my prior guess is a memory/storage-complex de-rating (pricing-peak fears) rather than a company-specific break.

## 6. Implications for ratings (flags only — nothing decided here)

Neither is an error and neither is Sandisk contamination; both lean in WDC's favour at the same time, right after a fiscal-year roll. The rank-10 reading is real under the rules as written, but roughly "rank on FY28 consensus being right".

- **Rule 15 / EPS YoY:** evidence supports a documented blank for quarter-end 2026-07-03 (section 2.3). Ex-item reference values: +109% (non-GAAP EPS) or +130% (operating income).
