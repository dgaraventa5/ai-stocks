## 1. Pre-research mental model (written before searching)

What I believed going in:
- Nabtesco is "the value way to own the reducer bottleneck": ~60% global share of RV cycloidal reducers, CMP segment = **25.8% of revenue** disclosed, at ~1/5th of Harmonic Drive's multiple (P/S 1.78x, trailing P/E 27.9x).
- FY2026 guidance was ¥327.0B sales / ¥27.7B OP (set 2026-02-19), with precision reducers the single largest growth contributor.
- It is entering strain-wave ("wave gear system"), attacking HDS's core.
- The offset is conglomerate dilution — doors, rail, marine, aircraft.
- Humanoid is "optionality, not revenue."

The headline financials turned out to be **two days stale and materially too low.** See §4.

## 5. Rating implications

- **D1** — CMP is **25.5–25.8% of revenue, company-disclosed** — the cleanest robotics-revenue disclosure in this five-name block. Sits just inside band 4 (25–50%). Caveat: precision reduction gears also serve machine tools, FPD, semiconductor equipment, positioners and AGVs, so a strict "robots only" read lands in band 3.
- **D2** — 60% world share in medium/large robot joints, **Changzhou at 115% utilization** (hard capacity-tightness evidence), four straight quarters of order growth, orders ¥82.4B in 2025. But **6.8% segment OPM** contradicts pricing power. → **4**, not 5.
- **D3** — Share + design-in switching costs (a joint reducer swap is a full arm redesign) are genuine; the sub-7% margin blocks a 5.
- **D4** — Best-evidenced expansion in the block: capacity 1,150k units/yr with Hamamatsu ramping, China plant above nameplate, **capex +24% to ¥12.5B**, new RVmini/Monocrank line, wave-gear entry.
- **D5 (L11 lens, proposed)** — Lineup map and a wave-gear product aimed at humanoid/cobot, **no shipments or orders disclosed** → roadmap-only. → **2**.
- **M1** — Beat-and-raise printed **two days ago**, EPS surprise +25.9%, guidance raised across all three lines, stock +8.2%. → **5**.
- **M2** — +66.45% over one year vs XLI +20% (1H 2026) → >20pp outperformance. Tempered by the −23.2% drawdown. Flag the identical YTD/1-yr readings on stockanalysis as a possible data artifact.
- **R2** — Worst plausible shock: **US–China trade rupture or accelerated Chinese domestic reducer substitution**, impairing **14.1% of revenue** and stranding the **Changzhou plant (14.8% of precision-reducer capacity)**. Currently absorbed — China grew 32%. Two legs (policy + partial asset) → **3**; a strict "asset leg present" reading of the rubric would say 2.
- **R3** — **Net cash ¥41,892M** against ¥296.4B equity, dividend raised. → **5**.
- **R5** — Physical component. Default **5**.
