- Sandersville, GA campus: infrastructure lease supporting **175 MW of critical IT load**. Tenant: "a leading global technology company with a high investment grade credit profile" — **confidential**; Ex-99.1 notes the credit profile is what is "facilitating CleanSpark's financing options and the multi-decade term."
- **Triple-net (NNN)** — Item 8.01 defines it: "all costs, charges, indemnities and expenses of every kind and nature will be paid by the Tenant" — with **annual escalators**; **20-year initial term + two 5-year extension options**.
- Economics: **~$6.6B expected contract value over the initial term; ~$11.6B if both extensions exercised; cumulative NOI contribution margin "nearly 100%," avg annual NOI ≈ $330M** (Ex-99.1 Transaction Details).
- **Landlord project cost $10–12M per MW of critical IT load** → **~$1.75–2.10B** total build for 175 MW. Deliveries **begin Q4 2027**.
- **Conditionality (the key risk sentence, Item 8.01):** "The Lease requires the Company to satisfy specified financing, construction and delivery milestones and other covenants and conditions. Failure to timely satisfy applicable milestones may result in rent abatements or termination of the Lease." The $6.6B is contingent on CLSK financing and building on time.
- Ex-99.1 forward-looking-statements section enumerates the funding risk explicitly: "the Company's need for, and ability to raise, substantial additional capital to fund the development of the Sandersville project; risks related to the significant additional indebtedness that the Company may incur"; plus **"the Company's dependence on a third party for development of the Sandersville project"** (an unnamed third-party developer) and equipment-procurement/competition risk.
- **Texas LOI:** tenant "also executed a letter of intent and exclusivity arrangement covering 718 acres of the Company's Texas portfolio with up to 885 megawatts of secured **and planned** power capacity" (Item 8.01) — Sealy 271 acres / ~300 MW + Brazoria 447 acres / initial 300 MW with "potential to expand to 600 MW" (Ex-99.1). **Non-binding, and the 885 MW figure mixes secured with planned/speculative** — do not read it as contracted capacity.
- Advisors: Morgan Stanley (financial), Davis Polk (legal) — institutional-caliber deal team (Ex-99.1). Signed by Gary A. Vecchiarelli, **President and CFO** (title now includes President).
- No financing plan disclosed: no lender, committed amount, pricing, sponsor equity, or draw schedule anywhere in the 8-K or Ex-99.1.

## 6. Implications for ratings (FLAGS ONLY — no numbers assigned here)

Per rule 2/12, ratings happen in the collaborative session. Evidence flags:

- **D1 (AI revenue %) — affected.** Still **zero AI revenue today** (100% BTC mining; first deliveries Q4 2027). What changed: a signed, executed 20-yr lease worth ~$6.6B with an IG tenant is *contracted* future AI revenue, not intent. The rater must decide how D1 treats contracted-but-not-yet-flowing revenue; the prior briefing's re-rate trigger ("a SIGNED AI/HPC lease… would be the event") has fired.
- **D2 (supply-chain position) — affected.** The lease validates CLSK's power+land as a monetizable bottleneck asset at institutional terms (NNN, ~100% NOI margin, escalators, 20 yrs, IG counterparty, tenant exclusivity over the *rest* of the TX portfolio). Sandersville is "the first chapter of a substantially larger relationship" (Ex-99.1).
- **D5 (hyperscaler/AI-buildout tie) — affected.** A confidential "leading global technology company" with high-IG credit committing $6.6–11.6B over 20 years is direct evidence of AI-buildout tie; rated 1 in the pre-lease era. Caveat for the session: one tenant, identity unknown, and TX is only an LOI.
- **D3 (moat) — mildly affected.** The signed terms (second-mover, low-cost power, speed) are evidence about asset quality; weaker signal than D1/D2/D5.
- **R3 (balance sheet/financing) — affected, NEGATIVE direction.** ~$1.75–2.1B build obligation vs $260M cash / ~$925M BTC HODL, LT debt already ~$1.8B, no committed financing, and the lease itself can *terminate* on missed financing milestones. Ex-99.1 pre-announces "significant additional indebtedness." BTC at ~$63k (−22% in 3m) weakens the mining cash engine and the collateral value of the HODL simultaneously. - **R1 (customer concentration) — newly relevant.** The entire AI/HPC story currently rests on **one confidential tenant** (GA lease + TX exclusivity). Concentration risk where previously there was none (BTC mining has no customer concentration).
