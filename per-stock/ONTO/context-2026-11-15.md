## 1. Pre-research mental model

(From the 2026-06-22 briefing plus the 2026-11-14 news-log line, written before pulling new data.) ONTO is a Layer 04 process-control name whose AI exposure is back-end advanced-packaging inspection (Dragonfly G5, 3Di) for 2.5D logic and HBM, plus front-end OCD metrology (Atlas G6) at gate-all-around and DRAM nodes. In June it had printed a Q1 of $291.9M (+9.5% YoY), guided FY2026 growth above 30% (advanced packaging above 50%), and had just raised a $1.3B 0% convert to buy 27% of Rigaku for ~$710M. I expected Q2/Q3 to confirm the acceleration, the balance sheet to have moved from clean net cash toward roughly neutral, and the main open questions to be cycle durability into 2027 and how the market would price a back-end name after a big run.
