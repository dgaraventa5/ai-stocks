ATM GM already 27.3% in Q2. (Q2 2026 call transcript, Globe & Mail / Yahoo Finance 2026-07-30.) The June briefing flagged the reset as coming "next year"; it's now explicitly Q4-2026-triggered.

Co-packaged optics (CPO) to **start low-volume launch by year-end 2026**; fully-automated 310×310mm fan-out panel-level packaging (FOPLP) targets **mass production Q1 2027**; full-process CoWoS "line of sight" for 2027, details deferred ~2 quarters. 5. **OSAT framed as the "next AI bottleneck"; ~15+ new sites building.** Trade press now frames advanced packaging as the emerging AI supply-chain chokepoint, with ASE managing **13 greenfield + 8 brownfield projects simultaneously**. (igorslab.de, "ASE is building 15 new sites…"; Q2 transcript.) Capacity-expansion story (D4) is textbook and accelerating.

## 7. Implications for ratings (FLAG ONLY — do not pre-decide; main loop owns canonical scores)

- **D1 (AI revenue %):** LEAP >$3.5B doubling to ~$7B in 2027 on ~US$22–24B revenue, PLUS AI-driven test + AI-peripheral packaging + now the general segment explicitly "supporting AI infrastructure." AI is unambiguously the growth and profit driver (ATM = 94% of op profit). Still not a *majority* of consolidated revenue directly AI-labeled, but the direct-AI slice is climbing fast and the profit attribution is overwhelming. Prior pass leaned **3 (bordering 4)** — the case for **4** is stronger now. Revisit.
- **D2 (supply-chain position):** #1 OSAT, now openly framed as an AI *bottleneck* with >20% pricing power and pull-forward capex. Prior **4** looks well-supported, possibly firmer.
- **D3 (moat):** the >20% price hikes + pass-through pricing power + widening lead times are the strongest moat evidence yet for a historically commodity business. Prior **3–4** — the upper end is better supported now. Still contestable (TSMC forward-integration in CoWoS, substrate dependence). Revisit.
- **D4 (capacity expansion):** textbook **5** — capex $8.5B→$10.5B, 13 greenfield + 8 brownfield, 2027 doubling. Cleanest 5 in the package; unchanged.
- **D5 (hyperscaler/AI-buildout tie, supplier framework):** packages/tests AI accelerators for the fabless/hyperscaler-silicon ecosystem; the named >10% customer is still Apple (consumer), no disclosed hyperscaler concentration, but the AI-buildout tie is now quantified and dominant in the profit mix. Prior **3** — case for a notch higher exists; keep disciplined (no disclosed hyperscaler anchor). Revisit.
- **Momentum:** M2 relative strength **verified strong** (ADR +250% trailing year, 50DMA% 75.8→90 band, near 52wk high). M1 EPS revisions now a clearer beat-and-raise (Q2 NI +180%, guidance raised on both LEAP and general, forward P/E compressed to 20.4). M1 likely **4**; M2 **4–5**. M3 stays flagged default **3** (no Form 4). Revisit M1/M2 upward.
- **R1 (customer concentration):** unchanged FY2025 disclosure (one customer ~25%, top-5 46.5%) → **3**. No new data.
- **R2 (geographic):** unchanged and still the standout risk — ~58.9% of revenue produced in Taiwan (asset leg), plus dedicated Taiwan power-disruption risk factor; capex doubling *concentrates* more capacity in Taiwan. Per revised R2 bands (asset leg in a risk geography) → **2**. Mandatory rationale: "Taiwan Strait/seismic/grid shock; ~59% of revenue produced in Taiwan (20-F)."
- **R3 (balance sheet):** ND/E crept 0.40→0.47, added US$1.0B convert, FCF negative on the capex ramp — but OCF strongly positive, NT$419B unused credit, EBITDA rising fast. Prior **3–4**; the lever-up is modest and funding accretive growth. Hold ~**3–4**; note the convert overhang.
- **R4 (regulatory/litigation):** no federal docket in RECAP, no material litigation surfaced; routine matters. **4** stands (pending a fresh 20-F Legal Proceedings read at next annual).
- **R5 (disruption risk):** non-Layer-10 → **default 5**. Advanced packaging is becoming *more* essential as scaling shifts value to the package; AI is a tailwind, not a disruptor. Unchanged.

**Thesis-break signals to watch:** (1) AI/LEAP demand air-pocket stranding the now-US$10.5B specialized capex (the 20-F's own named risk — the asymmetric downside, larger than in June because the capex bet is larger); (2) TSMC forward-integrating advanced packaging / compressing OSAT's AI-package share; (3) Taiwan-Strait/seismic/grid shock (now more concentrated capacity); (4) Apple (~25% customer) in-sourcing SiP or shifting volume; (5) advanced-packaging pricing power reversing if capacity floods in 2027.
