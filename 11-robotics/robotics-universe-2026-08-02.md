**Purpose:** Dom flagged that the portfolio is underexposed to robotics. Six parallel research passes (see `research/` — humanoid/industrial, components/vision/sensing, warehouse/service/medical, defense/autonomy/perception, US-EU motion deep pass, machine-vision deep pass) enumerated ~90 public names. This file is the decision record: what enters the Watchlist as new **Layer 11 — Robotics & Physical AI** (plus two Layer-6 sensor-semi adds), what was investigated and excluded (with reasons), and what stays on watch.

**Framing finding (all passes agree):** the existing 173-name watchlist covers the AI "brain" (silicon, cloud, power) heavily; the "body" (actuators, reducers, vision, surgical/warehouse/defense robots) is essentially uncovered — TSLA (Optimus optionality), TER (owns Universal Robots, scored as semi-test), AMBA (edge vision SoC) were the only robotics-adjacent names. ~20 of the KraneShares KOID humanoid ETF's 50 holdings were already on our watchlist — all on the brain side.

**Inclusion bar (per Dom's instruction "don't be selective"):** genuine robotics/physical-AI exposure that is material to the return (the Layer-11-adopter lesson: quality masquerading as AI exposure is disqualifying), plus practical investability (scoreable via yfinance; not going-concern; not A-share/Korea access-blocked; not dead). Speculative names are included **with flags carried into Risk ratings** rather than silently dropped — the SHAZ precedent.
