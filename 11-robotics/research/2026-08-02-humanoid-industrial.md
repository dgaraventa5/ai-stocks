## Bucket 2 — Humanoid supply chain (the actual investable edge)

The structural argument for this bucket: **actuators represent >60% of a humanoid's material cost**, each robot needs **14–28 harmonic drives**, and strain-wave gearbox capacity is precision-bound, capital-intensive and *structurally harder to scale than electronics* — dedicated tooling, metrology, long qualification cycles. That is a textbook bottleneck: the picks-and-shovels layer captures margin before the robot makers do. [McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/industrials/our-insights/turning-humanoid-supply-chain-constraints-into-billion-dollar-wins) / [KraneShares](https://kraneshares.com/humanoid-robotics-in-2026-the-race-from-pilot-to-platform/)

### Harmonic Drive Systems — 6324.T — $3.7B — INCLUDE ⭐
HQ Japan. **The purest listed humanoid-bottleneck asset I found.** FY3/26 net sales ¥59.56B (+7.0%), but operating income collapsed 94.4% YoY to ¥2.57B. Booked **~¥1.3B of humanoid orders in Q2 alone**, guiding to **¥2.5B for FY3/26**, and management indicated the figure could **double or triple in FY3/27** on improving visibility into prototype production orders from **U.S. clients**. FY3/27 guidance: sales ¥68.0B (+14.2%), OP ¥6.2B (**+141.5%**), net income ¥4.5B (+179.7%). [Quartr](https://quartr.com/events/harmonic-drive-systems-inc-6324-q4-2026_F9xDWNfe) / [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/this-japanese-stock-is-surging-on-positive-outlook-for-humanoid-robot-orders-4367329)

**Thesis:** World #1 in precision strain-wave reducers — the single component every humanoid design needs 14-28 of, and the one that cannot be capacity-added quickly. Humanoid is only ~4% of revenue today, which is precisely the point: you are buying the ramp, not the run-rate, and management has now put dated, quantified order numbers behind it with named U.S. prototype customers. The +141% OP guidance means the earnings trough is explicitly behind it.
**Red flags:** **No US ADR line at all** — Tokyo-listed only, requires international access, JPY-denominated. Operating margin is currently depressed to near-nothing (4.3%), so the multiple on trailing earnings looks absurd. Direct Chinese price attack from Leaderdrive at 30-40% below Japanese pricing is the core bear case. Highly cyclical to semicap and industrial robots.

HQ Germany. Revenue €23.3B. The most *contractually* advanced Western humanoid actuator supplier. Three developments, all 2026:

**Thesis:** Signed three humanoid actuator partnerships in five months — no other Western component supplier has that density of *binding* design wins, and a 5-year >50%-share preferred-supplier contract is the strongest contractual evidence of a real ramp anywhere in this research. ## My read on where the actual edge is

If the goal is independent conviction rather than theme-chasing, three observations:

1. **The bottleneck layer is where the evidence is strongest and the crowding is lowest.** Harmonic Drive, Schaeffler, Regal Rexnord, Novanta and Allient all have *dated, quantified, named* humanoid design wins or order books. Most of the humanoid-maker names have narratives and valuations but not order books. Rule-14 expectations-flag discipline matters enormously here — VPG (+269% YTD) and Ouster (+150% YTD) are exactly the "peak multiple" profile the flag exists to catch.

2. **The single most actionable US-listed names are RRX, ALNT, NOVT and SYM** — NYSE/Nasdaq, liquid, no ADR currency handling, no Stock Connect problem. Everything more levered than these (Harmonic Drive, Hiwin, Hyundai Mobis, the entire Chinese Optimus complex) carries an access or currency penalty that our rule-19 machinery can handle for the Japanese/European ADRs but *cannot* solve for the A-shares.

3. **ABB needs a thesis correction now.** It is on our watchlist and is divesting the exact business that would justify a robotics-linked AI-thesis rating. That's a rule-6/rule-12 action item independent of anything else in this research.
