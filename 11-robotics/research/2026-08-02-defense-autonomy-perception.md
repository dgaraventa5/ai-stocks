## The single most important 2026 fact

The **FY2027 DoD budget request contains ~$75B for drones and autonomy** — $53.6B for autonomy/drone platforms/contested logistics plus $21B for munitions, counter-UAS and CCA ([Greenberg Traurig FY27 budget analysis](https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2026/5/understanding-the-presidents-fy-2027-budget-request-for-the-department-of-war)). Within that, the new **Defense Autonomous Warfare Group jumps from $225M (FY26) to $54.6B (FY27)** — a ~240x line-item increase, and a new sub-unified command for autonomous warfare ([InsideDefense](https://insidedefense.com/daily-news/funding-new-autonomous-drone-warfare-group-slated-skyrocket-546b-fy-27), [DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2026/04/21/dod-plans-largest-ever-investment-drones-anti-drone-weapons/)).

**And the cohort de-rated violently into it.** From 52-week highs: AVAV −64%, KTOS −65%, RCAT −60%, MBLY −50%, JOBY −66%, ACHR −68%. Backlogs went the other way ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/analysis/drone-stocks-are-down-but-defense-backlogs-tell-a-different-story-200683545)). This is a valuation-reset-into-a-budget-inflection setup — exactly the asymmetry this project looks for, and not a consensus-crowded trade right now.

**Private cohort stays private.** Anduril is $61B post-Series H (May 2026), holds a $20B/10yr Army enterprise contract (Mar 2026) — but its CEO said in July 2026 it's bad to IPO "in the middle of a hype cycle"; IPO delayed ([Value Add VC](https://valueaddvc.com/blog/anduril-ipo-2026-valuation-defense-tech-palmer-luckey)). Shield AI ($12.7B), Saronic ($9.25B), Skydio ($4.5B) all still private. **There is no public Anduril proxy** — the listed names are the only access.

**Thesis:** Marine Corps made Valkyrie a program of record — Northrop won MUX TACAIR CCA using Kratos' Valkyrie as the airframe ([Kratos/Northrop, Jan 2026](https://www.kratosdefense.com/newsroom/northrop-grumman-to-rapidly-develop-marine-corps-cca-with-kratos-valkyrie-uas)). ## The eVTOL call: OUT OF SCOPE — EXCLUDE JOBY and ACHR

1. **The binding constraint is certification, not autonomy.** Both launch with pilots; no US eVTOL certifies in 2026 ([Low Altitude Economy](https://lowaltitudeeconomy.aero/evtol-news-and-electric-aircraft-news/advanced-air-mobility/no-us-evtol-will-certify-2026)). Autonomy is a 2030s option; owning them is an FAA-process/aerospace-manufacturing bet wearing an autonomy costume.
2. ACHR TTM revenue $1.90M vs $3.52B cap; JOBY $77.7M revenue, −$957M net loss, $7.03B cap. No robotics revenue line to score.
3. The genuine autonomy angle accrues to partners (Anduril "Omen" licensed Archer's powertrain; Joby's S4-T with L3Harris).
4. Cleaner expression exists: KTOS and AVAV have revenue, backlog, programs of record today.

**Revisit trigger:** Group 4/5 UAS program-of-record win with disclosed value >$300M, or a certified uncrewed variant ships. **EH (EHang)** — only certified pilotless eVTOL (CAAC) but Q1 2026 revenue US$3.7M, 40% aerial light shows. EXCLUDE.
