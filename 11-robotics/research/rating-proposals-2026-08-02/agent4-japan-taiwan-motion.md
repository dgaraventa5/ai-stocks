## Method notes applying to all five

- **D1 convention (proposed for Layer 11):** score revenue tied to *robotics/precision-motion as the demand driver* (the layer's thesis object), discounted where the exposure is AI-*adjacent* (industrial-automation capex) rather than AI-*driven*. Humanoid-ramp specificity is carried by D5, not double-counted in D1.
- **D5 = "L11 lens (proposed)"** — strength of contracted/quantified humanoid tie, on rule-23's parallel 5-max-for-role scale. Bands used: **5** material quantified humanoid revenue w/ named commitments · **4** quantified orders, small base, dated ramp, identified prospects · **3** shipping samples/small batches into identified programs · **2** roadmap/lineup only · **1** marketing only.
- **M2 benchmark:** XLI **+20%** in 1H 2026 ([etfdb](https://etfdb.com/sector-investing-content-hub/xlk-xle-xli-top-performing-sector-spdrs/)).
- **M3 = 3 "foreign filer default"** for the four Asian names — no Form 4 regime. This is a *data gap*, not a judgment.
- **R5 = 5** for all five (rule 16: non-Layer-10 default; physical components, AI is demand not substitution).
