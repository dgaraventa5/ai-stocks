## SYM — Symbotic Inc. | US | $26.0B | **INCLUDE**

**Robotics %: ~100%.** Pure-play warehouse automation: "Our robotic based automation systems, which include hardware and essential software, move, store and sort cases and eaches in warehouses" ([10-Q Q2 FY2026, filed 2026-05-06, MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1837240/000183724026000024/sym-20260328.htm)).

**2026 state — verified from the primary filing:**
- **Backlog $22.7B** as of March 28, 2026, "of which our agreements with Walmart and Exol comprise the vast majority" (10-Q Q2 FY2026). Conversion is slow: only ~14% of remaining performance obligations convert to revenue in the next 12 months, ~61% over the following 13–60 months.
- **H1 FY2026 revenue $1,306.5M, +26% YoY** (Systems $1,224.8M +25%, Software maint. $23.8M +95%, Operation services $57.9M +24%). Systems in Deployment 70 vs 46 prior year; Operational Systems under software contracts 52 vs 37 (10-Q Q2 FY2026).
- **Walmart Advanced Systems & Robotics (ASR) — the actual date is January 27, 2025, not 2026.** $200.0M cash for all outstanding equity, alongside the 2025 Walmart Master Automation Agreement (10-Q Q2 FY2026, Note on business combinations). Several press articles date this to January 2026 — **that is wrong**; the purchase-price allocation was merely *finalized* in Q2 FY2026. The MAA's contingent promise to purchase 400 store-level online-pickup/delivery systems "could increase the Company's future remaining performance obligation by more than $5.0 billion."
- **Q3 FY2026 guidance:** revenue $700–720M, adj. EBITDA $80–85M ([Zacks/TradingView preview](https://www.tradingview.com/news/zacks:e118c7ba5094b:0-symbotic-gears-up-to-report-q3-earnings-here-s-what-to-expect/)). Q3 report due imminently (Q2 was filed 2026-05-06).

**Thesis:** The only US-listed pure-play at industrial scale in the highest-conviction physical-AI end market. $22.7B backlog against $2.5B TTM revenue is ~9 years of contracted work, and the ASR deal opened a genuinely new category (store-level micro-fulfillment) that Walmart alone could grow by >$5B of RPO. Software/services lines are compounding faster (+95% / +24%) than Systems, which is the margin-mix story that matters.

**Red flags (serious):**
1. **Customer concentration is the whole story: Customer A = 84.5% of Q2 FY2026 revenue, 85.0% of H1 FY2026, and 68.7% of accounts receivable** (10-Q Q2 FY2026, "Significant Customers"). The filing states plainly: "The concentration in the volume of business transacted with this customer may lead to a material impact on the Company's results from operations if a total or partial loss of the business relationship were to occur." This is Walmart. Diversification talk (Target, Albertsons, C&S, the Exol/SoftBank JV) has not moved the number — it was 85.9% a year ago.
2. **Accounting history:** restated three FY2024 quarters in Dec 2024 over revenue recognition ([Hagens Berman](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/01/09/3007311/32716/en/Symbotic-SYM-Restates-Financial-Results-Amid-Securities-Class-Action-Over-Improper-Accounting-Hagens-Berman.html)). Securities class actions **still pending** — *Decker v. Symbotic* (24-cv-12976) and amended *Traina v. Symbotic* (24-cv-12196), plus derivative suits consolidated 2025-11-24 (10-Q Q2 FY2026, Legal Proceedings).
3. **FLAG — inference, not a documented fact:** the SEC subpoena and Rule 21F-17 inquiry disclosed in the FY2025 Q3 10-Q **do not appear anywhere** in the FY2025 10-K (filed 2025-11-24) or the Q2 FY2026 10-Q — I grepped both full documents; zero hits on "subpoena." That is consistent with resolution/closure but the filings do not affirmatively say so. Do not assume it is cleanly resolved without confirming.
4. Valuation: 10.3x TTM sales, ~60x forward earnings, on 20.4% gross margin. Stock is up >255% YTD per [Barchart](https://markets.financialcontent.com/borgernewsherald/article/barchart-2025-12-1-symbotic-stock-surges-on-automation-driven-revenue-growth-is-sym-a-buy-now-for-2026) — this is a crowded-momentum name, exactly the profile CLAUDE.md rule 14 (expectations red-flag) exists to catch.

**Bucket:** warehouse-logistics. **Verdict: INCLUDE** — the segment's anchor name, but score the concentration honestly (R1 should be near the floor).
