# Robotics Components Roster — Precision Motion, Machine Vision, Sensing (2026-08-02)

*Research agent deliverable (segment pass: precision motion / machine vision / robot-grade sensing). Preserved verbatim as scoring evidence.*

## The single most important finding

**The theme is real; the US-investable expression of it is thin.** The four companies with the highest genuine humanoid revenue content — Harmonic Drive, Hiwin, Minebea Mitsumi, Melexis — are either unlisted in the US or have ADRs with near-zero volume. Meanwhile a SEC full-text search across **all** 10-K/10-Q filings from 2026-01-01 to today returns **zero** filers mentioning "strain wave," "harmonic drive," or "force torque sensor," and only 24 mentioning "humanoid" at all. Cognex, Zebra, Novanta, Teradyne, Regal Rexnord, Allient, Moog, Ametek, Parker, Timken, RBC and Sensata mention humanoids **nowhere** in their 2026 filings. For US-listed names, humanoid leverage is narrative, not disclosed materiality — Allegro is the notable exception.

## Detail — motion components

### HSYDF / 6324.T — Harmonic Drive Systems (Japan, ~$3.8B)
The classic humanoid pick-and-shovel, and the most over-owned. ~80–85% global share of strain-wave reducers; humanoids use 20–30 joints and 15–40 precision reducers vs a handful in an industrial arm ([Next Financial](https://nextfinancial.substack.com/p/the-joint-problem-who-owns-the-most)).

- FY3/26 actual net sales **¥59,557M**; operating profit revised to **¥2,500M** from ¥1,500M on "steady demand for robot and semiconductor manufacturing equipment" ([TDnet revision, 2026-04-21](https://finance.biggo.com/news/jpx_tdnet_140120260421507113)).
- FY3/27 guidance: sales ¥68,000M (+14.2%), OP ¥6,200M (+141.5%), NI ¥4,500M (+179.7%).
- Humanoid orders ~¥1.3B in one quarter, ~¥2.5B expected FY3/26 — roughly **4% of revenue today**. Management targets ¥10–20B; Macquarie called even ¥10B by FY3/27 "aggressive" ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/this-japanese-stock-is-surging-on-positive-outlook-for-humanoid-robot-orders-4367329)).

**Red flags, and they are serious.** Computed from the local line: **P/S 9.8x, trailing P/E 361x, forward P/E ~129x** on guided FY3/27 net income. Stock +242% YoY ([note.com survey](https://note.com/umaki11/n/n8e511bfa3dfc?hl=en)). Chinese suppliers took harmonic share from **15% (2022) to ~38% (Q1 2024)** and undercut on price by **20–40%**; Zhejiang Shuanghuan adds 500,000 units/yr of Suzhou capacity in 2026. Currency trap (USD trade / JPY report) requires rule-19 handling. **Verdict: INCLUDE for tracking — but this is priced for a capture rate it is not yet demonstrating.**

### NCTKY / 6268.T — Nabtesco (Japan, ~$3.7B)
The better-value way to own the same theme, and the one most likely to be underappreciated. Primary source: [FY2025 results briefing, 2026-02-19](https://www.nabtesco.com/cms/wp-content/uploads/Results_Briefing_Material_for_FY2025_e.pdf).

- FY2025/12: sales **¥307.9B (+10%)**, OP ¥20.7B (+60%), OPM 6.7%. **CMP segment (precision reduction gears) = ¥79.3B, i.e. 25.8% of revenue**, OP ¥5.4B.
- FY2026/12 guide: sales ¥327.0B, OP ¥27.7B (+34%). CMP guided to ¥88.6B, and **precision reducers are the single largest growth contributor (+¥9.6B of the +¥19.1B total)**.
- ~60% global share of RV cycloidal reducers. Orders rebounded four consecutive quarters from 2025/Q1.
- Slide 7: the 2026-onward lineup map explicitly adds **Humanoid, Cobot, SCARA and 3C/semiconductor**, launching RVmini/Monocrank **and a "wave gear system"** — i.e. Nabtesco is entering strain-wave, attacking Harmonic Drive's core.

**Valuation: P/S 1.78x, trailing P/E 27.9x** — roughly a fifth of HDS's multiple for 26% disclosed robotics revenue. Red flags: humanoid is optionality not revenue (2026 CMP growth is industrial-robot recovery from EV capex in China/Korea); diversified across railway, aircraft, marine and automatic doors; **ADR median $19k/day**. **Verdict: INCLUDE.**

## Detail — sensing

### ALGM — Allegro MicroSystems (US, $7.7B) — INCLUDE, top pick in bucket
The only US-listed component name with humanoid exposure written into its **10-K** ([FY2026 10-K, filed 2026-05-21](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/866291/000119312526233537/algm-20260327.htm)):

> "Robotics applications, including warehouse, industrial, collaborative, and humanoid robots, require high-accuracy, reliability, and safety specifications that align closely with our automotive ADAS heritage."

The 10-K also names "the rapidly expanding AI data center cooling and humanoid robotics markets" and describes "multiple Allegro devices within a single robotic platform" per joint. FY2026 (ended 2026-03-27) net sales **$890.1M, +22.8%**; magnetic sensors $538.5M, +13.5%. Content ladder: **~$5 in a home robot, ~$55 in a cobot, ~$150 in a humanoid**. Design wins secured with large Chinese humanoid OEMs (current sensors) and a North American humanoid company (inductive position sensors in joints). Humanoids need **30–50 magnetic sensors, ~2x EV density**.

Red flags, stated plainly: robotics revenue is **not broken out** (it sits inside "Industrial and other"); management says humanoid becomes meaningful only around **2030–31**; the company posted a **GAAP net loss of $14.9M** in FY2026, so yfinance's 544x trailing P/E is unusable — use P/S 8.2x. Liquidity $102M/day, currency-clean.

### NOVT — Novanta (US, $5.1B) — INCLUDE
The answer to the force/torque question. Novanta owns **ATI Industrial Automation**, the dominant multi-axis force/torque sensor supplier, and has launched a **Varo force/torque sensor for humanoids**; over a third of the business serves robotics/automation. It is a **1.85% weight in the KOID humanoid ETF** ([KraneShares](https://kraneshares.com/humanoid-robotics-in-2026-the-race-from-pilot-to-platform/)). $68.6M/day, currency-clean, P/S 5.05x, and up only ~19% over a year — no crowding. Not a pure play (medical/advanced-industrial diversified).

### VPG, MELE.BR — BORDERLINE
- **VPG ($1.2B)** — strain gauges and load cells are the sensing element inside most force/torque sensors, and VPG markets them for humanoids. But **+257% over one year and now 39% below its high**, with operating margin of 0.9% and no disclosed robotics revenue. A broken momentum trade, flagged rather than recommended.
- **Melexis** — the only sensing name with humanoid revenue **booking today** (CEO Q2 2026: "we have now real business... real order," anchored on a US humanoid customer, plus Tactaxis tactile and Arcminaxis joint-specific magnetics). **MLXSF trades zero shares/day** — only actionable on Brussels.

### Confirmed: no pure-play force/torque or encoder company is publicly investable
ATI sits inside Novanta. Schunk, OnRobot, Robotiq, Bota Systems are private. Heidenhain is private by structure (foundation-owned). Renishaw is public but its US line traded **2 days out of 63**.

## Sizing reality check
BofA projects **~90,000 humanoid shipments in 2026**, rising to 1.2M by 2030; the 2025 China-built BOM was ~$35,000, heading below $17,000 by 2030. Actuators are **40–50% of build cost, 28–44 per robot, $16,000–40,000 per robot at low volume**. That implies a 2026 humanoid actuator TAM in the low billions — real, but Harmonic Drive's ~$17M of humanoid orders against it says the **Japanese incumbents are not capturing it; Chinese suppliers are**. As of mid-2026 no humanoid has been deployed above the low hundreds in sustained commercial production.
