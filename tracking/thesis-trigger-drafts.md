**Rests on:** context-2026-10-28 §1 (Azure +48%, Microsoft Cloud +30%, commercial RPO $677B). The OpenAI route rests on thesis section 1 and context-2026-10-18 §3 item 2 (RPO +84%, but +25% excluding OpenAI). Thesis section 1 owns it for paying AI demand through Azure and Copilot, with buildout cost and OpenAI as the risk.

**Threshold:** my judgment

**Caveats:** Weak support. The newest briefing did not read the call or the second 8-K and has no capex, guidance or AI run-rate figure. The OpenAI commitment is not in it; that fact comes from the 2026-10-18 briefing, which cites a call recap. 30% is my line, set at Microsoft Cloud's current growth rate. Do not use a bare 'RPO fell' test: RPO was $678B at FY26 year-end and is $677B now, so it would already be met.
